# Q100-61-ACC-NACELLE-FWD — Forward Nacelle Access

## Location

Upper centerline of nacelle forward section.

## Access Provisions

- Fan blade visual inspection
- Pitch mechanism viewing
- Spinner retention verification

## Opening Procedure

1. Release 4 quarter-turn latches
2. Lift panel from forward hinge
3. Support panel in open position

## Inspection Frequency

Daily/Preflight inspection required.
