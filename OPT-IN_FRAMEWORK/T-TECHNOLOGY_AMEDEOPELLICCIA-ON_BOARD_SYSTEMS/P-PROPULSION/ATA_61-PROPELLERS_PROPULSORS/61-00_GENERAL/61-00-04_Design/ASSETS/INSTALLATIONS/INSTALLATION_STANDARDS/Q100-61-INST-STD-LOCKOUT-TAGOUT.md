# Q100-61-INST-STD-LOCKOUT-TAGOUT — Lockout/Tagout Procedures

## Purpose

Energy isolation requirements for propulsion system maintenance.

## Scope

All maintenance activities requiring:
- Electrical isolation
- Hydraulic depressurization
- Stored energy release

## Energy Sources

| Source | Voltage/Pressure | Isolation Point |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| DC power bus | 800 VDC | Main disconnect |
| AC motor power | 560 VAC | Controller disconnect |
| Hydraulic pitch | 200 bar | Accumulator bleed valve |
| Cooling system | 6 bar | Pump isolation valve |
| Lube system | 10 bar | Pump isolation valve |

## Lockout Procedure

1. Notify all affected personnel
2. Shut down equipment normally
3. Identify all energy sources
4. Isolate each energy source
5. Apply lockout devices
6. Apply "DO NOT OPERATE" tags
7. Release stored energy
8. Verify zero energy state

## Tagout Requirements

Each tag must include:
- Worker name
- Date and time
- Reason for lockout
- Expected duration
- Contact information

## Removal

1. Verify work complete
2. Verify all personnel clear
3. Verify guards replaced
4. Remove lockout devices
5. Remove tags
6. Notify personnel
7. Restore energy
