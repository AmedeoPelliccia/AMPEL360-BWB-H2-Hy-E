# Q100-61-ACC-NACELLE-AFT — Aft Nacelle Access

## Location

Upper section of nacelle aft.

## Access Provisions

- Motor rear section access
- Cooling line connections
- Electrical umbilical inspection

## Opening Procedure

1. Release 12 Dzus fasteners
2. Remove panel and store safely

## Inspection Frequency

A-Check interval.
