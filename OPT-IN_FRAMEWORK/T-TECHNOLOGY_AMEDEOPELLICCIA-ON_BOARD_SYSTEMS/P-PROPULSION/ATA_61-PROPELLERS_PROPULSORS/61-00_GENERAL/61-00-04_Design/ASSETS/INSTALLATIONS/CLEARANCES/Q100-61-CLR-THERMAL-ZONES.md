# Q100-61-CLR-THERMAL-ZONES — Thermal Clearance Zones

## Overview

Defines clearance requirements from heat-generating components.

## Thermal Zones

### Zone 1 — Hot Zone (Motor Housing)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Surface temperature | Up to 120°C |
| Clearance from cables | 50 mm minimum |
| Clearance from plastics | 75 mm minimum |
| Clearance from composites | 50 mm minimum |

### Zone 2 — Warm Zone (Controller)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Surface temperature | Up to 85°C |
| Clearance from cables | 25 mm minimum |
| Clearance from plastics | 50 mm minimum |

### Zone 3 — Cooling Lines

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Surface temperature | Up to 60°C |
| Clearance | Standard routing |

## Material Limitations

| Material | Max Temp | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Standard wiring | 105°C | Use high-temp in Zone 1 |
| PTFE wiring | 200°C | Suitable all zones |
| Composite structure | 120°C | Avoid Zone 1 contact |
| Rubber seals | 100°C | Silicone in hot zones |
