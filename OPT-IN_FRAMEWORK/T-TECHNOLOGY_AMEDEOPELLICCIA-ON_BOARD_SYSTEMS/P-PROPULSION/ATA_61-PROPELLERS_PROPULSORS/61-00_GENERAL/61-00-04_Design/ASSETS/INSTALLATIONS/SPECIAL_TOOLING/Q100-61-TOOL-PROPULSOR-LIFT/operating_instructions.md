# Q100-61-TOOL-PROPULSOR-LIFT — Operating Instructions

## Purpose

Safely lift and position the propulsor assembly for installation or removal.

## Capacity

- Maximum load: 1,000 kg
- Safety factor: 4:1

## Pre-Use Inspection

1. Inspect all welds for cracks
2. Check attachment hardware condition
3. Verify safety clips present
4. Check spreader bar condition
5. Inspect sling attachment points

## Operating Procedure

1. Position fixture over propulsor
2. Align bolt holes with propulsor lift points
3. Install attachment bolts (4x)
4. Torque bolts to 35 Nm
5. Attach crane hook to central lift point
6. Verify safety latch engaged
7. Perform lift test (50mm, 2 min hold)
8. Proceed with lift operation

## Safety Warnings

⚠️ Never exceed rated capacity

⚠️ Always use tag lines

⚠️ Maintain exclusion zone during lift
