## Fluid Systems

### Cooling System

- **Medium**: Propylene glycol/water 50/50
- **Pressure**: 6 bar max
- **Temperature**: 35-60°C
- **Flow rate**: 50 LPM per propulsor

### Lubrication System

- **Medium**: MIL-PRF-23699 synthetic oil
- **Pressure**: 10 bar max
- **Temperature**: 40-90°C
- **Flow rate**: 15 LPM
