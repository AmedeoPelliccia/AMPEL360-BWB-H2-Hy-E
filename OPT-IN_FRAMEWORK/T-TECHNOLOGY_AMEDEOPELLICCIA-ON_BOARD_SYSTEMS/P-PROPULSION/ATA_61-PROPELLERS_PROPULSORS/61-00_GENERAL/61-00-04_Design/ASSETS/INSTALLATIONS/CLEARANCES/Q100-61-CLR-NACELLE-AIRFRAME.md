# Q100-61-CLR-NACELLE-AIRFRAME — Nacelle to Airframe Clearance

## Overview

Defines clearance between nacelle structure and BWB airframe.

## Clearance Requirements

| Location | Minimum | Design Target |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| Nacelle to pylon | 25 mm | 40 mm |
| Nacelle to wing | 100 mm | 150 mm |
| Nacelle to fuselage | 150 mm | 200 mm |

## Dynamic Considerations

| Motion | Allowance |
|--------|-----------|
| Wing flex (1g to 2.5g) | 50 mm |
| Pylon deflection | 15 mm |
| Thermal expansion | 5 mm |

## Maintenance Access

| Zone | Clearance Required |
|------|-------------------|
| Upper access | 1,000 mm envelope |
| Lower access | 800 mm envelope |
| Forward access | 600 mm envelope |
