# Q100-61-INST-STD-SAFETY-WIRE — Safety Wire Requirements

## Purpose

Safety wire installation requirements per MS33540.

## Wire Specifications

| Application | Wire Size | Material |
|-------------|-----------|----------|
| Standard | 0.032" | Stainless steel |
| Fine | 0.020" | Stainless steel |
| Heavy | 0.041" | Stainless steel |

## Installation Requirements

- Direction: Wire in direction to prevent loosening
- Twist rate: 6-8 twists per inch
- Pigtail: 3-6 turns, end tucked
- Maximum span: 6 inches between fasteners

## Patterns

### Single Wire

For individual fasteners, wire to adjacent structure.

### Double Wire

For pairs of fasteners, wire in opposing directions.

### Series Wire

For multiple fasteners in line.

## Inspection

- No nicks or kinks
- Proper twist rate
- Correct tension (not loose, not over-tight)
- Pigtail properly formed
