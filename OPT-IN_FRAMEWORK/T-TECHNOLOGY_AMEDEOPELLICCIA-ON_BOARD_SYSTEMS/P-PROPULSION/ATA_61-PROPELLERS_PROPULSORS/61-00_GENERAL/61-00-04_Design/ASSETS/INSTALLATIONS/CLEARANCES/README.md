# CLEARANCES — Envelope and Clearance Definitions

## Purpose

This directory contains clearance envelope definitions for the propulsion system, ensuring adequate space for:

- Operational movements
- Thermal expansion
- Maintenance access
- Safety zones

## Clearance Categories

### Operational Clearances

Static and dynamic clearances required during normal operation.

### Maintenance Clearances

Access space required for inspection, repair, and replacement.

### Thermal Clearances

Separation required from hot zones to prevent damage.
