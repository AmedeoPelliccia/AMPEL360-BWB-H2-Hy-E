# Q100-61-CLR-BLADE-TIP — Blade Tip Clearance

## Overview

Defines the clearance requirements for fan blade tips.

## Clearance Requirements

| Location | Minimum | Design Target |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| Tip to nacelle (radial) | 50 mm | 65 mm |
| Tip to ground (static) | TBD | Per aircraft |
| Tip to adjacent structure | 100 mm | 150 mm |

## Allowances Included

| Factor | Allowance |
|--------|-----------|
| Thermal growth | 3 mm |
| Centrifugal growth | 2 mm |
| Vibration | ±2 mm |
| Manufacturing tolerance | 2 mm |

## Operating Conditions

| Condition | Tip Growth |
|-----------|------------|
| Cold static | 0 mm (datum) |
| Ground idle | +2 mm |
| Max continuous | +4 mm |
| Max transient | +5 mm |
