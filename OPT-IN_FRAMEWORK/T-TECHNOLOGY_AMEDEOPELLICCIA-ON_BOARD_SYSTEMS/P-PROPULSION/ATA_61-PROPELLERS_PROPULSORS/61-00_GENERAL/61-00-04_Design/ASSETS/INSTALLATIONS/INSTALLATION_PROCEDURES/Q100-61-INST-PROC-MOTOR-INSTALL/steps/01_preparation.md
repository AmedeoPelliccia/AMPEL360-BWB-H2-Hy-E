# Step 01 — Preparation

## Purpose

Prepare workspace and verify equipment for motor installation.

## Instructions

1. Verify gearbox installation is complete
2. Stage motor on transport cart
3. Set up laser alignment system
4. Position lifting equipment
5. Review alignment requirements

## Verification

- [ ] Gearbox inspection complete
- [ ] Motor staged and inspected
- [ ] Alignment equipment ready
- [ ] Lift equipment in position
