# Q100-61-INST-STD-ALIGNMENT-TOL — Alignment Tolerances

## Purpose

Alignment tolerances for propulsion system component installations.

## Motor-to-Gearbox Alignment

| Parameter | Limit | Target |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Angular misalignment | 0.5 mrad | 0.2 mrad |
| Parallel offset | 0.05 mm | 0.02 mm |
| Axial position | ±1.0 mm | ±0.5 mm |

## Propulsor-to-Pylon Alignment

| Parameter | Limit | Target |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Forward mount position | ±0.5 mm | ±0.25 mm |
| Aft mount position | ±0.3 mm | ±0.15 mm |
| Thrust link adjustment | ±5 mm | ±2 mm |

## Fan Balance

| Parameter | Limit | Target |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Static imbalance | 10 g·mm | 5 g·mm |
| Dynamic imbalance | 50 g·mm² | 25 g·mm² |

## Measurement Methods

- Laser alignment for shaft alignment
- Dial indicators for position
- Balance machine for fan assembly
