# Step 03 — Positioning

## Purpose

Lift the propulsor into position and align with the pylon mounting interfaces.

## Safety Warnings

⚠️ **WARNING**: Maintain exclusion zone during lift operations.

⚠️ **WARNING**: Use tag lines to control propulsor swing.

⚠️ **CAUTION**: Stop lift if any interference is observed.

## Tools Required

- Crane with operator
- Tag lines (2x)
- Alignment jig Q100-61-TOOL-002
- Dial indicators
- Flashlights for inspection

## Instructions

### 3.1 Pre-Lift Verification

1. Verify pylon mount surfaces are clean
2. Inspect pylon fittings for damage or corrosion
3. Verify guide pins are in place (if applicable)
4. Position alignment jig on pylon aft mount
5. Clear all personnel from exclusion zone

### 3.2 Lift to Position

1. Signal crane operator to begin lift
2. Raise propulsor slowly (max 0.5 m/min)
3. Tag line operators control swing
4. Position propulsor approximately 300mm below pylon
5. Pause and verify clearances

### 3.3 Horizontal Positioning

1. Move propulsor forward toward pylon
2. Tag line operators guide alignment
3. Align propulsor forward mount holes with pylon
4. Continue forward until approximately 50mm from pylon
5. Pause and verify alignment

### 3.4 Final Alignment

1. Install alignment pins in forward mount (hand-tight)
2. Raise propulsor to engage forward mount
3. Verify alignment pin engagement
4. Lower propulsor aft to engage aft mount
5. Verify aft mount alignment with alignment jig
6. Adjust position as needed until aligned within tolerance

### 3.5 Load Transfer

1. Once aligned, continue lowering until weight transfers to mounts
2. Verify propulsor is fully seated on mounts
3. Maintain crane support until fasteners installed (Step 4)
4. Do not release crane until instructed

## Verification

- [ ] Pylon surfaces inspected and clean
- [ ] Propulsor lifted and positioned without interference
- [ ] Forward mount aligned and pins engaged
- [ ] Aft mount aligned per alignment jig
- [ ] Weight transferred to mounts
- [ ] Crane maintaining support

## Notes

- If alignment cannot be achieved, lower propulsor and investigate
- Photograph alignment pin engagement for records
- Record any shimming required
