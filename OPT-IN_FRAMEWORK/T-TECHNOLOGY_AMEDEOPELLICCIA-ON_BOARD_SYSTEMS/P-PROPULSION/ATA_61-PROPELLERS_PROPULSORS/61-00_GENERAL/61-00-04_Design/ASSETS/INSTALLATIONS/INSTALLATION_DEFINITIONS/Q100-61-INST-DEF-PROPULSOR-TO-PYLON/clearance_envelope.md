# Q100-61-INST-DEF-PROPULSOR-TO-PYLON — Clearance Envelope

## 1. Overview

This document defines the clearance envelopes for the propulsor-to-pylon installation, covering operational clearances, maintenance access zones, and thermal exclusion areas.

## 2. Operational Clearances

### 2.1 Blade Tip Clearance

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Minimum radial clearance | 50 mm | From nacelle inner surface |
| Blade growth allowance | 5 mm | Thermal + centrifugal |
| Foreign object margin | 10 mm | Additional safety margin |
| **Total envelope** | **65 mm** | Blade tip to structure |

### 2.2 Nacelle-to-Pylon Clearance

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Forward clearance | 25 mm | Minimum gap |
| Aft clearance | 25 mm | Minimum gap |
| Vibration allowance | ±5 mm | Dynamic motion |
| **Minimum static gap** | **30 mm** | With vibration margin |

### 2.3 Nacelle-to-Airframe Clearance

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Inboard clearance | 100 mm | Wing/body junction |
| Outboard clearance | 50 mm | Wing tip direction |
| Ground clearance | TBD | See aircraft-level spec |

## 4. Thermal Zones

### 4.1 Motor Thermal Zone

| Zone | Temperature Range | Clearance |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Hot zone (motor housing) | Up to 120°C | 50 mm to combustibles |
| Warm zone (cooling lines) | Up to 80°C | 25 mm to wiring |
| Ambient zone | Up to 60°C | Standard clearances |

### 4.2 Controller Thermal Zone

| Zone | Temperature Range | Clearance |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Hot zone (power electronics) | Up to 100°C | 30 mm to combustibles |
| Heat sink zone | Up to 85°C | 20 mm air gap |
| Ambient zone | Up to 55°C | Standard clearances |

## 5. Clearance Verification

### 5.1 Static Verification

- Dimensional inspection after installation
- Go/no-go gauge checks at critical points
- Photographic documentation

### 5.2 Dynamic Verification

- Ground run vibration check
- Blade tracking verification
- Thermal survey during operation
