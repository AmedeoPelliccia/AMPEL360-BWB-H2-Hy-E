# Q100-61-INST-STD-SEALANT-SPEC — Sealant and Adhesive Specifications

## Purpose

Approved sealants and adhesives for propulsion system installations.

## Approved Sealants

| Application | Material | Part Number | Cure Time |
|-------------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Thread sealant | PTFE | P/S 890 | Immediate |
| Faying surface | Polysulfide | PR-1440 | 24 hours |
| Fuel resistant | Fluorosilicone | MIL-S-83430 | 24 hours |
| High temp | Silicone RTV | RTV-106 | 24 hours |

## Application Notes

### Thread Sealant

- Apply thin coat to male threads only
- Do not over-apply
- Verify not contaminating electrical contacts

### Faying Surface Sealant

- Apply thin bead to mating surfaces
- Install fasteners before cure
- Fillet excess sealant

### High Temperature

- Use only where specified
- Clean surfaces with MEK before application
- Allow full cure before operation
