# Step 02 — Rigging

## Purpose

Properly rig the propulsor assembly for lifting and transport to the installation position.

## Safety Warnings

⚠️ **WARNING**: Never stand under suspended load.

⚠️ **WARNING**: Only certified riggers shall attach lifting equipment.

⚠️ **CAUTION**: Do not exceed 10° tilt during lift.

## Tools Required

- Lift fixture Q100-61-TOOL-001
- Spreader bar
- Lifting slings (4x rated 500 kg minimum)
- Shackles (4x rated 1000 kg minimum)
- Tag lines (2x minimum 5m length)

## Instructions

### 2.1 Lift Fixture Installation

1. Position lift fixture over propulsor
2. Align fixture mounting holes with propulsor lifting points
3. Install attachment bolts (4x NAS6204-12)
4. Torque bolts to 35 Nm ±5%
5. Verify engagement of all attachment points

### 2.2 Spreader Bar Connection

1. Attach spreader bar to lift fixture center point
2. Verify clevis pin engagement and safety clip
3. Attach main lift hook to spreader bar center
4. Verify hook safety latch is engaged

### 2.3 Sling Verification

1. Inspect all slings for damage, wear, or fraying
2. Verify sling ratings meet load requirements
3. Attach slings to spreader bar ends
4. Connect sling lower ends to fixture outboard points
5. Verify equal sling lengths for level lift

### 2.4 Lift Test

1. Apply slight tension to lifting arrangement
2. Verify all attachment points are secure
3. Lift propulsor 50mm off stand
4. Verify level attitude (±2°)
5. Attach tag lines to propulsor (fore and aft)
6. Hold for 2 minutes and re-verify all connections

## Verification

- [ ] Lift fixture properly attached and torqued
- [ ] Spreader bar connected with safety clip
- [ ] All slings inspected and properly attached
- [ ] Lift test complete and level verified
- [ ] Tag lines attached

## Notes

- If propulsor does not lift level, adjust sling lengths
- Record lift test results in installation log
- Two tag line operators required during lift
