# 61-00-05-01-04A - Thrust Bearings Interface

**Document ID:** 61-00-05-01-04A

**ATA Chapter:** 61 — Propellers and Propulsors  
**Version:** A

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the thrust bearing interface within the AMPEL360 Q100 propulsor system. Thrust bearings accommodate axial loads generated by the ducted fan while supporting the rotating assembly and transferring loads to the stationary structure.

---

## 2. Scope

This specification covers:
- Thrust bearing design and configuration
- Load capacity and life requirements
- Lubrication system interface
- Temperature monitoring and health management
- Installation and maintenance procedures

### 2.1 Applicable Units
- All four Q100 propulsor units
- Forward and aft thrust bearing sets
