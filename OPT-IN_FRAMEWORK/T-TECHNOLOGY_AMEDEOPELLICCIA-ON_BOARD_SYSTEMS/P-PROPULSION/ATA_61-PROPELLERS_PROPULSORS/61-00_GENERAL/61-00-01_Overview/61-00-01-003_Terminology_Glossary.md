# 61-00-01-003 Terminology Glossary

## Document Information

- **Document ID**: 61-00-01-003

- **Version**: 1.0
- **Date**: 2025-12-03
- **Status**: Draft
- **Category**: Overview / Glossary
- **ATA Chapter**: 61 — Propellers/Propulsors

## Purpose

This glossary defines and standardizes terms specific to ATA 61 Propellers/Propulsors within the AMPEL360 BWB H2/Hybrid-Electric aircraft context. Consistent terminology ensures clear communication across all lifecycle phases and with regulatory authorities.

## Glossary

### A

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Advance Ratio** | J | Ratio of aircraft speed to propeller tip speed (V / nD), a key dimensionless parameter for propeller performance |
| **AFDX** | – | Avionics Full-Duplex Switched Ethernet; a deterministic network protocol used for avionics data communication |
| **Asymmetric Thrust** | – | Condition where thrust differs between port and starboard propulsors, creating a yawing moment |

### B

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Blade Pitch** | β | Angle between the blade chord and the plane of rotation; controls thrust and efficiency |
| **Blade Root** | – | The inboard end of a blade where it attaches to the hub or rotor assembly |
| **Blade Tip** | – | The outboard end of a blade; critical for aerodynamic and noise performance |
| **BLI** | – | Boundary Layer Ingestion; technique where propulsors ingest the aircraft's boundary layer to improve propulsive efficiency |
| **BWB** | – | Blended Wing Body; an aircraft configuration where the wing and fuselage are integrated into a single lifting surface |

### C

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Chord** | c | The distance from the leading edge to the trailing edge of a blade section |
| **Cooling Loop** | – | The thermal management circuit that removes heat from motor and power electronics components |
| **Cryo-Assisted Cooling** | – | Cooling approach that leverages the cold temperature of cryogenic LH₂ to improve thermal management efficiency |

### D

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **DAL** | – | Development Assurance Level; a classification (A through E) indicating the rigor required for software/hardware development per [DO-178C](https://www.rtca.org/content/do-178c-software-considerations-airborne-systems-and-equipment-certification)/[DO-254](https://www.rtca.org/content/do-254-design-assurance-guidance-airborne-electronic-hardware) |
| **DEP** | – | Distributed Electric Propulsion; propulsion architecture using multiple electrically-driven propulsors distributed across the airframe |
| **Ducted Fan** | DF | A fan enclosed within an aerodynamic shroud (duct) that improves thrust efficiency and reduces tip losses |

### E

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **EDF** | – | Electric Ducted Fan; a ducted fan unit powered by an electric motor |
| **Efficiency Map** | – | A performance chart showing motor or propulsor efficiency as a function of speed and torque/thrust |
| **EMI** | – | Electromagnetic Interference; unwanted electromagnetic signals that may affect electronics operation |

### F

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **FADEC** | – | Full Authority Digital Engine Control; the primary electronic control system for an engine or propulsion unit |
| **Fan Rotor** | – | The rotating assembly of blades in a ducted fan that produces thrust |
| **Feather** | – | Blade pitch position (approximately 90°) that minimizes drag when the propulsor is windmilling or shut down |
| **FHA** | – | Functional Hazard Assessment; a safety analysis method to identify hazards associated with system functions |

### G

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Governor** | – | A control device or algorithm that maintains a set rotational speed by adjusting blade pitch or motor power |

### H

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **H₂** | – | Hydrogen; the fuel used in the AMPEL360 Q100 hydrogen-electric power train |
| **Health Monitoring** | HM | The process of continuously measuring and analyzing system parameters to detect degradation or impending failures |
| **Hub** | – | The central component of a propeller or fan rotor to which blades are attached |

### I

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **ICD** | – | Interface Control Document; a document defining the interfaces between systems |
| **Inverter** | – | Power electronics device that converts DC power to variable-frequency AC power for the motor |

### L

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **LH₂** | – | Liquid Hydrogen; cryogenic hydrogen stored at approximately 20 K (−253 °C) |
| **LRI** | – | Line Replaceable Item; a component that can be replaced at line (flight line) maintenance level |
| **LRU** | – | Line Replaceable Unit; a self-contained module that can be quickly replaced for maintenance |

### M

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Motor Efficiency** | η_m | Ratio of mechanical output power to electrical input power for the electric motor |
| **MTBF** | – | Mean Time Between Failures; a reliability metric indicating average operating time between failures |

### N

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Nacelle** | – | The streamlined housing that encloses the propulsor, motor, and associated systems |
| **N1, N2** | – | Rotational speed designations; N1 typically refers to fan/propulsor speed, N2 to high-pressure spool (if applicable) |

### O

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Overspeed** | – | Condition where rotational speed exceeds design limits, potentially leading to structural failure |

### P

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **PCU** | – | Propulsor Control Unit; the electronic unit that controls and monitors the propulsor, including inverter and control logic |
| **Pitch Actuator** | – | The mechanism that changes blade pitch angle in response to control commands |
| **Power Electronics** | – | Electronic systems that convert and control electrical power (inverters, converters, etc.) |
| **Propulsive Efficiency** | η_p | Ratio of thrust power to shaft power (η_p = TV / P_shaft) |
| **Propulsor** | – | A device that produces thrust for aircraft propulsion; includes propellers, fans, and jets |

### R

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Rotor** | – | The rotating assembly in a motor (containing magnets) or fan (containing blades) |
| **RPM** | – | Revolutions Per Minute; a measure of rotational speed |

### S

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **SHM** | – | Structural Health Monitoring; systems that monitor structural integrity in real time |
| **Stall (Blade)** | – | Aerodynamic condition where blade angle of attack exceeds critical value, causing flow separation and loss of lift |
| **Stator** | – | The stationary component in a motor (containing windings) or ducted fan (containing guide vanes) |

### T

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Thrust** | T | The force produced by the propulsor in the direction of flight |
| **Thrust Coefficient** | C_T | Dimensionless thrust coefficient (C_T = T / ρn²D⁴) |
| **Tip Speed** | V_tip | The velocity of the blade tip (V_tip = π × D × n), limited for noise and structural reasons |
| **Torque** | Q | Rotational force applied by the motor to the propulsor shaft |

### V

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Variable Pitch** | VP | Propulsor design where blade pitch angle can be changed during operation |
| **V&V** | – | Verification and Validation; processes to confirm that requirements are correctly implemented and that the system meets its intended use |

### W

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Windmilling** | – | Condition where the propulsor rotates due to ram air flow (not motor power), typically after shutdown or failure |
