## 1. Purpose

This glossary defines the key terms and concepts used in the ANCHORS system documentation.

## 2. System Terms

### ANCHORS
**Aircraft Networks, Circular, Harvesting, Operating & Renewable Systems**

The integrated environmental and energy management system for the AMPEL360 Q100 aircraft. ANCHORS encompasses battery thermal management, CO2 capture, water treatment, and energy optimization subsystems.

### Bucket
**OPT-IN Framework Organizational Unit**

A functional grouping within the OPT-IN documentation framework, numbered XX-00 through XX-90. Each bucket contains related technical documentation for a specific aspect of the system (e.g., 53-90 for Tables/Schemas/Diagrams).

### Band
**Bucket Subdivision**

A subdivision within a bucket, typically numbered 00-99, that organizes documents by specific topic (e.g., Band 10 for Signal Dictionary within Bucket 90).

### Mode Manager
**Operating Mode Control Software**

The software component responsible for managing ANCHORS operating modes based on flight phase, aircraft configuration, and system status.

### Safety Supervisor
**Safety Monitoring Software Function**

The DAL B software function that monitors safety-critical parameters and initiates protective actions when limits are exceeded.

## 3. Technical Terms

### Circularity Index
**Circular Economy Performance Metric**

A composite metric (0.0 to 1.0) measuring overall circular economy performance of a component, considering recyclability, recycled content, remaining life, repair potential, and carbon performance.

### Development Assurance Level (DAL)
**DO-178C Software Criticality Classification**

A classification (A through E) indicating the rigor of software development and verification required based on failure effect severity:
- **DAL A**: Catastrophic failure effect
- **DAL B**: Hazardous failure effect
- **DAL C**: Major failure effect
- **DAL D**: Minor failure effect
- **DAL E**: No effect

### Digital Product Passport (DPP)
**Component Lifecycle Tracking System**

A digital record that tracks a component throughout its lifecycle, from manufacturing through end-of-life, including installation, maintenance, and sustainability data.

### Minerite
**Mineral Carbonation Material**

The solid mineral product formed when captured CO2 reacts with metal oxide substrate in the CO2 cartridges, resulting in permanent carbon sequestration.

### QuickSwap
**Rapid Ground Exchange System**

The mechanical and electrical interface system enabling rapid exchange of battery packs and CO2 cartridges during ground turnaround, targeting 10-minute swap time.

### Regeneration
**Energy Recovery from Motors**

The process of recovering electrical energy from the distributed electric fan motors during aircraft descent, storing the energy in batteries for later use.

### Thermal Bus
**Closed-Loop Coolant System**

A liquid coolant distribution network for transferring heat between ANCHORS components:
- **HT Bus**: High Temperature bus (70-100°C) for waste heat distribution
- **LT Bus**: Low Temperature bus (20-70°C) for cooling

## 4. Operational Terms

### Turnaround
**Ground Service Sequence**

The complete sequence of ground operations between flights, including passenger handling, cargo, refueling, and ANCHORS QuickSwap operations.

### Load Shedding
**Power Reduction Strategy**

A progressive reduction of non-essential electrical loads when battery state of charge falls below defined thresholds, to preserve power for essential functions.

### Fault Isolation
**Protective Disconnection**

The automatic or commanded disconnection of a system or component to prevent fault propagation or protect personnel.

## 5. Sustainability Terms

### Embodied Carbon
**Manufacturing Emissions**

The total greenhouse gas emissions (expressed as kg CO2 equivalent) associated with manufacturing a component, from raw material extraction through factory gate.

### Operational Carbon
**In-Use Emissions**

The greenhouse gas emissions associated with operating a component over its service life, expressed per flight hour (kg CO2e/FH).

### Recyclability
**Material Recovery Potential**

The percentage of a component's mass that can be recycled at end-of-life through established recycling processes.

### Recycled Content
**Recycled Material Usage**

The percentage of a component's mass that originates from recycled materials.

## 6. Data Terms

### Signal
**Input/Output Data Element**

A discrete data value transmitted between system components, characterized by ID, type, range, units, source, destination, and update rate.

### Parameter
**Configurable Value**

A tunable or configurable value that affects system behavior, with defined default, minimum, and maximum values.

### Virtual Link (VL)
**AFDX Communication Path**

A unidirectional communication path in the AFDX network, characterized by source, destination(s), bandwidth allocation gap (BAG), and maximum transmission unit (MTU).

### Schema
**Data Structure Definition**

A formal definition of data structure and validation rules, typically expressed in JSON Schema or XML Schema format.
