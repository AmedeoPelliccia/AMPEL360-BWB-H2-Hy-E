## Design Requirements

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Ultimate strength | 100% restoration |
| Fatigue life | Equal to remaining parent |
| Damage tolerance | Meet original requirements |
| Weight penalty | Minimize and document |

## Approval Process

### Minor Repairs (SRM)

1. Assess damage and compare to SRM limits
2. Select applicable SRM repair
3. Perform repair per instructions
4. Document in aircraft records

### Major Repairs

1. Submit damage report to OEM
2. Receive engineering disposition
3. Perform repair per engineering order
4. NDI and documentation
