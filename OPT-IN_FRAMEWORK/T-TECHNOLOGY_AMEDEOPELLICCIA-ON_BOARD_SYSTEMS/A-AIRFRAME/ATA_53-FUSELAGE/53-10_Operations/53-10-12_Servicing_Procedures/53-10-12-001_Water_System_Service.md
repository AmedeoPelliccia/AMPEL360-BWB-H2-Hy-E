## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the water system servicing operations for ANCHORS water recycling systems.

## 2. Scope

Applicable to all AMPEL360 BWB aircraft equipped with ANCHORS water recycling systems.

## 3. Servicing Requirements

| Parameter | Threshold | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Water tank level | < 30% | Top up |
| Filter life | > 80% used | Replace |
| Water quality | Per spec | Test |

## 4. Servicing Procedure

| Step | Action | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Set ANCHORS → SERVICING | Mode change |
| 2 | Connect service cart | Standard fitting |
| 3 | Check water level | Via panel indicator |
| 4 | Top up as required | Potable water only |
| 5 | Check filter status | Replace if > 80% used |
| 6 | Disconnect service cart | — |
| 7 | Set ANCHORS → GROUND | Mode change |
| 8 | Verify system status | Self-test |

## 5. Water Quality Requirements

| Parameter | Requirement | Standard |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Potability | Potable grade | WHO guidelines |
| Chlorine | < 0.5 mg/L | — |
| Turbidity | < 1 NTU | — |
| pH | 6.5–8.5 | — |
