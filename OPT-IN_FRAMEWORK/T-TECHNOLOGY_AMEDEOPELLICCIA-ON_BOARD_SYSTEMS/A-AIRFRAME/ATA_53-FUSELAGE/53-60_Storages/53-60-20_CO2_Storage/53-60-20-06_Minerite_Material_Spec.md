## Purpose

This document defines the material specification for Minerite, the proprietary mineral carbonation media used in CO₂ storage cartridges.

## Scope

This specification covers:
- Material composition
- Physical properties
- Chemical properties
- Performance requirements
- Quality control

## Material Description

Minerite is a proprietary engineered mineral substrate designed for permanent CO₂ sequestration through accelerated mineral carbonation (AMC). The material converts gaseous CO₂ into stable carbonate minerals.

## Composition

### Base Materials

| Component | Percentage | Function |
|-----------|------------|----------|
| Olivine (Mg₂SiO₄) | 60-70% | Primary carbonation mineral |
| Serpentine | 15-25% | Secondary carbonation mineral |
| Catalyst additives | 5-10% | Accelerate reaction rate |
| Binder | 5-10% | Structural integrity |

### Carbonation Products

| Product | Formula | Stability |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Magnesite | MgCO₃ | Thermodynamically stable |
| Calcite | CaCO₃ | Thermodynamically stable |
| Silica | SiO₂ | Inert byproduct |
