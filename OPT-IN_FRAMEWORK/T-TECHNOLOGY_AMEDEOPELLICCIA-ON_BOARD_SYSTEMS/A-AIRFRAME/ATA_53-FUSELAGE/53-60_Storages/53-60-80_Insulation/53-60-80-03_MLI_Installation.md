## MLI Overview

### Construction

Multi-Layer Insulation consists of alternating layers of reflective material and spacer material, typically:
- **Reflective layers**: Aluminized Mylar or Kapton
- **Spacer layers**: Dacron netting or silk

### Performance Principle

MLI reduces heat transfer by:
1. Radiation reflection (reflective layers)
2. Conduction minimization (spacer separation)
3. Gas conduction reduction (in evacuated environment)

### Typical Performance

| Configuration | Layers | Effective k | Application |
|---------------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Standard | 10-20 | 0.0001 W/m·K (vacuum) | Cryogenic |
| High-performance | 30-60 | 0.00005 W/m·K (vacuum) | Space |

## Installation Procedure

### Preparation

| Step | Action | Verification |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Verify cleanliness of substrate | Visual inspection |
| 2 | Check MLI material for defects | No tears, wrinkles |
| 3 | Identify attachment points | Per drawing |
| 4 | Prepare attachment hardware | Grommets, tape, ties |

### Layer Installation

| Step | Action | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Apply first spacer layer | Against substrate |
| 2 | Apply first reflector layer | Shiny side out |
| 3 | Alternate spacer and reflector | Per layer count |
| 4 | Maintain layer alignment | Minimize gaps |
| 5 | Secure at attachment points | Per drawing |
| 6 | Apply outer protective layer | As required |

### Critical Requirements

| Requirement | Specification | Reference |
|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Layer density | 2-4 layers/cm | REQ-MLI-010 |
| Overlap at seams | ≥ 50 mm | REQ-MLI-011 |
| Penetrations | Minimize and seal | REQ-MLI-012 |
| Compression | Avoid over-compression | REQ-MLI-013 |

## Handling Precautions

### Material Handling

| Precaution | Reason |
|------------|--------|
| Clean gloves | Prevent contamination |
| Avoid sharp tools near MLI | Prevent tears |
| Store flat | Prevent permanent creases |
| Keep dry | Prevent moisture damage |
