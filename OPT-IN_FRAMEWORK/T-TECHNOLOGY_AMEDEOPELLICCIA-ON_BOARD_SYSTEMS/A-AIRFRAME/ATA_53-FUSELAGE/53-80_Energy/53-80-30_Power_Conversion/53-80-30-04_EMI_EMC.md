## 1. Purpose

This document defines EMI/EMC requirements and compliance strategies for the ANCHORS power conversion equipment per DO-160G and MIL-STD-461G.

## 2. Applicable Standards

| Standard | Category | Application |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| DO-160G Section 21 | Conducted emissions | Power lines |
| DO-160G Section 22 | Radiated emissions | Equipment |
| DO-160G Section 20 | Susceptibility | RF immunity |
| MIL-STD-461G | Military compliance | HVDC systems |

## 3. Conducted Emissions Limits

### 3.1 Power Line Emissions

| Frequency | Limit (dBμA) | Category |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| 150 kHz - 2 MHz | 60 → 40 | M |
| 2 MHz - 30 MHz | 40 → 30 | M |

### 3.2 Filter Design

Each DC-DC converter includes:
- Common mode choke: 10 mH
- Differential mode inductor: 100 μH
- Y-capacitors: 4.7 nF (safety rated)
- X-capacitors: 10 μF

## 4. Radiated Emissions

### 4.1 Limits

| Frequency | Limit (dBμV/m @ 1m) |
|-----------|---------------------|
| 150 kHz - 30 MHz | 40 |
| 30 MHz - 100 MHz | 43 |
| 100 MHz - 1 GHz | 50 |

### 4.2 Shielding

| Component | Shield | Effectiveness |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| HVDC cables | Braid + foil | 60 dB |
| Converter enclosure | Aluminum | 50 dB |
| Signal cables | Braid | 40 dB |

## 5. Susceptibility Requirements

### 5.1 Conducted Susceptibility

| Test | Level | Performance |
|------|-------|-------------|
| CS101 | 6 V, 30 Hz - 50 kHz | No malfunction |
| CS114 | 1 W, 10 kHz - 400 MHz | No malfunction |
| CS115 | 30 A peak | Recovery < 1 s |
| CS116 | 10 A, 1 ms | No damage |

### 5.2 Radiated Susceptibility

| Test | Level | Performance |
|------|-------|-------------|
| RS103 | 200 V/m, 10 kHz - 40 GHz | No malfunction |

## 6. Grounding and Bonding

### 6.1 Grounding Scheme

- Single-point ground for signal circuits
- Multi-point ground for power circuits
- Shield grounding at both ends with ferrite

### 6.2 Bond Resistance

| Joint | Maximum Resistance |
|-------|-------------------|
| Equipment to structure | 2.5 mΩ |
| Shield to connector | 5 mΩ |
| Cable shield to enclosure | 10 mΩ |
