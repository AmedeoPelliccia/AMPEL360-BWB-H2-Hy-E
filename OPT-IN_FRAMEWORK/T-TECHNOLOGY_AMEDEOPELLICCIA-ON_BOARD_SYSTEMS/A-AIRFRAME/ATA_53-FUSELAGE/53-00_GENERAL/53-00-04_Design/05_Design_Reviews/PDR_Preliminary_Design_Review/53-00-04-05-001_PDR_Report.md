## 2. Scope

This PDR covers the preliminary design of the AMPEL360 BWB fuselage structure including:

- Zones 100 through 600
- Primary and secondary structure
- Pressure shell and floor structure
- Landing gear bay structure
- Major interfaces (wings, empennage, doors)
