## 4. Fatigue and Damage Tolerance

### 4.1 Design Life

- Economic design life: 60,000 flight hours / 36,000 flight cycles
- Inspection threshold: Per DT analysis

### 4.2 Damage Tolerance Philosophy

Per [CS-25.571](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/certification-specifications/cs-25-large-aeroplanes):
- Category 1: Fail-safe structure
- Category 2: Damage tolerant structure
- Category 3: Safe-life structure (limited application)
