## 4. Hazardous Materials Identification

### 4.1 Known Hazardous Substances

| Substance | Location | Handling Requirement |
|-----------|----------|---------------------|
| Cadmium plating | Legacy fasteners | Specialized removal |
| Chromate primers | Interior surfaces | Controlled disposal |
| Fuel residues | Integral tanks | Purging required |
| Hydraulic fluids | System components | Collection/recycling |
| Fire retardants | Insulation blankets | Specialized handling |

### 4.2 Removal Procedures

Prior to structure processing:

1. **Defuel** – Complete fuel removal and tank purging
2. **Drain fluids** – All hydraulic, oil, and coolant systems
3. **Remove batteries** – Special waste handling
4. **Remove insulation** – Per hazmat procedures
5. **Decontaminate** – As required by facility

## 5. Disassembly Sequence

### 5.1 Recommended Order

| Phase | Zone | Activity | Priority |
|-------|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | All | Systems removal | High-value recovery |
| 2 | 200/300 | Interior removal | Reuse potential |
| 3 | 600 | Tail section | Easy access |
| 4 | 100 | Nose section | Separate processing |
| 5 | 500 | Aft cabin | Structure access |
| 6 | 400 | Center wing box | Heaviest section |

### 5.2 Fastener Removal

| Fastener Type | Removal Method | Reuse Potential |
|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Titanium Hi-Lok | Drill-out or extraction | None (single use) |
| Steel bolts | Standard removal | Inspect for reuse |
| Blind rivets | Drill-out | None |
| Nutplates | Preserve if possible | Evaluate |
