### 5.1 Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Recovery Rate | Environmental Impact | Economic Viability |
|----------|---------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Full recycling | 75-85% | Low | Moderate |
| Partial recycling | 50-65% | Medium | High |
| Energy recovery | 90%+ energy | Medium-High | High |
| Landfill | 0% | High | Low cost |

### 5.2 Recommended Approach

Priority hierarchy per waste directive:
1. **Reuse**: Component reuse where airworthy
2. **Recycle**: Material recycling (metals, some composites)
3. **Recover**: Energy recovery from non-recyclable composites
4. **Dispose**: Minimal landfill for hazardous residues only
