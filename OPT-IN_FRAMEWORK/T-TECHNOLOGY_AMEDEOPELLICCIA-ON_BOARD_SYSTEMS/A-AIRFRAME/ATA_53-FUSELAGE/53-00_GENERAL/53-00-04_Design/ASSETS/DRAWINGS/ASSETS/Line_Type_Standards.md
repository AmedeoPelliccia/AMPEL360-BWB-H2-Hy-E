# Line Type Standards

## Standard Line Types

### Visible Lines
- **Usage**: Visible edges and contours
- **Type**: Solid
- **Color**: Black (#000000)
- **Weight**: 0.5-0.7mm (2 pixels at standard resolution)

### Hidden Lines
- **Usage**: Hidden edges and features
- **Type**: Dashed (3mm dash, 3mm gap)
- **Color**: Gray (#999999)
- **Weight**: 0.35mm (1 pixel)

### Center Lines
- **Usage**: Axes of symmetry, center of circles/arcs
- **Type**: Dash-dot (10mm dash, 5mm gap, 2mm dot, 5mm gap)
- **Color**: Red (#CC0000)
- **Weight**: 0.25mm (1 pixel)

### Dimension Lines
- **Usage**: Dimensioning and measurements
- **Type**: Solid with arrows
- **Color**: Blue (#0066CC)
- **Weight**: 0.25mm (1 pixel)

### Section Lines
- **Usage**: Section cuts and hatching
- **Type**: Solid at 45° angle
- **Color**: Black (#000000)
- **Weight**: 0.35mm (1 pixel)
- **Spacing**: 2-3mm between lines

### Phantom Lines
- **Usage**: Alternative positions, adjacent parts
- **Type**: Dash-double-dot
- **Color**: Purple (#9933CC)
- **Weight**: 0.25mm (1 pixel)
