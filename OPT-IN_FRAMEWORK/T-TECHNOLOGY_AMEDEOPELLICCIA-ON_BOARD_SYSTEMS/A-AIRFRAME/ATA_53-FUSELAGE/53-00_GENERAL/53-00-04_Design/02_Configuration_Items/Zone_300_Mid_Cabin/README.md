# Zone 300 – Mid Cabin

## 1. Overview

Zone 300 covers the **Mid Cabin** of the AMPEL360 BWB fuselage, spanning **Stations 300 to 450**. This zone encompasses the main economy cabin and the BWB blend onset structural components.

---

## 2. Zone Boundaries

```yaml
zone_300:
  ci: "CI-53-300"
  stations: "300 to 450"
  description: "Main economy cabin and BWB blend onset"
```

---

## 3. Major Components

The following major structural components are located in Zone 300:

- **Upper and Lower Shell Panels**
- **Mid Passenger Doors**
- **Overwing Exits**
- **Floor Beam Grid**
- **Wing-Body Blend Transition Structure**
