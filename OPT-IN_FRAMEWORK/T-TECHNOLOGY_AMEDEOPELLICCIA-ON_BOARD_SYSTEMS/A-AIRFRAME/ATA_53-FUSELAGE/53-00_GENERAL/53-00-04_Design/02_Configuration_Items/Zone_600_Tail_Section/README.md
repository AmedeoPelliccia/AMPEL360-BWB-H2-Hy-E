# Zone 600 – Tail Section

## 1. Overview

Zone 600 covers the **Tail Section** of the AMPEL360 BWB fuselage, spanning **Stations 750 to 900**. This zone encompasses the aft pressure bulkhead to the tail structural components.

- **Aft Pressure Bulkhead** (CI-53-600-BHD-AFT)
