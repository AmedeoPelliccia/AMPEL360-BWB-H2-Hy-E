# Zone 100 – Nose Section

## 1. Overview

Zone 100 covers the **Nose Section** of the AMPEL360 BWB fuselage, spanning **Stations 0 to 150**. This zone encompasses all structural components from the forward pressure bulkhead to the cockpit.
