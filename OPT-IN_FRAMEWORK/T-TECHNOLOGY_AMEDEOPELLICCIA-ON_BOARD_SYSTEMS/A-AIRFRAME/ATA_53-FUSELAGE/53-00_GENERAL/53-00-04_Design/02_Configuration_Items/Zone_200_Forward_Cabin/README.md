# Zone 200 – Forward Cabin

## 1. Overview

Zone 200 covers the **Forward Cabin** of the AMPEL360 BWB fuselage, spanning **Stations 150 to 300**. This zone encompasses the first class and forward economy cabin structural components.

---

## 2. Zone Boundaries

```yaml
zone_200:
  ci: "CI-53-200"
  stations: "150 to 300"
  description: "First class and forward economy cabin"
```

---

## 3. Major Components

The following major structural components are located in Zone 200:

- **Upper and Lower Shell Panels**
- **Forward Passenger Doors** (Door 1L, Door 1R)
- **Floor Beam Grid**
- **Cabin Frames and Stringers**
