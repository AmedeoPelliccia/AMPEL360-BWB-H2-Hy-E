# Zone 500 – Aft Cabin

## 1. Overview

Zone 500 covers the **Aft Cabin** of the AMPEL360 BWB fuselage, spanning **Stations 600 to 750**. This zone encompasses the aft cabin and empennage transition structural components.

---

## 2. Zone Boundaries

```yaml

  ci: "CI-53-500"
```

## 3. Major Components

The following major structural components are located in Zone 500:

- **Shell Panels** (Upper and Lower)
- **Aft Galley/Lavatory Structure**
- **Empennage Attachment Structure**
- **Aft Passenger Doors**
