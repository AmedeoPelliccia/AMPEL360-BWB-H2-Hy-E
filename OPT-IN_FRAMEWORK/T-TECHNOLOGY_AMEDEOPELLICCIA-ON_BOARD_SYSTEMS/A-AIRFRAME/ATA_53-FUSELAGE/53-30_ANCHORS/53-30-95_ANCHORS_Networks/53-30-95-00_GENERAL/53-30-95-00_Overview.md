## Purpose

## Subsystem Description

ANCHORS Networks provides four core buses:

1. **ResourceBus** — Electrical power routing
2. **ThermalBus** — Heat distribution
3. **CO₂Bus** — CO₂ flow management
4. **WaterBus** — Water distribution
