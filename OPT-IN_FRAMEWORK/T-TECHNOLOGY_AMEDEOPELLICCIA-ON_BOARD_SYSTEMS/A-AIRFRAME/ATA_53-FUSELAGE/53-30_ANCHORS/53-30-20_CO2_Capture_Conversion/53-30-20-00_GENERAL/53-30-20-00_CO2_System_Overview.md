## 1. Purpose

This document provides an overview of the CO₂ Capture and Conversion subsystems within ANCHORS.

---

## 2. Process Flow

```
Cabin Air → Extraction (53-30-10-03) → Manifold → Separation → Solidification → Minerite Cartridge
                                                                      ↑
                                                            Thermal Integration
```

---

## 3. Subsystems

### 3.1 Manifold Capture (53-30-20-01)

Distribution network collecting CO₂-rich air from extraction points.

Key features:
- Low-pressure drop manifold design
- Flow balancing valves
- Multiple intake points

### 3.2 Separation Modules (53-30-20-02)

Membrane and sorbent-based CO₂ separation from carrier gas.

Key features:
- Two-stage separation process
- > 90% CO₂ purity output
- Compact module design

### 3.3 Solidification Cartridges (53-30-20-03)

Mineralization of captured CO₂ into stable carbonate form (Minerite).

Key features:
- Quick-swap cartridge design
- Exothermic reaction (heat recovery)
- Ground-recyclable cartridges

### 3.4 Thermal Integration (53-30-20-04)

Heat management for separation and solidification processes.

Key features:
- Waste heat recovery from solidification
- Thermal regeneration of sorbents
- Integration with aircraft thermal bus

---

## 4. Safety Summary

CO₂ handling introduces specific safety considerations:

- CO₂ concentration monitoring throughout process
- Pressure relief provisions
- Ventilation of equipment bays
- Personnel protection during ground handling
