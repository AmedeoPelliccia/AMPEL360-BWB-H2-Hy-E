## 3. Scheduled Maintenance

### 3.1 Pre-flight Inspection

1. Visual check of duct connections
2. Verify no visible damage
3. Check BITE status (no faults)

**Time:** 2 minutes

### 3.2 A-Check Tasks

1. Download performance data
2. Compare to baseline
3. Clean inlet screen (if fitted)
4. Check connector tightness

**Time:** 15 minutes

---

## 4. Unscheduled Maintenance

### 4.1 LRU Replacement

**Removal:**
1. Isolate electrical
2. Disconnect electrical connector
3. Remove duct clamps (4x)
4. Remove harvester from duct

**Installation:**
1. Install harvester in duct
2. Secure duct clamps (4x)
3. Connect electrical connector
4. Restore power and test

**Time:** 30 minutes

---

## 5. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Probable Cause | Action |
|:--|:--|:--|
| No output | Power conditioning fault | Replace LRU |
| Low output | Bearing wear | Replace LRU |
| High vibration | Blade damage | Replace LRU |
| BITE fault | Electronics failure | Replace LRU |
