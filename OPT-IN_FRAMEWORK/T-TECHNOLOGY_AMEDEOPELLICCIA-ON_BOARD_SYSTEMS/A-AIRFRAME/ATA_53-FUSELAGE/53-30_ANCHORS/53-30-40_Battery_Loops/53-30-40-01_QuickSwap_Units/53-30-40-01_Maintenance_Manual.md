## 2. Turnaround Procedure

### 2.1 Battery Pack Removal

**Prerequisites:**
- Aircraft on ground power
- ANCHORS in ground mode
- Swap cart positioned

**Procedure:**
1. Verify ANCHORS ground mode (EICAS)
2. Disconnect ground cooling (if connected)
3. Open bay access door
4. Verify latch unlocked indicator
5. Release latch handle (1/4 turn CCW)
6. Extract pack onto cart rails
7. Complete pack removal

**Time:** 2 minutes

### 2.2 Battery Pack Installation

**Procedure:**
1. Position new pack on cart rails
2. Align pack with bay guides
3. Insert pack fully into bay
4. Engage latch handle (1/4 turn CW)
5. Verify latch locked indicator (green)
6. Connect ground cooling (if required)
7. Close bay access door
8. Verify ANCHORS ready (EICAS)

**Time:** 2 minutes

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Probable Cause | Action |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Latch won't release | Interlock active | Verify ground mode |
| Pack won't extract | Rail obstruction | Inspect rails |
| No HV after install | Latch incomplete | Re-seat latch |
| Cooling leak | Connector damage | Replace connector |
