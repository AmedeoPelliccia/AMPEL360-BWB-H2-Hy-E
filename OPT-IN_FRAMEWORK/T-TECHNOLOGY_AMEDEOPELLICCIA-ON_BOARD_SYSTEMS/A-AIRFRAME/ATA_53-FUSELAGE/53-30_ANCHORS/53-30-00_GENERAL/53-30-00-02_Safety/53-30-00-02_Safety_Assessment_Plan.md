## 3. ANCHORS-Specific Safety Considerations

### 3.1 H₂ Safety

ANCHORS interfaces with hydrogen systems require special provisions:

- Hydrogen leak detection and containment
- Ventilation requirements
- Ignition source isolation

See: 53-30-00-02_H2_CO2_Safety_Provisions.md

### 3.2 CO₂ Handling Safety

- CO₂ concentration monitoring
- Pressure relief provisions
- Cartridge handling safety

### 3.3 Battery Thermal Safety

- Thermal runaway prevention
- Propagation mitigation
- Fire suppression integration

See: 53-30-00-02_Thermal_Runaway_Mitigation.md
