## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the regenerative design principles guiding ANCHORS development.

---

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Close the Loop

Every material and energy flow should return to a useful state:

- Materials → Recycling or reuse
- Energy → Cascade utilization
- Information → Learning and optimization

### 2.2 Design Out Waste

No output should be classified as "waste":

| Traditional Waste | ANCHORS Approach |
|:--|:--|
| Cabin CO₂ | Captured and mineralized |
| Waste heat | Harvested for processes |
| Greywater | Recycled for technical use |
| Battery depletion | Second-life or recycling |

### 2.3 Regenerate Natural Systems

Operations should contribute positively:

- Net carbon negative operation
- Water purification exceeding consumption
- Support for circular supply chains

---

## 3. Design Guidelines

### 3.1 Material Selection

| Priority | Criteria |
|:--|:--|
| 1 | Recycled content |
| 2 | Recyclability at end-of-life |
| 3 | Low embodied energy |
| 4 | Non-toxic composition |
| 5 | Local availability |

### 3.2 Product Longevity

| Strategy | Implementation |
|:--|:--|
| Durability | Design for 20+ year life |
| Maintainability | Accessible, repairable |
| Upgradability | Modular interfaces |
| Adaptability | Multi-configuration support |

### 3.3 Service Model

Preference for service-based models:

- Battery-as-a-Service
- Cartridge exchange programs
- Performance guarantees

## 4. Biomimetic Inspiration

ANCHORS draws from natural systems:

| Natural System | ANCHORS Application |
|:--|:--|
| Plant photosynthesis | CO₂ capture and conversion |
| Kidneys | Water filtration and recovery |
| Circulatory system | Thermal loop distribution |
| Ecosystem nutrient cycling | Material passport tracking |
