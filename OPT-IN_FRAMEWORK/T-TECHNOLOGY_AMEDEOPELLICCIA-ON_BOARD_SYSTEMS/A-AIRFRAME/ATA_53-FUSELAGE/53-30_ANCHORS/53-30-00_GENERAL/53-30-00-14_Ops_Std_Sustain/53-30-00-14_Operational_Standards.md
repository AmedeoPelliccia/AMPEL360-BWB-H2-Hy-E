## 2. LCA Methodology

Life Cycle Assessment for ANCHORS follows ISO 14040/14044 standards:

- Cradle-to-grave analysis
- Functional unit: Per flight hour
- Impact categories: GWP, acidification, eutrophication, resource depletion
