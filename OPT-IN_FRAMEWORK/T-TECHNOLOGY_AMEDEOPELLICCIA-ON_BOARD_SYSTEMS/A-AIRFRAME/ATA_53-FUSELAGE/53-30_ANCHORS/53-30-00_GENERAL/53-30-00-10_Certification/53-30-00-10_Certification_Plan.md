## 2. Certification Basis

ANCHORS systems are certified against:

- [EASA CS-25](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/certification-specifications/cs-25-large-aeroplanes) — Large Aeroplanes

Special Conditions are anticipated for:
