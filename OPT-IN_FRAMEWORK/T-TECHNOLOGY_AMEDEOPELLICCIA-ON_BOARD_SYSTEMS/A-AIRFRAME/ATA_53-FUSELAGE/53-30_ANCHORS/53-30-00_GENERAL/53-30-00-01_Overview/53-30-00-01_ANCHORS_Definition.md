## 1. ANCHORS Acronym

**A**ircraft **N**etworks, **C**ircular, **H**arvesting, **O**perating & **R**enewable **S**ystems

---

## 2. Definition

ANCHORS represents a paradigm shift in aircraft design philosophy. These are systems whose primary mission is **closing loops of matter, energy, or information** within the fuselage structure.

Key principles:

- **Circularity**: All material flows are designed for recovery, reuse, or recycling
- **Harvesting**: Capture of otherwise wasted energy (thermal, kinetic, potential)
- **Regeneration**: Systems that restore and improve during operation
- **Traceability**: Full digital tracking via Digital Product Passport (DPP)

---

## 3. System Scope

ANCHORS encompasses four major subsystem families:

### 3.1 Harvesting Systems (53-30-10)
- Airflow energy harvesting
- Condensate water recovery
- Cabin CO₂ extraction
- Waste heat capture and conversion

### 3.2 CO₂ Capture & Conversion (53-30-20)
- Manifold capture networks
- Gas separation modules
- Solidification/mineralization cartridges
- Thermal integration for process efficiency

### 3.3 Water & Waste Recycling (53-30-30)
- Greywater filtering and treatment
- Condensate collection loops
- Atmospheric moisture recovery
- Atmospheric water generation (AWG)

### 3.4 Battery Loops (53-30-40)
- Quick-swap modular battery units
- Thermal regeneration loops
- State-of-health monitoring and optimization

---

## 4. Design Philosophy

ANCHORS systems are designed with the following priorities:

1. **Safety First**: All circularity features are subordinate to flight safety
2. **Weight Consciousness**: Net weight impact must be justified by circular benefits
3. **Modularity**: Components designed for easy replacement and ground processing
4. **Digital Integration**: Full connectivity to [ATA 95 Neural Networks](../../../../../../N-NEURAL_NETWORKS_USERS_TRACEABILITY/ATA_95-DIGITAL_PRODUCT_PASSPORT_NEURAL_NETWORKS/)
