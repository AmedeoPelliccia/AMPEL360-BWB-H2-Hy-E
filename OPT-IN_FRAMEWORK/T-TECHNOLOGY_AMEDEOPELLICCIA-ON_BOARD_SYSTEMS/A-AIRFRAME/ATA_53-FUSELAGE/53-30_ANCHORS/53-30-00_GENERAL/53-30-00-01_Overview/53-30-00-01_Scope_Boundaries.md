## 1. Scope of ANCHORS

### 1.1 Included Systems

The following systems and components are within the scope of ATA 53-30 ANCHORS:

- All energy harvesting equipment integrated into fuselage structure
- CO₂ capture, separation, and solidification systems
- Water recycling and recovery systems within the fuselage
- Battery quick-swap mechanisms and thermal management
- Associated sensors, controllers, and data interfaces
- Ground support equipment interfaces for circularity operations

### 1.2 Excluded Systems

The following are NOT within scope of ANCHORS:

- Primary flight controls
- Propulsion systems (except thermal interfaces)
- Emergency systems (handled by specific ATA chapters)
- Passenger convenience systems (galleys, entertainment)
- External aerodynamic surfaces

---

## 2. Physical Boundaries

### 2.1 Fuselage Integration

ANCHORS equipment is located within:

- Forward cargo bay (CO₂ cartridge storage)
- Aft equipment bay (thermal integration)
- Floor beam structure (battery swap bays)
- ECS duct network (harvesting taps)

### 2.2 Interface Boundaries

Physical interfaces are defined at:

- Structural attach points (documented in 53-30-00-05)
- Fluid connectors (quick-disconnect fittings)
- Electrical connectors (standardized per ATA 24)
- Data ports (per ATA 95 protocols)

---

## 3. Functional Boundaries

### 3.1 Normal Operations

During flight:

- Harvesting systems operate autonomously
- CO₂ capture may be active depending on cabin load
- Water recycling operates as needed
- Battery thermal management always active

### 3.2 Ground Operations

On ground:

- Enhanced circularity mode (cartridge swap, battery swap)
- GSE interfaces active
- Data upload to DPP systems
