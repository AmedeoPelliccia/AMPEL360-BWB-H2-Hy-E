## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the pre-flight inspection and servicing requirements for the windshield rain repellent system, ensuring adequate protection for operations in precipitation.

## 2. Scope

Applicable to all AMPEL360 Q100 aircraft equipped with:

- Rain repellent dispensing system (ATA 56-26)
- Hydrophobic windshield coating
- Rain repellent fluid reservoir

## 3. Prerequisites

- Windshield visual inspection completed (56-10-01-001)
- Rain repellent fluid available (approved type)
- Servicing equipment clean and serviceable
- AMM rain repellent chapter reviewed

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 System Inspection

| Step | Action | Expected Result | Notes |
|------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 | Inspect rain repellent nozzles | No blockage, aligned | External check |
| 2 | Verify tubing condition | No leaks or kinks | Accessible areas |
| 3 | Check windshield hydrophobic coating | Uniform water beading | If recently applied |
| 4 | Review rain repellent QTY indication | Quantity displayed | Cockpit gauge/MFD |

### 4.2 Fluid Level Check

| Step | Action | Expected Result | Notes |
|------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| 5 | Access rain repellent reservoir | Reservoir accessible | Per AMM |
| 6 | Check fluid level | ≥ 50% for dispatch | Minimum level |
| 7 | Verify fluid condition | Clear, no contamination | Visual check |
| 8 | Top up if required | Fill to MAX indication | Approved fluid only |

### 4.3 Functional Test (If Required)

| Step | Action | Expected Result | Notes |
|------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| 9 | Select RAIN REPEL → TEST | Brief spray activation | Ground test only |
| 10 | Verify spray pattern | Even coverage | Both windshields |
| 11 | Select RAIN REPEL → OFF | System disarms | Normal state |
| 12 | Wipe test residue | Clean surface | Prevent streaking |

## 6. Dispatch Criteria

| Condition | Dispatch Allowed | Notes |
|-----------|------------------|-------|
| Rain repel QTY ≥ 50% | Yes | Normal dispatch |
| Rain repel QTY < 50% | Conditional | Non-precipitation forecast required |
| Rain repel INOP | Per MEL | With restrictions |
| Nozzles blocked | No | Maintenance required |

## 7. Completion Criteria

- Rain repellent system inspected
- Fluid quantity ≥ 50% or serviced to acceptable level
- No leaks or blockages identified
- System ready for dispatch
