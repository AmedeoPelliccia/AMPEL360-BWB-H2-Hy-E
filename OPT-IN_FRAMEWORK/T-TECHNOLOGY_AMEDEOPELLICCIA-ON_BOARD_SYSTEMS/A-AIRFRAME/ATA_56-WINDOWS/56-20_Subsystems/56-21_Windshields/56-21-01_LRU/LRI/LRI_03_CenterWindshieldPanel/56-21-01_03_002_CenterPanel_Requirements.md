## 1. Purpose

This document defines the requirements for the Center Windshield Panel unique to the BWB configuration.

---

## 2. Functional Requirements

| Req ID | Requirement | Verification |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| REQ-56-21-03-001 | The panel shall provide unobstructed central forward visibility | Inspection |
| REQ-56-21-03-002 | The panel shall extend horizontal FOV by minimum 15° | Analysis/Test |
| REQ-56-21-03-003 | The panel shall maintain structural integrity at 9.0 psi differential | Test |
| REQ-56-21-03-004 | The panel shall resist bird strike per [FAR 25.775](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-25) | Test |

## 3. BWB-Specific Requirements
