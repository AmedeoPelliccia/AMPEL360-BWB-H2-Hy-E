## 1. Purpose

This document defines the requirements for the Left Windshield Panel (Captain's side) of the Forward Windshield Assembly.

---

## 2. Functional Requirements

| Req ID | Requirement | Verification |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| REQ-56-21-01-001 | The panel shall provide unobstructed forward visibility for the Captain | Inspection |
| REQ-56-21-01-002 | The panel shall maintain structural integrity at 9.0 psi differential pressure | Test |
| REQ-56-21-01-003 | The panel shall resist bird strike per [FAR 25.775](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-25) | Test |
| REQ-56-21-01-004 | The panel shall support integrated heating elements | Inspection |

---

## 3. Performance Requirements

| Req ID | Requirement | Verification |
|--------|-------------|--------------|

| REQ-56-21-01-011 | Optical distortion shall be ≤ 0.5 milliradians | Test |
| REQ-56-21-01-012 | The panel shall operate from -55°C to +70°C | Test |

## 4. Safety Requirements

| Req ID | Requirement | Verification |
