## 6. FHA-to-SSA Handoff

FHA cases classified as Catastrophic or Hazardous SHALL be further analyzed in the System Safety Assessment (SSA):
- [`../54-00-02-30_SSA/`](../54-00-02-30_SSA/00_INDEX.md)

SSA SHALL demonstrate that:
- residual probability of each Catastrophic failure condition is ≤ 10⁻⁹ per flight hour,
- residual probability of each Hazardous failure condition is ≤ 10⁻⁷ per flight hour,
- appropriate design and verification measures are in place.
