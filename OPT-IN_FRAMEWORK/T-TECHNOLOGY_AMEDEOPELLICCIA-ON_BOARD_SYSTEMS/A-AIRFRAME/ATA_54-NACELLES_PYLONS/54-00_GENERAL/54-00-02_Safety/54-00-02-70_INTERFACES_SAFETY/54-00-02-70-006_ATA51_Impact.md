# 54-00-02-70-006 — ATA 51 Impact (Standard Practices / Structures)

## 1. Purpose

Document the **safety interface impact** between **ATA 54 (Nacelles / Pylons)** and **ATA 51 (Standard Practices / Structures)** for AMPEL360 AIR-T.

## 2. Interface Description

ATA 51 structural practices interface with ATA 54 through:
- pylon-to-wing/fuselage attachment design,
- damage tolerance and fail-safe requirements,
- repair and maintenance structural practices,
- material specifications and processes.

## 3. Safety Concerns

| Concern ID | Description | Potential Consequences |
|------------|-------------|------------------------|
| SC-51-001 | Structural attachment failure | Nacelle separation, catastrophic |
| SC-51-002 | Fatigue crack growth beyond limits | Structural failure |
| SC-51-003 | Corrosion in critical attachments | Loss of structural margins |
| SC-51-004 | Repair beyond allowable limits | Inadequate structural integrity |
| SC-51-005 | Material degradation | Loss of structural properties |

## 5. Interface Requirements

| Requirement | Description | Verification |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| IFR-51-001 | Attachments SHALL meet fail-safe requirements | Analysis, test |
| IFR-51-002 | Damage tolerance SHALL be demonstrated | Analysis, test |
