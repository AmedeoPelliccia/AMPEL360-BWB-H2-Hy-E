# 54-00-02-70-005 — ATA 30 Impact (Ice & Rain Protection)

## 1. Purpose

Document the **safety interface impact** between **ATA 54 (Nacelles / Pylons)** and **ATA 30 (Ice & Rain Protection)** for AMPEL360 AIR-T.

## 2. Interface Description

ATA 30 ice and rain protection systems interface with ATA 54 through:
- anti-ice/de-ice system routing through nacelle/pylon,
- thermal loads on nacelle structure from anti-ice systems,
- drainage of melted ice/rain,
- inlet anti-ice provisions.

## 3. Safety Concerns

| Concern ID | Description | Potential Consequences |
|------------|-------------|------------------------|
| SC-30-001 | Ice accretion on nacelle/pylon surfaces | Structural load exceedance, vibration |
| SC-30-002 | Anti-ice system failure | Ice accumulation, performance degradation |
| SC-30-003 | Thermal damage from anti-ice overheat | Structural damage, fire risk |
| SC-30-004 | Inadequate drainage of melted ice | Re-freezing, runback ice |
| SC-30-005 | Ice shedding damage | Impact damage to structure/components |
