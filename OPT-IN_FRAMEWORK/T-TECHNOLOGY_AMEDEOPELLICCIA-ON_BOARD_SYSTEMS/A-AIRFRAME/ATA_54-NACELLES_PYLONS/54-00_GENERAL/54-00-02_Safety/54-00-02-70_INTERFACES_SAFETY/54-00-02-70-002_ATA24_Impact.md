# 54-00-02-70-002 — ATA 24 Impact (Electrical Power)

## 1. Purpose

Document the **safety interface impact** between **ATA 54 (Nacelles / Pylons)** and **ATA 24 (Electrical Power)** for AMPEL360 AIR-T.

## 2. Interface Description

ATA 24 electrical power systems interface with ATA 54 through:
- electrical harness routing through pylon structure,
- power distribution to nacelle-mounted loads,
- grounding/bonding through nacelle/pylon structure,
- generator connections (if nacelle-mounted).

## 3. Safety Concerns

| Concern ID | Description | Potential Consequences |
|------------|-------------|------------------------|
| SC-24-001 | Electrical arcing in pylon routing bay | Fire, cable damage, power loss |
| SC-24-002 | Cable chafing against structure | Short circuit, smoke, fire |
| SC-24-003 | Loss of power to critical nacelle loads | Loss of engine controls, fire detection |
| SC-24-004 | Inadequate grounding/bonding | EMI, static discharge, fire risk |
| SC-24-005 | Thermal effects on electrical insulation | Insulation degradation, short circuit |
