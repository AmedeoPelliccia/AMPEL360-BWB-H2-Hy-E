# 54-00-02-50-001 — ZSA Overview (ATA 54)

## 1. Purpose

Define the **Zonal Safety Analysis (ZSA)** approach for **ATA 54 (Nacelles / Pylons)** to systematically identify hazards arising from the physical installation, routing, and environment within nacelle/pylon zones.

## 2. Scope

ZSA for ATA 54 addresses:
- physical zones within nacelle/pylon volumes,
- environmental conditions (temperature, pressure, contamination, vibration),
- installed equipment and routed items,
- potential interactions between systems in shared zones,
- zone-specific maintenance/inspection constraints.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Zone Identification

Zones SHALL be defined per the program zone map (see `54-00-02-50-002_Zone_Definitions.md`). Each zone is characterized by:
- physical boundaries,
- environmental conditions (thermal, pressure, contamination),
- access provisions for maintenance.

### 3.2 Hazard Identification

For each zone, identify:
- equipment and routed items present,
- potential hazard sources (heat, fluids, electrical, mechanical),
- potential failure propagation paths,
- segregation adequacy between incompatible items.

### 3.3 Analysis Outputs

ZSA SHALL produce:
- zone-specific hazard findings (linked to hazard log),
- segregation adequacy assessments,
- installation design recommendations,
- maintenance/inspection considerations.

## 4. Interfaces

| Interface | ATA | Relevance |
|-----------|-----|-----------|
| Electrical systems | ATA 24 | Cable routing, arcing hazards |
| Fire protection | ATA 26 | Detection/suppression zones |
| Fuel / H₂ | ATA 28 | Leak paths, ignition sources |
| Ice & rain protection | ATA 30 | Thermal blankets, drainage |
| Structures | ATA 51 | Zone boundaries, access panels |
| Powerplant | ATA 71/72 | Engine interfaces, vibration |

## 5. Traceability

ZSA findings SHALL:
- reference hazards by HID from `../54-00-02-10_HAZARD_ANALYSIS/HAZARD_LOGS/54-00-02-10-001_Hazard_Log_Master.csv`,
- link to safety requirements in `../54-00-02-80_REQUIREMENTS_LINKS/`,
- feed common cause analysis in `../54-00-02-60_COMMON_CAUSES/`.
