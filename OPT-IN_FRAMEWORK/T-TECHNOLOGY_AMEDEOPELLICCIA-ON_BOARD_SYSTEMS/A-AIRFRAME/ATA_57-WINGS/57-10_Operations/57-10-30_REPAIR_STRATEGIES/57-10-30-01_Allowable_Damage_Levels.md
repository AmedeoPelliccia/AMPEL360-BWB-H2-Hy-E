# 57-10-30-01 — Allowable Damage Levels

## Purpose

Define the damage tolerance limits for wing structures that determine when
repair, continued operation, or grounding is required.

## Damage Classification

### Category A – Negligible Damage

No repair required; document and monitor.

| Damage Type | Limit | Action |
|-------------|-------|--------|
| Surface scratches | Depth < 0.25 mm | Polish if necessary |
| Minor dents (metallic) | Depth < 1 mm, Ø < 25 mm | Monitor |
| Paint damage | No substrate exposure | Touch-up at convenience |
| Erosion (LE) | < 20% protective coating | Monitor |

### Category B – Repairable Damage

Repair per SRM; may operate under restrictions until repair.

| Damage Type | Limit | Action |
|-------------|-------|--------|
| Scratches | Depth 0.25–0.5 mm | Blend and re-protect |
| Dents (metallic) | Depth 1–3 mm, Ø 25–100 mm | SRM repair |
| CFRP impact | Ø < TBD mm, depth < TBD mm | SRM repair |
| Disbond (CFRP) | Area < TBD cm² | SRM repair |
| Corrosion (metallic) | Depth < TBD mm | Blend and protect |

### Category C – Major Damage

Ground until repaired; engineering evaluation required.

| Damage Type | Threshold | Action |
|-------------|-----------|--------|
| Cracks | Any visible crack | Ground, evaluate |
| Through-penetration | Any | Ground, evaluate |
| Large disbond | > TBD cm² | Ground, evaluate |
| Deformation | Visible | Ground, evaluate |
| Fire/heat damage | Any evidence | Ground, evaluate |

## Zone-Specific Limits

Different wing zones may have different allowable damage limits based on
structural criticality:

| Zone | Criticality | Special Limits |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Wing root | High | Reduced damage limits |
| Spar caps | High | Zero tolerance for cracks |
| Skin panels | Medium | Standard limits apply |
| Fairings | Low | Extended limits |
| Control surfaces | High | Zero tolerance for cracks |
