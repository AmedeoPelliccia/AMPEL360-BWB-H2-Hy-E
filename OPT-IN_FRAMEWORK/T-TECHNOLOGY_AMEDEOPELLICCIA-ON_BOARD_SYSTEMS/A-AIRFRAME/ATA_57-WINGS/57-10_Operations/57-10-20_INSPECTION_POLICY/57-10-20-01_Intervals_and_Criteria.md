# 57-10-20-01 — Intervals and Criteria

## Purpose

Define inspection intervals and acceptance criteria for wing structural elements
during operational service.

## Inspection Intervals

### Scheduled Inspections

| Inspection Type | Interval | Elements Covered |
|-----------------|----------|------------------|
| Pre-flight | Each flight | Visual inspection of accessible surfaces |
| Daily | 24 hours | Control surfaces, visible structure |
| Weekly | 7 days | Access panels, visible fasteners |
| A-Check | TBD FH | Detailed visual, selected NDT zones |
| C-Check | TBD FH | Comprehensive structural inspection |
| D-Check | TBD FH | Full structural overhaul |

### Condition-Based Triggers

| Trigger | Response |
|---------|----------|
| Hard landing (≥TBD G) | Special inspection per SRM |
| Lightning strike | Inspection per zone |
| Bird strike | Visual + NDT as required |
| SHM alert | Follow alert-specific procedure |
| Exceedance event | Engineering evaluation |

## Acceptance Criteria

### Visual Inspection

| Finding | Classification | Action |
|---------|----------------|--------|
| No visible damage | Acceptable | Continue ops |
| Surface scratches (depth <TBD mm) | Minor | Monitor |
| Cracks visible | Major | Ground, evaluate |
| Deformation | Major | Ground, evaluate |
| Corrosion (depth <TBD mm) | Minor | Schedule repair |
| Corrosion (depth ≥TBD mm) | Major | Immediate action |
