# 57-00-03-40 — Certification Basis and Standards

## Purpose

This document defines the certification basis and applicable standards for ATA 57 (WINGS).

## Scope

Covers all regulatory requirements and industry standards applicable to wing design, manufacture, and operation.

## Primary Certification Basis

### EASA Certification Specifications

The AMPEL360 wing is certified to [CS-25](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/certification-specifications/cs-25-large-aeroplanes) (Certification Specifications for Large Aeroplanes).

#### Applicable CS-25 Sections

| Section | Title | Applicability |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| Subpart C | Structure | Primary |
| CS-25.301 | Loads | Structural loads requirements |
| CS-25.303 | Factor of Safety | Ultimate load factor |
| CS-25.305 | Strength and Deformation | Structural strength |
| CS-25.307 | Proof of Structure | Test and analysis |
| CS-25.321–365 | Flight Loads | Maneuver and gust loads |
| CS-25.391–459 | Ground Loads | Ground handling loads |
| CS-25.471–519 | Water Loads | If applicable |
| CS-25.561–563 | Emergency Landing | Crash loads |
| CS-25.571 | Damage Tolerance | Fatigue and damage tolerance |
| CS-25.581 | Lightning Protection | Lightning strike |
| CS-25.601–609 | Design and Construction | General design |
| CS-25.629 | Aeroelastic Stability | Flutter requirements |
