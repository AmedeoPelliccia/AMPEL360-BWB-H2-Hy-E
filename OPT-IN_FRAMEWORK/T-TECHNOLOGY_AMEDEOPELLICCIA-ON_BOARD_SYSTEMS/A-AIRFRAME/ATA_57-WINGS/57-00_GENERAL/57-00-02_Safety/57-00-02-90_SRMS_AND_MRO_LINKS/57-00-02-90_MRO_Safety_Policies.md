## 1. Purpose

This document defines **high-level maintenance safety policies** for the ATA 57 Wing structure.

---

## 2. Maintenance Safety Philosophy

### 2.1 Core Principles

1. **Damage tolerance**: Structure is designed to sustain damage until detected and repaired.  
2. **Scheduled inspections**: Primary means of damage detection at defined intervals.  
3. **Condition monitoring**: SHM provides additional visibility between inspections.  
4. **Repair quality**: Repairs must restore required structural capability.  

### 2.2 Safety Objectives

| Objective | Policy |
| :-- | :-- |
| Prevent undetected failures | Inspection program coverage |
| Prevent incorrect repairs | Approved data only |
| Prevent maintenance-induced damage | Procedures, training |
| Enable continued safe operation | Inspection intervals |
