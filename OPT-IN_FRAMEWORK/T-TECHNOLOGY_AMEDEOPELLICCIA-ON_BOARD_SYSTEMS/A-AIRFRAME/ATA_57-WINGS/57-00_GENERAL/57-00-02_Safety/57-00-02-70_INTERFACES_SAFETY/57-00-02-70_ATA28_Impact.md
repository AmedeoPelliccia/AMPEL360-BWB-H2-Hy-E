## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **safety interface** between ATA 57 (Wings) and ATA 28 (Fuel).

---

## 2. Interface Description

The Fuel system (ATA 28) interfaces with the wing structure through:

- Integral fuel tanks (wing box as tank structure)  
- Fuel system equipment mounting  
- Fuel lines and connections  
- Vent and drain provisions
