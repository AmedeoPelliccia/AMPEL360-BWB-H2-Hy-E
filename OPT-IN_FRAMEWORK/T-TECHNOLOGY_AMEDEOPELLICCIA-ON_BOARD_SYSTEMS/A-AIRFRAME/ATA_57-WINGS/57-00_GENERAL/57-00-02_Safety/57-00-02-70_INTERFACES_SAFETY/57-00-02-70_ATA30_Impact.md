## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **safety interface** between ATA 57 (Wings) and ATA 30 (Ice and Rain Protection).

---

## 2. Interface Description

The Ice Protection system (ATA 30) interfaces with the wing structure through:

- Leading edge ice protection (thermal or pneumatic)  
- Structural provisions for ice protection equipment  
- Thermal management (if thermal anti-ice)  
- Boot bonding (if pneumatic de-ice)  

---

## 3. Safety Interface Items

### 3.1 Leading Edge Ice Protection

| System Type | Structural Interface | Safety Relevance |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Thermal anti-ice | Piccolo tubes, ducting, leading edge structure | Heat management, structural temperature |
| Electrical anti-ice | Heating elements, wiring | Electrical routing, heat management |
| Pneumatic de-ice | Boot attachment, pressure routing | Surface integrity, ice shedding |

| Issue | Structural Impact | Safety Relevance |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
