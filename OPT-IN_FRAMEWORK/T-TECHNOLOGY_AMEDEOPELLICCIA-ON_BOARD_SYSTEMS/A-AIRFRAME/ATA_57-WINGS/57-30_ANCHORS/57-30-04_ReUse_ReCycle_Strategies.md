## 3. Re-Use Strategies

### 3.1 Component Re-Use Decision Matrix

| Component | Re-Use Potential | Criteria | Destination |
|-----------|------------------|----------|-------------|
| Wing Spars | Medium | Fatigue life remaining > 50% | Same aircraft type |
| Wing Ribs | High | No corrosion, NDT pass | Same or smaller aircraft |
| Control Surface Actuators | High | Functional test pass | Overhaul/reinstall |
| Fasteners | Low | One-time use design | Recycle only |
| Composite Skins | Low | Limited repair capability | Recycle/downcycle |

### 3.2 Re-Use Process Flow

1. **Removal Assessment**: Document condition at removal  
2. **NDT Inspection**: Non-destructive testing per specification  
3. **Certification Review**: Verify remaining certified life  
4. **Refurbishment**: Perform required repairs/overhaul  
5. **Re-Certification**: Issue new certification documents  
6. **Re-Installation**: Track via DPP system

## 4. Recycling Pathways

### 4.1 Material-Specific Recycling

| Material | Process | Recovery Rate | Output |
|----------|---------|---------------|--------|
| CFRP | Pyrolysis | 60-70% fiber | Reclaimed carbon fiber |
| CFRP | Mechanical grinding | 95% mass | Filler material |
| Aluminum | Remelting | 95%+ | Ingots for reuse |
| Titanium | Remelting | 90%+ | Aerospace-grade ingots |
| Thermoplastics | Reprocessing | 90%+ | Pellets for remolding |
