## Section Briefings

### 31-00 General
Architecture end-to-end (sensors → concentrators → alerts → displays → recorders), HMI philosophy, event/time model, interfaces with ATA 22/24/42/45/46, and baseline/compliance.

### 31-10 Panels
Physical panels (overhead/glareshield/maintenance), dimming/lighting, annunciators, test controls, wiring and BITE associated.

### 31-20 Independent Instruments
Standby/independent (display or instruments), independent power supply, clock/chronometer and time synchronization for correlation with recorders.

### 31-30 Recorders
FDR/CVR/QAR, DAU, offload, tests, location/crashworthiness and dataset traceability (FOQA/health).

### 31-40 Central Computers
Concentrators, acquisition, alert engine, rendering control, IMA hosting/partitions, BITE/diagnostics.

### 31-50 Central Warning
CAS/ECAM/EICAS, priorities/inhibits, aural alerts, master caution/warning, consistency and rules (BREX/Business rules).

### 31-60 Central Display
DUs (PFD/ND/MFD), synoptics, source switching, degraded/reversion, calibration and test.

### 31-70 Automatic Data Reporting
ACMS/health reporting, triggers, datasets, datalink integration and hardening (integrity, hashes/evidence), packaging to ground tools.
