# SSOT — Single Source of Truth

## Overview

This directory contains the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for ATA 31-50-10 (CAS/ECAM/EICAS Message Model and Priorities). It follows the complete product lifecycle from problem statement through retirement.

## Lifecycle Phases

### LC01_PROBLEM_STATEMENT
Initial problem definition, context, and stakeholder needs.

### LC02_SYSTEM_REQUIREMENTS
- **REQUIREMENTS/**: Requirements specifications (CSV format)
- **INTERFACES/**: Interface Control Documents (ICDs) with other systems

### LC03_DESIGN_MODELS
Design specifications, architecture diagrams, and system models.

### LC04_ENGINEERING_ANALYSIS
Engineering analysis, calculations, simulations, and trade studies.

### LC05_INTEGRATION_TESTING_PROTOTYPING
Integration plans, test procedures, and prototype development.

### LC06_QUALITY
Quality assurance plans, metrics, and quality control records.

### LC07_SAFETY_SECURITY
Safety analysis (FHA, FTA, FMEA), security assessments, and hazard analysis.

### LC08_CERTIFICATION_FIRST_FLIGHT
Certification evidence, compliance matrices, and first flight readiness.

### LC09_GREEN_BASELINES
Configuration baselines, approved designs, and as-built documentation.

### LC10_INDUSTRIALIZATION_CM
Manufacturing plans, configuration management, and production readiness.

### LC11_OPERATIONS
Operational procedures, flight crew guidance, and normal operations documentation.

### LC12_SUPPORT_SERVICES
Support services, training materials, and logistics support.

### LC13_MRO_SUSTAINMENT
Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul procedures, sustainment plans, and obsolescence management.

### LC14_RETIREMENT_CIRCULARITY
End-of-life planning, decommissioning procedures, and circular economy considerations.

## Usage

Each lifecycle phase contains relevant documentation, data, and evidence for that phase of development. Cross-references between phases are maintained through traceability matrices.
