## 2. Human-Centred Design Principles

### 2.1 User-Centered Approach
- Flight crew as primary monitors and decision-makers
- Cabin crew as secondary users for passenger comfort feedback
- Maintenance personnel as system operators

### 2.2 Design Principles
- Transparency: System status and decisions are visible to users
- Controllability: Users can override AI recommendations
- Predictability: System behaviour is consistent and understandable
- Feedback: Clear indication of system state and actions
