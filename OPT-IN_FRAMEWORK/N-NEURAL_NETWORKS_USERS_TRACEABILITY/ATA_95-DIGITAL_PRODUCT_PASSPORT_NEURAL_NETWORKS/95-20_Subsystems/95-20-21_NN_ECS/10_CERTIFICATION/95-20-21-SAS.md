## 1. Introduction

This System Assurance Summary (SAS) provides an overview of the assurance approach and evidence for the NN-ECS (Environmental Control System Neural Networks) subsystem, demonstrating compliance with applicable certification requirements.

## 2. System Overview

### 2.1 System Description
The NN-ECS subsystem provides AI-driven control and optimization for cabin environmental management, comprising six neural network components:
1. Cabin Temperature Predictor
2. Air Quality Monitor
3. HVAC Optimizer
4. Pressure Control NN
5. Humidity Management
6. CO₂ Scrubbing Optimizer
