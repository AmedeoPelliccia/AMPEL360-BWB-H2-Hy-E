# Pressure Vessel Analysis — AMPEL360 Q100 INTEGRA BWB

## 1. Summary

This document presents the baseline structural geometry parameters for the
AMPEL360 Q100 INTEGRA Blended Wing Body pressure vessel. Values listed here
are frozen and monitored by the Geometry Baseline Watchdog CI workflow.

## 2. Pressure Vessel Dimensions

- Equivalent radius: **3.0 m**
- Length: **35.0 m**
- Depth: **3.5 m**

## 3. Design Conditions

The pressure vessel is sized for a cabin differential pressure of 0.585 bar
(8.48 psi) at maximum operating altitude FL 430, with a 1.33 proof factor
and 2.0 ultimate factor per CS-25.365.

## 4. Structural Concept

The BWB centre-body pressure vessel uses a multi-bubble arrangement with
shared webs to react the internal pressure loads efficiently. The equivalent
radius quoted above corresponds to the single-bubble radius that produces
the same cross-sectional cabin area.
