# 03-50-04-02A - Access Stairs and Ladders

## 1. Purpose
Specification for structural design of access stairs and ladders for Ground Support Equipment (GSE), ensuring safe personnel access to elevated work areas and platforms.

## 2. Scope
- Fixed and portable stairs
- Fixed and portable ladders
- Handrails and guards
- Step-over platforms

## 3. Applicable Documents
- OSHA 1910.23 (Ladders), 1910.24 (Step Bolts), 1910.25 (Stairways)
- ANSI A14.3 (Fixed Ladders), A14.5 (Portable Ladders)
- EN 131 (Ladders - Terms, Types, Functional Sizes)
- IBC 1011 (Means of Egress - Stairways)

## 4. Structural Description

### 4.1 Stair Design Requirements
| Parameter | Requirement | Standard |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Tread Depth | 230-280 mm | OSHA 1910.25 |
| Riser Height | 150-190 mm | OSHA 1910.25 |
| Tread Width | 560 mm minimum | OSHA 1910.25 |
| Handrail Height | 860-965 mm | OSHA 1910.28 |
| Slope | 30-50° from horizontal | OSHA 1910.25 |
| Live Load | 2.4 kPa uniform | IBC 1607 |

### 4.2 Ladder Design Requirements
| Parameter | Requirement | Standard |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Rung Spacing | 250-380 mm | OSHA 1910.27 |
| Rung Diameter | 16-19 mm (round) | ANSI A14.3 |
| Side Rail Spacing | 300-450 mm | OSHA 1910.27 |
| Cage (>6 m height) | Required | OSHA 1910.28 |
| Rest Platform (>9 m) | Every 9 m max | OSHA 1910.28 |
| Ladder Capacity | 1.1 kN minimum | ANSI A14.3 |

### 4.3 Materials
- **Stairs**: Steel (A36) or aluminum alloy (6061-T6)
- **Ladders**: Aluminum alloy (6061-T6) or steel
- **Handrails**: Steel tube or aluminum tube, 32-38 mm diameter
- **Anti-slip**: Serrated treads or adhesive coating

## 5. Structural Requirements

### 5.1 Design Criteria
| Component | Safety Factor | Deflection Limit |
|-----------|---------------|------------------|
| Stair Stringers | 2.0 on yield | L/240 |
| Ladder Rails | 2.5 on yield | L/200 |
| Handrails | 2.0 on yield | Not applicable |
| Platforms | 2.0 on yield | L/240 |

### 5.2 Load Testing
- Stairs: 1.5× design live load
- Ladders: 1.25× rated capacity (275 kg typical)
- Handrails: 890 N horizontal load test
