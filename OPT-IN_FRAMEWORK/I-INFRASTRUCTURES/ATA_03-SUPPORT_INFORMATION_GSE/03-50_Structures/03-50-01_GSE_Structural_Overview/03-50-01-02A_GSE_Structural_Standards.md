# 03-50-01-02A - GSE Structural Standards

## 1. Purpose
This document identifies and summarizes the applicable structural standards, codes, and regulations for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) design, fabrication, and certification. It provides a reference framework for GSE structural compliance.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- International and national structural codes
- Pressure vessel and piping standards
- Welding and fabrication standards
- Material standards
- Hydrogen-specific standards
- Testing and inspection standards

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- All standards listed in Section 4 of this document

## 4. Structural Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE structural standards provide the regulatory and technical framework ensuring safety, reliability, and interoperability of ground support equipment. Compliance with these standards is mandatory for certification and operation.

## 5. Structural Requirements

### 5.1 Compliance Matrix
| GSE Type | Primary Standards | Secondary Standards |
|----------|------------------|---------------------|
| LH2 Storage Tanks | ASME VIII Div 1, EN 13458, ISO 21009 | ASME B31.12, NFPA 2 |
| Mobile GSE Frames | AISC 360, AWS D1.1/D1.2 | ASTM material specs |
| H2 Piping Systems | ASME B31.12, EN 13480 | SAE AS6968, NFPA 2 |
| Maintenance Platforms | AISC 360, OSHA 1910.29 | AWS D1.1, ASTM A36 |
| Pressure Relief Systems | ASME Section VIII, API 520 | NFPA 2, ISO 4126 |

### 5.2 Certification Requirements
- All pressure vessels require ASME U-stamp or equivalent CE marking
- Welding procedures must be qualified per AWS D1.1 or EN ISO 15614
- H2 systems require additional safety analysis per NFPA 2
- Third-party inspection required for all critical structures

### 5.3 Documentation Requirements
- Design calculations stamped by licensed Professional Engineer
- Material certificates (MTRs) for all structural materials
- Welding procedure specifications (WPS) and qualification records (PQR)
- Nondestructive testing reports
- Hydrostatic/pneumatic test reports for pressure systems
