# 03-50-05-04A - Collision Protection

## 1. Purpose
Specification for structural design of collision protection systems for Ground Support Equipment (GSE), including barriers, bollards, bumpers, and impact-absorbing structures to prevent or mitigate vehicle and equipment collisions.

## 2. Scope
- Protective barriers and bollards
- Impact-absorbing bumpers
- Crash cushions and attenuators
- Traffic separators and guards
- Equipment collision protection

## 3. Applicable Documents
- AASHTO MASH (Manual for Assessing Safety Hardware)
- ASTM F2656 (Vehicle Crash Testing of Perimeter Barriers)
- ISO 14120 (Safety of Machinery - Guards)
- IBC 1607 (Live Loads - Impact Loads)
- AWS D1.1 (Structural Welding Code)

## 4. Structural Description

### 4.1 Protection System Types
| Type | Application | Impact Rating | Installation |
|------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| Steel Bollard | Equipment/building protection | 50-500 kN | Fixed (embedded) or removable |
| Concrete Barrier | Traffic separation | 100-1000 kN | Cast-in-place or precast |

### 4.2 Bollard Design
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Diameter | 100-300 mm | Larger for higher impact |
| Height Above Ground | 1.0-1.5 m | Prevent vehicle override |
| Embedment Depth | 1.0-1.5 m | Resist overturning |
| Material | Steel pipe, SCH 80 or XXH | High-strength |
| Impact Height | 450-600 mm | Bumper height |
| Spacing | 1.0-1.5 m center-to-center | Prevent vehicle penetration |

### 4.4 Load Cases
| Scenario | Vehicle Mass | Impact Speed | Kinetic Energy |
|----------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| Pedestrian Cart | 500 kg | 5 km/h | 0.5 kJ |
| Light GSE Vehicle | 2,000 kg | 10 km/h | 8 kJ |
| Tug (laden) | 10,000 kg | 15 km/h | 87 kJ |
| Service Truck | 15,000 kg | 20 km/h | 230 kJ |
| Runaway Heavy Vehicle | 25,000 kg | 30 km/h | 870 kJ |

### 4.5 Energy Absorption
- **Elastic Deformation**: Structure deforms but returns (bollards)
- **Plastic Deformation**: Permanent deformation absorbs energy (crash cushions)
- **Friction**: Sliding/rotation dissipates energy (cable barriers)
- **Material Crushing**: Cellular materials compress (foam bumpers)

## 5. Structural Requirements

### 5.1 Design Criteria
| Parameter | Requirement | Standard |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Impact Force | Per design vehicle scenario | Site risk analysis |
| Deflection (bollards) | < 100 mm at design load | Prevent vehicle passage |
| Energy Absorption | 100% of kinetic energy | No rebound into traffic |
| Residual Strength | Structure remains functional | Post-impact inspection |
| Safety Factor | 1.5 on yield (impact loading) | Dynamic load consideration |
| Corrosion Protection | Hot-dip galvanized or epoxy | 20-year service life |

### 5.2 Bollard Structural Design
- **Pipe Diameter**: 150-300 mm (SCH 80 or XXH)
- **Wall Thickness**: 10-20 mm minimum
- **Embedment**: 1.0-1.5 m in concrete (450 mm diameter)
- **Concrete Strength**: 30 MPa minimum
- **Yielding**: Acceptable at base, prevents vehicle passage

### 5.3 Barrier Structural Design
- **Concrete Strength**: 30-40 MPa
- **Reinforcement**: Per impact analysis (typically #5 @ 200 mm)
- **Anchorage**: Bolts or embedded, resist sliding and overturning
- **Joint Design**: Allow thermal movement, prevent separation

### 5.4 Crash Cushion Design
- **Type**: Sand-filled barrels, foam cartridges, or honeycomb
- **Capacity**: Match design impact scenario
- **Reusability**: Single-use or resettable
- **Visibility**: High-visibility markings and reflectors

### 5.5 Testing and Certification
| Test Type | Requirement | Frequency |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Static Load Test | 1.5× design impact force | Prototype validation |
| Impact Test | Full-scale vehicle impact | Per ASTM F2656 or MASH |
| Visual Inspection | Check for damage, corrosion | Quarterly |
| Post-Impact Inspection | Assess damage, repair/replace | After each significant impact |

### 5.6 Installation Requirements
- **Location**: Clear zone from building/equipment ≥ deflection distance
- **Visibility**: High-visibility paint or reflective markers
- **Signage**: Warning signs per local traffic standards
- **Lighting**: Illumination in low-light conditions
- **Maintenance Access**: Allow inspection and repair
