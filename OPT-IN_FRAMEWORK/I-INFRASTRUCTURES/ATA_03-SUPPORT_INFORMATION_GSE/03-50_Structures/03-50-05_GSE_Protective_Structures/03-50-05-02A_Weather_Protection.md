# 03-50-05-02A - Weather Protection

## 1. Purpose
Specification for structural design of weather protection systems for Ground Support Equipment (GSE), including canopies, shelters, covers, and environmental barriers.

## 2. Scope
- Equipment canopies and shelters
- Weather covers and enclosures
- Windbreaks and barriers
- Solar shading structures
- Rain and snow protection

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ASCE 7 (Minimum Design Loads - Wind, Snow)
- IBC 3105 (Membrane Structures)
- ASTM D4851 (Coated Fabrics for Awnings)
- EN 13782 (Temporary Structures - Tents)
- AWS D1.1 (Structural Welding Code)

## 4. Structural Description

### 4.1 Protection System Types
| Type | Function | Structure | Material |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Fixed Canopy | Equipment shelter | Steel or aluminum frame | Fabric, polycarbonate, metal |
| Retractable Canopy | Adjustable coverage | Steel frame + mechanism | Fabric, aluminum panels |
| Temporary Shelter | Mobile/seasonal protection | Tubular frame | PVC-coated fabric |
| Equipment Covers | Individual equipment protection | Fabric | PVC, polyester, tarp |
| Windbreak | Wind speed reduction | Panel or mesh | Perforated metal, fabric |

### 4.2 Load Requirements
| Load Type | Magnitude | Standard |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Wind (open terrain) | 160 km/h basic speed | ASCE 7 |
| Wind (enclosed structure) | ±1.0 kPa pressure | ASCE 7 |
| Snow | Per local climate (0.5-2.5 kPa) | ASCE 7 |
| Ice | 25 mm radial thickness | ASCE 7 (ice-prone regions) |
| Dead Load | Structure + cladding weight | Design analysis |
| Ponding | Prevent water accumulation | 100 mm min slope |

### 4.3 Canopy Structures
| Component | Material | Design Criteria |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Columns | Steel tube or W-section | Compression, buckling |
| Beams/Trusses | Steel or aluminum | Bending, deflection |
| Roof Panels | Polycarbonate, metal, fabric | Wind uplift, snow |
| Anchors | Concrete foundations | Uplift, overturning |
| Gutters/Drainage | PVC or metal | Roof drainage |

### 4.4 Fabric Structures
- **Material**: PVC-coated polyester or PTFE-coated fiberglass
- **Strength**: 4-6 kN/m warp/fill direction
- **UV Resistance**: 10 year minimum life
- **Fire Rating**: Class A or B per ASTM E84
- **Seams**: Welded or sewn with reinforcement

## 5. Structural Requirements

### 5.1 Design Criteria
| Parameter | Requirement | Standard |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Wind Load | 160 km/h or local code | ASCE 7 |
| Snow Load | Per local ground snow load | ASCE 7 |
| Safety Factor (structure) | 2.0 on yield | AISC 360 |
| Deflection (beams) | L/180 under snow + dead | AISC 360 |
| Anchorage | 1.5× uplift load | ACI 318 |
| Corrosion Protection | Hot-dip galvanized or painted | ASTM A123 |

### 5.2 Wind Analysis
- **Enclosed Structure**: Pressure coefficients per ASCE 7 Fig 27.3-1
- **Open Canopy**: Uplift coefficient -1.0 to -1.2
- **Partial Enclosure**: Increased internal pressure
- **Gust Effects**: Gust factor 0.85-0.90 for flexible structures

### 5.3 Drainage Design
- **Minimum Slope**: 2% (1:50) for rigid roofs
- **Fabric Tension**: Sufficient to prevent ponding
- **Gutter Capacity**: 100-year storm intensity
- **Overflow**: Emergency drainage paths

### 5.4 Temporary Structures
- **Assembly Time**: < 8 hours for large shelter
- **Anchoring**: Ground stakes, weights, or concrete blocks
- **Wind Limit**: Dismantle if wind forecast > 80 km/h
- **Inspection**: Before each use and after wind events
