# 03-30_ANCHORS

## Purpose

**ANCHORS** (Aircraft Networks, Circular, Harvesting, Operating, Renewable Systems) provides a comprehensive sustainability framework for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) supporting the AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft. This bucket encompasses circular economy practices, renewable energy integration, energy harvesting, and zero-emission operational strategies.

## Scope

### ATA 03 Context

ANCHORS in ATA 03 focuses exclusively on **Ground Support Equipment (GSE)** and airport infrastructure supporting aircraft operations. This includes:

- **Electric and H₂-powered GSE** (refueling equipment, ground power units, towing vehicles, etc.)
- **Renewable energy infrastructure** (solar, wind, hydrogen production)
- **Circular economy programs** (reuse, refurbishment, end-of-life management)
- **Energy harvesting systems** (solar panels on GSE, regenerative braking, kinetic harvesting)
- **Smart grid integration** (V2G, demand response, energy storage)
- **Digital connectivity** (IoT, fleet management, Digital Product Passport integration)

### ANCHORS Pillars

1. **A**ircraft Networks — GSE-to-aircraft connectivity and data integration
2. **N**etworks — IoT, 5G, and communication infrastructure
3. **C**ircular — Circular economy and lifecycle management
4. **H**arvesting — Energy harvesting and recovery systems
5. **O**perating — Sustainable zero-emission operations
6. **R**enewable — Renewable energy systems and green hydrogen
7. **S**ystems — Integrated smart systems and infrastructure
