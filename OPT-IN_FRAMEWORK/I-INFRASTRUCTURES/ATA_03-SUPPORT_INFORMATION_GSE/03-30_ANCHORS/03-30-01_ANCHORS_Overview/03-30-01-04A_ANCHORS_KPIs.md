# 03-30-01-04A — ANCHORS KPIs

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** and measurement framework for monitoring, evaluating, and reporting on the ANCHORS program performance. It establishes targets, measurement methodologies, and reporting requirements aligned with international sustainability standards.

## 2. Scope

This KPI framework covers:
- Environmental performance metrics
- Operational efficiency indicators
- Financial performance measures
- Innovation and technology adoption metrics
- Stakeholder satisfaction indicators

## 3. Applicable Documents

- **[GRI Standards](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/)** — Global Reporting Initiative
- **[TCFD](https://www.fsb-tcfd.org/)** — Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures
- **[ISO 14031](https://www.iso.org/standard/52297.html)** — Environmental Performance Evaluation
- **[SASB](https://www.sasb.org/)** — Sustainability Accounting Standards Board
- **[Airport Carbon Accreditation](https://www.aci.aero/about-aci/priorities/environment/airport-carbon-accreditation/)** — ACI Program
- [03-30-01-01A — ANCHORS Framework](./03-30-01-01A_ANCHORS_Framework.md)

## 4. KPI Framework Structure

### 4.1 KPI Hierarchy

```
Level 1: Strategic KPIs (Board/Executive)
    ├── Net-Zero Progress
    ├── Total Investment ROI
    └── Regulatory Compliance

Level 2: Tactical KPIs (Management)
    ├── Environmental Performance
    ├── Operational Efficiency
    ├── Financial Performance
    └── Technology Adoption

Level 3: Operational KPIs (Daily Operations)
    ├── Energy Consumption
    ├── Fleet Availability
    ├── Service Level Achievement
    └── Maintenance Performance
```

## 5. Strategic KPIs

### 5.1 Net-Zero Progress

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement | Frequency |
|-----|------------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Total GHG Emissions** | Scope 1+2+3 CO₂e | -80% by 2030, Net-zero by 2035 | ISO 14064 inventory | Quarterly |
| **Carbon Intensity** | kg CO₂e per turnaround | <5 kg by 2030 | Calculated | Monthly |
| **Renewable Energy Share** | % energy from renewables | 100% by 2030 | Metered | Monthly |
| **Carbon Offset Quality** | Verified carbon credits | Gold Standard/VCS | Portfolio review | Annual |

**Baseline (2024)**: 12,500 tons CO₂e/year, 85 kg CO₂e/turnaround

### 5.2 Investment ROI

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement | Frequency |
|-----|------------|--------|-------------|-----------|

| **Payback Period** | Time to recover investment | <7 years average | Project tracking | Per project |
| **Avoided Costs** | Savings vs. business-as-usual | >€200M by 2035 | Cost comparison | Quarterly |
| **Green Finance Access** | Green bonds/loans secured | €150M by 2028 | Finance records | Annual |

### 5.3 Regulatory Compliance

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement | Frequency |
|-----|------------|--------|-------------|-----------|

## 11. Reporting Framework

### 11.1 Reporting Cadence

| Report Type | Audience | Frequency | Format | Standards |
|-------------|----------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **KPI Dashboard** | Operations teams | Real-time | Web dashboard | Internal |
| **Management Report** | Leadership | Monthly | PDF + presentation | Internal |
| **Sustainability Report** | Public/investors | Annual | GRI format | GRI, TCFD, SASB |
| **Regulatory Filings** | Authorities | As required | Varies | ISO 14001, ISO 50001 |
| **Investor ESG Briefing** | Investors | Quarterly | Presentation | TCFD, CDP |

### 11.2 Data Quality Standards

- **Accuracy**: >99% for financial KPIs, >95% for operational KPIs
- **Timeliness**: Real-time for operational, monthly close for financial
- **Completeness**: <1% missing data points
- **Consistency**: Single source of truth (DPP system)
- **Auditability**: Full data lineage and version control

## 12. Continuous Improvement

### 12.1 KPI Review Process

- **Quarterly**: Operational KPI performance review, corrective actions
- **Semi-Annual**: Tactical KPI assessment, target adjustments
- **Annual**: Strategic KPI evaluation, framework refresh

### 12.2 Benchmarking

- **Internal**: Year-over-year comparison, best practice sharing
- **External**: Industry benchmarks (ACI, IATA), peer comparison
- **Best-in-Class**: Target top quartile performance by 2030
