# 03-70-01-04A - Zero Emission GSE Propulsion Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the **strategic roadmap** for transitioning AMPEL360's Ground Support Equipment (GSE) fleet from conventional fossil fuel propulsion to **zero-emission propulsion technologies**. It aligns with AMPEL360's commitment to sustainability, carbon neutrality, and leadership in green aviation ground operations.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Coverage

This strategy covers:
- Vision and objectives for zero-emission GSE fleet
- Phased transition plan (2025-2035)
- Technology prioritization (H₂ fuel cell, battery electric, hybrid)
- Infrastructure development roadmap
- Regulatory and policy alignment
- Stakeholder engagement and training

### 2.2 Strategic Horizon

- **Short-term (2025-2027):** Pilot programs and early adoption
- **Medium-term (2028-2031):** Fleet-scale deployment
- **Long-term (2032-2035):** Full zero-emission fleet achievement

## 6. Zero-Emission Technology Strategy

### 6.1 Technology Selection Framework

Based on technical assessment (see [03-70-01-03A](./03-70-01-03A_GSE_Propulsion_Selection.md)):

#### 6.1.1 H₂ Fuel Cell Propulsion — Primary Technology for Heavy-Duty GSE

**Target Applications:**
- Heavy-duty tractors (150-300 kW)
- Pushback tugs (100-200 kW)
- High-utilization cargo loaders (100+ kW, >12 h/day operation)

**Rationale:**
- Extended range and endurance
- Fast refueling (3-10 minutes)
- High power density suitable for heavy loads
- Operational flexibility equivalent to diesel

#### 6.1.2 Battery Electric Propulsion — Primary Technology for Light-Medium Duty GSE

**Target Applications:**
- Belt loaders (30-80 kW)
- Ground power units (50-100 kW)
- Service vehicles (20-50 kW)
- Moderate-duty cargo loaders (<100 kW, <8 h/day operation)

**Rationale:**
- Lowest total cost of ownership
- Mature technology with extensive supplier base
- Simple infrastructure (grid electricity)
- Excellent for short-range, intermittent-duty operations

#### 6.1.3 H₂-Electric Hybrid Propulsion — Niche Applications

**Target Applications:**
- Specialized heavy-duty GSE with peak power demands
- GSE requiring extended range + regenerative braking

**Rationale:**
- Optimized energy management for variable load profiles
- Downsized fuel cell with battery for peak power

### 6.2 Diesel-Electric Hybrid — Transitional Technology Only

- **Purpose:** Interim solution for select heavy-duty GSE during infrastructure build-out
- **Phase-Out Target:** 2030 (no new diesel-hybrid procurements after 2028)

## 7. Phased Transition Plan

### 7.1 Phase 1: Foundation and Pilot Programs (2025-2027)

#### 7.1.1 Objectives
- Validate H₂ fuel cell and battery electric technologies in operational environment
- Establish initial infrastructure
- Build organizational capability and expertise

#### 7.1.2 Key Actions

| **Action** | **Target** | **Timeline** |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **H₂ Fuel Cell Pilot Expansion** | 10 heavy-duty tractors, 10 pushback tugs | 2025-2026 |
| **Battery Electric Deployment** | 40 belt loaders, 20 GPUs, 30 service vehicles | 2025-2027 |
| **H₂ Refueling Infrastructure** | 1 mobile H₂ refueling station (200 kg/day capacity) | 2026 |
| **EV Charging Infrastructure** | 50 AC chargers, 10 DC fast chargers | 2025-2026 |
| **Renewable Energy Integration** | 2 MW solar PV for charging infrastructure | 2027 |
| **Training Programs** | H₂ safety and HV electrical certification for 100 personnel | 2025-2027 |

#### 7.1.3 Expected Outcomes
- **Zero-Emission GSE Fleet:** 30% by end of 2027
- **CO₂ Reduction:** 25% vs. 2024 baseline
- **Lessons Learned:** Operational data for Phase 2 scaling

### 7.2 Phase 2: Fleet-Scale Deployment (2028-2031)

#### 7.2.1 Objectives
- Large-scale fleet conversion
- On-site green H₂ production
- 100% renewable energy for GSE operations

#### 7.2.2 Key Actions

| **Action** | **Target** | **Timeline** |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **H₂ Fuel Cell Deployment** | 35 heavy-duty tractors, 20 pushback tugs, 30 cargo loaders | 2028-2031 |
| **Battery Electric Deployment** | Complete conversion of all light-medium duty GSE | 2028-2030 |
| **On-Site Green H₂ Production** | 1 MW electrolyzer + storage (500 kg/day capacity) | 2028 |
| **H₂ Refueling Infrastructure** | 2 permanent H₂ refueling stations | 2028-2029 |
| **EV Charging Expansion** | 80 AC chargers, 20 DC fast chargers | 2028-2030 |
| **Renewable Energy Expansion** | 5 MW solar PV + 2 MWh battery storage | 2029 |
| **Diesel Fleet Phase-Out** | Retire 80% of diesel GSE | 2028-2031 |

#### 7.2.3 Expected Outcomes
- **Zero-Emission GSE Fleet:** 80% by end of 2031
- **CO₂ Reduction:** 75% vs. 2024 baseline
- **Green H₂ Self-Sufficiency:** 70% of H₂ produced on-site

### 7.3 Phase 3: Full Zero-Emission Achievement (2032-2035)

#### 7.3.1 Objectives
- 100% zero-emission GSE fleet
- Full green H₂ self-sufficiency
- Continuous improvement and optimization

#### 7.3.2 Key Actions

| **Action** | **Target** | **Timeline** |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Final H₂ Fuel Cell Deployment** | Remaining heavy-duty GSE | 2032-2033 |
| **Diesel Fleet Retirement** | Complete phase-out of all diesel GSE | 2034 |
| **H₂ Production Expansion** | 2 MW electrolyzer + storage (1,000 kg/day capacity) | 2032 |
| **Fleet Optimization** | Replace first-generation zero-emission GSE with advanced models | 2034-2035 |
| **Circular Economy Integration** | Battery and fuel cell recycling programs | 2033 |

#### 7.3.3 Expected Outcomes
- **Zero-Emission GSE Fleet:** 100% by 2035
- **CO₂ Reduction:** 95% vs. 2024 baseline (residual 5% from upstream emissions)
- **Green H₂ Self-Sufficiency:** 100%
