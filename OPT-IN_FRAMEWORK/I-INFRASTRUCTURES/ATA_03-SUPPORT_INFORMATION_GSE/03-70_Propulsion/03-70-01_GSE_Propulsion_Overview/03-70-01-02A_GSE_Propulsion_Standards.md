# 03-70-01-02A - GSE Propulsion Standards

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the **standards landscape** and **compliance requirements** for propulsion systems used in Ground Support Equipment (GSE) at AMPEL360 facilities. It provides a comprehensive reference to international, national, and industry-specific regulations applicable to zero-emission and conventional GSE propulsion technologies.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Coverage

This document covers:
- International standards for hydrogen, electric, and hybrid propulsion
- Safety standards and regulations
- Fuel quality and refueling standards
- Testing and certification requirements
- Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) requirements
- Environmental and emissions regulations

### 2.2 Applicability

Applies to:
- All new GSE procurements
- Retrofitted GSE propulsion systems
- In-service GSE modifications
- Third-party GSE operating at AMPEL360 facilities

## 3. Applicable Documents

This section consolidates all applicable standards and regulations for GSE propulsion systems.

## 13. Testing and Certification Requirements

### 13.1 Type Approval Process

1. **Pre-Design Review:** Standards applicability assessment
2. **Design Verification:** Compliance with design standards
3. **Prototype Testing:** Physical testing per applicable standards
4. **Type Approval:** Certification by competent authority
5. **Production Quality Control:** Ongoing compliance verification

### 13.2 Required Testing

- Fuel cell system safety testing (ISO 23273)
- Battery safety and performance testing (IEC 62619, UN 38.3)
- EMC testing (ISO 11451/11452, CISPR 12)
- Emissions testing (EU Stage V / EPA Tier 4 for ICE systems)
- Functional safety validation (ISO 26262 where applicable)
