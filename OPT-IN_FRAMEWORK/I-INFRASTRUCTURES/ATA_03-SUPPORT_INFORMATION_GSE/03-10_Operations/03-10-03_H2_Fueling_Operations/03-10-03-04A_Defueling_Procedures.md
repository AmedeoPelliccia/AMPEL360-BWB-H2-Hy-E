# 03-10-03-04A - Defueling Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized liquid hydrogen (LH2) defueling procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring safe removal of H2 fuel when required for maintenance, weight reduction, or system servicing.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Reasons and authorization for defueling
- Pre-defueling preparations and safety measures
- LH2 defueling operations
- Post-defueling purging and verification
- Special considerations for emergency defueling
- Fuel quality preservation during defueling

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Defueling Authorization

#### 4.1.1 Approved Reasons for Defueling

| Reason | Authorization Required | Priority |
|--------|------------------------|----------|
| **Weight reduction for maintenance** | Maintenance Control | Routine |
| **Fuel system maintenance/inspection** | Maintenance Control + Engineering | Routine |
| **Fuel quality issue** | Quality Assurance + Ops Manager | Immediate |
| **Emergency (leak, contamination, fire risk)** | Captain or Senior Manager on duty | Immediate |
| **Long-term storage (>30 days)** | Fleet Management | Routine |
| **Aircraft sale or transfer** | Fleet Management | Routine |

#### 4.1.2 Defueling Request and Approval

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Submit defueling request with reason | Requesting Manager |
| 2 | Review safety and operational impact | Safety Officer |
| 3 | Approve or deny request | Authorized Approver |
| 4 | Schedule defueling operation | Ground Operations |
| 5 | Coordinate with H2 fueling team | Fueling Supervisor |
| 6 | Document authorization | All parties |

### 4.2 Prerequisites

- Defueling authorization obtained and documented
- Aircraft properly positioned (outdoor, well-ventilated area preferred)
- Parking brake set and chocks installed
- Engines shut down and cool
- APU shut down
- All passengers and non-essential personnel removed from aircraft
- Defueling equipment inspected and certified
- Weather acceptable for H2 operations
- Emergency equipment positioned
- Safety perimeter established
- H2 emergency response team notified

### 4.3 Equipment Required

**Same as fueling operations (03-10-03-01A, Section 4.2) plus**:
- Inert gas purge system (GN2)
- Fuel sampling equipment (if fuel quality preservation required)
- Fuel transfer containers or truck with adequate capacity
- Fuel quality test kit
- Additional H2 detectors for venting operations

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 Defueling-Specific Hazards

**WARNING**:
- Defueling increases H2 vapor generation and venting
- Fuel system may be at higher pressure than during fueling
- Residual LH2 remains in system even after defueling
- Inert gas purging can create oxygen-deficient atmosphere
- Greater risk of H2 accumulation in confined spaces during defueling

**CAUTION**:
- Defueling is generally slower than fueling for safety
- Tank venting is continuous during defueling
- Temperature changes during defueling can cause pressure fluctuations
- Moisture can enter system if purge gas not properly dried
- Multiple defueling/fueling cycles stress equipment

### 5.2 Emergency Defueling

When defueling required for emergency (leak, contamination, fire risk):
- Prioritize personnel safety over fuel preservation
- Use maximum safe defueling rate
- Continuous H2 monitoring essential
- Larger safety perimeter may be required
- Emergency services on standby
- May require fuel disposal rather than preservation
- Document emergency thoroughly for investigation

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 H2 Leak During Defueling

**Same as 03-10-03-01A Section 6.1 with these additions**:
- Defueling operations may exacerbate leak
- May need to complete emergency defueling to safe level
- Consider fuel system pressure relief if safe
- Coordinate with engineering before resuming

### 6.2 Equipment Failure During Defueling

1. Stop defueling immediately
2. Close aircraft fuel valve
3. Assess failure nature and severity
4. Do not disconnect until pressure equalized
5. If H2 leak present, follow leak emergency procedures
6. If no leak, secure system and troubleshoot
7. Do not resume until cause identified and corrected
8. Consider alternate defueling method if primary fails

### 6.3 Weather Deterioration During Defueling

If weather becomes unacceptable:
1. Accelerate defueling if safely possible
2. If cannot complete, secure in safe configuration
3. Evacuate personnel
4. Resume when conditions improve
5. Do NOT leave system in partially connected state
6. If lightning within 5 miles, follow 03-10-03-01A Section 6.3
