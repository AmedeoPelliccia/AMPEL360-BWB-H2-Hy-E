# 03-10-03-03A - Fueling Equipment

### 4.4 Equipment Inspection and Maintenance

#### 4.4.1 Daily Inspection Checklist (Before Operations)

| Item | Check | Accept Criteria |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Fuel Tank** | Visual for leaks, frost, damage | No leaks, frost pattern normal |
| **Hoses** | Visual for damage, kinks, wear | No visible damage |
| **Connectors** | Inspect seals, locking mechanism | Seals intact, locks functional |
| **H2 Detectors** | Calibration check, battery level | Pass calibration, >50% battery |

| **Emergency Shutoff** | Functional test | Operates smoothly, full closure |
| **Safety Barriers** | Condition of cones, signs, tape | Good condition, complete |

#### 4.4.2 Weekly Inspection Requirements

- Detailed hose inspection (bend test, visual)
- Connector leak test (soap solution or helium)
- H2 detector full calibration
- Fire extinguisher weight check
- Grounding cable continuity test
- Emergency equipment function test
- Documentation review

#### 4.4.3 Monthly Inspection Requirements

- Pressure relief valve test
- Emergency shutdown response time test
- Flow meter accuracy check
- All safety interlocks test
- Complete equipment function test
- Maintenance records review
- Training records review

#### 4.4.4 Annual Certification Requirements

- Full system pressure test (1.5x working pressure)
- Hose and connector leak test (helium)
- Flow meter calibration (certified lab)
- Pressure gauge calibration (certified lab)
- H2 detector sensor replacement
- Complete safety system audit
- Equipment re-certification per SAE AS6968

### 4.5 Equipment Storage and Handling

#### 4.5.1 Storage Requirements

- Store hoses coiled loosely, off ground
- Protect connectors with dust caps
- Store detectors in climate-controlled environment
- Store PPE in clean, dry location
- Maintain equipment logs with each item
- Segregate damaged equipment from serviceable

#### 4.5.2 Handling Requirements

- Use proper lifting techniques for heavy equipment
- Never drop or impact cryogenic hoses
- Handle connectors by body, not by seals
- Keep all equipment clean and free of contamination
- Transport secured to prevent damage
- Use designated equipment transport vehicles

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 Equipment-Specific Hazards

**WARNING**:
- Cryogenic equipment can cause instant frostbite
- Pressurized equipment can fail catastrophically if damaged
- H2 leaks from equipment can ignite without warning
- Equipment electrical systems must be intrinsically safe
- Never use equipment beyond certification dates

**CAUTION**:
- Equipment exposed to LH2 becomes extremely cold
- Thermal cycling stresses equipment over time
- Gaskets and seals have limited service life
- Equipment malfunction may not be immediately apparent
- Always use equipment per manufacturer procedures

### 5.2 Equipment Failure Indicators

Stop operations immediately if:
- Visible H2 leaks (white frost, detector alarm)
- Unusual sounds (hissing, popping, grinding)
- Excessive ice formation
- Difficulty operating valves or connectors
- Flow meter or pressure gauge anomalies
- H2 detector continuous alarms
- Fire suppression system activation

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 Equipment Failure During Fueling

1. Stop fuel flow immediately using emergency shutoff
2. Do not disconnect hose until pressure equalized
3. Assess nature and severity of failure
4. Evacuate if H2 leak suspected
5. Monitor H2 concentration continuously
6. Tag equipment "OUT OF SERVICE"
7. Do not attempt field repairs
8. Contact equipment manufacturer/specialist
9. Document failure in equipment log

### 6.2 H2 Detector Alarm

1. Stop fueling immediately
2. Activate emergency shutdown
3. Evacuate to upwind location
4. Monitor H2 levels from safe distance
5. Do not re-approach until levels <10% LEL
6. Investigate source with backup detector
7. Do not resume until cause found and corrected

### 6.3 Fire Suppression System Activation

1. Evacuate area immediately
2. Activate emergency services
3. Do not re-approach for equipment
4. Allow professional firefighters to assess
5. Assume H2 involvement until proven otherwise
6. Establish wide safety perimeter
7. Account for all personnel
