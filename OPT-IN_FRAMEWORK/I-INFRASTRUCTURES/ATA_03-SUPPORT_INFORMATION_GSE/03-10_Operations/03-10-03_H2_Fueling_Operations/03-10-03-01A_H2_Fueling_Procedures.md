# 03-10-03-01A - H2 Fueling Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized liquid hydrogen (LH2) fueling procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring safe, efficient, and compliant refueling operations.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Pre-fueling inspections and preparations
- LH2 connection and fueling operations
- Monitoring and safety protocols during fueling
- Post-fueling disconnection and verification
- Documentation and quality assurance
- Coordination with flight crew and ground operations

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prerequisites

- Aircraft properly positioned and secured
- Parking brake set and wheel chocks installed
- Engines shut down and APU off (or as specified)
- All passenger doors closed
- H2 fueling personnel trained and certified
- H2 fuel truck/hydrant positioned and inspected
- Weather conditions acceptable for H2 fueling
- Emergency equipment available and positioned
- Safety perimeter established

### 4.2 Equipment Required

**Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**:
- Cryogenic-rated gloves
- Face shield or safety goggles
- Fire-resistant clothing
- Safety shoes (non-sparking)
- Hard hat
- H2 gas detector (personal)

**Fueling Equipment**:
- H2 fuel truck or hydrant dispenser (certified)
- Grounding cables and clamps
- LH2 fuel hose (cryogenic-rated)
- Coupling adapters (aircraft-specific)
- H2 leak detector (portable and fixed)
- Fire extinguishers (Class D, minimum 2)
- Safety cones and barriers
- Communication equipment
- Fuel quantity measurement system

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 LH2 Specific Hazards

**WARNING - EXTREME COLD**:
- Liquid hydrogen temperature: -253°C (-423°F)
- Contact with skin causes instant frostbite
- Material embrittlement at cryogenic temperatures
- Always wear cryogenic-rated PPE
- Keep LH2 away from incompatible materials

**WARNING - FLAMMABILITY**:
- Hydrogen is extremely flammable (4-75% concentration in air)
- Very low ignition energy required (0.02 mJ)
- Flame nearly invisible in daylight
- Wide explosive range
- Eliminate all ignition sources within safety zone

**WARNING - ASPHYXIATION**:
- Hydrogen displaces oxygen in confined spaces
- Can cause asphyxiation without warning
- Ensure adequate ventilation
- Use H2 detectors continuously
- Never enter confined space with H2 present

**CAUTION - VENTING**:
- H2 boil-off is normal during fueling
- Vent hydrogen rises rapidly and disperses
- Keep personnel away from vent areas
- Monitor concentration with detectors
- Venting increases in warm weather

### 5.2 Critical Safety Rules

1. **No Smoking** - Enforce strictly within 100m of fueling operation
2. **No Open Flames** - Including cutting, welding, or hot work
3. **No Radio Transmissions** - Unless using intrinsically safe equipment
4. **No Mobile Phones** - In immediate fueling area (except approved)
5. **No Running Engines** - APU off during fueling (unless specifically approved)
6. **No Passengers** - All passengers off aircraft during fueling
7. **Continuous Fire Watch** - Maintain throughout fueling operation
8. **Continuous Monitoring** - H2 detectors active at all times

### 5.3 Environmental Conditions

**DO NOT FUEL** if:
- Lightning within 5 miles (8 km)
- Wind speed exceeds 40 knots
- Heavy precipitation affecting visibility or operations
- Temperature extremes outside equipment ratings
- Any condition that compromises safety

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 H2 Leak During Fueling

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS**:
1. **STOP FUEL FLOW IMMEDIATELY**
2. Announce "H2 LEAK - H2 LEAK - H2 LEAK"
3. Activate emergency fuel shut-off
4. Do NOT disconnect hose (may increase leak)
5. Evacuate personnel upwind to safe distance (minimum 50m)
6. Activate H2 emergency response team
7. Eliminate all potential ignition sources
8. Monitor H2 concentration continuously
9. Allow natural ventilation - do NOT use fans
10. Do not approach leak until H2 concentration safe
11. Refer to 03-10-03-02A Fueling Safety Protocols

### 6.2 Fire During Fueling

**LH2 FIRE**:
1. Announce "FIRE - FIRE - FIRE"
2. Activate fire alarm and emergency services
3. Evacuate all personnel to safe distance
4. Do NOT attempt to extinguish H2 fire unless trained
5. Close fuel valves if safely accessible
6. Allow H2 fire to burn out if safely possible
7. Cool surrounding structures with water fog
8. Evacuate aircraft if fire threatens
9. Establish wide safety perimeter
10. Defer to professional fire brigade

**Equipment Fire (non-H2)**:
1. Announce "FIRE - FIRE - FIRE"
2. Shut off fuel flow
3. Use appropriate fire extinguisher
4. If fire not controllable, evacuate and call fire brigade
5. Prevent fire spread to H2 system

### 6.3 Lightning in Vicinity

If lightning observed within 5 miles:
1. Immediately stop fueling operation
2. Secure fuel system in safe configuration
3. Leave hose connected (do not disconnect in storm)
4. Evacuate personnel to safe shelter
5. Wait 30 minutes after last lightning before resuming
6. Inspect all systems before resuming fueling

### 6.4 Fuel Spill

**LH2 Spill**:
1. Stop fuel flow immediately
2. Announce "SPILL - SPILL - SPILL"
3. Evacuate upwind
4. Monitor H2 concentration
5. Allow LH2 to evaporate naturally
6. Do NOT apply water directly to LH2 pool
7. Ventilate area until H2 concentration safe
8. Inspect for system damage
9. Do not resume fueling until cause determined and corrected

### 6.5 Equipment Malfunction

If fueling equipment malfunctions:
1. Stop fuel flow using emergency shut-off
2. Secure aircraft fuel system
3. Assess nature of malfunction
4. Do not attempt to fix during fueling
5. Disconnect and replace equipment if necessary
6. Complete inspection before resuming
7. Document malfunction in equipment log
