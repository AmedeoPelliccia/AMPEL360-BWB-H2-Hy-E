# 03-10-02-01A - Arrival Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized arrival procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring safe and efficient ground operations from runway exit to gate positioning.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Taxi from runway to gate/parking
- Communication with ground control and ramp
- H2 system shutdown or standby procedures
- Aircraft parking and securing
- Post-flight procedures
- Passenger disembarkation coordination

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prerequisites

- After-landing checklist completed
- Ground control taxi clearance received
- Gate/parking assignment confirmed
- Ground crew notified of arrival
- H2 fuel system in appropriate configuration
- Passengers briefed to remain seated

### 4.2 Equipment Required

- Airport diagram/taxi chart
- Ground control radio frequency
- Ramp/gate assignment information
- Marshaller communication capability
- H2 system monitoring displays
- Parking checklist

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 Taxi Safety

**WARNING**:
- Maintain constant vigilance for other aircraft and vehicles
- Never cross a runway without explicit clearance
- Stop immediately if uncertain about taxi clearance
- Be especially cautious in low visibility conditions
- Watch for "hot spots" identified in airport briefings

**CAUTION**:
- BWB aircraft may have limited visibility to sides
- Use marshaller or wing walkers in congested areas
- Be aware of propwash/jetwash affecting other aircraft
- Maintain safe taxi speeds at all times

### 5.2 H2 Fuel System Ground Operations

**WARNING**:
- Ensure H2 system properly configured before engine shutdown
- Monitor for any H2 leaks during parking operations
- Keep ignition sources away from H2 vent areas
- Follow proper H2 system shutdown procedures

**CAUTION**:
- H2 tank venting may occur during warm weather operations
- Ensure ground crew aware of H2 hazard zones
- Post H2 warning signs as required
- Maintain proper clearances from buildings and equipment

### 5.3 Gate Parking

- Verify correct gate assignment before entering ramp
- Ensure adequate clearance for adjacent aircraft
- Follow marshaller signals precisely
- Set parking brake before ground crew approaches
- Ensure jetbridge/stairs positioned safely

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 H2 Leak Detected During Taxi

If H2 leak detected or suspected:
1. Stop aircraft immediately in safe location
2. Set parking brake
3. Notify ground control of emergency
4. Shut down H2 fuel system per emergency procedures
5. Request H2 emergency response team
6. Evacuate aircraft if directed
7. Establish safety perimeter
8. Refer to 03-10-07-02A H2 Emergency Response

### 6.2 Fire During Taxi or at Gate

1. Stop aircraft immediately
2. Set parking brake
3. Notify ground control/tower of fire
4. Discharge fire extinguisher if appropriate
5. Initiate evacuation if fire not controllable
6. Request emergency services
7. Account for all passengers and crew
8. Follow fire emergency procedures per AFM

### 6.3 Ground Collision or Incident

If collision with aircraft, vehicle, or structure:
1. Stop aircraft immediately
2. Set parking brake
3. Assess damage and safety situation
4. Notify ground control and company
5. Do not move aircraft unless safety requires
6. Request maintenance inspection
7. Preserve evidence for investigation
8. Complete incident report

### 6.4 Brake Failure During Taxi

1. Reduce speed using reverse thrust if available
2. Notify ground control immediately
3. Steer to avoid collision with obstacles
4. Use emergency/parking brake if necessary
5. Stop in safe area if possible
6. Request tug assistance to move to gate
7. Do not attempt further taxi under own power
