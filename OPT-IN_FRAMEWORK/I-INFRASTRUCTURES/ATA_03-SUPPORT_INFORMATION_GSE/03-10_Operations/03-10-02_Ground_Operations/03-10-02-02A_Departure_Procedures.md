# 03-10-02-02A - Departure Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized departure procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring safe and efficient ground operations from gate/parking to takeoff.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Gate preparation and pre-departure checks
- H2 fuel system startup and verification
- Passenger boarding coordination
- Pushback and engine start procedures
- Taxi preparation
- Coordination with ground services and ATC

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prerequisites

- Aircraft properly parked and secured
- Pre-flight inspection completed per 03-10-01-01A
- H2 fueling completed if required
- Flight plan filed and clearance available
- Crew on board and briefed
- Ground support equipment available
- Weather acceptable for departure

### 4.2 Equipment Required

- Pushback tug or tractor
- Ground power unit (if not using APU)
- Headset for ground crew communication
- Wheel chocks
- Fire extinguisher (nearby)
- H2 leak detection equipment
- Airport diagram/taxi chart

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 H2 Fuel System Startup

**WARNING**:
- Verify H2 system properly configured before engine start
- Monitor for H2 leaks during system activation
- Ensure H2 fuel pressure within normal range before start
- Verify no H2 warnings or cautions before proceeding

**CAUTION**:
- H2 system requires adequate warm-up time in cold weather
- Monitor H2 tank pressure during ground operations
- Ensure proper ventilation around H2 systems
- Keep ignition sources away from H2 vent areas

### 5.2 Pushback Safety

**WARNING**:
- Never release brakes until tug driver signals ready
- Maintain constant communication with tug driver
- Monitor pushback direction and clearance continuously
- Stop pushback immediately if any safety concern arises

**CAUTION**:
- Ensure adequate clearance from adjacent aircraft and structures
- Be aware of ground equipment and personnel around aircraft
- Monitor brake temperature after prolonged ground operations
- Verify tug properly connected before releasing brakes

### 5.3 Engine Start Safety

- Verify area clear before engine start
- Monitor engine parameters closely during start
- Be prepared to abort start if abnormal indications
- Ensure fire extinguisher available during start
- Follow proper start sequence per AFM

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 H2 Leak During Ground Operations

If H2 leak detected before or during departure:
1. Stop all operations immediately
2. Do not start engines or shut down running engines
3. Evacuate aircraft if leak is significant
4. Notify ground crew and emergency services
5. Activate H2 emergency response team
6. Establish safety perimeter
7. Do not proceed with departure
8. Refer to 03-10-07-02A H2 Emergency Response

### 6.2 Fire During Engine Start

1. Continue engine start (to blow out fire) or
2. If fire persists, discharge fire extinguisher
3. Shut down affected engine
4. Evacuate aircraft if fire not controllable
5. Notify ATC and emergency services
6. Do not attempt pushback or taxi
7. Follow fire emergency procedures per AFM

### 6.3 Hot Start or Hung Start

**Hot Start** (EGT exceeds limits):
1. Shut down engine immediately
2. Monitor EGT decay
3. Investigate cause before restart attempt
4. Notify maintenance if limits exceeded

**Hung Start** (engine fails to accelerate):
1. Continue cranking to clear engine
2. If no acceleration after appropriate time, abort start
3. Investigate cause before restart attempt
4. Follow AFM procedures for hung start

### 6.4 Pushback Emergency

If tug failure, collision risk, or other emergency during pushback:
1. Announce "STOP PUSHBACK" on intercom
2. Set parking brake immediately
3. Assess situation
4. Notify ATC if blocking taxiway/runway
5. Request assistance as needed
6. Do not proceed until situation resolved
