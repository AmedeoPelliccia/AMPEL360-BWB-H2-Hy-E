# 03-10-01-03A - Cruise Operations

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized cruise operations procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring optimal efficiency, safety, and passenger comfort during the cruise phase of flight.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Cruise level establishment and maintenance
- H2-electric hybrid power management
- Fuel efficiency optimization
- In-flight monitoring and system management
- Route modifications and weather avoidance
- Cruise performance monitoring

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 H2 Fuel System Monitoring

**CAUTION**:
- Monitor H2 fuel quantity continuously during cruise
- Verify fuel consumption rates match flight plan predictions
- Watch for abnormal fuel consumption patterns
- Ensure H2 tank pressures remain within normal range
- Monitor H2 system temperatures throughout cruise

**Note**: H2 fuel consumption is affected by:
- Altitude and outside air temperature
- Aircraft weight and configuration
- Wind conditions
- Power settings and flight profile

### 5.2 High Altitude Operations

- Monitor cabin pressurization closely
- Be aware of reduced engine performance margins
- Watch for ice crystal icing conditions
- Maintain awareness of diversion airports
- Monitor passenger oxygen system status

### 5.3 Weather Avoidance

- Avoid thunderstorms by at least 20 NM
- Do not penetrate severe turbulence areas
- Consider requesting altitude changes for smooth air
- Monitor icing conditions and activate anti-ice as required
- Track weather developments along route

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 H2 Fuel System Malfunction in Cruise

If H2 system warning or malfunction occurs:
1. Assess nature and severity of malfunction
2. Execute appropriate H2 emergency procedure per AFM
3. Consider diversion to nearest suitable airport
4. Calculate fuel remaining and endurance
5. Declare emergency if fuel critical
6. Coordinate with dispatch and ATC
7. Brief cabin crew of situation
8. Refer to 03-10-07-02A H2 Emergency Response

### 6.2 Engine Failure in Cruise

1. Maintain aircraft control
2. Execute engine failure procedure per AFM
3. Assess aircraft performance capability
4. Determine if cruise altitude can be maintained
5. Request altitude change if necessary
6. Calculate diversion options
7. Notify ATC and declare emergency if appropriate
8. Consider ETOPS implications if applicable

### 6.3 Rapid Descent Required

If rapid descent necessary (pressurization loss, fire, etc.):
1. Don oxygen masks
2. Initiate emergency descent per AFM
3. Notify ATC immediately
4. Set transponder to 7700 if emergency
5. Descend to safe altitude (14,000 ft or MEA)
6. Assess situation and determine diversion
7. Brief cabin crew and passengers

### 6.4 Severe Turbulence Encounter

1. Reduce speed to turbulence penetration speed
2. Disconnect autopilot if necessary
3. Do not attempt to maintain altitude exactly
4. Notify ATC of turbulence severity
5. Turn on seatbelt sign
6. Brief cabin crew to secure cabin
7. Consider route or altitude change
