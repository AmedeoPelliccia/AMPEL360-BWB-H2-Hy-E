# 03-10-01-01A - Pre-Flight Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized pre-flight procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring all safety checks, system verifications, and regulatory requirements are met before flight operations commence.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- External walk-around inspection
- Cockpit preparation and system checks
- H2 fuel system verification
- Documentation and flight plan review
- Pre-flight briefings
- System initialization and testing

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prerequisites

Before commencing pre-flight procedures, the following conditions must be met:
- Aircraft properly positioned and secured at gate/parking
- Ground support equipment available and operational
- All required documentation available (flight plan, weather, NOTAMs)
- Qualified flight crew present
- Minimum ground crew available (2 personnel)
- H2 fueling operations completed (if required)
- Ground power connected (if applicable)

### 4.2 Equipment Required

- Aircraft documentation package
- Flashlight (for external inspection)
- H2 leak detection equipment
- Communication equipment (headset/radio)
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for H2 areas
- Pre-flight checklist (electronic or paper)
- Weather and NOTAM information

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 H2 Fuel System Specific Safety

**WARNING**: Hydrogen fuel systems require special attention during pre-flight checks:
- Always use H2 leak detection equipment during external inspection
- Verify all H2 fuel system indicators are in normal range
- Ensure proper ventilation around H2 storage areas
- Check for ice formation on H2 tanks (indicator of potential leak)
- Verify H2 pressure relief valves are not venting

**CAUTION**: 
- Do not approach H2 storage areas with potential ignition sources
- Ensure all personnel are aware of H2 hazard zones
- Verify emergency shutdown systems are operational

### 5.2 General Safety

- Ensure proper communication with ground crew during all phases
- Verify fire extinguishing equipment is accessible
- Check weather conditions do not exceed aircraft limitations
- Ensure all required MEL (Minimum Equipment List) items are addressed
- Verify sufficient fuel (H2) for planned flight plus reserves

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 H2 Leak Detection During Pre-Flight

If H2 leak is detected during pre-flight inspection:
1. Immediately evacuate the area
2. Notify ground safety personnel
3. Activate H2 emergency response team
4. Do not proceed with flight operations
5. Follow ATA 03-10-07-02A H2 Emergency Response procedures

### 6.2 System Malfunction Discovery

If critical system malfunction is discovered:
1. Document the finding in aircraft log
2. Consult MEL for dispatch eligibility
3. Contact maintenance control
4. Delay departure until issue is resolved or MEL approval obtained
5. Inform ATC of potential departure delay

### 6.3 Weather Below Minimums

If weather deteriorates below departure minimums:
1. Delay departure until conditions improve
2. Consider alternate departure procedures
3. Coordinate with dispatch for flight plan amendment
4. Keep passengers informed of status
