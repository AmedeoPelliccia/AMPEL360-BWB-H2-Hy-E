# 03-10-01-02A - Takeoff Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This document defines standardized takeoff procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring safe and efficient departure operations in accordance with regulatory requirements and manufacturer specifications.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Taxi procedures to runway
- Pre-takeoff checks and configurations
- Normal takeoff procedures
- Engine (H2-electric hybrid) power application
- Abort procedures
- Initial climb procedures

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prerequisites

- Pre-flight inspection completed per 03-10-01-01A
- ATC clearance for taxi and takeoff obtained
- Takeoff briefing completed
- Aircraft configured for takeoff
- Performance calculations verified
- Weather within takeoff limitations
- Runway length adequate for aircraft weight and conditions

### 4.2 Equipment Required

- Aircraft Flight Manual (AFM)
- Takeoff performance charts/calculator
- Current weather information
- Runway condition assessment
- Communication equipment operational
- H2 system monitoring displays functional

### 4.3 Step-by-Step Procedure

#### Phase 1: Taxi to Runway

| Step | Action | Responsible | Notes |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Receive taxi clearance from ATC | Captain | Read back clearance |
| 2 | Release parking brake | FO | Verify brake pressure |
| 3 | Initiate taxi with symmetric power | Captain | Use differential thrust for steering |
| 4 | Monitor H2 system parameters during taxi | FO | Verify normal fuel flow and pressure |
| 5 | Perform taxi checks | Both Pilots | Flight controls, instruments |
| 6 | Brief takeoff abort criteria | Captain | V1, engine failure procedures |
| 7 | Review takeoff performance data | FO | V1, VR, V2, flex temp if applicable |

#### Phase 2: Pre-Takeoff

| Step | Action | Responsible | Notes |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| 8 | Position aircraft at runway holding point | Captain | Align with centerline |
| 9 | Perform pre-takeoff checklist | Both Pilots | Cross-check all items |
| 10 | Verify flight controls in takeoff position | FO | Flaps/slats as required |
| 11 | Set takeoff thrust parameters | FO | Input takeoff data into FMS |
| 12 | Arm spoilers/speedbrakes | FO | Verify armed indication |
| 13 | Final H2 fuel system check | Both Pilots | Verify quantity, pressure, temp |
| 14 | Contact tower for takeoff clearance | Captain | Read back clearance |

#### Phase 3: Takeoff Roll

| Step | Action | Responsible | Notes |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| 15 | Announce "Cleared for takeoff" | Captain | Final crew coordination |
| 16 | Release brakes and advance thrust levers | Captain | Smooth, symmetric application |
| 17 | Monitor engine parameters | FO | N1, EGT, fuel flow normal |
| 18 | Announce thrust set and engines stabilized | FO | At 40% N1 approximately |
| 19 | Monitor airspeed acceleration | FO | Call out 80 knots |
| 20 | Announce "V1" | FO | Commit to takeoff point |
| 21 | Announce "Rotate" | FO | At VR speed |
| 22 | Initiate rotation at VR | Captain | Smooth, positive rotation |
| 23 | Establish initial climb attitude | Captain | Target V2 + 10 to 20 knots |
| 24 | Announce "Positive rate" | FO | Verify climb on VSI |
| 25 | Command "Gear up" | Captain | After positive rate confirmed |
| 26 | Retract landing gear | FO | Monitor gear retraction |

#### Phase 4: Initial Climb

| Step | Action | Responsible | Notes |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| 27 | Maintain runway heading until 400 ft AGL | Captain | Unless otherwise directed |
| 28 | Verify H2 system transition to climb mode | FO | Monitor fuel consumption rate |
| 29 | Retract flaps on schedule | FO | Per AFM or ATC restriction |
| 30 | Maintain V2 + 10 until acceleration altitude | Captain | Minimum 400 ft AGL |
| 31 | Accelerate and clean up configuration | Both Pilots | Retract remaining flaps/slats |
| 32 | Complete after-takeoff checklist | FO | Cross-check completion |
| 33 | Contact departure control | Captain | Check in with altitude and heading |

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 H2 Fuel System Specific

**WARNING**: 
- Monitor H2 fuel consumption closely during high-power takeoff phase
- Verify H2 fuel pressure remains within normal operating range
- Watch for any H2 system warnings or cautions during takeoff roll
- Be prepared for immediate abort if H2 system malfunction occurs before V1

**CAUTION**:
- H2 fuel flow rates are significantly higher during takeoff power
- Ensure adequate H2 fuel quantity for takeoff and initial climb
- Monitor H2 tank temperatures during high-demand operations

### 5.2 Takeoff Abort Considerations

**Below V1**:
- Abort for: engine failure, fire warning, configuration warning, windshear alert, runway obstruction
- Apply maximum braking and reverse thrust
- Maintain directional control on runway centerline

**Above V1**:
- Continue takeoff for all but catastrophic failures
- Execute engine failure procedure if applicable
- Maintain directional control and achieve V2 minimum

### 5.3 Weather Limitations

- Do not attempt takeoff if:
  - Visibility below minimums
  - Crosswind exceeds aircraft limitations
  - Runway contaminated beyond acceptable limits
  - Windshear reported or forecast
  - Thunderstorms in vicinity of airport

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 Rejected Takeoff (Before V1)

1. Announce "ABORT, ABORT, ABORT"
2. Throttles - Idle
3. Speedbrakes - Deploy
4. Brakes - Maximum (anti-skid functional)
5. Reverse thrust - As available
6. Maintain directional control
7. Notify ATC of rejected takeoff
8. Assess situation and taxi clear of runway if able

### 6.2 Engine Failure After V1

1. Continue takeoff
2. Rotate at VR (normal rotation rate)
3. Maintain directional control with rudder
4. Establish V2 speed (do not allow airspeed to drop below V2)
5. Retract gear when positive rate established
6. Execute engine failure procedure per AFM
7. Notify ATC of situation
8. Consider returning to departure airport or divert

### 6.3 H2 System Warning During Takeoff

**Before V1**:
- Abort takeoff
- Follow rejected takeoff procedures
- Refer to 03-10-07-02A H2 Emergency Response

**After V1**:
- Continue takeoff
- Monitor H2 system closely
- Execute H2 emergency procedures per AFM
- Plan for immediate return or diversion
- Declare emergency with ATC if required
