# 03-10-01-04A - Landing Procedures

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized landing procedures for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, ensuring safe approach, landing, and rollout operations in accordance with regulatory requirements and manufacturer specifications.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Descent and approach procedures
- Landing configuration
- Final approach and landing
- Go-around procedures
- Rollout and taxi to gate
- H2 system management during landing phase

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Prerequisites

- Approach briefing completed
- Landing performance calculated and verified
- Weather at destination within limits
- Runway length adequate for landing weight
- Approach charts reviewed
- H2 fuel quantity adequate for landing plus go-around
- Landing clearance obtained or expected

### 4.2 Equipment Required

- Aircraft Flight Manual (AFM)
- Approach charts for destination airport
- Current weather information (ATIS/METAR)
- Landing performance data
- Airport diagrams
- H2 system monitoring displays operational

## 5. Safety Considerations

### 5.1 Stabilized Approach Criteria

An approach must be stabilized by 1000 ft AGL (IMC) or 500 ft AGL (VMC):
- Aircraft in landing configuration (gear down, flaps landing)
- On correct flight path (localizer, glideslope, or visual)
- Airspeed not more than VREF + 20 kt and not less than VREF
- Sink rate not greater than 1000 fpm
- Engine power appropriate for configuration
- All briefings and checklists completed

**If not stabilized, execute go-around**

### 5.2 H2 Fuel System Considerations

**CAUTION**:
- Verify adequate H2 fuel for landing plus go-around reserves
- Monitor H2 fuel pressure during approach and landing
- Be prepared for H2 system failure procedures if warning occurs
- H2 fuel consumption increases during go-around

### 5.3 Landing Performance Factors

Consider and account for:
- Runway length and condition (dry, wet, contaminated)
- Wind component (headwind, tailwind, crosswind)
- Runway slope
- Aircraft weight and landing flap setting
- Braking action reports
- Altitude and temperature effects

### 5.4 Weather Limitations

Do not attempt landing if:
- Visibility or ceiling below minimums
- Crosswind exceeds aircraft limitations
- Runway contaminated beyond acceptable limits
- Windshear reported on final approach
- Thunderstorm over airport

## 6. Emergency Actions

### 6.1 Go-Around Procedure

Execute go-around if:
- Approach not stabilized at required altitude
- Runway not clear for landing
- Windshear alert or encounter
- Visual reference lost below minimums
- ATC go-around instruction
- Any unsafe condition exists

**Go-Around Procedure**:
1. Announce "GO-AROUND, GO-AROUND"
2. Advance thrust levers to go-around power
3. Pitch to go-around attitude (approximately 15°)
4. Verify positive rate of climb
5. Retract landing gear
6. Retract flaps on schedule per AFM
7. Follow missed approach procedure
8. Notify ATC of go-around
9. Configure H2 system for climb power

### 6.2 Windshear on Final Approach

If windshear encounter or alert on final:
1. Disconnect autopilot if engaged
2. Apply maximum thrust
3. Pitch to 15° nose up (do not stall)
4. Do not change configuration
5. Do not attempt to land
6. Follow windshear recovery procedure per AFM
7. Notify ATC of windshear
8. Report conditions for following aircraft

### 6.3 Hard Landing

If hard landing occurs or suspected:
1. Continue rollout and exit runway
2. Stop aircraft in safe location
3. Set parking brake
4. Notify ATC and company
5. Request maintenance inspection
6. Do not shutdown H2 systems until inspection
7. Document event in aircraft logbook
8. Consider evacuation if fire or H2 leak suspected

### 6.4 Runway Excursion

If aircraft departs runway:
1. Bring aircraft to controlled stop if possible
2. Set parking brake
3. Activate emergency systems as required
4. Assess situation for evacuation need
5. Notify ATC and emergency services
6. Evacuate if fire, H2 leak, or other hazard present
7. Account for all passengers and crew
