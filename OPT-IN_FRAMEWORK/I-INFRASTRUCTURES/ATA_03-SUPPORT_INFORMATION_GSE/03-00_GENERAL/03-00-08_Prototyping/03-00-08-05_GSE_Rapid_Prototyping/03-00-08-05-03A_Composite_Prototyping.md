# 03-00-08-05-03A - Composite Prototyping

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the prototype development using composite materials and manufacturing techniques for GSE components, providing lightweight, high-strength parts with corrosion resistance and design flexibility for ground support equipment.

## 2. Scope
This prototype covers composite manufacturing capabilities including hand layup, vacuum bagging, pre-preg processes, resin infusion, material selection, curing protocols, and applications for GSE component manufacturing requiring high strength-to-weight ratios.

## 4. Prototype Description

### 4.1 Overview
The Composite Prototyping initiative leverages advanced composite materials to create lightweight, durable GSE components. Composites offer excellent strength-to-weight ratios, corrosion resistance, and design flexibility for complex geometries not achievable with traditional materials.

### 4.2 Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Target |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Manufacturing Methods | Composite processes | Hand layup, vacuum bag, pre-preg, infusion |
| Fiber Types | Reinforcement options | Carbon fiber, fiberglass, aramid |
| Resin Systems | Matrix materials | Epoxy, polyester, vinyl ester |
| Weight Reduction | Mass savings vs metal | 30-50% weight reduction |
| Strength-to-Weight | Performance | 2-3x better than aluminum |
| Corrosion Resistance | Environmental durability | Excellent in harsh conditions |
| Production Lead Time | Part delivery | 1-3 weeks typical |
| Part Size Capability | Maximum dimensions | Up to 3m x 2m panels |

### 4.3 Materials and Components

#### 4.3.1 Reinforcement Materials
- **Carbon Fiber**: High strength/stiffness, lightweight
- **Fiberglass**: Cost-effective, good strength
- **Aramid (Kevlar)**: High impact and abrasion resistance
- **Hybrid Fabrics**: Optimized multi-material layups

#### 4.3.2 Resin Systems
- **Epoxy**: High performance, excellent adhesion
- **Polyester**: Cost-effective, good chemical resistance
- **Vinyl Ester**: Enhanced corrosion resistance
- **Phenolic**: Fire-resistant applications

#### 4.3.3 Manufacturing Equipment
- **Vacuum System**: Pumps, bags, sealant tape
- **Autoclave**: Pressure curing (if required)
- **Cure Ovens**: Temperature-controlled curing
- **Mixing Equipment**: Resin dispensing and mixing
- **Cutting Tools**: CNC fabric cutters, templates
- **Molds and Tooling**: Composite-specific tooling

#### 4.3.4 Core Materials (for sandwich structures)
- **Foam Cores**: PVC, PET, PMI for lightweight panels
- **Honeycomb**: Aluminum or Nomex for high rigidity
- **Balsa Wood**: Natural core material for non-critical parts

## 6. Testing Requirements

### 6.1 Material Property Testing
- **Tensile Strength**: ASTM D3039
- **Flexural Strength**: ASTM D790
- **Compression**: ASTM D695
- **Interlaminar Shear**: ASTM D2344 (short beam shear)
- **Impact Resistance**: ASTM D7136 (low-velocity impact)
- **Fiber Volume Fraction**: Burn-off or digestion method

### 6.2 Quality Control
- **Visual Inspection**: Surface quality, resin-rich/starved areas
- **Ultrasonic Inspection**: Delamination and void detection
- **Thickness Measurement**: Verify laminate thickness
- **Cure State**: DSC or DMA analysis
- **Moisture Content**: Gravimetric analysis

### 6.3 Environmental Testing
- **Temperature**: Performance from -40°C to +80°C
- **Humidity**: Moisture absorption and effects
- **UV Exposure**: Weathering and degradation
- **Chemical Resistance**: Jet fuel, hydraulic fluid, de-icing fluid
- **Thermal Cycling**: Dimensional stability

### 6.4 Applications for GSE

#### 6.4.1 Panels and Enclosures
- **Equipment Covers**: Lightweight protective covers
- **Access Panels**: Removable inspection panels
- **Fairing Components**: Aerodynamic fairings
- **Electronics Housings**: EMI-shielded enclosures

#### 6.4.2 Structural Components
- **Support Beams**: High-strength structural members
- **Platform Decks**: Lightweight walkways and platforms
- **Ladder Sections**: Portable access equipment
- **Frame Components**: Chassis and frame elements

#### 6.4.3 Specialty Applications
- **Tool Handles**: Ergonomic, lightweight tooling
- **Transport Cases**: Protective equipment cases
- **Insulation Panels**: Thermal or acoustic insulation
- **Cable Trays**: Lightweight cable management

### 6.5 Design Considerations
- **Fiber Orientation**: Optimize for load paths
- **Ply Drop-offs**: Gradual thickness transitions
- **Sandwich Construction**: Core selection and bonding
- **Insert Installation**: Hard points for fasteners
- **Edge Protection**: Moisture barrier and impact protection
- **Repairability**: Design for inspection and repair
