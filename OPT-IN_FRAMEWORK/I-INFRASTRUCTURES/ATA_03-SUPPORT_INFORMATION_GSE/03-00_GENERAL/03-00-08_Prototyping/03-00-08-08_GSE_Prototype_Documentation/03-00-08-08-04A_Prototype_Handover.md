# 03-00-08-08-04A - Prototype Handover

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the process for formally transferring GSE prototypes from development teams to operational or production teams, ensuring complete knowledge transfer, documentation handover, and acceptance.

## 2. Scope
This document covers the complete handover process including documentation package preparation, training delivery, acceptance testing, operational support transition, and formal transfer of responsibility.

## 4. Prototype Description

### 4.1 Overview
Prototype Handover ensures smooth transition of GSE prototypes from development to operations or production, with complete documentation, trained personnel, and clear acceptance criteria.

### 4.2 Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Target |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Documentation Completeness | All required documents | 100% package completeness |
| Training Delivery | Operators and maintainers | All personnel trained |
| Acceptance Demonstration | Operational validation | Successful demonstrations |
| Support Transition | Ongoing support plan | Clear support responsibilities |
| Formal Acceptance | Sign-off process | Written acceptance |

### 4.3 Materials and Components
- Complete documentation package
- Training materials and manuals
- Operating procedures
- Maintenance procedures
- Spare parts inventory
- Support contact information

## 5. Prototype Build Plan

| Phase | Activities | Duration | Deliverables |
|-------|------------|----------|--------------|
| Package Preparation | Compile all documentation | 1-2 weeks | Handover package |
| Training Delivery | Operator and maintainer training | 1 week | Trained personnel |
| Acceptance Testing | Demonstrate functionality | 2-3 days | Acceptance test results |
| Formal Handover | Transfer meeting, sign-off | 1 day | Signed acceptance |
| Support Transition | Establish ongoing support | 1 week | Support plan active |

## 6. Testing Requirements

### 6.1 Handover Documentation Package
- **Technical Specifications**: Complete design documentation
- **Operating Manual**: User procedures and instructions
- **Maintenance Manual**: Service and repair procedures
- **Test Reports**: All validation testing results
- **Lessons Learned**: Key insights from development
- **Training Materials**: Operator and maintainer guides
- **Parts List**: Bill of materials with part numbers
- **Support Contacts**: Technical support information

### 6.2 Training Requirements
- **Operator Training**: Safe and effective operation
- **Maintenance Training**: Routine service and troubleshooting
- **Emergency Procedures**: Abnormal situations and responses
- **Documentation Review**: How to use manuals and references
- **Hands-on Practice**: Supervised operational experience

### 6.3 Acceptance Criteria
- **Functional Demonstration**: All functions performed successfully
- **Performance Verification**: Meets specifications
- **Safety Validation**: All safety systems operational
- **Documentation Review**: Package complete and acceptable
- **Training Completion**: Personnel certified
- **Support Plan**: Ongoing support established

### 6.4 Handover Meeting Agenda
1. Introduction and overview
2. Documentation review
3. Training summary
4. Acceptance test results
5. Operational demonstrations
6. Support plan discussion
7. Questions and answers
8. Formal acceptance signature
