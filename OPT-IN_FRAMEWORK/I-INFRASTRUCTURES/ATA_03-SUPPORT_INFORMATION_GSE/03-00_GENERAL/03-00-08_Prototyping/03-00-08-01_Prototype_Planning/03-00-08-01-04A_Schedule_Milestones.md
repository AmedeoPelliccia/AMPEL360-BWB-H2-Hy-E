# 03-00-08-01-04A - Schedule Milestones

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the schedule framework and key milestones for prototyping activities within the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft development program.

## 2. Scope

This specification covers schedule planning, milestone definition, dependencies, critical path management, and progress tracking for all prototype development phases.

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The prototyping schedule establishes a timeline for all prototype activities, from concept validation through transition to production. The schedule is integrated with the overall program master schedule and identifies critical dependencies and decision gates.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Schedule Framework:**
- Align with program master schedule
- Define phase gates and decision points
- Identify critical path activities
- Establish buffer periods for high-risk activities
- Include review and approval cycles

**Key Milestone Categories:**

**Phase 1: Concept Validation (Months 1-12)**
- M1.1: Prototyping strategy approved
- M1.2: Digital prototype models complete
- M1.3: Technology feasibility demonstrations complete
- M1.4: Concept validation review complete

**Phase 2: System Development (Months 13-36)**
- M2.1: Functional prototype specifications approved
- M2.2: First structural prototype complete
- M2.3: Propulsion prototype testing complete
- M2.4: H2 system safety validation complete
- M2.5: Avionics integration prototype complete
- M2.6: System development review complete

**Phase 3: System Integration (Months 37-60)**
- M3.1: Full-scale structural prototype complete
- M3.2: Integrated systems ground testing complete
- M3.3: Flight test article readiness review
- M3.4: First flight milestone
- M3.5: Flight test campaign complete
- M3.6: Production transition readiness review

### 4.3 Methodology

**Schedule Development Process:**

1. **Define** work breakdown structure for prototyping
2. **Sequence** activities and identify dependencies
3. **Estimate** durations using historical data and expert judgment
4. **Develop** schedule network and critical path
5. **Optimize** schedule within resource and budget constraints
6. **Baseline** approved schedule for tracking

**Schedule Management:**
- Weekly progress updates for active prototypes
- Monthly milestone reviews
- Quarterly program schedule integration
- Risk-based schedule analysis and contingency planning
- Earned value management for cost-schedule integration

**Critical Dependencies:**
- Design freeze events
- Long-lead material procurement
- Facility availability
- Regulatory reviews and approvals
- Supplier deliveries

## 6. Quality Criteria

**Schedule Quality:**
- Logical dependencies verified
- Resource loading balanced
- Critical path identified and managed
- Float/slack appropriately distributed
- Schedule risk analysis completed

**Performance Metrics:**
- Milestone completion on-time rate > 85%
- Schedule variance within ±10%
- Critical path stability (changes < 3 per quarter)
- Predictability index > 0.9
