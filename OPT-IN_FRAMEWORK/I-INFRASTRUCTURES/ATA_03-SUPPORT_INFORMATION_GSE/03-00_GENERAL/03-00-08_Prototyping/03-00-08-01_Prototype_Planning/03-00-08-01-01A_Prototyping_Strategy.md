# 03-00-08-01-01A - Prototyping Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the overarching prototyping strategy for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft program, establishing the framework for all prototyping activities across the development lifecycle.

## 2. Scope

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The prototyping strategy establishes a systematic approach to validating design concepts, technologies, and system integrations for the Blended Wing Body (BWB) hydrogen-electric aircraft. This strategy emphasizes rapid iteration, risk mitigation, and early validation of critical systems.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Strategic Objectives:**
- Reduce technical risk through early physical and digital validation
- Enable rapid design iteration and feedback loops
- Validate novel hydrogen propulsion and BWB aerodynamics
- Support certification basis development
- Optimize production readiness

**Key Requirements:**
- Establish clear prototyping phases and maturity gates
- Define decision criteria for prototype-to-production transitions
- Ensure traceability from prototypes to requirements
- Integrate with safety assessment and certification activities
- Maintain configuration control throughout prototyping

### 4.3 Methodology

**Prototyping Phases:**

1. **Concept Validation** (TRL 3-4)
   - Proof-of-concept prototypes
   - Technology feasibility demonstrations
   - Digital mock-ups and simulations

2. **System Development** (TRL 5-6)
   - Functional prototypes
   - Subsystem integration prototypes
   - Ground test articles

3. **System Integration** (TRL 7-8)
   - Full-scale prototypes
   - Flight test articles
   - Production representative prototypes

**Prototyping Approaches:**
- **Digital Prototyping**: CAD models, digital twins, virtual reality, simulation
- **Rapid Prototyping**: 3D printing, CNC machining, composite layup
- **Functional Prototyping**: Structural, propulsion, avionics, H2 systems
- **Scale Prototypes**: Reduced-scale models for aerodynamic and structural testing

## 6. Quality Criteria

**Success Metrics:**
- Prototype objectives achievement rate > 90%
- Schedule adherence for prototype milestones > 85%
- Technical readiness level advancement per phase
- Risk reduction effectiveness
- Knowledge transfer to production

**Quality Gates:**
- Design review approval
- Safety assessment completion
- Test plan approval
- Results validation
- Configuration baseline establishment
