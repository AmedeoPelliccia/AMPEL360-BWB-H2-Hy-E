# 03-00-08-01-02A - Prototype Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the requirements framework for prototyping activities, establishing criteria for prototype design, fabrication, testing, and evaluation within the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E program.

## 2. Scope

This specification covers functional, performance, safety, and quality requirements for all prototype categories, ensuring alignment with program objectives and certification requirements.

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Prototype requirements ensure that all prototyping efforts are purpose-driven, traceable to system requirements, and provide meaningful data to advance the program. Requirements are categorized by prototype type and development phase.

### 4.2 Requirements

**General Prototype Requirements:**
- **REQ-PROTO-001**: All prototypes shall have documented objectives and success criteria
- **REQ-PROTO-002**: Prototypes shall be traceable to system-level requirements
- **REQ-PROTO-003**: Prototype specifications shall include acceptance criteria
- **REQ-PROTO-004**: Safety hazards shall be identified and mitigated for all prototypes
- **REQ-PROTO-005**: Configuration management shall be applied to all prototype versions

**Digital Prototype Requirements:**
- **REQ-DIGI-001**: CAD models shall comply with program modeling standards
- **REQ-DIGI-002**: Digital twins shall interface with simulation platforms
- **REQ-DIGI-003**: Virtual reality models shall support design review activities
- **REQ-DIGI-004**: Simulation models shall be validated against test data where available

**Rapid Prototype Requirements:**
- **REQ-RAPID-001**: Materials shall be compatible with intended test conditions
- **REQ-RAPID-002**: Manufacturing processes shall be documented and repeatable
- **REQ-RAPID-003**: Dimensional tolerances shall be specified and verified
- **REQ-RAPID-004**: Post-processing requirements shall be defined

**Functional Prototype Requirements:**
- **REQ-FUNC-001**: Structural prototypes shall represent critical load paths
- **REQ-FUNC-002**: Propulsion prototypes shall validate key performance parameters
- **REQ-FUNC-003**: Avionics prototypes shall demonstrate functional interfaces
- **REQ-FUNC-004**: H2 system prototypes shall validate safety-critical functions

### 4.3 Methodology

**Requirements Development Process:**

1. **Derive** prototype requirements from system requirements
2. **Allocate** requirements to specific prototype activities
3. **Document** requirements in prototype specifications
4. **Trace** requirements through design, build, and test
5. **Verify** requirement satisfaction through testing
6. **Validate** that prototype meets intended objectives

**Requirements Classification:**
- **Mandatory**: Must be satisfied for prototype acceptance
- **Desirable**: Should be satisfied if feasible
- **Deferred**: To be addressed in future prototype iterations

## 6. Quality Criteria

**Requirements Quality Attributes:**
- Clear and unambiguous
- Verifiable and testable
- Traceable to parent requirements
- Feasible within prototype constraints
- Complete and consistent

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All mandatory requirements verified
- Traceability matrix complete
- Compliance report approved
- Lessons learned documented
