# 03-00-08-01-03A - Resource Planning

## 1. Purpose

This document defines resource planning requirements for prototyping activities, including personnel, facilities, equipment, materials, and budget allocation for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E program.

## 2. Scope

This specification covers resource identification, allocation, scheduling, and management for all prototyping phases, ensuring adequate resources are available to meet program objectives.

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Effective resource planning ensures that prototyping activities have the necessary personnel, facilities, equipment, and materials to execute efficiently. Resource planning is integrated with schedule milestones and budget constraints.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Personnel Resources:**
- Design engineers (aerodynamics, structures, systems)
- Manufacturing engineers and technicians
- Test engineers and operators
- Quality assurance personnel
- Program management and support staff

**Facility Resources:**
- CAD/CAE workstations and software licenses
- Rapid prototyping facilities (3D printing, CNC machining)
- Composite manufacturing facilities
- Assembly and integration facilities
- Test facilities (structural, environmental, functional)
- Data acquisition and analysis infrastructure

**Equipment and Tooling:**
- Manufacturing equipment (additive, subtractive)
- Measurement and inspection equipment
- Test rigs and fixtures
- Safety equipment for H2 handling
- Specialized tooling for prototype assembly

**Material Resources:**
- Raw materials (metals, composites, polymers)
- Standard hardware and fasteners
- Instrumentation and sensors
- Consumables and supplies

**Budget Allocation:**
- Personnel costs (labor, contractors)
- Facility costs (rental, utilities, maintenance)
- Equipment procurement and maintenance
- Material procurement
- Travel and logistics
- Contingency reserve

### 4.3 Methodology

**Resource Planning Process:**

1. **Identify** resource requirements from prototype specifications
2. **Estimate** quantities, durations, and costs
3. **Allocate** resources to prototype activities
4. **Schedule** resource usage to avoid conflicts
5. **Monitor** resource utilization and availability
6. **Adjust** resource plans based on actuals and changes

**Resource Optimization:**
- Share resources across prototype activities where feasible
- Leverage external partners and suppliers for specialized capabilities
- Implement resource leveling to smooth demand
- Maintain flexible resource pools for unplanned needs

## 6. Quality Criteria

**Resource Planning Quality:**
- Requirements completeness > 95%
- Resource availability confirmation before commitment
- Budget accuracy within ±10% of final actuals
- Schedule alignment with resource availability
- Risk mitigation for critical resource constraints

**Success Metrics:**
- Resource utilization efficiency > 80%
- Unplanned resource shortages < 5% of activities
- Budget variance within tolerance
- Schedule adherence related to resource availability
