# 03-00-08-07-04A - Production Transition

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the process for transitioning GSE prototypes to production, establishing criteria for production readiness, manufacturing planning, supplier qualification, and knowledge transfer to production teams.

## 2. Scope
This document covers all activities required to transition from prototype to production including design freeze, manufacturing documentation, supplier selection, production validation, and handover to operations.

## 4. Prototype Description

### 4.1 Overview
Production Transition ensures that validated GSE prototypes are successfully transitioned to full-scale production with appropriate manufacturing processes, quality controls, and operational support systems in place.

### 4.2 Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Target |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Production Readiness | Completion criteria | All validation tests passed |
| Design Freeze | Configuration control | Approved production baseline |
| Manufacturing Documentation | Complete production package | Drawings, processes, specs |
| Supplier Qualification | Approved vendors | Quality and capability verified |
| Production Validation | First article inspection | Meets all specifications |
| Knowledge Transfer | Training completion | Production team ready |

### 4.3 Materials and Components
- Production drawings and specifications
- Manufacturing process documentation
- Quality control plans
- Supplier qualification records
- Training materials for production staff

## 5. Prototype Build Plan

| Phase | Activities | Duration | Deliverables |
|-------|------------|----------|--------------|
| Design Freeze | Final design approval | 1 week | Production baseline |
| Manufacturing Planning | Process development, tooling | 4-6 weeks | Manufacturing plan |
| Supplier Selection | RFQ, evaluation, qualification | 4-6 weeks | Qualified suppliers |
| Production Pilot | First production units | 4-8 weeks | Pilot production GSE |
| Production Validation | Testing, acceptance | 2-4 weeks | Production approval |
| Handover | Knowledge transfer, training | 2 weeks | Operational production |

## 6. Testing Requirements

### 6.1 Production Readiness Criteria
- **Design Validation**: All testing complete and successful
- **Reliability Demonstrated**: MTBF targets achieved
- **Safety Certified**: All safety approvals obtained
- **User Acceptance**: Operator approval received
- **Cost Target**: Within budget constraints
- **Manufacturability**: Production processes validated

### 6.2 Production Validation
- **First Article Inspection**: Complete dimensional and functional verification
- **Process Capability**: Cpk ≥ 1.33 for critical parameters
- **Quality System**: Production quality plan approved
- **Traceability**: Serialization and records system
- **Supplier Audits**: Manufacturing partners qualified
