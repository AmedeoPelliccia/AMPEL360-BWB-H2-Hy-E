# 03-00-08-02-04A - Material Selection

## 1. Purpose

This document defines material selection criteria, standards, and processes for prototype development within the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E program.

## 2. Scope

This specification covers material selection for all prototyping activities, including structural, functional, and representative materials for various manufacturing processes.

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Material selection for prototypes balances cost, availability, processability, and functional requirements. Materials may be production-representative or prototype-specific, depending on the prototype's objectives.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Material Selection Criteria:**

**Performance Requirements:**
- Mechanical properties (strength, stiffness, toughness)
- Thermal properties (operating temperature, thermal expansion)
- Environmental resistance (humidity, chemicals, UV)
- Compatibility with hydrogen systems (H2 embrittlement resistance)
- Electrical properties (conductivity, dielectric strength)

**Manufacturing Requirements:**
- Compatibility with manufacturing process
- Machinability or formability
- Curing/processing requirements
- Tool life and wear characteristics
- Surface finish capability

**Cost and Availability:**
- Material cost per unit
- Lead time for procurement
- Minimum order quantities
- Supplier availability
- Import/export restrictions

**Safety and Regulatory:**
- Fire, smoke, and toxicity (FST) requirements
- Material certifications and specifications
- Traceability requirements
- Handling and storage requirements
- Disposal and environmental considerations

### 4.3 Methodology

**Material Categories:**

**Metals:**
- **Aluminum Alloys**: 6061 (general), 7075 (high strength), 2024 (aerospace)
  - Applications: Structural prototypes, brackets, housings
  - Properties: Good strength-to-weight, excellent machinability
  
- **Titanium Alloys**: Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5)
  - Applications: High-temperature, high-strength components
  - Properties: Excellent strength-to-weight, corrosion resistance
  
- **Stainless Steel**: 304 (corrosion resistance), 316 (H2 compatible), 17-4 PH (high strength)
  - Applications: H2 system components, pressure vessels, fittings
  - Properties: Corrosion resistance, H2 embrittlement resistance

**Composites:**
- **Carbon Fiber Prepreg**: Various modulus (standard, intermediate, high)
  - Applications: Primary and secondary structures
  - Properties: High strength-to-weight, stiffness, fatigue resistance
  
- **Fiberglass**: E-glass, S-glass
  - Applications: Non-structural panels, fairings, tooling
  - Properties: Lower cost, good electrical insulation
  
- **Core Materials**: Aluminum honeycomb, Nomex honeycomb, foam
  - Applications: Sandwich structures
  - Properties: Lightweight, energy absorption

**Polymers:**
- **3D Printing Materials**: PLA, ABS, PETG, Nylon, TPU, resins
  - Applications: Concept models, fit checks, functional prototypes
  - Properties: Rapid fabrication, complex geometries
  
- **Engineering Plastics**: PEEK, Ultem, Delrin
  - Applications: Functional prototypes, wear surfaces, insulators
  - Properties: High temperature, chemical resistance, low friction

**Material Selection Process:**

1. **Define Requirements**
   - Prototype objectives and environment
   - Functional and performance requirements
   - Manufacturing process constraints

2. **Screen Materials**
   - Create candidate material list
   - Compare properties to requirements
   - Eliminate unsuitable materials

3. **Evaluate Candidates**
   - Detailed property comparison
   - Cost and availability analysis
   - Manufacturing feasibility

4. **Select Material**
   - Document selection rationale
   - Obtain approval
   - Procure with certifications

5. **Validate Selection**
   - Material testing (if required)
   - Prototype performance evaluation
   - Lessons learned capture

## 6. Quality Criteria

**Material Quality:**
- Certified material per applicable specification
- Traceability (heat/lot number) maintained
- Storage conditions maintained
- Shelf life monitored (for time-sensitive materials)
- Handling procedures followed

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Material certification provided
- Visual inspection passed (no defects, damage)
- Dimensional verification passed
- Properties meet specification
- Documentation complete
