# 03-00-08-01-03A - GSE Prototype Budget

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the financial framework for GSE prototype development, defining budget allocations, cost controls, and financial tracking mechanisms for all prototyping activities supporting the AMPEL360 hydrogen aircraft program.

## 2. Scope
The budget covers all GSE prototype development costs including materials, labor, equipment, testing, facilities, and associated overhead. It encompasses hydrogen-specific equipment, electrical systems, mechanical equipment, and rapid prototyping technologies.

## 4. Prototype Description

### 4.1 Overview
The GSE Prototype Budget provides comprehensive financial planning and control for all prototyping activities. It establishes cost baselines, tracks expenditures, and ensures efficient resource allocation across multiple GSE development programs while maintaining fiscal responsibility.

### 4.2 Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Target |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Total Prototype Budget | Full program budget allocation | TBD (Subject to approval) |
| H2 GSE Systems | Priority funding allocation | 40% of total budget |
| Electrical GSE Systems | Secondary priority allocation | 25% of total budget |
| Mechanical GSE Systems | Standard allocation | 20% of total budget |
| Rapid Prototyping Tech | Innovation allocation | 15% of total budget |
| Contingency Reserve | Risk mitigation funding | 10-15% of total budget |

### 4.3 Materials and Components
- Financial management and accounting systems
- Cost tracking and reporting tools
- Budget variance analysis software
- Resource planning systems
- Financial approval workflows

## 5. Prototype Build Plan

| Phase | Activities | Duration | Deliverables |
|-------|------------|----------|--------------|
| Budget Planning | Cost estimation, resource allocation | 1 month | Budget proposal document |
| Budget Approval | Management review and approval | 2 weeks | Approved budget baseline |
| Budget Execution | Expenditure tracking, cost control | Ongoing | Monthly financial reports |
| Budget Review | Variance analysis, reforecasting | Quarterly | Budget status reports |
| Budget Closeout | Final accounting, lessons learned | 1 month | Final budget report |
