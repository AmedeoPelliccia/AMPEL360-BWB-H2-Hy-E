# 03-00-08-01-04A - GSE Prototype Resources

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the resource requirements, allocation strategies, and management approaches for GSE prototype development, ensuring adequate personnel, facilities, equipment, and materials are available to support prototyping activities.

## 2. Scope
This document covers all resource categories required for GSE prototyping including human resources, fabrication facilities, testing equipment, materials and supplies, and external partnerships. It applies to all prototype development phases from concept through production transition.

## 4. Prototype Description

### 4.1 Overview
The GSE Prototype Resources document establishes the framework for identifying, acquiring, allocating, and managing all resources necessary for successful prototype development. It ensures efficient resource utilization while maintaining safety, quality, and schedule commitments.

### 4.2 Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Target |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Engineering Team Size | Core prototype development team | 15-20 engineers |
| Technician Staff | Fabrication and testing support | 10-15 technicians |
| Facility Space | Dedicated prototyping area | 5,000-7,000 sq ft |
| Equipment Investment | Major prototyping equipment | As per budget allocation |
| Material Inventory | Critical materials on hand | 30-day supply |
| External Partnerships | Specialized vendor support | 5-8 key partners |

### 4.3 Materials and Components
- Cryogenic-rated materials for H2 systems
- Electrical components and control systems
- Mechanical fabrication materials
- Rapid prototyping materials (3D printing, composites)
- Safety equipment and personal protective equipment
- Testing and measurement instruments

## 6. Testing Requirements

### 6.1 Human Resources

#### 6.1.1 Engineering Personnel
- **H2 Systems Engineers**: Specialized in cryogenic systems and hydrogen handling
- **Electrical Engineers**: Power systems, controls, and instrumentation
- **Mechanical Engineers**: Structural design, mechanisms, and materials
- **Safety Engineers**: Hazard analysis and safety system design
- **Systems Engineers**: Integration and requirements management

#### 6.1.2 Technical Personnel
- **Fabrication Technicians**: Welding, machining, assembly
- **Test Technicians**: Equipment testing and data collection
- **Quality Control**: Inspection and verification
- **Maintenance Personnel**: Equipment upkeep and repair

#### 6.1.3 Support Personnel
- **Project Management**: Schedule and budget coordination
- **Documentation Specialists**: Technical writing and record keeping
- **Procurement Staff**: Material and equipment acquisition

### 6.2 Facilities and Equipment

#### 6.2.1 Fabrication Facilities
- Machine shop with CNC capabilities
- Welding and assembly areas
- Cryogenic testing facilities
- Clean room for sensitive components
- Material storage and handling areas

#### 6.2.2 Testing Facilities
- H2 testing area with safety systems
- Electrical testing laboratory
- Mechanical testing equipment
- Environmental test chambers
- Non-destructive testing equipment

#### 6.2.3 Rapid Prototyping Equipment
- Industrial 3D printers (metal and polymer)
- CNC machining centers
- Composite layup and curing equipment
- Digital twin simulation workstations
- Scanning and reverse engineering tools

### 6.3 Material Resources

#### 6.3.1 H2-Specific Materials
- Cryogenic-rated stainless steels (304L, 316L)
- Hydrogen-compatible seals and gaskets
- Insulation materials for LH2 systems
- H2 sensors and detection equipment

#### 6.3.2 Electrical Materials
- Power cables and connectors
- Control system components
- Sensors and instrumentation
- Safety interlocks and switches

#### 6.3.3 Mechanical Materials
- Structural materials (aluminum, steel)
- Hydraulic and pneumatic components
- Fasteners and hardware
- Coatings and finishes

### 6.4 External Partnerships
- Cryogenic equipment suppliers
- H2 safety consultants
- Testing and certification laboratories
- University research partnerships
- Industry collaboration programs
