# 03-00-08-01-02A - GSE Prototype Schedule

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the master schedule for GSE prototype development activities, defining timelines, milestones, and dependencies for all prototyping efforts supporting the AMPEL360 BWB hydrogen aircraft program.

## 2. Scope
The schedule encompasses all GSE prototype categories including hydrogen fueling systems, electrical ground power, mechanical handling equipment, and rapid prototyping initiatives. It covers the period from initial concept through production transition.

## 4. Prototype Description

### 4.1 Overview
The GSE Prototype Schedule provides a comprehensive timeline for all prototyping activities, ensuring coordinated development across multiple GSE systems. The schedule is structured to support iterative development while maintaining alignment with aircraft development milestones.

### 4.2 Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Target |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Schedule Horizon | Full prototype lifecycle | 18-24 months |
| Milestone Reviews | Quarterly progress reviews | Every 3 months |
| Critical Path Items | H2 fueling and safety systems | Priority 1 |
| Schedule Buffer | Risk mitigation buffer | 15-20% of total time |
| Update Frequency | Rolling wave planning | Bi-weekly updates |

### 4.3 Materials and Components
- Project management software (MS Project, Primavera)
- Schedule tracking tools and dashboards
- Risk management databases
- Resource allocation systems
- Milestone tracking reports

## 5. Prototype Build Plan

| Phase | Activities | Duration | Deliverables |
|-------|------------|----------|--------------|
| Q1-Q2 2025 | H2 GSE prototype design & initial build | 6 months | LH2 fueling prototype, safety systems |
| Q3 2025 | Electrical GSE prototypes | 3 months | GPU prototype, charging stations |
| Q4 2025 | Mechanical GSE prototypes | 3 months | Tow tractors, maintenance platforms |
| Q1 2026 | Rapid prototyping initiatives | 3 months | 3D printed parts, digital twins |
| Q2 2026 | Integration testing & refinement | 3 months | Integrated GSE system validation |
| Q3-Q4 2026 | Production transition planning | 6 months | Production-ready designs |

## 6. Testing Requirements

### 6.1 Schedule Milestones
- **Milestone 1**: Concept design review (Month 1)
- **Milestone 2**: Preliminary design review (Month 3)
- **Milestone 3**: Critical design review (Month 6)
- **Milestone 4**: Prototype build completion (Month 9)
- **Milestone 5**: Testing completion (Month 12)
- **Milestone 6**: Production readiness review (Month 18)

### 6.2 Dependencies and Critical Path
- H2 fueling systems are on critical path
- Safety certification must precede operational testing
- Aircraft interface requirements must be finalized early
- Airport infrastructure readiness gates

### 6.3 Risk Management
- Schedule risk assessments monthly
- Mitigation strategies for critical delays
- Alternative development paths identified
- Resource contingency planning
