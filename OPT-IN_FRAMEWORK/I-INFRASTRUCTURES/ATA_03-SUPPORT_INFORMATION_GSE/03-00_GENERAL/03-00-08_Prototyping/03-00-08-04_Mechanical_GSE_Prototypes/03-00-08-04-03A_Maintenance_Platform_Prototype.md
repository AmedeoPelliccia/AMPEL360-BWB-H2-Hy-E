# 03-00-08-04-03A - Maintenance Platform Prototype

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the prototype development for Maintenance Platform equipment designed to provide safe access to all areas of the AMPEL360 BWB aircraft for inspection, maintenance, and servicing operations, accommodating the unique geometry and low-profile characteristics of the blended wing body design.

## 2. Scope
This prototype covers maintenance platform design including adjustable height systems, telescoping platforms, safety railings, access stairs, positioning systems, stability controls, and specialized features for BWB aircraft maintenance access requirements.

## 4. Prototype Description

### 4.1 Overview
The Maintenance Platform Prototype provides safe, ergonomic access to all maintenance zones of the AMPEL360 BWB aircraft. It features multiple working levels, adjustable platforms, anti-slip surfaces, fall protection systems, and precise positioning capabilities for the unique BWB configuration.

### 4.2 Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Target |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Platform Capacity | Personnel and equipment load | 450 kg (1,000 lbs) per platform |
| Working Height Range | Elevation capability | 1.0m to 8.0m |
| Platform Size | Work area dimensions | 2.0m x 1.5m minimum |
| Number of Platforms | Multi-level access | 2-4 working levels |
| Mobility | Self-propelled | Electric drive, 0-8 km/h |
| Positioning Accuracy | Platform placement | ±50mm positioning |
| Railing Height | Fall protection | 1.1m minimum, OSHA compliant |
| Access Stairs | Climbing angle | 45-60° with handrails |
| Stability | Outrigger system | Automatic leveling and stabilization |
| Surface Contact | Protect aircraft | Soft-touch bumpers, no-mar materials |

### 4.3 Materials and Components
- **Frame**: Aluminum or steel construction
- **Platform Decks**: Anti-slip aluminum or composite
- **Railings**: Removable safety railings, powder-coated aluminum
- **Lift Mechanism**: Hydraulic or electric scissors lift
- **Outriggers**: Hydraulic stabilizers with load sensors
- **Drive System**: Electric motors with regenerative braking
- **Steering**: All-wheel steering for maneuverability
- **Stairs**: Retractable stairs with handrails
- **Bumpers**: Soft rubber or foam aircraft contact protection
- **Lighting**: LED work lights on platforms
- **Controls**: Platform and ground-level operator stations
- **Safety Systems**: Emergency lowering, tilt sensors, overload protection

## 6. Testing Requirements

### 6.1 Structural Testing
- **Load Capacity**: Static and dynamic load testing
- **Platform Strength**: Distributed and concentrated loads
- **Railing Strength**: 200 lbs horizontal force at top rail
- **Stair Strength**: Concentrated load on each step
- **Frame Fatigue**: Cyclic loading analysis
- **Weld Integrity**: Non-destructive testing of critical welds

### 6.2 Stability Testing
- **Outrigger Effectiveness**: Stability on slopes
- **Wind Resistance**: Operational wind limits
- **Tip-over Resistance**: Worst-case loading scenarios
- **Uneven Surface**: Operation on non-level ground
- **Emergency Conditions**: Sudden load shifts
- **Seismic**: Earthquake resistance (if applicable)

### 6.3 Lift System Testing
- **Lift Speed**: Verify elevation rates
- **Smoothness**: Vibration and jerk measurement
- **Height Accuracy**: Position repeatability
- **Emergency Lowering**: Manual descent capability
- **Hydraulic System**: Pressure testing, leak detection
- **Multiple Platforms**: Synchronized or independent operation

### 6.4 Mobility Testing
- **Drive Performance**: Speed, acceleration, grading
- **Steering**: Turning radius and precision
- **Braking**: Stopping distance and parking brake hold
- **Battery Performance**: Operating range
- **Maneuverability**: Navigation in congested areas
- **Surface Conditions**: Ramp, apron, hangar floor testing

### 6.5 Aircraft Interface Testing
- **Positioning Accuracy**: Alignment to access points
- **Contact Protection**: No aircraft damage from bumpers
- **Clearance**: Avoid interference with aircraft surfaces
- **Multiple Access Points**: Reach all maintenance zones
- **Fuselage Curvature**: Adaptation to BWB profile
- **Wing Access**: Safe working height for wing surfaces

### 6.6 Safety System Testing
- **Fall Protection**: Railing integrity and gate operation
- **Emergency Stop**: E-stop response time
- **Overload Detection**: Automatic lockout at overload
- **Tilt Sensors**: Platform leveling alarms
- **Ground Contact**: Load cell calibration
- **Electrical Safety**: GFCI protection, grounding

### 6.7 Environmental Testing
- **Temperature Range**: -20°C to +50°C operation
- **Weather**: Wind, rain operational limits
- **Corrosion**: Salt spray and chemical resistance
- **UV Exposure**: Material degradation testing
- **Noise**: Acoustic emissions

### 6.8 Operational Testing
- **Setup Time**: Deploy outriggers and position
- **Access Time**: Personnel movement to work areas
- **Tool Handling**: Equipment transport on platforms
- **Multi-platform Coordination**: Team working scenarios
- **Training**: Operator and maintainer training effectiveness
- **Maintenance Access**: Platform serviceability
