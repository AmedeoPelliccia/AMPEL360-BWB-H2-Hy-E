# 03-00-08-03-04A - H2 System Prototypes

## 1. Purpose

This document defines requirements and processes for hydrogen system prototypes within the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E program, ensuring safe and effective development of cryogenic hydrogen storage and distribution systems.

## 2. Scope

This specification covers H2 system prototypes including cryogenic storage tanks, distribution piping, safety systems, refueling interfaces, and integrated system test articles.

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

H2 system prototypes validate the safety, performance, and integration of cryogenic hydrogen storage and distribution for aircraft applications. Given the novel nature of this technology for aviation, extensive prototyping and testing are essential.

### 4.2 Requirements

**H2 System Prototype Categories:**

**Component-Level Prototypes:**
- Cryogenic tank construction (inner vessel, insulation, outer shell)
- Tank mounting and support systems
- Fill and vent valves
- Pressure relief devices
- Sensors (pressure, temperature, level, leak detection)
- Vacuum-insulated piping sections

**Subsystem Prototypes:**
- Complete cryogenic tank assembly
- H2 distribution manifold
- Safety and vent system
- Refueling interface and controls
- Thermal management system
- Boil-off management system

**System-Level Prototypes:**
- Integrated H2 storage and distribution system
- Ground test facility interface
- H2-to-fuel-cell integration
- Full-scale tank installation in representative structure

**H2 System Requirements:**
- **REQ-H2-001**: All H2 prototypes shall comply with safety standards
- **REQ-H2-002**: Cryogenic insulation shall maintain LH2 temperature
- **REQ-H2-003**: Leak detection systems shall be verified
- **REQ-H2-004**: Pressure relief systems shall be validated
- **REQ-H2-005**: Material compatibility with H2 shall be demonstrated
- **REQ-H2-006**: Boil-off rates shall be measured and minimized
- **REQ-H2-007**: Structural integrity under flight loads shall be proven

### 4.3 Methodology

**H2 Prototype Development Process:**

1. **Safety Planning**
   - Hazard identification (HAZID)
   - Safety risk assessment
   - Safety system design
   - Emergency procedures
   - Personnel training requirements

2. **Design and Analysis**
   - Thermal analysis (boil-off, insulation performance)
   - Structural analysis (pressure loads, flight loads, thermal stresses)
   - CFD analysis (venting, purging)
   - Material selection (H2 embrittlement resistance)
   - Safety system design

3. **Fabrication**
   - Specialized welding and joining (TIG, friction stir)
   - Vacuum insulation installation
   - Instrumentation integration
   - Leak testing
   - Pressure testing

4. **Test Facility Preparation**
   - H2-qualified test facility
   - Safety systems (ventilation, detection, suppression)
   - H2 supply and control
   - Data acquisition
   - Emergency response equipment

5. **Testing**
   - Pressure testing (hydrostatic and pneumatic)
   - Leak testing (helium mass spectrometry)
   - Thermal performance testing (boil-off rate)
   - Fill and drain operations
   - Safety system validation
   - Environmental testing (vibration, thermal cycling)
   - Long-term hold testing

6. **Evaluation**
   - Performance against requirements
   - Safety system effectiveness
   - Thermal performance
   - Structural integrity
   - Lessons learned and improvements

**Critical Safety Considerations:**
- H2 embrittlement of metals
- Cryogenic material properties
- Leak detection and mitigation
- Fire and explosion prevention
- Personnel safety and training
- Emergency response procedures
- Ventilation and dispersion
- Static discharge prevention

## 6. Quality Criteria

**Prototype Quality:**
- Design review approved by safety board
- Material certifications for H2 compatibility
- Weld inspections passed (X-ray, UT)
- Pressure vessel certifications
- Instrumentation calibrated

**Test Quality:**
- Test facility H2-qualified
- Safety systems operational and tested
- Personnel trained and certified
- Test plan approved by safety board
- Authority witness (as required)

**Success Criteria:**
- All safety requirements met
- Leak rates within specification
- Boil-off rates acceptable
- Structural integrity demonstrated
- Safety systems validated
- No hazardous incidents
