# 03-00-10-01-04A - GSE Certification Schedule

## 1. Purpose

This document provides the **detailed certification schedule** for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) supporting the AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft, including task dependencies, resource allocation, and critical path analysis.

## 2. Scope

This schedule covers:

- Certification activities for all GSE categories
- Task dependencies and critical path
- Resource requirements and allocation
- Schedule risk factors and contingencies
- Integration with aircraft certification program

## 4. Certification Requirements

### 4.1 Overview

The certification schedule spans **36 months** from program initiation to certification approval, organized into five phases with 30 major milestones.

### 4.2 Regulatory Basis

Schedule accounts for:

- EASA/FAA review cycles (typically 3-6 months)
- Authority meeting cadence (quarterly minimum)
- Issue resolution time (2-4 weeks per issue)
- Re-submission cycles if required

### 4.3 Compliance Methods

Schedule management through:

- Weekly progress reviews
- Monthly milestone tracking
- Quarterly stakeholder reviews
- Critical path monitoring
- Risk-based schedule updates

### 5.3 Critical Path Analysis

**Critical Path Activities** (36 months total):

1. H2 requirements (M1-M2): 2 months
2. H2 concept design (M3-M5): 3 months
3. H2 safety assessment (M6-M9): 4 months
4. H2 detailed design (M10-M15): 6 months
5. H2 prototype build (M15-M18): 4 months
6. H2 type testing (M19-M26): 8 months
7. H2 operational trials (M27-M30): 4 months
8. H2 certification dossier (M28-M30): 3 months (parallel with trials)
9. Authority review (M31-M34): 4 months
10. Certification approval (M35-M36): 2 months

**Float**: Electrical and Mechanical GSE have 6-7 months float.

## 6. Safety Requirements

### 6.1 Safety Schedule Integration

Safety activities are integrated throughout:

- **M3**: Preliminary hazard analysis
- **M6-M9**: System safety assessment
- **M12**: Operating hazard analysis
- **M18**: Safety design review
- **M24**: Safety validation testing
- **M30**: Safety case finalization

### 6.2 Safety Milestones

Each safety milestone is a gate for subsequent activities. Delays in safety activities extend critical path.
