# 03-00-10-01-02A - GSE Certification Roadmap

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a **detailed certification roadmap** for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) supporting the AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft. It outlines key milestones, deliverables, dependencies, and critical paths to achieve certification approval from regulatory authorities.

## 2. Scope

This roadmap covers:

- Certification milestones for all GSE categories
- Key deliverables and evidence packages
- Authority engagement touchpoints
- Critical path activities
- Risk checkpoints and mitigation triggers

## 3. Applicable Documents

- [EASA Regulation (EU) 2018/1139](https://www.easa.europa.eu/document-library/regulations/regulation-eu-20181139) — Common rules in civil aviation
- [FAA 14 CFR Part 139](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-G/part-139) — Certification of airports
- [SAE AS6968](https://www.sae.org/standards/content/as6968/) — Hydrogen Aircraft Refueling
- [03-00-10-01-01A_GSE_Certification_Strategy](./03-00-10-01-01A_GSE_Certification_Strategy.md) — Certification strategy

## 4. Certification Requirements

### 4.1 Overview

The GSE certification roadmap is organized into **five major phases**:

1. **Planning Phase** (Months 1-6)
2. **Development Phase** (Months 7-18)
3. **Verification Phase** (Months 19-30)
4. **Approval Phase** (Months 31-36)
5. **In-Service Phase** (Ongoing from Month 37+)

### 4.2 Regulatory Basis

Certification activities are structured to comply with:

- EASA certification procedures for GSE
- FAA operational approval requirements
- National aviation authority requirements
- Airport authority acceptance criteria

### 4.3 Compliance Methods

Progress is tracked through:

- Milestone completion reviews
- Deliverable acceptance gates
- Authority meeting outcomes
- Risk assessment updates
- Quality audits

## 6. Safety Requirements

### 6.1 Safety Milestones

Safety activities are integrated throughout the roadmap:

- **Month 3**: Preliminary hazard analysis
- **Month 12**: System hazard analysis complete
- **Month 18**: Operating hazard analysis complete
- **Month 24**: Safety validation testing complete
- **Month 30**: Safety case finalized

### 6.2 Safety Gates

Each phase gate includes safety review:

- Safety assessment completion verified
- Hazard log reviewed and mitigations confirmed
- Safety evidence adequacy confirmed
