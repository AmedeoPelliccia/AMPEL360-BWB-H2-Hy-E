# 03-00-10-01-04A - Authority Coordination

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the **authority coordination strategy** for ATA Chapter 03 — Support Information and Ground Support Equipment (GSE) certification. It defines the approach for engaging with EASA, FAA, and other certification authorities throughout the certification process.

## 2. Scope

This coordination strategy covers:

- Pre-application engagement with authorities
- Special conditions development and approval
- Issue papers and technical discussions
- Conformity inspections and audits
- Findings resolution and corrective actions
- Final certification approval process

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Effective coordination with certification authorities is critical for successful certification of the AMPEL360 aircraft GSE systems. This strategy establishes a proactive, transparent, and collaborative approach to authority engagement, particularly important given the novel hydrogen propulsion system requiring special conditions.

### 4.2 Regulatory Requirements

#### 4.2.1 EASA Coordination Requirements

Under EASA Part 21:
- Pre-application meeting (Subpart B)
- Type certification application
- Compliance demonstration
- Conformity inspection
- Type certificate issuance

#### 4.2.2 FAA Coordination Requirements

Under FAA regulations:
- Pre-application statement of conformity
- Type certification application (FAA Form 8110-12)
- Issue papers for special conditions
- Conformity inspections
- Type certificate data sheet approval

### 4.3 Compliance Approach

#### 4.3.1 Authority Engagement Strategy

```
Authority Coordination Framework
├── Pre-Application Phase
│   ├── Initial engagement meetings
│   ├── Certification basis discussion
│   └── Special conditions identification
├── Application Phase
│   ├── Formal application submission
│   ├── Certification plan review
│   └── Compliance demonstration plan
├── Development Phase
│   ├── Regular progress meetings
│   ├── Issue papers and technical discussions
│   └── Interim reviews and inspections
├── Conformity Phase
│   ├── Conformity inspections
│   ├── Test witnessing
│   └── Evidence package review
└── Approval Phase
    ├── Findings resolution
    ├── Final review
    └── Type certificate issuance
```

#### 4.3.2 Communication Protocols

| Activity | Frequency | Participants | Format | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|--------------|--------|---------|
| Pre-application meeting | One-time | Program management, EASA/FAA | Face-to-face | Initial coordination |
| Progress meetings | Quarterly | Certification team, authorities | Video conference | Status updates |
| Technical meetings | As needed | Subject matter experts, authorities | Video/Face-to-face | Issue resolution |
| Conformity inspections | Per schedule | Certification team, inspectors | On-site | Compliance verification |
| Final review | One-time | Senior management, authorities | Face-to-face | Certification approval |
