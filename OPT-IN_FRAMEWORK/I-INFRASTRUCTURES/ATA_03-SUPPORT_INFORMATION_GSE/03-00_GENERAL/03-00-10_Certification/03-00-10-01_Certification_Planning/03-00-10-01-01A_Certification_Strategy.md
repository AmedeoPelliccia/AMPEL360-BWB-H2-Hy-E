# 03-00-10-01-01A - Certification Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **certification strategy** for ATA Chapter 03 — Support Information and Ground Support Equipment (GSE) for the AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft. It establishes the overall approach, methodology, and coordination required to achieve certification of GSE and support information systems.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Coverage

This certification strategy applies to:

1. **Ground Support Equipment (GSE)**
   - Hydrogen refuelling systems
   - Electrical ground power units
   - Maintenance platforms and access equipment
   - Towing and ground handling equipment
   - Environmental control service units

2. **Support Information Systems**
   - Technical documentation and publications
   - Maintenance management systems
   - Training programs and materials
   - Digital product passport integration

3. **Interface Requirements**
   - Aircraft-to-GSE interfaces
   - Airport infrastructure compatibility
   - Data communication and digital integration

### 2.2 Out of Scope

- Aircraft system certification (covered under respective ATA chapters)
- Airport infrastructure certification
- Manufacturing equipment certification

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The certification strategy establishes a systematic approach to demonstrate compliance with all applicable regulatory requirements for GSE and support information systems. This strategy recognizes the unique challenges posed by:

- **Hydrogen fuel systems**: Novel cryogenic LH₂ handling equipment
- **BWB configuration**: Non-standard aircraft geometry requiring specialized GSE
- **Digital integration**: Enhanced connectivity between GSE and aircraft systems
- **Sustainability goals**: Environmental compliance and renewable energy integration

### 4.2 Regulatory Requirements

#### 4.2.1 Primary Requirements

The certification strategy addresses the following key regulatory requirements:

| Requirement | Regulation | Description | Certification Approach |
|-------------|------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Ground service interfaces | CS-25.1581, §25.1581 | Operational interface requirements | Analysis + Testing |
| Servicing information | CS-25.1583, §25.1583 | Required servicing information | Documentation Review |
| Fuel system safety | CS-25.981, §25.981 | Fuel tank explosion prevention | Safety Assessment + Testing |
| Equipment compatibility | CS-25.1309, §25.1309 | Equipment, systems, and installations | Interface testing |

#### 4.2.2 Special Conditions

Due to the novel hydrogen propulsion system, special conditions will be required for:

- Cryogenic hydrogen ground handling procedures
- LH₂ refuelling equipment safety requirements
- Hydrogen leak detection and mitigation systems
- Emergency response procedures for hydrogen incidents

### 4.3 Compliance Approach

#### 4.3.1 Compliance Methodology

The certification strategy employs a multi-faceted approach:

```
Certification Strategy
├── Regulatory Compliance
│   ├── CS-25/Part 25 Compliance
│   ├── Special Conditions
│   └── Environmental Standards
├── Safety Assurance
│   ├── Functional Hazard Assessment (FHA)
│   ├── System Safety Assessment (SSA)
│   └── Common Cause Analysis
├── Verification & Validation
│   ├── Ground Testing
│   ├── Interface Testing
│   └── Operational Validation
└── Documentation & Evidence
    ├── Certification Plans
    ├── Compliance Reports
    └── Test Evidence Packages
```

#### 4.3.2 Means of Compliance

The following means of compliance will be employed:

1. **Analysis**
   - Safety analysis (FHA, PSSA, SSA)
   - Failure modes and effects analysis (FMEA)
   - Interface compatibility analysis

2. **Testing**
   - GSE functional testing
   - Interface testing with aircraft systems
   - Environmental testing (temperature, humidity, vibration)
   - Safety system validation

3. **Inspection**
   - Design data review
   - Manufacturing process inspection
   - Quality assurance audits

4. **Demonstration**
   - Operational procedures demonstration
   - Emergency response exercises
   - Training program effectiveness

### 4.4 Certification Phases

#### Phase 1: Certification Basis Definition (Months 1-3)
- Define applicable regulations and standards
- Identify special conditions required
- Establish certification plan
- Coordinate with authorities

#### Phase 2: Compliance Planning (Months 4-6)
- Develop detailed compliance matrix
- Define verification methods
- Plan certification tests
- Establish documentation requirements

#### Phase 3: Design and Development (Months 7-18)
- GSE design and development
- Safety assessments
- Interface development
- Documentation preparation

#### Phase 4: Verification & Validation (Months 19-30)
- Ground testing of GSE
- Interface testing
- Safety validation
- Operational demonstrations

#### Phase 5: Certification Evidence (Months 31-36)
- Compile certification packages
- Authority reviews and audits
- Address findings and compliance issues
- Final certification approval
