# 03-00-10-01-03A - Certification Schedule

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **certification schedule** for ATA Chapter 03 — Support Information and Ground Support Equipment (GSE). It establishes the timeline, milestones, and dependencies for achieving certification of GSE and support information systems for the AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft.

## 2. Scope

This schedule encompasses all certification activities for:

- Ground Support Equipment (GSE) design and qualification
- Support information systems and documentation
- Interface certification with aircraft systems
- Safety assessments and compliance demonstrations
- Coordination with certification authorities

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The certification schedule is structured in five major phases spanning approximately 36 months, aligned with the overall aircraft development and certification program. Critical path items include hydrogen safety system qualification, special conditions approval, and GSE interface testing.

### 4.2 Regulatory Requirements

Certification schedule must account for:

- Authority review cycles (typically 60-90 days)
- Special conditions development and approval (6-12 months)
- Test campaign planning and execution
- Documentation review and approval cycles
- Issue resolution and re-testing periods

### 4.3 Compliance Approach

The schedule employs a phased approach with defined gates and milestones to ensure systematic progress toward certification approval.
