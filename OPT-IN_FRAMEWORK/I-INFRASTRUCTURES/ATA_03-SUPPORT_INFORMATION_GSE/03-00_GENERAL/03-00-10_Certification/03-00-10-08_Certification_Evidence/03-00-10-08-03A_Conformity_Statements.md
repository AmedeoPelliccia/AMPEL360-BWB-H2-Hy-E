# 03-00-10-08-03A - Conformity Statements

## 1. Purpose

This document addresses **conformity statements** for ATA Chapter 03 — Support Information and Ground Support Equipment (GSE) supporting the AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Coverage

This document covers:

1. **Ground Support Equipment**
   - Hydrogen refuelling systems
   - Electrical ground power units
   - Maintenance access equipment
   - Ground handling equipment

2. **Support Information Systems**
   - Technical documentation
   - Digital integration systems
   - Training and procedures

3. **Interface Requirements**
   - Aircraft-to-GSE interfaces
   - Safety and emergency systems
   - Data communication systems

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Statements of conformity and compliance. This documentation supports the overall certification strategy for GSE systems, ensuring compliance with applicable airworthiness requirements and industry standards.

### 4.2 Regulatory Requirements

The following regulatory requirements apply:

- CS-25.1309 / §25.1309 — Equipment, systems, and installations
- CS-25.1581 / §25.1581 — General (operating information)
- CS-25.1583 / §25.1583 — Operating limitations
- CS-25.981 / §25.981 — Fuel tank explosion prevention (hydrogen systems)

### 4.3 Compliance Approach

Compliance is demonstrated through:

1. **Analysis**: Engineering analysis and safety assessments
2. **Testing**: Functional and environmental testing
3. **Inspection**: Design review and conformity inspection
4. **Demonstration**: Operational demonstrations and procedures validation
