## 1. Purpose

This document defines the logistics planning approach for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, ensuring efficient movement of materials, components, and finished aircraft.

## 2. Scope

This document addresses:
- Inbound logistics (supplier to factory)
- Internal logistics (within facility)
- Outbound logistics (factory to customer)
- Transportation planning and management
- Packaging and handling requirements
- Special logistics for large assemblies and hydrogen systems

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Logistics planning encompasses all activities related to the physical movement and storage of materials throughout the production and delivery process. Efficient logistics reduces costs, ensures on-time delivery, and maintains product quality during transport.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Logistics Planning Objectives:**
- On-time delivery to support production schedule
- Cost-effective transportation solutions
- Damage-free material handling
- Compliance with safety regulations
- Optimized routing and consolidation
- Real-time visibility and tracking

**Special Considerations:**
- Large component transport (BWB fuselage sections, wings)
- Hydrogen system components (special handling, safety)
- International shipping (customs, documentation)
- Temperature-sensitive materials
- Hazardous materials (batteries, chemicals)

### 4.3 Methodology

**Logistics Planning Framework:**

1. **Inbound Logistics**
   - Supplier delivery schedules
   - Receiving procedures
   - Inspection and acceptance
   - Material staging
   - Dock scheduling

2. **Internal Logistics**
   - Material flow mapping
   - Material handling equipment
   - Kitting and staging
   - Line-side delivery
   - WIP movement

3. **Outbound Logistics**
   - Aircraft delivery planning
   - Customer site preparation
   - Flight delivery vs. ground transport
   - Documentation and handover
   - After-sales support logistics

4. **Transportation Management**
   - Carrier selection and management
   - Route optimization
   - Freight consolidation
   - Mode selection (air, truck, rail, ship)
   - Cost management

5. **Packaging and Handling**
   - Packaging design standards
   - Protective packaging for sensitive items
   - Reusable container programs
   - Labeling and marking
   - Handling instructions

6. **Tracking and Visibility**
   - Shipment tracking systems
   - Real-time status updates
   - Exception management
   - Performance metrics
   - Continuous improvement
