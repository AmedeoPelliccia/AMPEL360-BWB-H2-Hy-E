## 1. Purpose

This document defines the procurement strategy for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, establishing the approach for acquiring materials, components, and services.

## 2. Scope

This document addresses:
- Procurement approach and philosophy
- Sourcing strategies (single, dual, multiple)
- Contracting approaches and terms
- Cost management and negotiation
- Risk mitigation strategies
- Supplier relationship management

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The procurement strategy establishes how the program will acquire necessary materials, components, and services to meet production requirements. The strategy balances cost, quality, schedule, and risk considerations while building strategic supplier partnerships.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Strategic Objectives:**
- Cost-effective procurement
- Quality assurance throughout supply chain
- On-time delivery of materials
- Supply chain resilience
- Innovation and value creation
- Ethical and sustainable sourcing

**Procurement Approaches:**
- **Direct Materials:** Long-term agreements, strategic partnerships
- **Commodity Items:** Competitive bidding, framework agreements
- **Services:** Performance-based contracts
- **Critical Items:** Dual-source strategy, inventory buffer
- **Emerging Technologies:** Early supplier involvement

### 4.3 Methodology

**Procurement Strategy Framework:**

1. **Category Management**
   - Spend analysis
   - Category strategies by commodity
   - Sourcing approach definition
   - Supplier portfolio optimization

2. **Sourcing Strategy**
   - Single source (strategic, critical items)
   - Dual source (risk mitigation)
   - Multiple source (commodities)
   - Global vs. local sourcing

3. **Contract Strategy**
   - Fixed price contracts
   - Cost-plus arrangements
   - Incentive-based contracts
   - Long-term agreements (LTAs)
   - Framework contracts

4. **Negotiation Approach**
   - Total cost of ownership focus
   - Win-win negotiation
   - Value engineering collaboration
   - Risk-sharing mechanisms
   - Performance incentives

5. **Relationship Management**
   - Tiered supplier management
   - Strategic partnerships
   - Regular business reviews
   - Continuous improvement programs
   - Innovation collaboration
