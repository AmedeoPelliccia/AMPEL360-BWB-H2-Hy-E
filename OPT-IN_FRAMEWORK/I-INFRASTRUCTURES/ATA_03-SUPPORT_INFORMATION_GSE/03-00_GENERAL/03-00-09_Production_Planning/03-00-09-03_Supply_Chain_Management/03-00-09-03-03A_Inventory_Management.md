## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the inventory management approach for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, optimizing inventory levels to support production while minimizing carrying costs.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Inventory planning and control methodology
- Safety stock determination
- Just-in-time (JIT) delivery implementation
- Warehouse management and organization
- Inventory tracking and traceability
- Obsolescence management

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Inventory management ensures that materials and components are available when needed for production while minimizing inventory carrying costs and waste. The approach balances just-in-time principles with safety stock requirements for risk mitigation.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Inventory Management Objectives:**
- Material availability for production schedule
- Minimized inventory carrying costs
- Reduced obsolescence risk
- Full traceability of aerospace materials
- Compliance with shelf-life requirements
- Efficient warehouse space utilization

**Inventory Categories:**
- **Raw Materials:** Metals, composites, chemicals
- **Purchased Components:** Avionics, systems, hardware
- **Work-in-Process (WIP):** Sub-assemblies, partially completed items
- **Finished Goods:** Completed aircraft awaiting delivery
- **Consumables:** Tools, supplies, shop materials
- **Spares:** Service parts inventory

### 4.3 Methodology

**Inventory Management Approach:**

1. **Inventory Planning**
   - Material Requirements Planning (MRP)
   - Safety stock calculation
   - Reorder point determination
   - Economic order quantity (EOQ) analysis
   - ABC classification

2. **Inventory Control**
   - Cycle counting procedures
   - Physical inventory audits
   - Shortage management
   - Excess and obsolescence review
   - Slow-moving item analysis

3. **JIT Implementation**
   - Supplier delivery schedules
   - Kanban systems
   - Point-of-use storage
   - Milk runs and sequenced delivery
   - Vendor-managed inventory (VMI)

4. **Warehouse Management**
   - Location management system
   - FIFO/FEFO enforcement
   - Environmental controls (temp/humidity)
   - Shelf-life tracking
   - Material handling procedures

5. **Traceability**
   - Serial number tracking
   - Lot and batch control
   - Certificate of Conformance (C of C)
   - Material test reports
   - Chain of custody documentation
