## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the criteria and process for selecting suppliers for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program.

## 2. Scope

This document defines:
- Supplier selection criteria and evaluation methodology
- Qualification requirements for aerospace suppliers
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Supplier performance expectations
- Strategic partnership development

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Supplier selection is a critical process that ensures the right partners are chosen to deliver high-quality components and services on time and at competitive costs. The process evaluates technical capability, quality systems, financial stability, and strategic alignment.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Selection Criteria:**
- **Quality:** AS9100 certification, quality track record
- **Technical Capability:** Manufacturing capabilities, technology level
- **Capacity:** Production volume capability, scalability
- **Cost Competitiveness:** Pricing, total cost of ownership
- **Delivery Performance:** On-time delivery history
- **Financial Stability:** Financial health, business continuity
- **Innovation:** R&D capability, continuous improvement culture
- **Sustainability:** Environmental and social responsibility

**Qualification Requirements:**
- AS9100 or equivalent quality certification
- Proven aerospace experience
- Satisfactory facility audit
- Approved quality plans
- Supply chain security compliance
- Conflict minerals policy

### 4.3 Methodology

**Supplier Selection Process:**

1. **Needs Identification**
   - Component/service requirements
   - Volume and schedule needs
   - Technical specifications
   - Quality requirements

2. **Market Research**
   - Potential supplier identification
   - Capability assessment
   - Market analysis
   - Preliminary screening

3. **Evaluation**
   - Request for Information (RFI)
   - Request for Proposal (RFP)
   - Technical evaluation
   - Financial assessment
   - Site audits

4. **Qualification**
   - Quality system audit
   - Process capability studies
   - First article inspection
   - Trial production

5. **Selection and Award**
   - Final evaluation and scoring
   - Negotiation
   - Contract award
   - Partnership agreement
