## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the training program requirements for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production workforce, ensuring employees have necessary skills and knowledge.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Training program development and delivery
- New hire orientation and onboarding
- Skills training and development
- Certification and qualification programs
- Continuing education and refresher training
- Training effectiveness measurement

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Training programs ensure the workforce has the knowledge, skills, and abilities required to perform their jobs safely and effectively. Comprehensive training supports quality, productivity, and continuous improvement objectives.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Training Program Objectives:**
- Competent workforce for all operations
- Safety awareness and compliance
- Quality consciousness and practices
- Process adherence and consistency
- Continuous skill development
- Regulatory and certification compliance

**Training Categories:**
- **Orientation:** Company policies, safety, quality culture
- **Basic Skills:** Hand tools, blueprint reading, measurement
- **Technical Skills:** Assembly, fabrication, systems installation
- **Special Processes:** Certified processes (welding, NDT, etc.)
- **Safety Training:** Emergency response, H2 safety, hazardous materials
- **Quality Training:** Inspection, documentation, problem-solving
- **Leadership:** Supervisory skills, team leading

### 4.3 Methodology

**Training Program Framework:**

1. **Training Needs Analysis**
   - Job task analysis
   - Skill gap identification
   - Competency requirements
   - Regulatory requirements
   - New technology integration

2. **Program Development**
   - Curriculum design
   - Training materials creation
   - Hands-on exercise development
   - Simulation and virtual training
   - Instructor qualification

3. **Training Delivery**
   - Classroom instruction
   - On-the-job training (OJT)
   - Computer-based training (CBT)
   - Virtual reality training
   - Mentoring and coaching

4. **Training Tracking**
   - Training records database
   - Certification tracking
   - Expiration management
   - Training matrix maintenance
   - Compliance monitoring

5. **Effectiveness Evaluation**
   - Knowledge assessments
   - Skills demonstrations
   - Performance observation
   - Quality metrics correlation
   - Feedback and surveys

6. **Continuous Improvement**
   - Program evaluation
   - Curriculum updates
   - New method integration
   - Best practice sharing
   - Lessons learned incorporation
