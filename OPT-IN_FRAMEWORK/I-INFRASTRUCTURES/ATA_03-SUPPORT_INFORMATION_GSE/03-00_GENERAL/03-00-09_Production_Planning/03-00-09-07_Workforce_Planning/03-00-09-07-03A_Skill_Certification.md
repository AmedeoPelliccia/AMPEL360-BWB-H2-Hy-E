## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the skill certification system for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, ensuring workers are qualified to perform critical operations.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Certification requirements by skill category
- Certification assessment and testing
- Certification maintenance and renewal
- Certification documentation and records
- Instructor and evaluator qualification
- Integration with quality and special process requirements

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Skill certification provides formal recognition that workers have demonstrated competence to perform specific operations. Certification is mandatory for safety-critical operations, special processes, and quality-critical tasks.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Certification Objectives:**
- Ensure workforce competency
- Meet regulatory requirements
- Support quality assurance
- Enable traceability
- Maintain certification currency
- Support continuous improvement

**Certification Categories:**
- **Special Processes:** Welding, NDT, heat treat, chemical processing
- **Critical Operations:** Major assembly joins, pressure testing
- **Quality Functions:** Inspection, first article inspection
- **Safety Operations:** H2 systems, hazardous materials, confined space
- **Equipment Operation:** Cranes, forklifts, automated systems
- **Technical Skills:** Composites, avionics, electrical systems

### 4.3 Methodology

**Certification Framework:**

1. **Certification Requirement Definition**
   - Job task analysis
   - Risk assessment
   - Regulatory requirement review
   - Customer requirements
   - Industry standards

2. **Certification Standards Development**
   - Performance criteria
   - Knowledge requirements
   - Practical demonstration standards
   - Assessment methods
   - Passing scores/criteria

3. **Certification Process**
   - Prerequisite verification (training completion, experience)
   - Written examination
   - Practical demonstration
   - Performance evaluation
   - Certification approval
   - Certificate issuance

4. **Certification Levels**
   - **Level I:** Basic competency, work under supervision
   - **Level II:** Independent work, standard operations
   - **Level III:** Advanced work, instruction, evaluation
   - **Instructor/Evaluator:** Qualified to train and certify others

5. **Certification Maintenance**
   - Recertification intervals (typically annual)
   - Continuing education requirements
   - Performance monitoring
   - Proficiency demonstration
   - Refresher training

6. **Certification Management**
   - Certification database
   - Expiration tracking and alerts
   - Qualification matrix
   - Statistical analysis
   - Audit support
