## 1. Purpose

This document establishes safety protocols for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production workforce, ensuring a safe working environment and compliance with safety regulations.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- General workplace safety requirements
- Hydrogen safety protocols
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements
- Emergency response procedures
- Safety training and awareness
- Incident reporting and investigation
- Safety performance monitoring

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Safety protocols define the policies, procedures, and practices to protect workers from occupational hazards during aircraft production. Special emphasis is placed on hydrogen system assembly and testing due to unique safety considerations.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Safety Program Objectives:**
- Zero workplace injuries and illnesses
- Full compliance with safety regulations
- Proactive hazard identification and mitigation
- Safety-conscious workforce culture
- Effective emergency response
- Continuous safety improvement

**Key Safety Areas:**
- **General Safety:** Housekeeping, walking/working surfaces, fall protection
- **Hydrogen Safety:** H2 handling, storage, leak detection, ventilation
- **Chemical Safety:** Hazardous materials, MSDS, exposure controls
- **Electrical Safety:** Lockout/tagout, arc flash, electrical work permits
- **Material Handling:** Lifting, rigging, crane operations, ergonomics
- **Fire Safety:** Prevention, detection, suppression, evacuation
- **Confined Space:** Entry permits, atmospheric testing, rescue

### 4.3 Methodology

**Safety Protocol Framework:**

1. **Hazard Identification and Assessment**
   - Job hazard analysis (JHA)
   - Process hazard analysis (PHA)
   - Risk assessment matrices
   - Safety audits and inspections
   - Incident investigation findings

2. **Safety Control Hierarchy**
   - Elimination: Remove the hazard
   - Substitution: Replace with less hazardous
   - Engineering Controls: Ventilation, guarding, interlocks
   - Administrative Controls: Procedures, training, signage
   - Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Last line of defense

3. **Hydrogen Safety Protocols**
   - H2 detection and monitoring systems
   - Ventilation requirements
   - Ignition source controls
   - Grounding and bonding
   - Emergency shutdown procedures
   - Leak testing procedures
   - Qualified personnel requirements

4. **PPE Requirements**
   - Safety glasses/face shields
   - Hard hats and safety shoes
   - Hearing protection
   - Respiratory protection (where required)
   - Protective clothing (chemical, welding, etc.)
   - Fall protection equipment

5. **Emergency Response**
   - Emergency action plan (EAP)
   - Fire evacuation procedures
   - Medical emergency response
   - Chemical spill response
   - H2 leak/fire response
   - Emergency contact list

6. **Safety Training and Communication**
   - New hire safety orientation
   - Job-specific safety training
   - Refresher training (annual)
   - Safety meetings (weekly/monthly)
   - Safety alerts and bulletins
   - Toolbox talks

7. **Incident Management**
   - Injury/illness reporting
   - Incident investigation
   - Root cause analysis
   - Corrective action implementation
   - Lessons learned dissemination
   - Near-miss reporting
