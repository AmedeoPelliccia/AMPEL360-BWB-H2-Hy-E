## 1. Purpose

This document defines the staffing requirements for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, establishing workforce needs to support production targets.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Workforce planning methodology
- Staffing level determination
- Skill mix requirements
- Shift planning and coverage
- Hiring and onboarding plans
- Workforce flexibility strategies

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Staffing requirements planning ensures adequate workforce availability to meet production schedules while maintaining quality and safety standards. The plan addresses both direct production labor and indirect support functions.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Workforce Planning Objectives:**
- Adequate staffing for production targets
- Appropriate skill mix and levels
- Cost-effective labor deployment
- Workforce flexibility and scalability
- Employee satisfaction and retention
- Compliance with labor regulations

**Workforce Categories:**
- **Direct Labor:** Assembly, fabrication, systems installation
- **Indirect Labor:** Material handling, inspection, maintenance
- **Support Functions:** Engineering, quality, planning, supervision
- **Specialized Skills:** H2 systems, composites, avionics, testing

### 4.3 Methodology

**Staffing Requirements Framework:**

1. **Workforce Demand Analysis**
   - Production volume and rate
   - Work content by skill category
   - Labor hours per aircraft
   - Productivity assumptions
   - Efficiency factors

2. **Headcount Calculation**
   - Standard hours per period
   - Efficiency and utilization factors
   - Absenteeism allowance
   - Training time allocation
   - Buffer for variability

3. **Skill Mix Planning**
   - Skill category definition
   - Experience level requirements (entry, intermediate, expert)
   - Certification requirements
   - Supervisory ratios
   - Cross-training targets

4. **Shift Planning**
   - Shift configuration (1, 2, or 3 shifts)
   - Coverage requirements
   - Weekend/overtime planning
   - Crew rotation strategies
   - Work-life balance considerations

5. **Hiring Plan**
   - Ramp-up timeline
   - Hiring sources and channels
   - Selection criteria
   - Onboarding duration
   - Training pipeline

6. **Workforce Flexibility**
   - Core vs. flexible workforce
   - Temporary and contract labor
   - Overtime strategies
   - Cross-training programs
   - Workforce pooling
