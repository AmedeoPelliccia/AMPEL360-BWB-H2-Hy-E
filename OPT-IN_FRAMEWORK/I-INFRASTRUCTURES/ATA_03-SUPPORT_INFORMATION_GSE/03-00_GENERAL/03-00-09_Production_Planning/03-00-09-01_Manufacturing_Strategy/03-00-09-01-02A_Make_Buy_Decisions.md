## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the framework and criteria for make/buy decisions across all components and assemblies of the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft program.

## 2. Scope

This document defines:
- Make/buy decision criteria and process
- Core competency identification
- Supplier capability assessment
- Cost-benefit analysis methodology
- Risk evaluation for outsourcing decisions

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Make/buy decisions are strategic determinations of whether to manufacture components in-house or procure them from external suppliers. These decisions impact cost, quality, schedule, and intellectual property protection.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Decision Criteria:**
- Strategic importance and core competency alignment
- Technical capability and capacity
- Cost competitiveness
- Quality and reliability considerations
- Intellectual property protection
- Supply chain risk assessment
- Regulatory and certification requirements

**Process Requirements:**
- Multi-disciplinary review team
- Documented decision rationale
- Periodic review and reassessment
- Alignment with business strategy
- Supplier qualification requirements

### 4.3 Methodology

**Decision Framework:**
1. **Strategic Assessment**
   - Core competency analysis
   - Long-term strategic alignment
   - Technology roadmap considerations

2. **Technical Evaluation**
   - Manufacturing capability assessment
   - Technology maturity evaluation
   - Quality assurance feasibility

3. **Financial Analysis**
   - Total cost of ownership
   - Capital investment requirements
   - Operating cost comparison
   - Break-even analysis

4. **Risk Assessment**
   - Supply chain vulnerability
   - Intellectual property risks
   - Quality and reliability risks
   - Schedule impact analysis

5. **Decision Matrix**
   - Weighted scoring system
   - Multi-criteria evaluation
   - Sensitivity analysis
