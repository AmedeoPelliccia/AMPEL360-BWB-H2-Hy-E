## 1. Purpose

This document defines the methodology for planning and managing production rates for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft program, balancing market demand with manufacturing capacity.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Production rate planning methodology
- Capacity analysis and constraints
- Ramp-up and ramp-down strategies
- Resource allocation across production phases
- Integration with supply chain and facility planning

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Production rate planning establishes the target aircraft delivery schedule and ensures alignment of manufacturing resources, supply chain capacity, and facility capabilities to meet program objectives.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Planning Requirements:**
- Market demand forecasting integration
- Manufacturing capacity assessment
- Supply chain capability validation
- Workforce availability planning
- Capital investment timing
- Quality maintenance during rate changes

**Operational Requirements:**
- Flexible production system design
- Scalable processes and tooling
- Buffer capacity for variability
- Risk mitigation for rate changes
- Continuous monitoring and adjustment

### 4.3 Methodology

**Rate Planning Process:**

1. **Demand Analysis**
   - Market forecast review
   - Customer commitment validation
   - Backlog analysis
   - Long-term demand projections

2. **Capacity Assessment**
   - Current capability evaluation
   - Bottleneck identification
   - Resource availability analysis
   - Supplier capacity verification

3. **Rate Scenario Development**
   - Multiple rate scenarios
   - Ramp-up profiles
   - Steady-state production targets
   - Flexibility analysis

4. **Resource Planning**
   - Workforce planning
   - Equipment and tooling requirements
   - Facility space allocation
   - Supply chain synchronization

5. **Implementation Roadmap**
   - Phased rate increase plan
   - Investment timing
   - Risk mitigation measures
   - Performance monitoring
