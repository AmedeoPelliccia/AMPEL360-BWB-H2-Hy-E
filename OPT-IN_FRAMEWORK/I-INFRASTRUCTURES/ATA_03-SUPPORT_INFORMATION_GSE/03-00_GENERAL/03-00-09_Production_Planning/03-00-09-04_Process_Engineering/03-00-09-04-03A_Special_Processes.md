## 1. Purpose

This document defines the requirements and controls for special processes in the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, ensuring quality and compliance for critical manufacturing operations.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Special process identification and classification
- Process qualification requirements
- Operator certification requirements
- Quality control and documentation
- Special processes for H2 systems and hybrid-electric propulsion
- Non-destructive testing (NDT) procedures

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Special processes are manufacturing operations that cannot be fully verified by inspection or test of the product. These processes require strict controls, qualified procedures, certified operators, and continuous monitoring to ensure acceptable quality.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Special Process Categories:**
- **Heat Treatment:** Stress relief, age hardening, solution treatment
- **Welding:** TIG, MIG, resistance welding, electron beam
- **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** X-ray, ultrasonic, dye penetrant, eddy current
- **Chemical Processing:** Anodizing, chemical milling, passivation
- **Composite Processing:** Autoclave curing, vacuum bag processing
- **Surface Treatment:** Shot peening, painting, coating
- **Hydrogen System Assembly:** Special handling and leak testing

**Control Requirements:**
- Written, qualified procedures
- Process parameter monitoring and recording
- Certified/qualified operators
- Calibrated equipment
- Process validation and revalidation
- Non-conformance procedures
- Traceability and documentation

### 4.3 Methodology

**Special Process Control Framework:**

1. **Process Qualification**
   - Process specification development
   - Parameter range determination
   - Qualification testing
   - NADCAP accreditation (where required)
   - Documentation approval

2. **Operator Certification**
   - Training program development
   - Practical and written examinations
   - Certification records maintenance
   - Recertification requirements
   - Continuous assessment

3. **Equipment Control**
   - Equipment qualification
   - Calibration procedures
   - Preventive maintenance
   - Performance verification
   - Spare parts management

4. **Process Monitoring**
   - Real-time parameter monitoring
   - Statistical process control
   - Trend analysis
   - Audit and surveillance
   - Corrective action

5. **Documentation and Traceability**
   - Process records (travelers, logs)
   - Material certifications
   - Test results
   - Non-conformance reports
   - Certificate of conformance
