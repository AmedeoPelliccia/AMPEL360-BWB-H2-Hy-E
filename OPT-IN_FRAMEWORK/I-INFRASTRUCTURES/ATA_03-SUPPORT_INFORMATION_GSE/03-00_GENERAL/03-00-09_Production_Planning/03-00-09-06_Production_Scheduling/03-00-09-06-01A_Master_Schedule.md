## 1. Purpose

This document defines the Master Production Schedule for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft program, establishing the overall production timeline and delivery commitments.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Master schedule development methodology
- Production planning horizon
- Customer delivery commitments
- Schedule integration with supply chain and resources
- Schedule monitoring and updates
- Risk management and contingency planning

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The Master Production Schedule (MPS) is the top-level production plan defining when aircraft will be built and delivered. It serves as the basis for all detailed production planning, material procurement, and resource allocation activities.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Master Schedule Objectives:**
- Meet customer delivery commitments
- Optimize resource utilization
- Balance production flow
- Support financial planning
- Enable supply chain synchronization
- Provide visibility to stakeholders

**Schedule Elements:**
- Aircraft serial numbers and customer assignments
- Major milestone dates
- Production start and completion dates
- Delivery dates
- Rate changes and ramp profiles
- Buffer time allocations

### 4.3 Methodology

**Master Schedule Development:**

1. **Demand Planning**
   - Customer order book review
   - Delivery commitment validation
   - Backlog analysis
   - Forecast integration
   - Capacity constraint review

2. **Schedule Creation**
   - Aircraft sequencing
   - Cycle time allocation
   - Lead time consideration
   - Resource leveling
   - Buffer time insertion

3. **Constraint Analysis**
   - Capacity constraints
   - Supply chain constraints
   - Tooling availability
   - Workforce availability
   - Facility constraints

4. **Schedule Optimization**
   - Load balancing
   - Work stream smoothing
   - Cost optimization
   - Risk mitigation
   - Flexibility preservation

5. **Schedule Management**
   - Weekly/monthly reviews
   - Variance analysis
   - Risk assessment updates
   - Recovery planning
   - Stakeholder communication

6. **Schedule Updates**
   - Change control process
   - Impact assessment
   - Customer communication
   - System updates
   - Documentation
