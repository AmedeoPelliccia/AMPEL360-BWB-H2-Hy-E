## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the requirements and design principles for the factory layout supporting the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program.

## 2. Scope

This document defines:
- Factory layout design principles
- Material flow optimization
- Workspace organization
- Safety and ergonomics considerations
- Scalability and flexibility requirements

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The factory layout defines the physical arrangement of production areas, equipment, tooling, and support facilities to optimize efficiency, safety, and quality in aircraft manufacturing. The layout supports lean manufacturing principles and accommodates the unique requirements of BWB aircraft assembly.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Layout Requirements:**
- Optimized material flow (minimize transport distance)
- Flexible configuration for different production rates
- Clear segregation of work areas by function
- Adequate space for tooling and equipment
- Support for just-in-time delivery
- Compliance with safety and fire codes

**Specific Considerations:**
- Large component handling (BWB fuselage sections)
- Hydrogen system assembly areas (special safety zones)
- Hybrid-electric propulsion integration
- Clean room areas for sensitive systems
- Quality inspection stations
- Storage and staging areas

### 4.3 Methodology

**Layout Design Process:**

1. **Requirements Analysis**
   - Production volume and rate
   - Component sizes and weights
   - Process flow mapping
   - Support service requirements

2. **Conceptual Design**
   - Space allocation planning
   - Flow pattern optimization
   - Equipment placement
   - Material handling system design

3. **Detailed Design**
   - 3D facility modeling
   - Utility routing (power, compressed air, H2)
   - Safety zone demarcation
   - Accessibility planning

4. **Validation**
   - Flow simulation
   - Ergonomics assessment
   - Safety review
   - Stakeholder approval

5. **Implementation Planning**
   - Phased construction/modification
   - Equipment installation
   - Commissioning procedures
