## 1. Purpose

This document defines the tooling requirements for manufacturing and assembly operations in the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program.

## 2. Scope

This document addresses:
- Production tooling categories and requirements
- Tool design criteria and standards
- Tooling procurement and qualification
- Tool management and maintenance
- Special tooling for unique aircraft features

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Tooling requirements encompass all tools, fixtures, jigs, and equipment necessary for manufacturing and assembly operations. This includes both standard and specialized tooling for the unique BWB configuration and hydrogen propulsion system integration.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Tooling Categories:**
- **Assembly Jigs and Fixtures:** Major structure alignment and joining
- **Drilling and Fastening Tools:** Automated and manual systems
- **Material Handling Equipment:** Lifting, positioning, and transport
- **Measurement and Inspection Tools:** Metrology equipment
- **Special Process Tools:** Composite layup, H2 system assembly
- **Testing Equipment:** Functional test fixtures

**Design Requirements:**
- Repeatability and accuracy specifications
- Ergonomic design principles
- Safety interlocks and guards
- Calibration and maintenance provisions
- Modular and adaptable design
- Digital integration capability

### 4.3 Methodology

**Tooling Development Process:**

1. **Requirements Definition**
   - Manufacturing process analysis
   - Accuracy and repeatability needs
   - Production rate requirements
   - Safety and ergonomic considerations

2. **Design Phase**
   - Concept development
   - Detailed design
   - Simulation and validation
   - Design for manufacturability

3. **Procurement/Fabrication**
   - Make/buy decision
   - Supplier selection
   - Fabrication oversight
   - Acceptance testing

4. **Qualification**
   - First article inspection
   - Capability studies
   - Process validation
   - Operator training

5. **Lifecycle Management**
   - Preventive maintenance
   - Calibration schedules
   - Performance monitoring
   - Upgrade and modification
