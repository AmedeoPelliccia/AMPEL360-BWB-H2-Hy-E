## 1. Purpose

This document identifies and specifies the infrastructure requirements necessary to support the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production facility.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Utilities and services requirements
- Building infrastructure specifications
- Special infrastructure for hydrogen operations
- IT and communication systems
- Environmental control systems
- Safety and security infrastructure

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Infrastructure needs encompass all building systems, utilities, and support services required to operate a modern aircraft manufacturing facility, with special considerations for hydrogen fuel system assembly and hybrid-electric propulsion integration.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Utilities Infrastructure:**
- **Electrical Power:** High-capacity distribution, backup power systems
- **Compressed Air:** Clean, dry air for tools and processes
- **Water:** Potable and process water systems
- **HVAC:** Climate control for production and clean rooms
- **Lighting:** High-bay LED systems with emergency lighting
- **Hydrogen Infrastructure:** Storage, distribution, and safety systems

**Building Infrastructure:**
- **Structural:** Heavy load capacity for equipment and aircraft
- **Flooring:** Industrial-grade, chemical-resistant
- **Crane Systems:** Overhead and gantry cranes
- **Doors and Access:** Large bay doors for aircraft movement
- **Storage Areas:** Raw materials and finished goods
- **Clean Rooms:** For sensitive electronics and systems

**IT/Communications:**
- **Network Infrastructure:** High-speed wired and wireless
- **Manufacturing Execution System (MES):** Production tracking
- **Digital Work Instructions:** Tablets and displays
- **Security Systems:** Access control and surveillance
- **Communication Systems:** Voice and data

### 4.3 Methodology

**Infrastructure Planning Process:**

1. **Needs Assessment**
   - Production requirements analysis
   - Process energy consumption
   - Special requirements (H2 safety)
   - Growth and flexibility considerations

2. **Design Specifications**
   - Capacity calculations
   - Redundancy requirements
   - Safety and code compliance
   - Energy efficiency targets

3. **Implementation Planning**
   - Phased construction approach
   - Utility coordination
   - Commissioning procedures
   - Performance verification

4. **Operations and Maintenance**
   - Preventive maintenance programs
   - Monitoring and control systems
   - Emergency response procedures
   - Continuous improvement
