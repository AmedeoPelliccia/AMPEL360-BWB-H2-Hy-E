# 03-00-09-01-03A - GSE Production Roadmap

**Version:** A  
**Date:** 2025-12-07  
**Status:** DRAFT

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic roadmap for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) production in support of the AMPEL360 BWB H2 Hy-E aircraft program. It establishes key milestones, production phases, and timeline for GSE industrialization from initial production through full-scale manufacturing.

---

## 2. Scope

This roadmap covers:
- GSE production phases and milestones
- Technology readiness progression
- Production capacity development
- Quality gate requirements
- Resource deployment timeline
- Risk management and contingency plans

---

## 3. Applicable Documents

- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- SAE ARP1796 (GSE Design Requirements)
- AMPEL360 Aircraft Production Schedule
- Reference: 03-00-09-01-01A (GSE Production Philosophy)
- Reference: 03-00-09-01-04A (GSE Capacity Planning)

## 4. Production Roadmap Overview

### 4.1 Strategic Phases

| Phase | Period | Focus | Key Deliverables |
|-------|--------|-------|------------------|
| Phase 0: Foundation | 2024-2025 | Design freeze, supplier qualification | Production-ready designs |
| Phase 1: Pilot Production | 2025-2026 | Low-rate initial production | Prototype GSE sets |
| Phase 2: Production Ramp | 2026-2027 | Capacity expansion, process optimization | Pre-EIS GSE fleet |
| Phase 3: Full Production | 2027-2028 | High-rate production | EIS support |
| Phase 4: Sustaining Production | 2029+ | Steady-state production | Lifecycle support |

### 4.2 Production Philosophy

- Risk-managed approach with progressive gate reviews
- Parallel development of conventional and H2-specific GSE
- Front-loading of safety-critical equipment production
- Continuous improvement and lessons learned integration

---

## 5. Detailed Phase Planning

### 5.1 Phase 0: Foundation (2024-2025)

#### Objectives
- Finalize GSE designs and specifications
- Complete supplier qualification
- Establish production facilities
- Develop production processes and tooling

#### Key Milestones

| Milestone | Target Date | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| GSE Design Freeze | Q2 2025 | Approved design packages |
| Production Readiness Review | Q3 2025 | PRR completed |
| Facility Setup Complete | Q3 2025 | Operational facilities |
| Supplier Qualification Complete | Q4 2025 | Qualified supplier base |
| Tooling and Equipment Ready | Q4 2025 | Production tooling |

#### Critical Success Factors
- Timely design maturation and freeze
- Successful qualification of H2 equipment suppliers
- Facility readiness for cryogenic operations
- Workforce training completion

---

### 5.2 Phase 1: Pilot Production (2025-2026)

#### Objectives
- Produce first article GSE units
- Validate production processes
- Conduct first article inspections
- Build initial GSE fleet for flight test program

#### Key Milestones

| Milestone | Target Date | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| First LH2 Fueling Unit | Q1 2026 | FAI completed |
| First Cryogenic Storage GSE | Q2 2026 | FAI completed |
| First Ground Power Unit | Q2 2026 | FAI completed |
| Test Program GSE Set Complete | Q3 2026 | 5 complete GSE sets |
| Production Process Validation | Q4 2026 | Validated processes |

#### Production Targets
- 2-3 complete GSE sets for flight test program
- 5-10 units of critical H2 equipment
- Conventional GSE: 15-20 units

#### Quality Gates
- First Article Inspection (FAI) for all critical GSE
- Production process validation
- Supplier quality audits
- Safety certification for H2 equipment

---

### 5.3 Phase 2: Production Ramp (2026-2027)

#### Objectives
- Scale production capacity
- Optimize manufacturing processes
- Build GSE fleet for EIS preparation
- Establish global support infrastructure

#### Key Milestones

| Milestone | Target Date | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Production Rate: 2 sets/month | Q1 2027 | Capacity achieved |
| EIS GSE Fleet 50% Complete | Q2 2027 | 10 GSE sets |
| Training GSE Sets Delivered | Q3 2027 | 3 training sets |
| Production Rate: 4 sets/month | Q4 2027 | Capacity achieved |
| EIS GSE Fleet Complete | Q4 2027 | 20 GSE sets |

#### Production Targets
- 20 complete GSE sets for initial airline customers
- 50+ LH2 fueling units
- 30+ ground power units
- 100+ conventional GSE items

#### Process Improvements
- Manufacturing cycle time reduction: 30%
- First-pass yield improvement: 95%+
- Supplier lead time reduction: 20%

---

### 5.4 Phase 3: Full Production (2027-2028)

#### Objectives
- Achieve sustained production rate
- Support aircraft EIS and delivery schedule
- Expand global GSE availability
- Implement continuous improvement programs

#### Key Milestones

| Milestone | Target Date | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Aircraft EIS | Q1 2028 | GSE support ready |
| Production Rate: 6 sets/month | Q2 2028 | Capacity achieved |
| Global Support Centers Equipped | Q3 2028 | Worldwide support |
| Production Rate: 8 sets/month | Q4 2028 | Capacity achieved |

#### Production Targets
- 8 complete GSE sets per month
- Cumulative: 100+ GSE sets by end of phase
- Support 50+ aircraft in operation

---

### 5.5 Phase 4: Sustaining Production (2029+)

#### Objectives
- Maintain steady-state production
- Support fleet growth
- Lifecycle upgrades and modifications
- Technology refresh programs

#### Strategy
- Demand-driven production based on aircraft deliveries
- Aftermarket support and spare parts
- Obsolescence management
- Technology insertion opportunities

## 8. Risk Management

### 8.1 Key Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|------|--------|------------|-------|
| H2 supplier delays | High | Multiple sources, early engagement | Procurement |
| Design changes post-freeze | Medium | Change control process, margin | Engineering |
| Workforce shortage | High | Training programs, recruitment | HR |
| Cryogenic testing delays | Medium | External test facilities, schedule buffer | Operations |
| Cost overruns | Medium | Rigorous cost control, make-buy optimization | Finance |

### 8.2 Contingency Plans

- Alternative supplier qualification for critical items
- Schedule buffers at major milestones (10-15%)
- Temporary capacity solutions (contract manufacturers)
- Technology fallback options for high-risk systems

---

## 9. Quality Gates

### 9.1 Phase Gates

Each phase requires gate review and approval:
- Design Review (DR)
- Production Readiness Review (PRR)
- First Article Inspection (FAI)
- Production Validation Review (PVR)
- Operational Readiness Review (ORR)

### 9.2 Gate Criteria

- Technical maturity and readiness
- Quality system compliance
- Safety assessment completion
- Cost and schedule performance
- Risk mitigation effectiveness
