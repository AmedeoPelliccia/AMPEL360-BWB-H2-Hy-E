## 1. Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive analysis of make-or-buy decisions for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) production. It establishes the criteria, methodology, and strategic framework for determining which GSE components should be manufactured in-house versus procured from external suppliers.

---

## 2. Scope

This analysis covers:
- Decision criteria and evaluation methodology
- Strategic make-buy recommendations by GSE category
- Risk assessment for make and buy options
- Cost-benefit analysis framework
- Supplier qualification requirements
- Long-term sourcing strategy

## 4. Make-Buy Decision Framework

### 4.1 Decision Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Make Favored If | Buy Favored If |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Strategic Importance | 25% | Critical to competitive advantage | Commodity item |
| Technical Capability | 20% | Core competency exists | Specialized expertise needed |
| Cost | 20% | Lower total cost | Supplier economies of scale |
| Quality Control | 15% | Tight tolerance/critical | Standard quality acceptable |
| IP Protection | 10% | Proprietary technology | Standard technology |
| Capacity/Resources | 10% | Capacity available | Capacity constrained |

### 4.2 Evaluation Process

1. **Categorize GSE item** - Define category and criticality
2. **Assess capabilities** - Internal vs. external capabilities
3. **Cost analysis** - Total cost of ownership comparison
4. **Risk evaluation** - Supply chain and quality risks
5. **Strategic alignment** - Fit with long-term strategy
6. **Recommendation** - Make, buy, or strategic partnership

## 6. Risk Assessment

### 6.1 Make Risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| Capacity constraints | Medium | Phased capacity expansion |
| Technology gaps | Medium | Partner/license technology |
| Capital investment | High | Phased investment plan |
| Quality issues | Low | Robust QMS implementation |
| Schedule delays | Medium | Risk management planning |

### 6.2 Buy Risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| Supplier failure | High | Multiple source strategy |
| Quality control | Medium | Rigorous supplier audits |
| IP exposure | Medium | Strong NDAs and contracts |
| Supply chain disruption | High | Strategic inventory |
| Cost volatility | Medium | Long-term agreements |

## 7. Cost Analysis Framework

### 7.1 Make Cost Components

- Direct material costs
- Direct labor costs
- Manufacturing overhead
- Equipment and tooling
- Quality assurance
- Facility costs
- Engineering support
- Inventory carrying costs

### 7.2 Buy Cost Components

- Unit purchase price
- Transportation and logistics
- Incoming inspection
- Supplier quality management
- Inventory costs
- Supplier development
- Contract management
- Potential supply disruption costs

### 7.3 Total Cost of Ownership

TCO = Direct Costs + Indirect Costs + Risk Costs + Opportunity Costs

## 8. Strategic Sourcing Approach

### 8.1 Supplier Tiers

**Tier 1: Strategic Partners**
- Critical/complex GSE systems
- Long-term collaboration
- Joint development programs
- Examples: Cryogenic systems, advanced H2 equipment

**Tier 2: Preferred Suppliers**
- Important but less critical components
- Multi-year agreements
- Regular performance reviews
- Examples: Standard safety equipment, quality instruments

**Tier 3: Commodity Suppliers**
- Standard/commodity items
- Competitive bidding
- Multiple sources
- Examples: Hardware, standard tools, consumables

### 8.2 Supplier Qualification

Requirements for GSE suppliers:
- Quality management certification (ISO 9001 or equivalent)
- Industry-specific certifications (aviation GSE experience)
- H2 equipment suppliers: specific hydrogen safety training
- Financial stability and business continuity
- Technical capability assessment
- Audit and inspection rights

## 9. Implementation Roadmap

| Item | Decision | Action |
|------|----------|--------|

| Cryogenic systems | Strategic Partner | Select and qualify partners |

### 9.2 Phase 2: Production Ramp (2027-2028)

- Expand in-house capacity for high-volume items
- Optimize supplier portfolio
- Evaluate make-buy reassessments

### 9.3 Phase 3: Mature Production (2029+)

- Continuous improvement of make-buy decisions
- Strategic insourcing/outsourcing based on business conditions
- Global sourcing optimization
