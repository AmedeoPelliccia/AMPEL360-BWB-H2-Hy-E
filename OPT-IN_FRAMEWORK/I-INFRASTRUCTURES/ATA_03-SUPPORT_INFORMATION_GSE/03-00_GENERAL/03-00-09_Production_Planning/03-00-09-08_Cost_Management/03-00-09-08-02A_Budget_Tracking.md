## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the budget tracking system for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, monitoring actual costs against budget and providing variance analysis.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Budget development and approval process
- Cost collection and allocation
- Budget vs. actual tracking
- Variance analysis and reporting
- Forecast-at-completion (FAC) updates
- Corrective action for budget overruns

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Budget tracking monitors production costs in real-time, compares actual costs to budget, identifies variances, and provides visibility to enable proactive cost management. Effective tracking supports financial control and program success.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Budget Tracking Objectives:**
- Accurate and timely cost data
- Clear visibility to cost performance
- Early identification of cost issues
- Informed decision-making
- Accountability and ownership
- Continuous cost improvement

**Tracking Elements:**
- Budget baseline (authorized budget)
- Actual costs (incurred to date)
- Committed costs (purchase orders, contracts)
- Variance (budget - actual)
- Forecast at completion (FAC)
- Estimate to complete (ETC)

### 4.3 Methodology

**Budget Tracking Framework:**

1. **Budget Development**
   - Cost estimate as basis
   - Risk and contingency allocation
   - Management reserve
   - Budget phasing by period
   - Budget approval and baseline

2. **Cost Collection**
   - Time collection (labor hours)
   - Material issues and receipts
   - Purchase order accruals
   - Overhead allocation
   - Indirect cost allocation
   - Integration with ERP/accounting

3. **Budget Tracking**
   - Period closing (weekly/monthly)
   - Actual cost summarization
   - Budget comparison
   - Variance calculation
   - Variance categorization (favorable/unfavorable)
   - Trend analysis

4. **Earned Value Management (EVM)**
   - Planned Value (PV): Budgeted work scheduled
   - Earned Value (EV): Budgeted work performed
   - Actual Cost (AC): Cost of work performed
   - Cost Performance Index (CPI = EV/AC)
   - Schedule Performance Index (SPI = EV/PV)

5. **Variance Analysis**
   - Variance identification
   - Root cause determination
   - Impact assessment
   - Corrective action planning
   - Responsibility assignment
   - Follow-up and closure

6. **Forecasting**
   - Estimate at completion (EAC) methods
   - Estimate to complete (ETC)
   - Variance at completion (VAC)
   - To-complete performance index (TCPI)
   - Risk-adjusted forecasts

7. **Reporting**
   - Weekly cost flash reports
   - Monthly cost performance reports
   - Quarterly business reviews
   - Executive dashboards
   - Exception reporting
