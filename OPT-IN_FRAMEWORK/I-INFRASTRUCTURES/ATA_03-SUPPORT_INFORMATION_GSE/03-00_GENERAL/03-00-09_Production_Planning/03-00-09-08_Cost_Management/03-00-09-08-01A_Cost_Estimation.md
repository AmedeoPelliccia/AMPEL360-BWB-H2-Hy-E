## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Cost estimation provides predicted costs for aircraft production, supporting program planning, pricing, make/buy decisions, and financial management. Accurate estimates enable informed business decisions and effective cost control.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Cost Estimation Objectives:**
- Accurate and defensible cost estimates
- Consistent methodology application
- Timely estimate updates
- Risk and uncertainty quantification
- Support for various decision contexts
- Continuous improvement of estimating accuracy

**Cost Categories:**
- **Direct Materials:** Raw materials, purchased components
- **Direct Labor:** Touch labor, assembly, fabrication
- **Indirect Costs:** Supervision, quality, material handling
- **Tooling and Equipment:** Non-recurring and recurring costs
- **Facilities:** Depreciation, utilities, maintenance
- **Overhead:** G&A, engineering support, IT

### 4.3 Methodology

**Cost Estimation Framework:**

1. **Estimation Approach Selection**
   - **Analogous Estimating:** Similar past programs
   - **Parametric Estimating:** Cost estimating relationships (CERs)
   - **Bottoms-Up Estimating:** Detailed element-by-element
   - **Hybrid Approach:** Combination of methods
   - Approach based on information availability and accuracy needs

2. **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)**
   - Hierarchical cost structure
   - Aircraft level → Assembly level → Component level
   - Alignment with production breakdown
   - Traceability to requirements

3. **Material Cost Estimation**
   - Bill of materials (BOM) analysis
   - Material pricing (quoted, historical, indexed)
   - Scrap and yield factors
   - Supplier quotes and market data
   - Material overhead factors

4. **Labor Cost Estimation**
   - Work content analysis (standard hours)
   - Learning curve application
   - Labor rates by skill category
   - Production efficiency factors
   - Indirect labor allocation

5. **Overhead and Burden**
   - Overhead rate development
   - Cost pool allocation methods
   - Activity-based costing (ABC)
   - Burden rate application
   - Period cost allocation

6. **Risk and Contingency**
   - Cost risk identification
   - Probabilistic analysis (Monte Carlo)
   - Contingency allocation
   - Reserve management
   - Risk mitigation cost

7. **Estimate Documentation**
   - Assumptions and basis of estimate
   - Data sources and references
   - Calculation methods
   - Confidence level and range
   - Review and approval
