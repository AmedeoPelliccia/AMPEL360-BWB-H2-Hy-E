## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the Quality Control Plan for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft production program, defining inspection and testing activities to ensure product conformance.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Quality control strategy and approach
- Inspection and test planning
- Quality checkpoints throughout production
- Sampling plans and acceptance criteria
- Quality documentation and records
- Nonconformance management integration

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The Quality Control Plan defines all inspection, testing, and verification activities performed during production to ensure aircraft meet design specifications and regulatory requirements. The plan integrates quality checkpoints throughout the manufacturing process.

### 4.2 Requirements

**Quality Control Objectives:**
- 100% conformance to specifications
- Zero-defect manufacturing approach
- Early defect detection and correction
- Traceability of all quality activities
- Continuous improvement focus
- Regulatory compliance

**Quality Control Elements:**
- Receiving inspection
- In-process inspection
- Final inspection
- Functional testing
- Non-destructive testing
- Documentation review

### 4.3 Methodology

**Quality Control Framework:**

1. **Quality Planning**
   - Process FMEA review
   - Critical characteristic identification
   - Inspection method selection
   - Sampling plan development
   - Acceptance criteria definition

2. **Inspection Planning**
   - Inspection point identification
   - Hold point determination
   - Witness point definition
   - Inspection instructions
   - Gage and fixture requirements

3. **Quality Execution**
   - Incoming material inspection
   - In-process verification
   - Assembly verification
   - Final inspection and test
   - Customer acceptance activities

4. **Quality Documentation**
   - Inspection reports
   - Test results
   - Certificate of conformance
   - Material certifications
   - Discrepancy reports

5. **Quality Metrics and Improvement**
   - Key quality indicators
   - Trend analysis
   - Root cause analysis
   - Corrective actions
   - Preventive actions
