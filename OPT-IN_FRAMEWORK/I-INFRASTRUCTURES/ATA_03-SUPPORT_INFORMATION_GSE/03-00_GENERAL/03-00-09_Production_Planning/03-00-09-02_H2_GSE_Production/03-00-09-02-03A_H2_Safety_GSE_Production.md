## 1. Purpose

This document defines the production requirements for hydrogen safety-related Ground Support Equipment, including detection, monitoring, firefighting, and emergency response systems.

---

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Hydrogen gas detection systems
- Fire suppression equipment
- Emergency shutdown systems
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Ventilation and purge systems
- Safety monitoring and alarm systems

## 4. Production Planning Requirements

### 4.1 H2 Safety GSE Overview

Safety GSE is essential for protecting personnel and equipment during hydrogen ground operations.

### 4.2 Production Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement | Target |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| H2 Detection Sensitivity | 0.1% to 4% by volume | < 0.05% alarm |
| Response Time | < 2 seconds | < 1 second |
| Fire Suppression Capacity | Per facility size | NFPA compliant |
| System Availability | 99.9% | 24/7 operational |
| Calibration Frequency | Monthly | Automated check |
