## 4. Production Planning Requirements

### 4.1 Supplier Qualification Overview

H2 GSE suppliers must demonstrate specialized capabilities in hydrogen technology and safety.

### 4.2 Qualification Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement | Verification |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| H2 Experience | 5+ years in H2 equipment | Reference checks |
| Quality System | ISO 9001 certified | Audit |
| H2 Safety Training | All staff trained | Training records |
| Testing Capability | H2-compatible test facilities | Site visit |
| Financial Stability | Credit rating | Financial review |
