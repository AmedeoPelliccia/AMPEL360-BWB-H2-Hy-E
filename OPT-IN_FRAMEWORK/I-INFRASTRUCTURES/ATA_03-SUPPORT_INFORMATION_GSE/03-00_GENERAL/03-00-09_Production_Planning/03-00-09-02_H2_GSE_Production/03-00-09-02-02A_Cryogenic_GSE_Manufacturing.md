## 1. Purpose

This document defines the manufacturing processes and requirements for cryogenic Ground Support Equipment operating at extreme low temperatures (-253°C for LH2 service).

---

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- Cryogenic storage containers and dewars
- Vacuum-insulated transfer lines
- Cryogenic pumps and compressors
- Cold box assemblies
- Insulation systems and vapor barriers
- Pressure relief and safety systems

## 4. Production Planning Requirements

### 4.1 Cryogenic Manufacturing Overview

Cryogenic GSE requires specialized manufacturing capabilities to handle extreme temperature differentials and maintain thermal efficiency.

### 4.2 Manufacturing Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement | Target |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Vacuum Level (insulation) | < 1×10⁻⁵ torr | Best achievable |
| Heat Leak | < 5 W/m (transfer line) | Minimize |
| Insulation Performance | < 1% boil-off/day (storage) | Target |
| Weld Quality | 100% RT for pressure | Zero defects |
| Surface Finish (interior) | Ra < 1.0 µm | Smooth |
