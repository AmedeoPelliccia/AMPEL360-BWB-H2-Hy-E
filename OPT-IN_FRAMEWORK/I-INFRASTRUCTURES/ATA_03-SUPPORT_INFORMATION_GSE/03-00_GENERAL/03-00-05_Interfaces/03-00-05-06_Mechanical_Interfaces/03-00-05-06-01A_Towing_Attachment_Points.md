# 03-00-05-06-01A - Towing Attachment Points

## 1. Purpose
Specifies towing attachment point locations and requirements for AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft ground movement.

## 2. Scope
Covers towing point design, load limits, and towing procedures for safe aircraft ground handling.

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200, SAE AS8080 (Aircraft Ground Support Equipment), ISO 15095 (Aircraft Tow Bars)

## 4. Interface Description

### 4.1 Overview
Nose landing gear towing attachment enables safe pushback and towing operations using certified ground equipment.

### 4.2 Physical Characteristics
| Parameter | Specification | Tolerance |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Towing Point Location | Nose landing gear | Centered |
| Connection Type | Lug fitting, 2-inch pin | Hardened steel |
| Maximum Tow Force | 50,000 lbs (222 kN) | Straight tow |
| Maximum Turn Angle | ±60 degrees | From centerline |
| Pin Diameter | 2.0 inches (50.8mm) | ±0.5mm |
| Load Rating | 3:1 safety factor | Ultimate strength |

### 4.3 Connection Procedure
Standard tow bar attachment, steering verification, and towing procedures per ground handling manual.

## 5. GSE Equipment Requirements
Tow bar (adjustable), tow tractor (50+ ton capacity), communication equipment.

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Verify aircraft weight within tow limits
- Check towing equipment certification
- Maintain communication during towing
- H2 aircraft: enhanced fire watch during movement
