# 03-00-05-06-04A - Ground Handling Interfaces

## 1. Purpose
Specifies general ground handling interfaces and procedures for AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft operations.

## 2. Scope
Covers wheel chocks, tow bars, ground power, stairs, and general ground handling equipment interfaces.

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200, IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM), SAE AS8080

## 4. Interface Description

### 4.1 Overview
Comprehensive ground handling interfaces enable safe aircraft servicing, maintenance, and passenger operations.

### 4.2 Physical Characteristics
| Parameter | Specification | Tolerance |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Main Wheel Chocks | Height: 450mm, Width: 600mm | Per landing gear |
| Passenger Stairs | Mobile, height-adjustable 2-6m | Door sill compatible |
| Service Platforms | Mobile, multiple heights | Modular design |
| Safety Zones | 30m radius around aircraft | During H2 operations |

### 4.3 Connection Procedure
Standard ground handling setup, safety zone establishment, equipment positioning procedures.

## 5. GSE Equipment Requirements
Wheel chocks (6), passenger stairs (2), work platforms, safety cones/barriers.

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Verify aircraft secured before operations
- Establish safety zones per operations
- Brief all ground crew on procedures
- H2 aircraft: enhanced safety protocols active
