# 03-00-05-06-03A - Mooring Points

## 1. Purpose
Specifies mooring point locations and requirements for securing AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft during ground parking and storage.

## 2. Scope
Covers mooring point design, tie-down procedures, and wind load requirements for aircraft security.

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200, SAE ARP5150 (Aircraft Ground Safety), MIL-STD-976 (Tie-Down and Mooring)

## 4. Interface Description

### 4.1 Overview
Twelve mooring points provide secure tie-down capability for outdoor parking and high-wind conditions.

### 4.2 Physical Characteristics
| Parameter | Specification | Tolerance |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Mooring Points | 12 total (wing and fuselage) | Symmetric |
| Load Capacity | 20,000 lbs (89 kN) per point | Tie-down tension |
| Connection Type | Forged steel ring, 3-inch ID | Hinged |
| Wind Load Rating | 65 knots (120 km/h) | All points engaged |
| Tie-Down Angle | 45 degrees ±10 degrees | From horizontal |

### 4.3 Connection Procedure
Mooring equipment inspection, sequential tie-down installation, tension verification procedures.

## 5. GSE Equipment Requirements
Tie-down chains/straps (12), ground anchors, tension gauges, chocks.

## 6. Safety Requirements
- All mooring points engaged for high winds
- Regular inspection of mooring equipment
- Proper tie-down angle critical
- H2 aircraft: ensure ventilation not blocked
