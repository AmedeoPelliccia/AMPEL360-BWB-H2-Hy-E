# 03-00-05-06-02A - Jacking Points

## 1. Purpose
Specifies jacking point locations and procedures for lifting AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft during maintenance operations.

## 2. Scope
Covers jacking point design, load capacity, and safety procedures for aircraft jacking operations.

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200, SAE AS1055 (Hydraulic System), Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM) Chapter 07

## 4. Interface Description

### 4.1 Overview
Eight primary jacking points enable safe aircraft lifting for landing gear maintenance, weighing, and inspections.

### 4.2 Physical Characteristics
| Parameter | Specification | Tolerance |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Jacking Points | 8 total (4 forward, 4 aft) | Symmetric arrangement |
| Load Capacity | 100,000 lbs (445 kN) per point | Maximum |
| Jack Pad Size | 150mm x 150mm | Contact area |
| Jack Pad Material | Aluminum, cushioned | Non-slip surface |
| Maximum Jack Height | 1.2m | Ground to jack point |
| Aircraft Level Tolerance | ±1 degree | During jacking |

### 4.3 Connection Procedure
Pre-jacking checks, synchronized jacking sequence, aircraft leveling, and safety support procedures.

## 5. GSE Equipment Requirements
Hydraulic jacks (8 required), jack adapters, level indicator, safety stands.

## 6. Safety Requirements
- All personnel clear during jacking
- Synchronized jacking sequence mandatory
- Install safety stands immediately
- H2 aircraft: verify fuel tanks stable, no transfer
