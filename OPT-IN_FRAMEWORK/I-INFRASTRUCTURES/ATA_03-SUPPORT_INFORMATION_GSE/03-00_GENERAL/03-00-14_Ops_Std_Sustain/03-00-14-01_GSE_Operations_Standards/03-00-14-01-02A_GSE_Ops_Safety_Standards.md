# 03-00-14-01-02A — GSE Operations Safety Standards

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes **safety standards** for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) operations supporting the AMPEL360 BWB H₂ Hy-E aircraft. It defines safety requirements, hazard controls, personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements, and safety management processes to ensure zero-harm GSE operations.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Coverage

This document covers safety standards for:

1. **Personnel Safety**
   - PPE requirements
   - Training and qualification
   - Fatigue management
   - Health and fitness standards

2. **Operational Safety**
   - Safety zones and clearances
   - Equipment operation limits
   - Weather restrictions
   - Communication protocols

3. **Equipment Safety**
   - Safety device requirements
   - Inspection and maintenance
   - Emergency shutdown systems
   - Fire protection systems

4. **Environmental Safety**
   - Spill prevention and containment
   - Emissions control
   - Noise management
   - Wildlife hazard management

### 2.2 Exclusions

- Aircraft-specific safety procedures (covered under respective ATA chapters)
- Hydrogen-specific safety (detailed in [03-00-14-02](../03-00-14-02_H2_GSE_Operations_Standards/))
- Emergency response plans (covered under airport emergency procedures)

## 4. Operations/Sustainment Requirements

### 4.1 Safety Management Overview

GSE operations safety is based on a comprehensive Safety Management System (SMS) with the following principles:

- **Risk-Based Approach**: Prioritize controls based on risk assessment
- **Layered Protection**: Multiple independent safety barriers
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Real-time safety performance tracking
- **Learning Culture**: Proactive learning from incidents and near-misses
- **Compliance Assurance**: Verification of regulatory and standard compliance

### 4.2 Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

#### 4.2.1 Hazard Categories

| Hazard Category | Examples | Risk Level |
|----------------|----------|------------|
| **Collision** | Aircraft contact, equipment collision, vehicle accidents | High |
| **Crushing** | Equipment pinch points, falling objects | High |
| **Electrical** | Electrocution, electrical burns | High |
| **Fire/Explosion** | Fuel fires, electrical fires, battery fires | Critical |
| **Falls** | Falls from height, slips, trips | Medium |
| **Ergonomic** | Repetitive strain, lifting injuries | Medium |
| **Environmental** | Extreme temperatures, weather hazards | Medium |
| **Chemical** | Fuel exposure, hydraulic fluid exposure | Medium |

#### 4.2.2 Risk Assessment Matrix

| Severity ↓ / Likelihood → | Rare | Unlikely | Possible | Likely | Almost Certain |
|---------------------------|------|----------|----------|--------|----------------|
| **Catastrophic** | High | High | Critical | Critical | Critical |
| **Major** | Medium | High | High | Critical | Critical |
| **Moderate** | Low | Medium | High | High | Critical |
| **Minor** | Low | Low | Medium | Medium | High |
| **Negligible** | Low | Low | Low | Medium | Medium |

**Risk Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Critical**: Unacceptable — Immediate action required
- **High**: Requires senior management approval and mitigation plan
- **Medium**: Requires mitigation with timeline
- **Low**: Acceptable with monitoring

### 4.3 Safety Zones and Clearances

#### 4.3.1 Safety Zone Requirements

| Zone Type | Description | Minimum Clearance | Access Control |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Aircraft Safety Zone** | Area around aircraft during GSE operations | 3 meters | Authorized personnel only |
| **Equipment Operation Zone** | Area of GSE operation | 5 meters | Operators and essential personnel |
| **Exclusion Zone** | High-hazard areas (LH₂ fueling) | 15 meters | Qualified personnel with PPE only |
| **Pedestrian Safety Zone** | Walkways and safe access paths | 2 meters from equipment | All personnel with high-visibility vests |

#### 4.3.2 Visual Safety Markings

All safety zones must be:
- **Clearly marked**: High-visibility tape, cones, or barriers
- **Well-lit**: Adequate lighting during night operations
- **Monitored**: Visual surveillance or dedicated safety observer
- **Enforced**: Access control procedures in place

### 4.4 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

#### 4.4.1 Mandatory PPE Requirements

| Operation Type | Required PPE |
|----------------|--------------|
| **All GSE Operations** | - High-visibility vest<br>- Safety shoes (steel toe)<br>- Hard hat (in designated areas)<br>- Hearing protection (≥85 dBA) |
| **LH₂ Fueling** | - Cryogenic gloves<br>- Face shield<br>- Insulated coveralls<br>- Safety goggles |
| **Electrical Work** | - Insulated gloves (rated for voltage)<br>- Arc flash protection<br>- Dielectric footwear |
| **Platform Work (>2m)** | - Fall arrest harness<br>- Lanyard with shock absorber<br>- Helmet with chin strap |
| **Chemical Handling** | - Chemical-resistant gloves<br>- Splash goggles<br>- Apron (if applicable) |

#### 4.4.2 PPE Inspection and Maintenance

- **Daily Inspection**: Visual check before each shift
- **Functional Testing**: Monthly for fall protection equipment
- **Replacement Criteria**: Damage, wear, or expiration date
- **Storage**: Clean, dry location away from sunlight and chemicals

### 4.5 Equipment Safety Standards

#### 4.5.1 Safety Device Requirements

All GSE must be equipped with:

| Safety Device | Function | Test Frequency |
|---------------|----------|----------------|
| **Emergency Stop (E-Stop)** | Immediate equipment shutdown | Daily |
| **Horn/Warning Alarm** | Alert nearby personnel | Daily |
| **Reverse Alarm** | Alert during reverse operation | Daily |
| **Fire Extinguisher** | Onboard fire suppression | Monthly |
| **First Aid Kit** | Emergency medical supplies | Monthly |
| **Lighting** | Operation in low-light conditions | Daily |
| **Deadman Switch** | Operator presence detection | Weekly |

#### 4.5.2 Inspection Requirements

| Inspection Type | Frequency | Performed By | Documentation |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Pre-Operation** | Before each use | Operator | Daily checklist |
| **Shift Inspection** | Start of shift | Supervisor | Shift log |
| **Weekly Inspection** | Weekly | Maintenance technician | Inspection report |
| **Monthly Inspection** | Monthly | Certified inspector | Inspection certificate |
| **Annual Certification** | Annually | Third-party inspector | Certification report |

### 4.6 Operational Limitations

#### 4.6.1 Weather Restrictions

| Weather Condition | Limit | Action |
|-------------------|-------|--------|
| **Wind Speed** | >40 km/h (25 mph) | Suspend elevated platform operations |
| **Wind Speed** | >60 km/h (37 mph) | Suspend all GSE operations |
| **Visibility** | <500 meters | Enhanced lighting and supervision required |
| **Lightning** | Within 8 km (5 miles) | Suspend all outdoor operations |
| **Temperature** | <-20°C or >45°C | Evaluate equipment operating limits |
| **Precipitation** | Heavy rain/snow | Evaluate slip hazards, suspend if unsafe |

#### 4.6.2 Equipment Operating Limits

Equipment must not be operated:
- Beyond manufacturer's rated capacity
- With known defects or malfunctions
- By unqualified or uncertified personnel
- In conditions exceeding environmental limits
- Without required safety devices operational

### 4.7 Communication Protocols

#### 4.7.1 Communication Requirements

| Scenario | Communication Method | Minimum Participants |
|----------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Aircraft Towing** | Two-way radio + hand signals | Tow driver, wing walkers, aircraft crew |
| **LH₂ Fueling** | Dedicated channel radio | Fueling operator, safety observer, airport ops |
| **Platform Operations** | Two-way radio or intercom | Platform operator, ground spotter |
| **Emergency** | Emergency frequency + PA system | All personnel, emergency services |

#### 4.7.2 Standard Phraseology

Standardized communication phrases must be used:
- **"Clear to proceed"**: Authorization to begin operation
- **"Hold position"**: Stop all movement immediately
- **"Emergency stop"**: Immediate shutdown required
- **"All clear"**: Operation completed, area safe
