# 03-00-07-07-04A - Type Certificate Data

## 1. Purpose

This document defines **Type Certificate Data Sheet (TCDS)** content requirements for ATA Chapter 03 GSE systems as part of aircraft type certification.

## 2. Scope

TCDS entries for GSE include:
- Equipment specifications
- Operating limitations
- Required markings
- Certification basis
- Required equipment list

## 3. Applicable Documents

| Document | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| [EASA Part 21](https://www.easa.europa.eu/) | Certification procedures |
| [FAA Part 21](https://www.ecfr.gov/) | Certification procedures |

## 4. Description

### 4.1 TCDS Content for GSE

- Equipment identification and specifications
- Interface requirements and limitations
- Operational limitations and procedures
- Required inspection and maintenance
- Certification notes and exceptions

### 4.2 Requirements

**TCDS-03-07-01**: All GSE specifications shall be documented in TCDS.

**TCDS-03-07-02**: Operating limitations shall be clearly stated.

**TCDS-03-07-03**: Required equipment list shall be maintained.
