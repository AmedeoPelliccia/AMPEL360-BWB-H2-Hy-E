# 03-00-07-07-01A - Compliance Checklists

## 1. Purpose

This document provides comprehensive **Compliance Checklists** for certification of [ATA Chapter 03](https://www.ata.org/resources/specifications) — Support Information and Ground Support Equipment (GSE). These checklists ensure systematic verification of all regulatory requirements.

## 2. Scope

Compliance checklists cover:
- EASA CS-25 and FAA Part 25 ground service requirements
- ISO 19880-8 hydrogen safety standards
- DO-160G environmental qualification
- Applicable industry standards (SAE, ASTM, etc.)

## 3. Applicable Documents

| Document | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| [EASA CS-25](https://www.easa.europa.eu/) | Large aircraft certification specifications |
| [FAA Part 25](https://www.ecfr.gov/) | Airworthiness standards |
| [ISO 19880-8](https://www.iso.org/) | Gaseous hydrogen fuelling stations |
| [DO-160G](https://www.rtca.org/) | Environmental conditions and test procedures |

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Compliance checklists provide systematic approach to ensure all certification requirements are identified, addressed, and evidenced.

### 4.2 Requirements

**CCL-03-07-01**: All applicable regulatory requirements shall be identified.

**CCL-03-07-02**: Compliance evidence shall be documented for each requirement.

**CCL-03-07-03**: Checklists shall be reviewed and approved by regulatory authority.
