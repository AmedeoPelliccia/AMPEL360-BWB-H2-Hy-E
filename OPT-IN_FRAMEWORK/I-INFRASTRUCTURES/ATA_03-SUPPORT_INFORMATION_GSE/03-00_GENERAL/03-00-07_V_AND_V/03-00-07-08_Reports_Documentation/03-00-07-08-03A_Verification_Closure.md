# 03-00-07-08-03A - Verification Closure

## 1. Purpose

This document defines **Verification Closure** procedures and criteria for completing ATA Chapter 03 GSE verification and validation activities.

## 2. Scope

Verification closure addresses:
- Completion criteria for V&V activities
- Evidence package review
- Outstanding items disposition
- Final approval and sign-off
- Transition to operations

## 3. Applicable Documents

| Document | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| [ARP4754A](https://www.sae.org/standards/content/arp4754a/) | V&V completion requirements |
| [AS9100](https://www.sae.org/standards/content/as9100d/) | Quality closure requirements |

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Closure Criteria

**Requirements Coverage**
- ✓ 100% of requirements verified/validated
- ✓ Verification matrix complete
- ✓ Traceability established

**Testing Completion**
- ✓ All planned tests executed
- ✓ Test reports approved
- ✓ Discrepancies resolved or dispositioned

**Documentation**
- ✓ All required documents complete
- ✓ Evidence packages assembled
- ✓ Configuration records current

**Approvals**
- ✓ Technical reviews complete
- ✓ Quality audits passed
- ✓ Management approval obtained
- ✓ Customer acceptance received

### 4.2 Closure Process

1. **Completeness Review**: Verify all activities completed
2. **Evidence Review**: Confirm evidence adequacy
3. **Open Items Disposition**: Close or formally accept open items
4. **Documentation Review**: Final document review
5. **Approval Process**: Obtain required approvals
6. **Certification Package**: Submit to regulatory authority
7. **Operational Readiness**: Declare ready for operations

### 4.3 Requirements

**VC-03-07-01**: Formal closure review required before operational use.

**VC-03-07-02**: All open items shall be resolved or have approved waivers.

**VC-03-07-03**: Closure shall be documented and approved.
