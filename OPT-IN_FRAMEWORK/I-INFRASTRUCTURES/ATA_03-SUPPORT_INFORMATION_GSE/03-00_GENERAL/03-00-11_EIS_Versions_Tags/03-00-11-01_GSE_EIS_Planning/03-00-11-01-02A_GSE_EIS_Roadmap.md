## 1. Purpose

This document defines the detailed roadmap for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) Entry Into Service, establishing timelines, dependencies, and phasing for GSE deployment in support of AMPEL360 BWB-H2 aircraft operations.

## 2. Scope

This roadmap covers:
- Temporal sequencing of GSE EIS activities
- Critical path analysis
- Resource allocation timelines
- Site-specific deployment schedules
- Integration with aircraft EIS program

## 5. Implementation Plan

### Roadmap Phases

#### Phase A: Initial Capability (Months 0-12)
**Objective:** Deploy minimal viable GSE set for initial aircraft operations

- **Conventional GSE:**
  - Towing tractors
  - Ground power units (GPU)
  - Air conditioning units
  - Cargo loaders
  
- **H2-Specific GSE (Initial):**
  - Basic LH2 refueling equipment (ref: [03-00-11-02-01A](../03-00-11-02_H2_GSE_EIS/03-00-11-02-01A_LH2_Fueling_GSE_EIS.md))
  - Safety and monitoring equipment
  - Basic cryogenic handling tools

- **Sites:** Primary test facility and launch customer base

#### Phase B: Expanded Capability (Months 12-24)
**Objective:** Scale to support initial commercial operations

- **Enhanced GSE:**
  - Advanced H2 refueling systems
  - Automated towing and positioning
  - Integrated maintenance platforms
  - Advanced diagnostics equipment

- **H2 Infrastructure:**
  - Full cryogenic GSE suite (ref: [03-00-11-02-02A](../03-00-11-02_H2_GSE_EIS/03-00-11-02-02A_Cryogenic_GSE_Deployment.md))
  - H2 safety systems (ref: [03-00-11-02-03A](../03-00-11-02_H2_GSE_EIS/03-00-11-02-03A_H2_Safety_GSE_Rollout.md))

- **Sites:** Expanded to 3-5 major hub airports

#### Phase C: Full Operational Capability (Months 24-36)
**Objective:** Achieve full network operational capability

- **Complete GSE Fleet:**
  - Full range of conventional GSE
  - Complete H2 infrastructure
  - Redundancy and spares positioning
  - Mobile and portable equipment sets

- **Network Coverage:**
  - Primary hubs (10+)
  - Secondary airports (20+)
  - Line maintenance stations

#### Phase D: Optimization and Expansion (Months 36+)
**Objective:** Continuous improvement and fleet growth

- Technology refresh programs
- Fleet optimization based on utilization data
- New capability integration
- Obsolescence management

### Critical Path Elements

1. **H2 Certification and Approval:** Longest lead item
2. **Site Infrastructure Readiness:** Critical dependency
3. **GSE Manufacturing and Delivery:** Supply chain management
4. **Personnel Training:** Capability development
5. **Regulatory Approvals:** Airport and civil aviation authority
