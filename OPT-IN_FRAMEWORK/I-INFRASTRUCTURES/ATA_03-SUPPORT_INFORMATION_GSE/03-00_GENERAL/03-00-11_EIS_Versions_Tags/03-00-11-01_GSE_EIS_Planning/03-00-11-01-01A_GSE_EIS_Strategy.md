## 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic approach for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) Entry Into Service (EIS), establishing the framework for deploying GSE assets to operational sites in support of AMPEL360 BWB-H2 aircraft operations.

## 2. Scope

This strategy covers:
- Overall GSE EIS approach and philosophy
- Stakeholder engagement strategy
- Phased deployment methodology
- Risk management framework for GSE EIS
- Success criteria and key performance indicators (KPIs)

## 4. EIS/Versioning Requirements

### 4.1 Overview

The GSE EIS Strategy provides the overarching framework for introducing new and upgraded GSE into operational service. This includes conventional GSE and specialized hydrogen infrastructure equipment required for AMPEL360 BWB-H2 operations.

**Key Strategic Principles:**
- Phased deployment aligned with aircraft EIS timeline
- Risk-based prioritization of critical GSE
- Collaboration with airport authorities and operators
- Continuous improvement through lessons learned
- Safety-first approach for hydrogen GSE
