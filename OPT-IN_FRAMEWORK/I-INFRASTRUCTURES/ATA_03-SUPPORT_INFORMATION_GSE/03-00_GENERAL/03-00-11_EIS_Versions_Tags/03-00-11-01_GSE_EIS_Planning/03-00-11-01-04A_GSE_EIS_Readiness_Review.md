## 1. Purpose

This document defines the readiness review process for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) Entry Into Service, establishing criteria and procedures for assessing preparedness to transition GSE from development to operational service.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:
- EIS readiness review framework
- Assessment criteria and checklists
- Review process and governance
- Decision-making authority
- Corrective action management

## 4. EIS/Versioning Requirements

### 4.1 Overview

The GSE EIS Readiness Review provides a systematic assessment of GSE readiness to enter operational service, ensuring all technical, operational, safety, and regulatory requirements are met before deployment.

**Review Principles:**
- Objective and evidence-based assessment
- Multi-stakeholder participation
- Risk-informed decision making
- Clear go/no-go criteria
- Documented approval trail

## 5. Implementation Plan

### Readiness Review Framework

#### Level 1: Component/Subsystem Readiness Review
**Scope:** Individual GSE items or subsystems

- **Participants:**
  - GSE Engineering
  - Quality Assurance
  - Manufacturing
  - Test/Commissioning

- **Assessment Areas:**
  1. Design compliance and verification
  2. Manufacturing quality and inspection
  3. Testing completion (FAT)
  4. Documentation completeness
  5. Configuration baseline established

- **Deliverables:**
  - Technical data package
  - Test reports
  - Quality records
  - Acceptance certificate

#### Level 2: Site Readiness Review
**Scope:** GSE deployment to specific operational site

- **Participants:**
  - Site Operations
  - GSE Deployment Team
  - Safety & Security
  - Maintenance Organization
  - Airport Authority (where applicable)

- **Assessment Areas:**
  1. Site infrastructure readiness
  2. GSE delivery and installation status
  3. Commissioning test completion (SAT)
  4. Personnel training and qualification
  5. Safety certification and permits
  6. Maintenance support establishment
  7. Operational procedures approval
  8. Spares and consumables provisioning

- **Special Considerations for H2 GSE:**
  - H2 safety systems operational (ref: [03-00-11-02-03A](../03-00-11-02_H2_GSE_EIS/03-00-11-02-03A_H2_Safety_GSE_Rollout.md))
  - Cryogenic equipment readiness (ref: [03-00-11-02-02A](../03-00-11-02_H2_GSE_EIS/03-00-11-02-02A_Cryogenic_GSE_Deployment.md))
  - Emergency response procedures
  - Airport authority H2 approval

- **Deliverables:**
  - Site readiness report
  - Commissioning records (ref: [03-00-11-07-03A](../03-00-11-07_GSE_Deployment_Tracking/03-00-11-07-03A_GSE_Commissioning_Log.md))
  - Training records
  - Safety certifications
  - Operational handover package

#### Level 3: Network/Program Readiness Review
**Scope:** Overall GSE program capability milestones (IOC, FOC)

- **Participants:**
  - GSE Program Director
  - Operations Leadership
  - Maintenance Leadership
  - Safety & Regulatory Affairs
  - Customer Representatives
  - Executive Management

- **Assessment Areas:**
  1. Fleet deployment status and readiness
  2. Network coverage and capability
  3. Operational performance and reliability
  4. Safety record and compliance
  5. Maintenance capability and spares
  6. Training program effectiveness
  7. Financial and commercial readiness
  8. Risk posture and mitigation effectiveness
  9. Continuous improvement process

- **Deliverables:**
  - Program readiness assessment
  - Executive summary for decision
  - Risk assessment and mitigation plan
  - Go/No-Go recommendation
  - Approval and authorization package

### Readiness Assessment Criteria

#### Technical Readiness
- [ ] Design verified and validated
- [ ] Configuration baseline established (ref: [03-00-11-03-02A](../03-00-11-03_GSE_Version_Control/03-00-11-03-02A_GSE_Configuration_Baseline.md))
- [ ] Manufacturing quality assured
- [ ] Testing completed successfully
- [ ] Technical documentation complete

#### Operational Readiness
- [ ] Operational procedures approved
- [ ] Personnel trained and qualified
- [ ] Site infrastructure adequate
- [ ] Support equipment available
- [ ] Communications and IT systems ready

#### Safety and Regulatory Readiness
- [ ] Safety assessments completed
- [ ] Regulatory approvals obtained
- [ ] Emergency procedures established
- [ ] Safety equipment and systems operational
- [ ] Compliance verification complete

#### Maintenance and Support Readiness
- [ ] Maintenance procedures approved
- [ ] Maintenance personnel trained
- [ ] Spares and tools provisioned
- [ ] Maintenance facilities ready
- [ ] Supply chain established

#### Management and Governance Readiness
- [ ] Organizational structure defined
- [ ] Roles and responsibilities clear
- [ ] Performance metrics established
- [ ] Reporting and escalation procedures defined
- [ ] Continuous improvement process active

### Review Process

1. **Preparation Phase (4 weeks before review)**
   - Review package compilation
   - Evidence gathering
   - Pre-review assessments
   - Stakeholder coordination

2. **Review Phase (1-2 days)**
   - Presentation of evidence
   - Criteria assessment
   - Issue identification
   - Discussion and deliberation

3. **Decision Phase (1 week after review)**
   - Review board deliberation
   - Go/No-Go decision
   - Conditional approval (if applicable)
   - Action item assignment

4. **Close-out Phase (ongoing)**
   - Action item execution
   - Verification of corrective actions
   - Final approval (if conditional)
   - Lessons learned capture

### Decision Outcomes

- **GO:** Approved to proceed to next phase/deployment
- **CONDITIONAL GO:** Approved with conditions/actions to be completed
- **NO-GO:** Not approved; return to previous phase with corrective actions
