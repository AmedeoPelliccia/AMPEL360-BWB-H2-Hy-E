# 03-00-12-06-04A - GSE Certification Training

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies certification training programs for Ground Support Equipment personnel, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and industry standards for AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Regulatory certification preparation
- Industry-specific certification programs
- Competency-based certification assessments
- Certification renewal and recurrency
- Multi-level certification pathways

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Certification Training Service provides structured training and assessment programs leading to recognized certifications for ground support equipment personnel, meeting regulatory and operational requirements.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Certification Levels | 3 levels (Basic/Intermediate/Advanced) | Progressive qualification |
| Training Duration | 40-120 hours | Level and specialty dependent |
| Assessment Rigor | Written + Practical + Oral | Comprehensive evaluation |
| Pass Rate Requirement | ≥85% | For certification award |
| Certification Validity | 12-36 months | Depends on certification type |
| Recertification Process | Refresher + Assessment | Streamlined for renewals |
| Industry Recognition | IATA/FAA/EASA recognized | Transferable credentials |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Certification Path Selection**: Identify appropriate certification for role and career goals
2. **Prerequisites Verification**: Confirm education, experience, and prerequisite certifications
3. **Study Materials Provision**: Comprehensive study guides, reference materials
4. **Preparatory Training**: Structured courses covering certification requirements
5. **Practice Assessments**: Mock exams, skill evaluations, feedback
6. **Formal Assessment**: Written exam, practical evaluation, oral board (if required)
7. **Certification Award**: Certificate issuance upon successful completion
8. **Continuing Education**: Ongoing education for certification maintenance

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Safety certifications prerequisite for operational certifications
- Practical assessments conducted in controlled, safe environments
- Supervision by certified evaluators during all practical tests
- Emergency procedures tested as part of certification assessment
- Safety violations during assessment result in automatic failure
- Re-assessment required after safety-related incidents
- Annual verification of medical fitness for safety-critical certifications
