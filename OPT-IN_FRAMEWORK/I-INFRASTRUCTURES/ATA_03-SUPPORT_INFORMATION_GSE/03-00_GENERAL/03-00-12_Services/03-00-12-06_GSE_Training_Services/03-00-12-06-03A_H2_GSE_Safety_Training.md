# 03-00-12-06-03A - H2 GSE Safety Training

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies specialized safety training programs for personnel working with hydrogen Ground Support Equipment, ensuring comprehensive understanding of hydrogen hazards and safe operational practices for AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Hydrogen properties and hazards training
- Cryogenic safety training
- H2 leak detection and emergency response
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) for H2 operations
- Regulatory compliance and certification requirements
- Emergency simulation and drills

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
H2 GSE Safety Training Service provides comprehensive, specialized training on hydrogen safety, covering the unique hazards of hydrogen fuel, cryogenic handling, and emergency response procedures specific to aviation ground operations.

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Prerequisites Verification**: Basic safety certification, medical clearance
2. **Hydrogen Properties**: Physical/chemical properties, flammability limits, behavior
3. **Cryogenic Hazards**: Ultra-low temperature hazards, material embrittlement, frostbite
4. **Detection Systems**: H2 leak detection equipment, monitoring systems, alarm response
5. **Personal Protection**: Cryogenic PPE, self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA)
6. **Operational Procedures**: Safe work practices, exclusion zones, communication protocols
7. **Emergency Response**: Fire response, leak management, evacuation procedures
8. **Practical Drills**: Simulated emergencies, equipment operation, team coordination
9. **Assessment**: Written exam, practical skills test, emergency response evaluation
10. **Certification**: H2 safety certification upon successful completion

## 6. Safety Requirements

### 6.1 Training Prerequisites
- Valid basic safety certification
- Medical clearance for SCBA use
- Physical capability for emergency response
- Background check for sensitive operations
- Minimum age: 21 years

### 6.2 Training Safety Protocols
- Maximum student-to-instructor ratio: 6:1 for practical training
- Emergency services on standby during live drills
- Safety observers assigned for all practical exercises
- Controlled training environment with safety systems active
- Progressive skill development (classroom → simulator → controlled real-world)
- Buddy system mandatory for all H2 training exercises

### 6.3 Certification Requirements
**Level 1 - H2 Awareness (4 hours)**
- Basic hydrogen properties and hazards
- Exclusion zones and safety signage
- Emergency notification procedures
- For all personnel working in H2 areas

**Level 2 - H2 Operations (40 hours)**
- Comprehensive H2 safety training
- Cryogenic handling procedures
- Emergency response procedures
- For operators of H2 GSE

**Level 3 - H2 Specialist (80 hours total)**
- Advanced H2 systems knowledge
- Emergency response team leadership
- Incident investigation
- For refueling operators and safety supervisors
