# 03-00-12-06-02A - GSE Maintenance Training

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies training programs for Ground Support Equipment maintenance technicians, ensuring competent and safe maintenance of all GSE assets supporting AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Maintenance technician qualification training
- Equipment-specific maintenance procedures
- Troubleshooting and diagnostic techniques
- Safety procedures and hazard recognition
- Recurrent training and continuing education

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- Equipment manufacturer maintenance manuals
- OSHA Training Requirements (or local equivalent)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- ATA 03-40 (Training), ATA 03-30 (Maintenance)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Maintenance Training Service provides comprehensive technical training to develop and maintain maintenance technician competency for all ground support equipment, emphasizing safety, quality, and regulatory compliance.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Initial Training Duration | 80-160 hours | Equipment/complexity dependent |
| Practical Training Ratio | 70% hands-on | Emphasis on practical skills |
| Assessment Pass Rate | ≥85% required | For certification |
| Recurrent Training | Annual | Mandatory refresher |
| Specialized Training (H2, HV) | Additional 40-80 hours | Critical systems |
| Instructor Qualification | Certified master technicians | 5+ years experience |
| Training Equipment | Operational GSE | Real equipment or high-fidelity trainers |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Prerequisites Verification**: Technical background, safety certifications, tools knowledge
2. **Classroom Instruction**: System theory, maintenance procedures, safety protocols
3. **Practical Training**: Hands-on maintenance tasks, troubleshooting exercises
4. **Diagnostic Training**: Systematic fault isolation, testing procedures
5. **Documentation Training**: Work order systems, maintenance records
6. **Assessment**: Written exam, practical skills evaluation, safety knowledge verification
7. **Certification**: Maintenance technician certification upon successful completion
8. **Continuing Education**: Updates for new equipment, procedures, regulations

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Safety certification prerequisite for maintenance training
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) mandatory for all practical training
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures emphasized and practiced
- Electrical safety training for high-voltage systems
- Cryogenic safety training for H2 systems maintenance
- Confined space entry training where applicable
- Fall protection training for elevated work
- Hazardous materials handling per safety data sheets
