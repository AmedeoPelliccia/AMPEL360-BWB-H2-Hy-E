# 03-00-12-06-01A - GSE Operator Training

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies training programs for Ground Support Equipment operators, ensuring safe and proficient operation of all GSE assets supporting AMPEL360 aircraft.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Initial operator qualification training
- Equipment-specific operational training
- Safety procedures and emergency response
- Practical hands-on training and assessment
- Recurrent training and skill maintenance

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- OSHA Training Requirements (or local equivalent)
- Equipment manufacturer operator manuals
- ATA 03-40 (Training)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Operator Training Service provides comprehensive training programs to develop and maintain operator competency for all ground support equipment, emphasizing safety, efficiency, and regulatory compliance.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Initial Training Duration | 40-80 hours | Equipment-dependent |
| Practical Training Ratio | 60% hands-on | Minimum practical time |
| Assessment Pass Rate | ≥80% required | For certification |
| Recurrent Training | Annual | Mandatory refresher |
| Instructor Qualification | Certified instructors | Subject matter experts |
| Training Capacity | 20 students/class | Maximum class size |
| Training Materials | Current and complete | Updated annually |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Needs Assessment**: Identify training requirements, equipment types, student prerequisites
2. **Course Scheduling**: Training calendar, student enrollment, resource allocation
3. **Classroom Instruction**: Theory, safety procedures, equipment systems
4. **Practical Training**: Hands-on operation, simulated scenarios, supervised practice
5. **Assessment**: Written examination, practical skills evaluation
6. **Certification**: Operator certification issuance upon successful completion
7. **Recurrent Training**: Annual refresher training, skill verification

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Safety briefing mandatory before all practical training
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) required during hands-on training
- Supervision by qualified instructor during all practical exercises
- Safety zone establishment for training operations
- Emergency procedures reviewed and practiced
- Medical certification requirements for specific equipment (e.g., heights, confined spaces)
- Zero tolerance for unsafe practices during training
