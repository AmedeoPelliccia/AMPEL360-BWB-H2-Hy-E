# 03-00-12-04-04A - Passenger Boarding Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies passenger boarding and deplaning services for AMPEL360 BWB aircraft, including procedures for safe and efficient passenger movement using boarding equipment.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Passenger boarding bridges (jet bridges) operation
- Air stairs and mobile passenger stairs operation
- Passenger boarding coordination
- Special assistance for passengers with reduced mobility (PRM)
- Emergency evacuation support

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- ICAO Annex 9 (Facilitation)
- ATA 52 (Doors - Aircraft perspective)
- Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) / equivalent regulations

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
Passenger Boarding Service provides safe, efficient, and comfortable passenger boarding and deplaning for AMPEL360 aircraft, utilizing the BWB configuration's multiple door locations and wide-body cabin advantages.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Boarding Rate | 30-40 passengers/minute | Multiple door operations |
| Door Access Points | Up to 4 doors simultaneously | BWB configuration |
| Jet Bridge Positioning Time | ≤ 3 minutes | From aircraft stop |
| PRM Boarding Time | ≤ 10 minutes | Priority boarding |
| Safety Clearance | Minimum 6 inches | Bridge to aircraft |
| Equipment Availability | 99% | Boarding equipment uptime |
| Personnel per Door | 1-2 agents | Ground staff |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Aircraft Arrival**: Aircraft positioning verification, parking brake set confirmation
2. **Equipment Positioning**: Jet bridge/stairs positioning, safety verification
3. **Door Opening Coordination**: Communication with cabin crew, door opening sequence
4. **PRM Boarding**: Priority boarding for passengers requiring assistance
5. **General Boarding**: Systematic passenger boarding, flow management
6. **Final Checks**: Head count verification, cabin secure confirmation
7. **Equipment Removal**: Jet bridge retraction, aircraft departure preparation

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Jet bridges must be properly positioned and locked before passenger boarding
- Safety interlock systems must be functional
- Ground personnel must verify aircraft door sill is level with jet bridge
- Weather limitations (wind speed, ice) must be observed
- Emergency stop capability must be tested before each boarding
- Passengers must be monitored during boarding for safety concerns
- PRM boarding equipment (wheelchair lifts, ramps) inspected daily
- Communication between ground crew and cabin crew mandatory throughout boarding
