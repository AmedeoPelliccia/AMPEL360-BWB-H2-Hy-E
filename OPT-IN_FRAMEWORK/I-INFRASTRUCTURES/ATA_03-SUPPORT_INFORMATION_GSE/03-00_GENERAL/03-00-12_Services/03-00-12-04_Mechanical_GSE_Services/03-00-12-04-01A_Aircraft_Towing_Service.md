# 03-00-12-04-01A - Aircraft Towing Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies aircraft towing services for AMPEL360 BWB aircraft, including pushback, towing to/from maintenance areas, and emergency towing operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Aircraft pushback from gates
- Towing to/from maintenance hangar
- Emergency/breakdown towing
- Towing equipment and procedures
- Safety protocols and towing limitations

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)

- ICAO Annex 14 (Aerodrome Design and Operations)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
Aircraft Towing Service provides safe movement of AMPEL360 aircraft on the ground using specialized towing equipment and trained personnel, accommodating the unique characteristics of the BWB configuration.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Maximum Towing Speed | 5 mph (8 km/h) | Per AFM limitations |
| Towing Force Available | Up to 250,000 lbs | Sufficient for BWB configuration |
| Minimum Turning Radius | Per aircraft specs | Compliant with BWB geometry |
| Towing Bar Attachment Points | Nose and wing-body junction | Per aircraft design |
| Response Time (Scheduled) | ≤ 10 minutes | Gate/stand operations |
| Response Time (Emergency) | ≤ 15 minutes | 24/7 availability |
| Crew Size | 1 driver + 2 wing walkers | Standard towing operation |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Pre-Towing Preparation**: Aircraft weight/CG verification, towing route planning, equipment inspection
2. **Aircraft Preparation**: Hydraulic systems check, brakes release verification, towing fixture attachment
3. **Communication Setup**: Intercom connection, radio checks with tower/ground control
4. **Towing Execution**: Gradual acceleration, constant speed maintenance, wing walker guidance
5. **Parking/Positioning**: Precise positioning at destination, chocks placement
6. **Disconnect**: Towing equipment removal, post-tow aircraft inspection
7. **Documentation**: Towing record completion, equipment status update

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Pre-tow safety briefing mandatory
- Wing walkers required for all towing operations
- Maximum towing speed strictly enforced
- Clear communication protocols between driver and wing walkers
- Aircraft hydraulic systems and brakes must be checked before towing
- Towing during adverse weather (high winds, ice) requires special authorization
- Emergency stop procedures trained and practiced regularly
- Towing equipment inspected daily before use
