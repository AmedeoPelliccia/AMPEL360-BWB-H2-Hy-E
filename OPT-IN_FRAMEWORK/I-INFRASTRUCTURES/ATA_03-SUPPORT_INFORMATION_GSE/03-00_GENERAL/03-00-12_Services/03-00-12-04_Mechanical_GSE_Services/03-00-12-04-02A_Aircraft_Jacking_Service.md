# 03-00-12-04-02A - Aircraft Jacking Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies aircraft jacking services for AMPEL360 BWB aircraft maintenance operations, including procedures for lifting the aircraft for landing gear work, weighing, and major maintenance.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Complete aircraft jacking operations
- Partial jacking (nose or main gear only)
- Aircraft weighing procedures
- Jack equipment and jacking points
- Safety procedures and load limitations

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
Aircraft Jacking Service provides safe lifting of AMPEL360 aircraft using specialized jacking equipment, accommodating the unique BWB configuration and distributed weight distribution.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Jack Capacity (per unit) | 50-100 tons | Per aircraft weight and configuration |
| Number of Jacking Points | 6-8 points | Distributed across BWB structure |
| Lifting Height | Up to 24 inches | Sufficient for landing gear work |
| Leveling Accuracy | ±0.1 degree | For weighing operations |
| Jack Setup Time | ≤ 2 hours | Complete aircraft jacking |
| Safety Factor | 3:1 minimum | Load capacity vs. applied load |
| Personnel Required | 4-6 technicians | Including supervisor |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Pre-Jacking Preparation**: Aircraft weight/CG calculation, jacking points inspection, hangar preparation
2. **Equipment Positioning**: Jack placement at designated points, hydraulic line connections
3. **Aircraft Preparation**: Fuel/fluid levels verification, center of gravity check, stability verification
4. **Jacking Operation**: Synchronized lifting (max 1 inch per cycle), continuous levelness monitoring
5. **On-Jacks Stabilization**: Jack stands positioning, hydraulic pressure verification, safety props installation
6. **Work Execution**: Maintenance operations performed while aircraft is jacked
7. **Lowering**: Gradual synchronized lowering, ground contact verification
8. **Post-Jacking**: Aircraft inspection, equipment removal, documentation

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Aircraft center of gravity must be verified before jacking
- Minimum two qualified personnel required for jacking operations
- Hydraulic jacks must be tested before each use
- Safety props and stands must be positioned before work under aircraft begins
- Maximum wind speed for outdoor jacking: 20 knots
- Load cells and pressure gauges must be monitored continuously during jacking
- Emergency lowering procedures must be established and rehearsed
- No personnel under aircraft during initial lifting or final lowering phases
