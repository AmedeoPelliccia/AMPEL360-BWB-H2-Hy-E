# 03-00-12-03-03A - Electrical Testing Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies electrical testing and diagnostic services for AMPEL360 aircraft electrical systems during ground operations and maintenance activities.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Electrical system functional testing
- Insulation resistance testing
- Ground continuity verification
- Power quality analysis
- Battery system diagnostics
- High-voltage system testing

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- MIL-STD-704 (Aircraft Electric Power Characteristics)
- SAE AS50881 (Wiring, Aerospace Vehicle)
- IEC 61557 (Electrical Safety in Low Voltage Distribution Systems)
- ATA 24 (Electrical Power)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
Electrical Testing Service provides comprehensive testing and diagnostic capabilities for aircraft electrical systems, ensuring safety, performance, and regulatory compliance.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Insulation Resistance Testing | 1-10 MΩ range | Per ATA 24 requirements |
| Ground Continuity Testing | <0.1Ω resistance | Safety-critical verification |
| Power Quality Analysis | THD, harmonics, transients | Per MIL-STD-704 |
| Battery Testing | Capacity, impedance, voltage | Full battery health assessment |
| HV System Testing | Up to 1000 VDC | Hybrid propulsion systems |
| Test Report Turnaround | ≤ 4 hours | From test completion |
| Equipment Calibration | ±2% accuracy | NIST-traceable calibration |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Test Planning**: Review maintenance requirements, select appropriate test procedures
2. **Pre-Test Verification**: Equipment calibration check, safety precautions
3. **Test Execution**: Systematic testing per approved procedures, data recording
4. **Data Analysis**: Review test results against acceptance criteria
5. **Discrepancy Resolution**: Identify and document any anomalies
6. **Test Report**: Detailed documentation of test results and findings
7. **Certification**: Sign-off by qualified electrical specialist

## 6. Safety Requirements
- All testing performed with aircraft systems de-energized unless specifically required
- High-voltage testing requires specialized safety procedures and PPE
- Test equipment must be properly grounded
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures mandatory for invasive testing
- Personnel must be qualified electrical technicians with appropriate certifications
- Safety briefings required before all high-voltage testing
- Emergency procedures and equipment readily available
