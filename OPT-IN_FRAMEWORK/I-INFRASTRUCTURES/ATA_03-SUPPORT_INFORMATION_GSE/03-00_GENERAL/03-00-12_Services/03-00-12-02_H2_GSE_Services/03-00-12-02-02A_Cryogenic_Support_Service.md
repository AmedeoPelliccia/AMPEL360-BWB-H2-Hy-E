# 03-00-12-02-02A - Cryogenic Support Service

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the cryogenic support services required for maintaining and managing liquid hydrogen systems and related ultra-low temperature equipment for AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Cryogenic system monitoring and maintenance
- Temperature management and control
- Boil-off gas management
- Cryogenic equipment inspection and testing
- Emergency cryogenic incident response

## 3. Applicable Documents
- NFPA 2 (Hydrogen Technologies Code)
- ISO 21013 (Cryogenic Vessels - Pressure Relief Accessories)
- CGA P-12 (Safe Handling of Cryogenic Liquids)
- SAE AS6968 (Hydrogen Aircraft Refueling)
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
Cryogenic support service provides specialized technical support for all ultra-low temperature systems associated with hydrogen aircraft operations. This includes monitoring, maintenance, and emergency response for cryogenic storage, transfer, and aircraft fuel systems.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Temperature Monitoring | Continuous real-time | 24/7 surveillance |
| Response Time (Normal) | ≤ 15 minutes | Standard maintenance calls |
| Response Time (Emergency) | ≤ 5 minutes | Cryogenic incident response |
| Boil-Off Management | <2% per day | Storage loss rate |
| System Inspection Frequency | Daily/Weekly/Monthly | Based on equipment criticality |
| Cryogenic Leak Detection | <30 seconds | Time to detect and alert |
| Personnel Availability | 24/7/365 | On-call specialist coverage |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Preventive Monitoring**: Continuous temperature and pressure monitoring of all cryogenic systems
2. **Scheduled Inspections**: Regular visual and instrumental inspections per maintenance schedule
3. **Boil-Off Management**: Monitor and manage hydrogen gas evolution from cryogenic systems
4. **Incident Response**: Rapid deployment for cryogenic-related emergencies
5. **System Optimization**: Analysis and recommendations for efficiency improvements
6. **Documentation**: Detailed records of all cryogenic system activities

## 6. Safety Requirements

### 6.1 Cryogenic Safety Protocols
- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Cryogenic-rated gloves, face shields, protective clothing mandatory
- **Frostbite Prevention**: Immediate medical response capability for cold injuries
- **Oxygen Displacement Monitoring**: Continuous O2 level monitoring in confined spaces
- **Thermal Stress Management**: Regular inspection of materials subject to thermal cycling
- **Pressure Relief Systems**: Regular testing and verification of all relief devices
- **Emergency Warming Procedures**: Protocols for controlled system warm-up if required

### 6.2 Operational Safety Requirements
- All cryogenic personnel must complete specialized training per NFPA 2
- Buddy system mandatory for all cryogenic work
- Pre-work safety briefings required for all cryogenic activities
- Cryogenic incident drills conducted quarterly
- Safety equipment inspection before each shift
- Environmental monitoring systems active at all times
