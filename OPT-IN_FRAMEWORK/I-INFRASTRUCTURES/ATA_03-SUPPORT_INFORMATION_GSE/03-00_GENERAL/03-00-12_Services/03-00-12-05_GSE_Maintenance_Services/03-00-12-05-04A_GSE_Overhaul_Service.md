# 03-00-12-05-04A - GSE Overhaul Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies comprehensive overhaul services for Ground Support Equipment, including major component replacement, system refurbishment, and life extension programs.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Major equipment overhaul programs
- Component-level overhaul and refurbishment
- Life extension and upgrade programs
- Zero-time equipment rebuilds
- Overhaul documentation and certification

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- Equipment manufacturer overhaul manuals
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- ATA 03-30 (Maintenance)
- Component Maintenance Manual (CMM) per equipment

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Overhaul Service provides comprehensive refurbishment and restoration of ground support equipment to like-new or improved condition, extending service life and improving reliability.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Overhaul Intervals | 5-10 years or operating hours | Per equipment type |
| Overhaul Duration | 2-6 weeks | Dependent on equipment complexity |
| Restoration Level | Like-new or better | Performance restored to specifications |
| Parts Replacement | 100% wear items | Per overhaul manual |
| Testing & Verification | Full functional test | Per OEM requirements |
| Warranty | 12-24 months | Post-overhaul warranty |
| Documentation | Complete overhaul records | Traceable to component level |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Overhaul Planning**: Equipment assessment, scope definition, parts ordering
2. **Equipment Receipt**: Induction inspection, condition documentation
3. **Disassembly**: Complete teardown, component inspection, defect identification
4. **Component Overhaul**: Cleaning, machining, repair, replacement per manual
5. **Assembly**: Reassembly with new/overhauled components
6. **Testing**: Functional testing, performance verification, load testing
7. **Finishing**: Painting, marking, corrosion protection
8. **Documentation**: Overhaul records, certification, warranty documents
9. **Delivery**: Return to customer with full documentation package

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Comprehensive safety assessment before overhaul initiation
- Hazardous materials handling per safety data sheets
- Proper ventilation for cleaning and painting operations
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) mandatory
- Load testing performed with safety barriers and personnel clear
- Quality control inspection before return to service
- Safety systems verification at multiple overhaul stages
