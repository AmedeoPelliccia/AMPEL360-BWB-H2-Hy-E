# 03-00-12-05-03A - GSE Calibration Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies calibration services for Ground Support Equipment measuring and test equipment, ensuring accuracy and traceability to national/international standards.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Calibration of measuring and test equipment
- Traceability to NIST or equivalent standards
- Calibration interval management
- Calibration documentation and certification
- Out-of-tolerance equipment handling

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ISO 17025 (Testing and Calibration Laboratories)
- ANSI/NCSL Z540.3 (Requirements for Calibration)
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- Equipment manufacturer calibration procedures
- NIST Handbook 44 (Specifications, Tolerances)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Calibration Service provides traceable calibration for all measuring and test equipment used in ground support operations, ensuring accuracy and compliance with regulatory and quality requirements.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Calibration Accuracy | Per equipment specifications | NIST-traceable standards |
| Calibration Intervals | Equipment-dependent | 6 months to 24 months |
| Turnaround Time (Standard) | ≤ 5 working days | From receipt to return |
| Turnaround Time (Priority) | ≤ 2 working days | Critical equipment |
| Calibration Uncertainty | As-specified | Per ISO 17025 |
| Certificate Delivery | Same as equipment | NIST-traceable documentation |
| Out-of-Tolerance Rate | <5% | Equipment within tolerance |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Calibration Scheduling**: Equipment due date tracking, scheduling coordination
2. **Equipment Receipt**: Identification, condition assessment, intake documentation
3. **Pre-Calibration Check**: As-found condition testing, out-of-tolerance identification
4. **Calibration**: Adjustment/verification against traceable standards
5. **Post-Calibration Verification**: As-left condition testing, acceptance criteria verification
6. **Certification**: Calibration certificate generation with traceability chain
7. **Equipment Return**: Calibration label application, return to service authorization

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Calibration performed in controlled environment (temperature, humidity)
- Safety verification for equipment handling hazards
- Electrical safety for high-voltage equipment calibration
- Contamination control for cryogenic equipment
- Proper handling of precision instruments
- Calibration standards secured and handled per manufacturer requirements
