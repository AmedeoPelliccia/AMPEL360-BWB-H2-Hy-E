# 03-00-12-05-02A - GSE Corrective Maintenance

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies corrective maintenance services for Ground Support Equipment, addressing equipment failures, malfunctions, and performance deficiencies to restore GSE to operational condition.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Unscheduled corrective maintenance
- Failure diagnosis and troubleshooting
- Component repair and replacement
- Emergency repair services
- Root cause analysis and corrective actions

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- Equipment manufacturer troubleshooting guides
- ATA 03-30 (Maintenance)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Corrective Maintenance Service provides rapid response to equipment failures and malfunctions, restoring GSE to operational status through effective diagnosis, repair, and verification.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Response Time (Standard) | ≤ 30 minutes | From failure notification |
| Response Time (Critical H2 Equipment) | ≤ 15 minutes | Emergency response |
| Diagnosis Time | ≤ 2 hours | Fault identification |
| Repair Completion (Minor) | ≤ 4 hours | Simple component replacement |
| Repair Completion (Major) | ≤ 24 hours | Complex repairs |
| Spare Parts Availability | 95% | Common parts in stock |
| First-Time Fix Rate | 85% | Repairs completed without return |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Failure Notification**: Equipment issue reported, initial information gathering
2. **Response Dispatch**: Technician deployment, tools and parts preparation
3. **Diagnosis**: Systematic troubleshooting, fault isolation
4. **Repair Planning**: Parts identification, repair method selection
5. **Repair Execution**: Component repair/replacement per procedures
6. **Functional Testing**: Post-repair verification, operational testing
7. **Documentation**: Failure analysis, repair actions, parts usage
8. **Root Cause Analysis**: For recurring failures or critical equipment

## 5. Service Level Agreement
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Response Time (Standard) | ≤ 30 minutes | Technician on-site |
| Response Time (Critical) | ≤ 15 minutes | H2 and safety-critical equipment |
| First-Time Fix Rate | 85% | Repairs without recurrence within 7 days |
| Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) | ≤ 4 hours | Average repair completion time |
| Equipment Return to Service | Same day | 90% of repairs |
| Customer Satisfaction | ≥ 4.5/5.0 | Service quality rating |
| Safety Incident Rate | Zero | Repair-related incidents |

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Safety assessment required before commencing repairs
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures for energy isolation
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) mandatory
- Work area secured and marked for safety
- Emergency stop systems verified functional before return to service
- Post-repair safety inspection mandatory
- Critical safety system repairs require dual verification
