# 03-00-12-05-01A - GSE Preventive Maintenance

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies preventive maintenance services for Ground Support Equipment, ensuring reliability, safety, and optimal performance of all GSE assets supporting AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Scheduled preventive maintenance programs
- Inspection intervals and procedures
- Lubrication and fluid services
- Component replacement schedules
- Maintenance tracking and documentation

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- Equipment manufacturer maintenance manuals
- ATA 03-30 (Maintenance)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Preventive Maintenance Service provides systematic, scheduled maintenance to prevent equipment failures, extend service life, and ensure safe operation of all ground support equipment.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Maintenance Intervals | Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Annual | Per equipment type and usage |
| Service Completion Time | Variable by complexity | Within scheduled maintenance window |
| Equipment Availability | ≥95% | Uptime after scheduled maintenance |
| Maintenance Compliance | 100% | All scheduled tasks completed |
| Parts Availability | 98% | Critical parts in stock |
| Technician Qualification | 100% | All personnel trained and certified |
| Documentation Accuracy | 100% | Complete maintenance records |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Maintenance Planning**: Equipment scheduling, task identification, parts provisioning
2. **Pre-Maintenance Inspection**: Equipment condition assessment, safety verification
3. **Maintenance Execution**: Systematic completion of scheduled tasks per procedures
4. **Quality Verification**: Post-maintenance inspection, functional testing
5. **Documentation**: Maintenance record completion, parts usage tracking
6. **Equipment Release**: Return to service authorization, operator notification
7. **Trend Analysis**: Performance monitoring, reliability tracking

## 5. Service Level Agreement
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Maintenance On-Time Completion | 95% | Within scheduled windows |
| Equipment Uptime | ≥95% | Post-maintenance availability |
| Repeat Maintenance Rate | <5% | Same issue within 30 days |
| Parts Availability | 98% | Critical parts in stock |
| Technician Utilization | 80-85% | Productive maintenance time |
| Documentation Completeness | 100% | All records complete |
| Customer Satisfaction | ≥ 4.5/5.0 | Service quality rating |

## 6. Safety Requirements
- All maintenance performed per manufacturer procedures
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures mandatory for energy isolation
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) required per task
- Confined space entry procedures for applicable equipment
- Fall protection for elevated maintenance work
- Fire extinguishers and safety equipment accessible
- Post-maintenance safety verification before return to service
