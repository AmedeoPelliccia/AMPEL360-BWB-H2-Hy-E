# 03-00-12-07-03A - GSE Field Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies on-site field service support for Ground Support Equipment, providing expert technicians for troubleshooting, repair, and technical assistance at AMPEL360 operational locations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- On-site technical support and troubleshooting
- Emergency field service response
- Complex repair services requiring specialist skills
- Equipment commissioning and installation support
- Technical consultation and optimization services

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- Equipment manufacturer service manuals
- ATA 03-30 (Maintenance)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Field Service provides highly skilled technicians for on-site support, repair, and technical assistance, addressing complex issues requiring hands-on expertise beyond helpdesk or remote diagnostic capabilities.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Response Time (Standard) | ≤ 4 hours | During business hours |
| Response Time (Priority) | ≤ 2 hours | 24/7 emergency response |
| Response Time (Critical H2) | ≤ 1 hour | H2 systems emergencies |
| Technician Skill Level | Senior/Specialist | 5+ years experience |
| Tool/Equipment Availability | 100% | Complete tool sets |
| First-Visit Fix Rate | 85% | Resolution on first visit |
| Service Coverage | 24/7/365 | Continuous availability |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Service Request**: Request received from helpdesk or customer
2. **Resource Assignment**: Technician selection, tools/parts preparation
3. **Dispatch**: Technician deployment to site
4. **On-Site Assessment**: Equipment inspection, diagnosis, customer consultation
5. **Repair/Service Execution**: Troubleshooting, repair, adjustment, testing
6. **Functional Verification**: Post-repair testing, performance validation
7. **Customer Handover**: Equipment return, documentation, training if needed
8. **Service Documentation**: Work order completion, parts usage, recommendations

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Site safety briefing mandatory before work commencement
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) appropriate to task
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures for energy isolation
- Confined space entry permits where applicable
- Hot work permits for welding/cutting operations
- Continuous communication with operations control
- Emergency response capability and equipment
- Post-work safety verification and sign-off
