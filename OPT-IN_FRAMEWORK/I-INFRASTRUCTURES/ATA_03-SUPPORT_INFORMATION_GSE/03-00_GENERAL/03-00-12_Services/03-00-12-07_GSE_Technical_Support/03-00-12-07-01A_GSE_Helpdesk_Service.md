# 03-00-12-07-01A - GSE Helpdesk Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies helpdesk technical support services for Ground Support Equipment, providing first-line support for operational issues, troubleshooting guidance, and service coordination for AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- 24/7 helpdesk availability
- Technical troubleshooting support
- Service request coordination
- Issue tracking and escalation
- Knowledge base management

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- ITIL Framework (IT Service Management)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- Equipment manufacturer support documentation
- ATA 03-30 (Maintenance)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Helpdesk Service provides centralized technical support for all ground support equipment, offering immediate assistance, troubleshooting guidance, and service coordination through trained support specialists.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Availability | 24/7/365 | Continuous operation |
| Response Time (Phone) | ≤ 30 seconds | Call answer time |
| Response Time (Email) | ≤ 2 hours | Initial response |
| First Call Resolution | 70% | Issues resolved on first contact |
| Escalation Time | ≤ 15 minutes | For unresolved issues |
| Support Channels | Phone, Email, Web Portal | Multi-channel access |
| Technician Availability | Minimum 3 agents | Per shift |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Issue Intake**: Customer contact via phone/email/portal, initial information gathering
2. **Issue Logging**: Ticket creation, categorization, priority assignment
3. **Initial Diagnosis**: Troubleshooting guidance, knowledge base consultation
4. **Resolution or Escalation**: Issue resolution or escalation to field service
5. **Service Coordination**: Dispatch field technicians, parts ordering, scheduling
6. **Follow-Up**: Verification of resolution, customer satisfaction check
7. **Documentation**: Issue resolution documentation, knowledge base update

## 5. Service Level Agreement
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Call Answer Time | ≤ 30 seconds | Average speed of answer |
| First Call Resolution | 70% | Issues resolved without escalation |
| Customer Satisfaction | ≥ 4.5/5.0 | Post-contact survey |
| Escalation Response | ≤ 15 minutes | Field service dispatch time |
| Ticket Closure Time | ≤ 24 hours | From resolution to closure |
| Knowledge Base Accuracy | ≥ 95% | Verified solutions |
| Agent Availability | 98% | Staffing levels maintained |

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Safety-critical issues prioritized and escalated immediately
- Emergency response protocols for H2 and electrical hazards
- Direct communication line to safety management
- Clear escalation path for safety concerns
- Incident reporting procedures integrated into helpdesk system
- Safety alerts broadcast to all affected personnel
