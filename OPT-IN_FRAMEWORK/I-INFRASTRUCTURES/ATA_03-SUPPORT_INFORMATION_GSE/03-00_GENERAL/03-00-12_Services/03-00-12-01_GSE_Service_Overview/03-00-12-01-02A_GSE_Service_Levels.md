# 03-00-12-01-02A - GSE Service Levels

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the service level tiers and associated capabilities for Ground Support Equipment services supporting AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- Service level definitions and classifications
- Performance criteria for each service tier
- Service level agreements (SLA) by tier
- Escalation paths and response requirements
- Quality metrics and monitoring procedures

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- IATA Ground Operations Manual (IGOM)

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE services are provided in three distinct service levels: Standard, Premium, and Critical. Each level offers different response times, service guarantees, and support capabilities tailored to operational requirements.

### 4.2 Service Level Specifications
| Service Level | Response Time | Availability | Support Hours | Priority |
|--------------|---------------|--------------|---------------|----------|
| Standard | ≤ 30 minutes | 98% | Business hours | Normal |
| Premium | ≤ 15 minutes | 99.5% | 24/7 | High |
| Critical (H2) | ≤ 5 minutes | 99.9% | 24/7/365 | Emergency |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Level Assessment**: Determine appropriate service level based on operational requirements
2. **Contract Establishment**: Formalize service level commitments
3. **Resource Provisioning**: Allocate equipment and personnel per service level requirements
4. **Performance Monitoring**: Track metrics against SLA targets
5. **Continuous Improvement**: Review and optimize service delivery

## 5. Service Level Agreement

### 5.1 Standard Service Level
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Response Time | ≤ 30 minutes | Time to service initiation |
| Availability | 98% | Operational uptime |
| Resolution Time | ≤ 4 hours | Service completion time |

### 5.2 Premium Service Level
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Response Time | ≤ 15 minutes | Time to service initiation |
| Availability | 99.5% | Operational uptime |
| Resolution Time | ≤ 2 hours | Service completion time |

### 5.3 Critical Service Level (H2 Operations)
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Response Time | ≤ 5 minutes | Emergency response activation |
| Availability | 99.9% | Operational uptime |
| Resolution Time | ≤ 1 hour | Critical issue resolution |

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Critical services (H2 operations) require enhanced safety protocols
- Service level agreements must not compromise safety standards
- Emergency escalation procedures mandatory for all service levels
- Regular safety audits required for Premium and Critical service providers
- Personnel certification requirements increase with service level tier
