# 03-00-12-01-01A - GSE Service Catalog

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the comprehensive catalog of Ground Support Equipment (GSE) services available for the AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft, including standard aviation GSE services and specialized hydrogen infrastructure services.

## 2. Scope
This catalog covers all GSE service offerings, including:
- Standard aviation ground handling services
- Hydrogen-specific services (LH2 refueling, cryogenic support)
- Electrical ground support services
- Mechanical handling services
- Maintenance, training, and technical support services
- Logistics and equipment pooling services

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
The GSE Service Catalog provides a structured listing of all ground support services required for AMPEL360 aircraft operations. Services are categorized by functional area and include standard aviation services as well as specialized services for hydrogen propulsion systems.

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Service Request**: Operator submits service request through designated channels
2. **Resource Allocation**: Service provider assigns appropriate equipment and personnel
3. **Service Execution**: Trained personnel perform service according to procedures
4. **Quality Verification**: Service completion verified against acceptance criteria
5. **Documentation**: Service records updated in GSE management system

## 6. Safety Requirements
- All GSE services must comply with airport safety regulations
- H2-specific services require specialized certification and safety protocols
- Personnel must complete appropriate training before service delivery
- Emergency response procedures must be in place for all service categories
- Safety audits conducted quarterly for all service providers
