# 03-00-12-01-04A - GSE Service Contracts

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the contract framework, terms, and management processes for Ground Support Equipment service agreements supporting AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- Contract types and structures
- Standard terms and conditions
- Performance guarantees and penalties
- Contract management procedures
- Dispute resolution and termination clauses

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- IATA Ground Operations Manual (IGOM)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- Commercial aviation service contract best practices

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE service contracts establish the legal and operational framework for service delivery. Contracts are structured to ensure service quality, safety compliance, and operational continuity while protecting the interests of both AMPEL360 and service providers.

### 4.2 Contract Types
| Contract Type | Duration | Scope | Use Case |
|--------------|----------|-------|----------|
| Master Service Agreement (MSA) | 3-5 years | Framework for multiple services | Long-term strategic partnerships |
| Service Level Agreement (SLA) | 1-3 years | Specific service with defined KPIs | Standard operational services |
| Task Order | Project-based | Discrete service delivery | Specialized or temporary needs |
| Emergency Service Agreement | On-call | Urgent/unplanned services | Emergency response and recovery |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Requirements Definition**: Identify service needs and specifications
2. **Provider Selection**: Qualification and competitive evaluation
3. **Contract Negotiation**: Terms, pricing, and performance criteria
4. **Contract Execution**: Formal agreement and onboarding
5. **Contract Management**: Ongoing monitoring and relationship management
6. **Contract Renewal/Termination**: Performance review and decision

## 6. Safety Requirements

### 6.1 Contract Safety Obligations
- Providers must maintain all required safety certifications throughout contract term
- Safety performance metrics included in contract KPIs
- Safety incidents trigger immediate review and may result in contract suspension
- Annual safety audits mandatory for all contract holders
- Insurance requirements must be maintained without lapse

### 6.2 H2-Specific Contract Requirements
- Enhanced insurance coverage for hydrogen operations
- Emergency response capability verification prior to contract execution
- Quarterly safety compliance reviews
- Real-time incident reporting systems required
- Specialized training records must be maintained and accessible
