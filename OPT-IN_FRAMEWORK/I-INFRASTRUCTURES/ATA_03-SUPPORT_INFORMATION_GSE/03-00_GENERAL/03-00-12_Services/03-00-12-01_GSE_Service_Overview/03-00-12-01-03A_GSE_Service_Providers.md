# 03-00-12-01-03A - GSE Service Providers

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the qualification criteria, responsibilities, and management framework for Ground Support Equipment service providers supporting AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- Service provider qualification requirements
- Provider categories and capabilities
- Performance monitoring and evaluation
- Contract management and compliance
- Provider certification and recertification processes

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- IATA Ground Operations Manual (IGOM)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- SAE AS6968 (Hydrogen Aircraft Refueling) - for H2 service providers
- NFPA 2 (Hydrogen Technologies Code) - for H2 service providers

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE service providers are qualified organizations authorized to deliver ground support services for AMPEL360 aircraft. Providers must meet stringent qualification criteria, particularly for hydrogen-related services.

### 4.2 Provider Categories
| Category | Services Provided | Qualification Requirements |
|----------|------------------|---------------------------|
| Tier 1 - Standard GSE | Mechanical handling, towing, boarding | Basic aviation ground handling certification |
| Tier 2 - Electrical GSE | Ground power, battery services, testing | Electrical systems certification + aviation experience |
| Tier 3 - H2 Specialized | LH2 refueling, cryogenic support, H2 safety | H2 handling certification + cryogenic training + NFPA 2 compliance |
| Tier 4 - Integrated Services | Full-service GSE operations | Multiple tier certifications + proven track record |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Provider Qualification**: Initial assessment and certification
2. **Contract Award**: Service agreements established based on capabilities
3. **Onboarding**: Integration with AMPEL360 systems and procedures
4. **Performance Monitoring**: Ongoing evaluation against KPIs
5. **Periodic Review**: Annual recertification and capability assessment

## 5. Service Level Agreement
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Provider Availability | 99.5% | Monthly operational readiness |
| Certification Compliance | 100% | All personnel properly certified |
| Safety Incident Rate | < 0.1 per 1000 services | Monthly safety audit |
| Service Quality Score | ≥ 4.5/5.0 | Customer feedback and performance metrics |
| Response to Audit Findings | ≤ 30 days | Time to implement corrective actions |

## 6. Safety Requirements

### 6.1 General Requirements
- All providers must maintain current safety certifications
- Safety management system (SMS) implementation required
- Regular safety audits (quarterly for H2 providers, semi-annual for others)
- Incident reporting procedures must be in place
- Emergency response capabilities verified annually

### 6.2 H2-Specific Provider Requirements
- Personnel must complete H2 safety training per NFPA 2
- Cryogenic handling certification mandatory
- Emergency response drills conducted quarterly
- Specialized insurance coverage for hydrogen operations
- Real-time safety monitoring systems required
