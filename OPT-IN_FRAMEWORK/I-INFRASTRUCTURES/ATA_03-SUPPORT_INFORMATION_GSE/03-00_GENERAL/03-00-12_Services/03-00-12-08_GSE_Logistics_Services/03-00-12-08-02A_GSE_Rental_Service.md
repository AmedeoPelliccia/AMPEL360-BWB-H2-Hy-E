# 03-00-12-08-02A - GSE Rental Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies equipment rental services for Ground Support Equipment, providing flexible access to GSE on short-term or long-term basis for AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Short-term and long-term equipment rental
- Emergency equipment provision
- Rental terms and conditions
- Equipment delivery and return logistics
- Rental rate structures

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- Commercial rental agreements and terms
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- Equipment manufacturer rental guidelines

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Rental Service provides flexible equipment access without capital investment, supporting peak demand, equipment replacement during maintenance, and evaluation of new equipment types.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Rental Periods | Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly | Flexible terms |
| Equipment Availability | 90% | Rental fleet availability |
| Delivery Time (Standard) | ≤ 24 hours | From order confirmation |
| Delivery Time (Emergency) | ≤ 4 hours | Critical equipment needs |
| Equipment Condition | Like-new or recent | Well-maintained units |
| Insurance Coverage | Comprehensive | Liability and damage |
| Rental Rate Structure | Tiered pricing | Volume discounts available |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Rental Inquiry**: Customer request, equipment needs assessment
2. **Availability Check**: Equipment availability verification, quote preparation
3. **Contract Execution**: Rental agreement, terms acceptance, payment arrangement
4. **Equipment Preparation**: Inspection, cleaning, functional verification
5. **Delivery**: Equipment transport to customer location, setup if required
6. **Rental Period**: Customer use, support services available
7. **Return Process**: Equipment pickup, inspection, damage assessment
8. **Billing**: Final invoice, security deposit return if applicable

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Equipment safety certification current before rental
- Safety briefing provided with equipment delivery
- Operator qualification verification recommended
- Safety equipment and manuals provided with rental
- Incident reporting procedures included in rental agreement
- H2 equipment rentals require additional safety verification
- Equipment recall authority for safety concerns
