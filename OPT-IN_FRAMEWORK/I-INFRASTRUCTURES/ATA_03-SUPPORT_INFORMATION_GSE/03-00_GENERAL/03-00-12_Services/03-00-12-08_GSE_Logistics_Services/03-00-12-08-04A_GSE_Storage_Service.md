# 03-00-12-08-04A - GSE Storage Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies storage services for Ground Support Equipment, providing secure, climate-controlled, and properly maintained storage facilities for GSE assets supporting AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Short-term and long-term equipment storage
- Climate-controlled storage for sensitive equipment
- Equipment preservation and protection
- Inventory management during storage
- Security and access control

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- Equipment manufacturer storage requirements
- OSHA Storage Requirements (or local equivalent)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- NFPA 2 (Hydrogen Technologies Code) - for H2 equipment storage

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Storage Service provides secure, properly maintained storage facilities for ground support equipment during periods of non-use, ensuring equipment preservation and readiness for future deployment.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Storage Types | Indoor, Outdoor, Climate-Controlled | Multiple options |
| Climate Control | 15-25°C, 40-60% humidity | For sensitive equipment |
| Security | 24/7 monitoring, access control | Video surveillance |
| Storage Duration | Daily to multi-year | Flexible terms |
| Equipment Access | 24/7 with notice | Retrieval within 4 hours |
| Preservation Services | Battery maintenance, fluid checks | Per storage plan |
| Capacity | Varies by facility | Scalable space |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Intake**: Equipment receipt, condition documentation, inventory recording
2. **Preparation**: Cleaning, preservation treatments, fluid management
3. **Storage Placement**: Location assignment, securing, environmental setup
4. **Periodic Inspection**: Regular condition checks, battery charging, fluid verification
5. **Maintenance**: Preservation maintenance, corrosion prevention
6. **Retrieval Preparation**: Equipment preparation for return to service
7. **Dispatch**: Equipment loading, transport coordination
8. **Billing**: Storage fees, preservation services, handling charges

## 6. Safety Requirements

### 6.1 General Storage Safety
- Fire suppression systems active in all storage areas
- Clear aisles and emergency exits maintained
- Equipment properly secured to prevent tipping
- Hazardous materials stored per safety data sheets
- Regular safety inspections conducted
- Personnel trained in equipment handling

### 6.2 H2 Equipment Storage Requirements
- Separate storage area for H2 equipment
- Enhanced ventilation systems
- Hydrogen gas detection systems installed
- Restricted access with specialized training required
- Regular purging and inerting of H2 systems
- Cryogenic equipment properly vented
- Emergency response equipment readily available
