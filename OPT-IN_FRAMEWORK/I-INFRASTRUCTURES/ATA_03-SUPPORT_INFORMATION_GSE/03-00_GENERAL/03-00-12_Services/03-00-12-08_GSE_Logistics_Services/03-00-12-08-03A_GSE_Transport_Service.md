# 03-00-12-08-03A - GSE Transport Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies transportation services for Ground Support Equipment, providing safe and efficient movement of GSE between locations to support AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Inter-airport GSE transportation
- Equipment repositioning services
- Emergency equipment deployment
- Heavy/oversized equipment transport
- International equipment shipping

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) - for H2 equipment
- Department of Transportation (DOT) regulations
- International shipping regulations (IMDG, IATA)
- Equipment manufacturer transport requirements

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Transport Service provides specialized logistics for moving ground support equipment safely and efficiently, accommodating various equipment sizes, weights, and special handling requirements.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Transport Methods | Road, Air, Sea, Rail | Multi-modal capability |
| Local Transport (Same City) | ≤ 6 hours | Door-to-door service |
| Regional Transport (500 mi) | ≤ 48 hours | Within region |
| National Transport | 3-7 days | Coast-to-coast |
| International Transport | 7-21 days | Depends on destination |
| Equipment Tracking | Real-time GPS | Live tracking available |
| Specialized Handling | Heavy-lift, oversized | Permitted transport |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Transport Request**: Equipment identification, origin/destination, timeline
2. **Logistics Planning**: Route selection, transport method, permits if needed
3. **Equipment Preparation**: Securing, padding, documentation
4. **Loading**: Proper loading techniques, weight distribution, securing
5. **Transportation**: En-route monitoring, ETA updates
6. **Delivery**: Unloading, placement, condition inspection
7. **Documentation**: Proof of delivery, condition reports, customs if applicable
8. **Billing**: Transport charges, fuel surcharges, special handling fees

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Equipment secured per DOT/IMDG regulations
- Hazardous materials (H2 equipment) require special permits and handling
- Load weight verification and proper distribution
- Over-dimensional permits obtained where required
- Driver qualification and training for specialized equipment
- Emergency contact information available during transport
- Insurance coverage for equipment value
- Cryogenic equipment transported with proper ventilation
