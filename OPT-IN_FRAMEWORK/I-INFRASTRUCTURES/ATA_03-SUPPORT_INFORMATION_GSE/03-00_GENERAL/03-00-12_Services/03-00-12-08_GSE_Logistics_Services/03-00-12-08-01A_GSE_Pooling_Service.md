# 03-00-12-08-01A - GSE Pooling Service

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies equipment pooling services for Ground Support Equipment, providing shared access to GSE assets across multiple operators to optimize utilization and reduce costs for AMPEL360 operations.

## 2. Scope
This service specification covers:
- Shared GSE fleet management
- Equipment allocation and scheduling
- Multi-operator coordination
- Utilization optimization
- Cost sharing and billing arrangements

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200 (Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance)
- IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM)
- IATA Ground Operations Manual (IGOM)
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management Systems)
- Equipment sharing agreements and contracts

## 4. Service Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Pooling Service manages shared equipment resources, coordinating access among multiple operators to maximize equipment utilization, reduce capital investment, and provide operational flexibility.

### 4.2 Service Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | SLA Target |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Equipment Availability | 95% | When scheduled |
| Allocation Response Time | ≤ 30 minutes | From request to assignment |
| Utilization Rate | 70-85% | Target equipment utilization |
| Multi-Operator Coordination | Real-time system | Automated scheduling |
| Equipment Condition | Like-new standard | Maintained per schedule |
| Cost Allocation | Usage-based | Fair cost distribution |
| Booking Window | 24 hours to 30 days | Advance scheduling |

### 4.3 Service Delivery Process
1. **Pool Management**: Equipment fleet management, maintenance scheduling
2. **Reservation System**: Online booking system, availability management
3. **Allocation**: Equipment assignment based on priority and availability
4. **Dispatch**: Equipment delivery to operator location
5. **Usage Monitoring**: Track equipment usage, condition monitoring
6. **Return Process**: Equipment inspection, cleaning, repositioning
7. **Usage Billing**: Time-based or utilization-based billing
8. **Performance Reporting**: Utilization metrics, cost analysis

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Equipment safety inspection before each allocation
- Operator qualification verification mandatory
- Safety incident reporting shared across pool participants
- Equipment lockout for safety issues until resolved
- Safety training requirements apply to all operators
- Pooled H2 equipment subject to enhanced safety protocols
- Regular safety audits of pooled equipment
