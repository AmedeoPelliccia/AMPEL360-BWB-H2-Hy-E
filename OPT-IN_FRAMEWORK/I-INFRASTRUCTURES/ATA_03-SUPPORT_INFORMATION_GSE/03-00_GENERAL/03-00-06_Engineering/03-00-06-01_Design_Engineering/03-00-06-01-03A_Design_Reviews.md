# 03-00-06-01-03A - Design Reviews

## 1. Purpose
Establish the framework for conducting systematic design reviews throughout the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft development, ensuring design maturity, compliance verification, risk mitigation, and stakeholder alignment at key milestones.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Design review types and objectives
- Review process and participants
- Entry and exit criteria for each review
- Documentation requirements
- Action item tracking and closure
- Review metrics and reporting
- Lessons learned capture

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview
Design reviews are formal, documented evaluations of design maturity at planned stages in the development lifecycle. For the BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, reviews address both conventional aircraft systems and novel aspects including the blended-wing-body configuration and hydrogen propulsion integration.

### 4.2 Requirements
**Review Types and Sequence:**
1. **Conceptual Design Review (CoDR)** - Validate initial concepts and requirements
2. **System Requirements Review (SRR)** - Verify system requirements completeness
3. **Preliminary Design Review (PDR)** - Assess design approach and feasibility
4. **Critical Design Review (CDR)** - Confirm design readiness for production
5. **Test Readiness Review (TRR)** - Verify test preparation and readiness
6. **Production Readiness Review (PRR)** - Approve transition to production
7. **Flight Readiness Review (FRR)** - Authorize first flight operations

**Review Requirements:**
- Reviews shall be scheduled at defined project milestones
- Entry criteria shall be met before conducting review
- Cross-functional participation including safety, certification, manufacturing
- Design data package shall be distributed minimum 2 weeks before review
- Action items shall be tracked with assigned owners and due dates
- Review minutes shall be documented and approved within 1 week
- Exit criteria shall be satisfied before proceeding to next phase

### 4.3 Methodology
**Standard Review Process:**
1. **Preparation Phase**
   - Define review scope and objectives
   - Assemble design data package
   - Distribute materials to review team
   - Schedule review meeting(s)

2. **Review Phase**
   - Present design status and analysis results
   - Discuss technical issues and risks
   - Capture questions and action items
   - Assess against exit criteria

3. **Follow-up Phase**
   - Distribute review minutes
   - Track action items to closure
   - Update design documentation
   - Report review outcomes

**Review Board Composition:**
- Chief Engineer (Chair)
- Technical leads from all disciplines
- Safety and certification representatives
- Quality assurance representative
- Manufacturing representative
- Program management

## 6. Verification & Validation
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All required participants present or represented
- Entry criteria documented and satisfied
- Design data package complete and reviewed
- Exit criteria assessed and documented
- Action items assigned with target dates
- Review minutes approved by review board
- Lessons learned captured and shared

**Review Success Metrics:**
- On-time review completion rate
- Action item closure rate
- Time from review to action closure
- Number of critical issues identified
- Design rework rate after reviews
