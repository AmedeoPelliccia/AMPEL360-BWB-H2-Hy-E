# 03-00-06-01-01A - Design Standards

## 1. Purpose
Establish and document the design standards, methodologies, and best practices applicable to the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft development, ensuring consistency, quality, and regulatory compliance across all design activities.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Design philosophy and principles for the blended-wing-body configuration
- Applicable design standards and guidelines
- Design process workflows and approval gates
- Documentation requirements and formats
- Design review criteria and acceptance standards

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview
The design standards for AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E establish a unified framework for all engineering disciplines, integrating novel hydrogen propulsion systems with the innovative blended-wing-body airframe configuration. These standards ensure safety, performance, manufacturability, and maintainability throughout the aircraft lifecycle.

### 4.2 Requirements
- All designs shall comply with [EASA CS-25](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/certification-specifications/cs-25-large-aeroplanes) certification requirements
- Designs shall follow [SAE ARP4754A](https://www.sae.org/standards/content/arp4754a/) development assurance guidelines
- Material selection shall consider hydrogen compatibility and sustainability
- Design for manufacturing (DFM) and design for assembly (DFA) principles shall be applied
- All designs shall include hydrogen safety considerations per applicable standards
- BWB-specific structural and aerodynamic considerations shall be documented
- Digital twin compatibility shall be maintained throughout design lifecycle

### 4.3 Methodology
**Design Process Flow:**
1. Requirements capture and analysis
2. Conceptual design and trade studies
3. Preliminary design and analysis
4. Detailed design and optimization
5. Design verification and validation
6. Design review and approval
7. Release to production or next phase

**Key Design Tools:**
- CAD systems for 3D modeling and digital mockup
- FEA tools for structural analysis
- CFD tools for aerodynamic analysis
- PLM systems for configuration management
- Requirements management tools
