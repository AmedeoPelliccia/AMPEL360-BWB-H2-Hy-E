# Roles & Responsibilities

This document defines the key roles involved in the creation, governance, and maintenance of ATA 03 Support Information and GSE documentation within AMPEL360, and clarifies their responsibilities and key outputs.

## 1. Role Overview

| Role                         | Responsibilities                                                        | Key Outputs                                                   |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| GSE Program Manager         | Overall GSE program governance, strategy, budget, vendor management     | GSE program plans, procurement strategy, vendor contracts     |
| GSE Engineering Lead        | Technical specifications, design approval, integration with aircraft    | GSE specifications, interface control documents, test plans   |
| Ground Operations Manager   | Operational procedures, training, turnaround optimization               | Operational procedures, training programs, performance KPIs   |
| Safety Engineer (GSE)       | GSE safety analyses, hazard identification, H₂ safety protocols         | Safety assessments, risk analyses, safety procedures          |
| H₂ Systems Specialist       | Hydrogen refueling systems, cryogenic safety, H₂ infrastructure         | H₂ system specifications, safety protocols, training materials|
| Electric Power Systems Lead | Ground power, battery charging, energy management                       | Power system specifications, charging protocols, grid interface|
| Airport Interface Coordinator| Airport compatibility, infrastructure requirements, stakeholder liaison | Airport compatibility matrix, infrastructure requirements     |
| Certification Focal (GSE)   | GSE certification requirements, compliance demonstration, authority liaison| Certification plans, compliance evidence, liaison records    |
| Asset Management Lead       | GSE fleet tracking, lifecycle management, digital twin integration      | Asset register, maintenance schedules, digital twin data      |
| Sustainability Coordinator  | GSE environmental impact, electrification strategy, lifecycle assessment| Sustainability reports, LCA, carbon footprint analysis        |
| Technical Publications      | Documentation standards, S1000D compliance, publication management      | Technical manuals, illustrated parts catalogs, training docs  |

## 2. Role Descriptions

### 2.1 GSE Program Manager

**Primary Responsibility:** Overall leadership and coordination of the AMPEL360 GSE program.

**Key Activities:**
- Define GSE program strategy aligned with aircraft development timeline
- Manage GSE procurement budget and vendor selection
- Coordinate across ATA chapters and stakeholders
- Oversee GSE fleet deployment and operational readiness
- Report program status to executive leadership

**Key Decisions:**
- GSE procurement strategy (buy vs. lease vs. partner-provided)
- Budget allocation across GSE categories
- GSE deployment priorities and timeline
- Vendor selection and contract approval
- Resolution of cross-functional conflicts

**Interfaces:**
- Aircraft Program Management
- Operations Management
- Airport Partners
- GSE Vendors and Suppliers
- Regulatory Authorities (for H₂ safety approvals)

### 2.2 GSE Engineering Lead

**Primary Responsibility:** Technical oversight of all GSE specifications, design, and integration.

**Key Activities:**
- Define GSE technical requirements and performance specifications
- Review and approve GSE designs and modifications
- Ensure GSE compatibility with aircraft interfaces
- Manage GSE engineering change control
- Coordinate GSE testing and validation

**Key Decisions:**
- Approval of GSE technical specifications
- Acceptance of GSE design solutions
- Resolution of technical interface issues
- Engineering change approval
- Test plan approval and test results acceptance

**Interfaces:**
- Aircraft Systems Engineering (ATA chapters 20-50, 70-80)
- ATA 02 Operations Information
- ATA 85 Infrastructure Interface Standards
- GSE Vendors (technical liaison)
- Certification Focal

### 2.3 Ground Operations Manager

**Primary Responsibility:** Define and optimize ground operations procedures involving GSE.

**Key Activities:**
- Develop GSE operational procedures and checklists
- Define turnaround sequence and timing requirements
- Establish GSE crew training requirements and programs
- Monitor GSE operational performance and efficiency
- Identify and implement operational improvements

**Key Decisions:**
- Approval of operational procedures
- Turnaround time targets and optimization strategies
- Training curriculum and certification requirements
- GSE operational performance targets (KPIs)
- Operational contingency procedures

**Interfaces:**
- Flight Operations
- Ground Handling Companies
- Airport Operations
- Maintenance Planning
- GSE Asset Management

### 2.4 Safety Engineer (GSE)

**Primary Responsibility:** Ensure all GSE operations meet safety requirements, especially for hydrogen and high-voltage systems.

**Key Activities:**
- Conduct Functional Hazard Assessments (FHA) for GSE systems
- Perform Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA) for critical GSE
- Define safety zones and exclusion areas (especially for H₂ refueling)
- Develop emergency response procedures for GSE incidents
- Ensure compliance with safety standards and regulations

**Key Decisions:**
- Acceptance of GSE safety analyses and risk assessments
- Approval of safety-critical procedures
- Safety zone definitions and access control requirements
- Emergency response procedure approval
- Safety training requirements

**Interfaces:**
- Aircraft Safety Engineering
- H₂ Systems Specialist
- Fire and Rescue Services
- Regulatory Authorities
- Airport Safety Officers

### 2.5 H₂ Systems Specialist

**Primary Responsibility:** Lead technical aspects of hydrogen refueling infrastructure and safety.

**Key Activities:**
- Define hydrogen refueling system requirements
- Specify cryogenic equipment and safety systems
- Develop H₂ handling procedures and safety protocols
- Coordinate with H₂ suppliers and infrastructure providers
- Ensure compliance with hydrogen safety standards

**Key Decisions:**
- H₂ refueling technology selection (mobile vs. fixed, flow rates)
- Cryogenic equipment specifications and safety requirements
- H₂ purity and quality specifications
- Safety protocol approval for H₂ operations
- Supplier qualification and approval

**Interfaces:**
- Aircraft H₂ Systems (ATA 28)
- Safety Engineer (GSE)
- Airport Fire Services
- H₂ Suppliers and Infrastructure Providers
- Regulatory Authorities (H₂ safety)

### 2.6 Electric Power Systems Lead

**Primary Responsibility:** Define and manage all electrical GSE, including ground power and battery charging.

**Key Activities:**
- Specify ground power unit (GPU) requirements (400 Hz AC, power levels)
- Define battery charging systems and protocols (DC fast charging)
- Develop power management and load balancing strategies
- Coordinate renewable energy integration at airports
- Ensure electrical safety and EMI/EMC compliance

**Key Decisions:**
- GPU specifications and procurement
- Battery charging technology and protocols
- Power distribution architecture
- Grid interconnection requirements
- Electrical safety procedures

**Interfaces:**
- Aircraft Electrical Systems (ATA 24)
- Airport Electrical Infrastructure
- Battery Systems Engineering (ATA 24)
- Renewable Energy Providers
- Electrical Safety Authorities

### 2.7 Airport Interface Coordinator

**Primary Responsibility:** Ensure GSE compatibility with airport infrastructure and coordinate with airport stakeholders.

**Key Activities:**
- Survey airport capabilities and infrastructure availability
- Identify airport modifications required for AMPEL360 operations
- Coordinate GSE deployment with airport operators
- Facilitate airport certification and approval processes
- Maintain airport compatibility database

**Key Decisions:**
- Airport compatibility assessment and approval
- Infrastructure modification requirements
- GSE deployment priorities by airport
- Airport-specific operational procedures
- Airport stakeholder agreements

**Interfaces:**
- Airport Authorities and Operators
- Airport Planning and Development
- ATA 02 Operations Information
- ATA 85 Infrastructure Interface Standards
- Ground Handling Agents at airports

### 2.8 Certification Focal (GSE)

**Primary Responsibility:** Manage GSE-related certification activities and regulatory compliance.

**Key Activities:**
- Define certification requirements for GSE (where applicable)
- Develop Means of Compliance (MoC) for GSE safety items
- Coordinate with authorities on GSE approval processes
- Track and maintain certification evidence
- Support aircraft certification activities related to GSE

**Key Decisions:**
- MoC selection and approval for GSE items
- Certification strategy and timeline
- Evidence sufficiency and acceptance
- Authority liaison and response to findings
- Certification documentation approval

**Interfaces:**
- Aircraft Certification Team
- Safety Engineer (GSE)
- Regulatory Authorities (EASA, FAA, national CAAs)
- GSE Vendors (for certification data)
- ATA 10 Certification focal

### 2.9 Asset Management Lead

**Primary Responsibility:** Manage GSE fleet throughout its lifecycle, from procurement to disposal.

**Key Activities:**
- Maintain GSE asset register and digital twin database
- Track GSE location, status, and utilization
- Schedule GSE maintenance and inspections
- Monitor GSE performance and reliability
- Plan GSE lifecycle and replacement strategy

**Key Decisions:**
- Asset tracking and management system selection
- GSE maintenance schedules and procedures
- GSE allocation and deployment strategy
- End-of-life and replacement decisions
- Digital twin data standards and integration

**Interfaces:**
- GSE Program Manager
- Ground Operations Manager
- Maintenance Organizations
- ATA 95 Digital Product Passport team
- IT Systems / IoT Platform

### 2.10 Sustainability Coordinator

**Primary Responsibility:** Ensure GSE fleet meets AMPEL360 sustainability goals and circular economy principles.

**Key Activities:**
- Conduct lifecycle assessments (LCA) for GSE
- Define GSE electrification and renewable energy strategy
- Track GSE carbon footprint and environmental impact
- Develop end-of-life recycling and circular economy plans
- Report sustainability metrics and progress

**Key Decisions:**
- GSE sustainability targets and KPIs
- Electrification priorities and timeline
- Renewable energy integration strategy
- Circular economy and recycling requirements
- Sustainability reporting and disclosure

**Interfaces:**
- ATA 30_ANCHORS (Sustainability focal for all ATAs)
- GSE Program Manager
- GSE Engineering Lead
- Environmental Compliance
- External Sustainability Reporting

### 2.11 Technical Publications

**Primary Responsibility:** Ensure all ATA 03 documentation meets standards and publication requirements.

**Key Activities:**
- Enforce ATA iSpec 2200 and S1000D compliance
- Review documentation for consistency and completeness
- Manage CSDB (Common Source Database) for ATA 03
- Coordinate publication releases and updates
- Maintain document metadata and traceability

**Key Decisions:**
- Acceptance of documentation for publication
- Documentation standards interpretation and guidance
- Publication release approval
- Document classification and access control
- Metadata and tagging standards

**Interfaces:**
- All ATA 03 content authors
- AMPEL360 Documentation Standards Team
- Configuration Management
- Translation Services (if applicable)
- Customer Documentation Requirements

## 4. Communication and Coordination

### 4.1 Regular Meetings

- **GSE Program Review:** Monthly — led by GSE Program Manager, all leads attend
- **GSE Technical Review Board:** Bi-weekly — GSE Engineering Lead chairs, technical specialists
- **Safety Review Board:** Quarterly — Safety Engineer chairs, all safety-related roles
- **Airport Coordination Meeting:** Monthly — Airport Interface Coordinator leads
- **Sustainability Working Group:** Quarterly — Sustainability Coordinator leads

### 4.2 Escalation Paths

**Technical Issues:**
1. GSE Engineering Lead
2. GSE Program Manager
3. Aircraft Program Chief Engineer

**Safety Issues:**
1. Safety Engineer (GSE)
2. Aircraft Safety Manager
3. Chief Safety Officer

**Operational Issues:**
1. Ground Operations Manager
2. GSE Program Manager
3. Director of Operations

**Certification Issues:**
1. Certification Focal (GSE)
2. Certification Manager
3. Accountable Manager

## 5. Training and Competency Requirements

Each role requires specific competencies and training:

| Role | Minimum Qualifications | Specialized Training |
|------|----------------------|---------------------|
| GSE Program Manager | Engineering degree + 10 years aviation/GSE experience | Program management, aviation regulations |
| GSE Engineering Lead | Mechanical/Electrical engineering degree + 8 years GSE | Systems engineering, certification processes |
| Ground Operations Manager | Aviation operations background + 5 years ground ops | Turnaround optimization, safety management |
| Safety Engineer (GSE) | Safety engineering certification + aviation experience | FHA/FTA/FMEA, H₂ safety, electrical safety |
| H₂ Systems Specialist | Mechanical/Chemical engineering + H₂ experience | Cryogenic systems, H₂ safety, ISO 19880 |
| Electric Power Systems Lead | Electrical engineering + power systems experience | High-voltage safety, charging systems, EMC |
| Airport Interface Coordinator | Aviation/airport operations experience | ICAO standards, airport planning |
| Certification Focal (GSE) | Certification experience + aviation regulations | EASA/FAA regulations, MoC development |
| Asset Management Lead | Asset management experience | Digital twins, IoT, predictive maintenance |
| Sustainability Coordinator | Environmental engineering/sustainability experience | LCA methodology, circular economy, carbon accounting |
| Technical Publications | Technical writing + aviation documentation | ATA iSpec 2200, S1000D, AMPEL360 standards |

## 6. Change Control Authority

| Change Type | Approval Authority |
|------------|-------------------|
| GSE specifications (non-safety) | GSE Engineering Lead |
| Safety-critical GSE changes | Safety Engineer + GSE Engineering Lead |
| H₂ system changes | H₂ Systems Specialist + Safety Engineer |
| Operational procedures | Ground Operations Manager |
| Certification approach | Certification Focal |
| Budget changes >10% | GSE Program Manager |
| Budget changes >50% | Aircraft Program Management |
| Documentation structure | Technical Publications + GSE Program Manager |
