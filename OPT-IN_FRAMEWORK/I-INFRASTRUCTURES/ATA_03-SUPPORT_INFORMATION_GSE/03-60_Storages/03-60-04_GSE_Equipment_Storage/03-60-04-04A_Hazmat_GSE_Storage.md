# 03-60-04-04A - Hazmat GSE Storage

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies requirements for storage of hazardous materials handling GSE equipment, including fuel trucks, chemical dispensing equipment, and contaminated equipment staging.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Hazmat storage area design and segregation
- Secondary containment requirements
- Ventilation and vapor control
- Spill response equipment and procedures
- Regulatory compliance and permits

## 3. Applicable Documents
- EPA 40 CFR 112 (Oil Pollution Prevention)
- OSHA 1910.120 (Hazardous Waste Operations)
- NFPA 30 (Flammable and Combustible Liquids Code)
- NFPA 400 (Hazardous Materials Code)
- Local environmental regulations

## 4. Storage Description

### 4.1 Overview
Hazmat GSE storage provides segregated, controlled facilities for equipment used in handling hazardous materials and for temporary staging of equipment contaminated with hazmat requiring decontamination.

### 4.2 Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Secondary Containment | 110% of largest container | EPA requirement |
| Separation Distance | Per NFPA 400, Table 6.1.2.1 | Incompatible materials |
| Ventilation | 6 ACH minimum, explosion-proof | Vapor control |
| Fire Protection | Foam/dry chemical | Appropriate for stored materials |
| Spill Kit Capacity | 200-gallon minimum | Readily accessible |
| Drainage | Contained, no direct discharge | Divert to treatment |

### 4.3 Capacity and Requirements
- **Staging Area**: 500-1,000 m² isolated area
- **Containment**: Curbed and sloped to sump
- **Decontamination**: Wash-down pad with water treatment
- **PPE Station**: Emergency shower/eyewash, PPE storage
- **Access Control**: Restricted, trained personnel only
