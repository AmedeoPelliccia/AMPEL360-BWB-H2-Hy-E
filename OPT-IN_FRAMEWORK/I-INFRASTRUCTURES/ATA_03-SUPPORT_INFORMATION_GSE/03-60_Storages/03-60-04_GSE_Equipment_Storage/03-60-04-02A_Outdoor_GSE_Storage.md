# 03-60-04-02A - Outdoor GSE Storage

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies requirements for outdoor storage of large mobile GSE equipment that does not require climate-controlled facilities.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Outdoor storage yard design and layout
- Pavement and drainage systems
- Weather protection (covered parking)
- Equipment security and access control
- Utility connections for equipment conditioning

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ATA iSpec 2200
- ASCE 7 (Minimum Design Loads for Buildings)
- ACI 330 (Guide for Design and Construction of Concrete Parking Lots)
- NFPA 1 (Fire Code)

## 4. Storage Description

### 4.1 Overview
Outdoor GSE storage provides hardstand areas for large mobile equipment including aircraft tugs, scissor lifts, fuel trucks, deicing trucks, and other vehicles designed for outdoor operation.

### 4.2 Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Hardstand Area | 5,000-10,000 m² | Typical airport |
| Pavement Type | Reinforced concrete | 300mm depth minimum |
| Pavement Strength | 100 kN/axle | Heavy equipment loads |
| Drainage Slope | 1-2% | Prevent standing water |
| Parking Layout | 10m × 5m spaces | Standard GSE size |
| Covered Parking | 30-50% of positions | Preferred for sensitive equipment |
| Lighting | 50-100 lux | Security and safety |

### 4.3 Capacity and Requirements
- **Drainage**: Oil-water separator for runoff
- **Utilities**: 480V power posts, compressed air
- **Security**: Perimeter fencing, CCTV, lighting
- **Traffic Flow**: One-way circulation preferred
- **Equipment Tethering**: Power conditioning for batteries

## 5. Safety Requirements
| Requirement | Standard | Implementation |
|-------------|----------|----------------|
| Traffic Control | Airport operations manual | Signage, pavement markings |
| Stormwater Management | EPA regulations | Oil-water separator, retention |
| Lighting | FAA AC 150/5340-30 | Non-glare, downward-directed |
