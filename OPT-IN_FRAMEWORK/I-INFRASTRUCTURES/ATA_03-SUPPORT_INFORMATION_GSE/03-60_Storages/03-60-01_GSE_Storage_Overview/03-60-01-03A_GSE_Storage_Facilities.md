# 03-60-01-03A - GSE Storage Facilities

## 1. Purpose
This document describes the physical storage facilities required for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) supporting H2-hybrid aircraft operations, including facility layouts, infrastructure, and operational characteristics.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Types of storage facilities
- Facility layout and design principles
- Infrastructure and utilities
- Access and logistics considerations
- Facility-specific operational procedures

## 4. Storage Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE storage facilities comprise a network of specialized buildings and outdoor areas designed to accommodate the diverse equipment portfolio required for H2-hybrid aircraft ground operations. Facilities are designed with emphasis on safety segregation, operational efficiency, and regulatory compliance.

### 4.2 Specifications

#### Facility Type Matrix
| Facility Type | Primary Use | Area (m²) | Key Features |
|---------------|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| H2 Storage Compound | LH2 tanks, GH2 cylinders | 2,000 | Isolated, explosion-proof, triple-redundant detection |
| Cryogenic Equipment Facility | Dewars, transfer equipment | 1,500 | Climate-controlled, vacuum pump station |
| Battery Storage & Charging | Electric GSE batteries | 800 | Fire suppression, thermal monitoring |
| Indoor Equipment Warehouse | Conventional GSE, tools | 3,000 | Climate-controlled, high-bay |
| Outdoor Equipment Yard | Large mobile GSE | 5,000 | Paved hardstand, covered parking |
| Spare Parts Warehouse | H2 components, consumables | 2,500 | Climate-controlled, inventory management |
| Hazmat Storage | Chemicals, fluids | 500 | Segregated, secondary containment |

#### Layout Principles
- **Separation**: Hazmat areas minimum 30m from occupied buildings
- **Accessibility**: All facilities within 500m of apron service points
- **Traffic Flow**: One-way circulation where possible
- **Emergency Access**: Clear emergency vehicle routes to all facilities
- **Expansion**: 25% reserved area for future growth

### 4.3 Capacity and Requirements

#### H2 Storage Compound (Detail)
- **Footprint**: 50m × 40m (2,000 m²)
- **Zones**:
  - LH2 bulk tank area (4 × 50,000L tanks)
  - LH2 dewar staging area (20 dewar capacity)
  - GH2 high-pressure cylinder storage (350 bar / 700 bar)
  - Vaporizer and pressure control equipment
  - Control room (explosion-proof, climate-controlled)
- **Safety Features**:
  - 3m high blast barrier walls
  - Multiple H2 detection zones
  - Water deluge system
  - Emergency isolation valves
  - Lightning protection system

#### Cryogenic Equipment Facility (Detail)
- **Footprint**: 30m × 50m (1,500 m²)
- **Temperature**: 15-25°C controlled
- **Features**:
  - Overhead crane (10-ton capacity)
  - Vacuum pump and helium recovery station
  - Dewar maintenance and testing area
  - Cryogenic valve and fitting storage
  - Personnel decontamination area

#### Battery Storage & Charging Facility (Detail)
- **Footprint**: 20m × 40m (800 m²)
- **Capacity**: 50 battery packs simultaneous charging
- **Features**:
  - Individual charging bays with monitoring
  - Thermal runaway detection and suppression
  - Exhaust ventilation for off-gassing
  - Firewall separation between bays
  - Battery testing and diagnostics station
