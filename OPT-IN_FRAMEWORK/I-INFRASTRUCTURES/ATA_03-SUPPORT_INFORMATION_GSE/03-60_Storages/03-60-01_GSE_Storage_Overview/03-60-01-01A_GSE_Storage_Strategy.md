# 03-60-01-01A - GSE Storage Strategy

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the overall storage strategy for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) related to the AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid aircraft, including facilities, management approaches, and strategic planning for hydrogen and cryogenic equipment storage.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Strategic planning for GSE storage facilities
- Storage location selection criteria
- Capacity planning and scalability
- Integration with airport infrastructure
- Long-term storage management approach

## 4. Storage Description

### 4.1 Overview
The GSE Storage Strategy establishes a comprehensive framework for storing all ground support equipment required for H2-hybrid aircraft operations. This includes specialized equipment for hydrogen handling, cryogenic systems, battery storage systems, and conventional GSE.

The strategy prioritizes:
- Safety and regulatory compliance
- Operational efficiency and accessibility
- Equipment protection and preservation
- Environmental sustainability
- Scalability for future fleet growth

### 4.2 Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Storage Facility Types | Indoor, Outdoor, Climate-Controlled, Hazmat | Multiple facility types required |
| Primary Storage Zones | H2/LH2, Cryogenic, Battery, Conventional | Safety-segregated areas |
| Temperature Control | -253°C to +50°C | Range covers LH2 to ambient |
| Security Classification | Restricted Access, Controlled Access, General | Based on hazard level |
| Capacity Planning Horizon | 10-15 years | Aligned with fleet expansion |
| Redundancy Factor | 120% of operational requirement | Ensures availability |

### 4.3 Capacity and Requirements

#### Storage Capacity Planning
- **Hydrogen Storage GSE**: Minimum 3-day operational buffer
- **Cryogenic Equipment**: Climate-controlled facilities for all dewars and transfer equipment
- **Battery Systems**: Dedicated charging and storage facilities with fire suppression
- **Spare Parts**: 24-month inventory for critical H2 components
- **Consumables**: 90-day operational stock

#### Facility Requirements
- Proximity to aircraft servicing positions (< 500m for mobile GSE)
- Segregated zones for incompatible materials
- Emergency access and egress routes
- Utility connections (power, water, nitrogen purge)
- Environmental monitoring systems
