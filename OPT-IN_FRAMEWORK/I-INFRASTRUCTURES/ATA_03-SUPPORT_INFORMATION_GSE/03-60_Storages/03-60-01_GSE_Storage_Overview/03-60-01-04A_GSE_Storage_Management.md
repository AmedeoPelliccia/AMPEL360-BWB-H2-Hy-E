# 03-60-01-04A - GSE Storage Management

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the management system for GSE storage operations, including inventory control, equipment tracking, maintenance scheduling, and operational procedures for all storage facilities.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Inventory management systems and procedures
- Equipment tracking and asset management
- Storage operational procedures
- Maintenance and inspection scheduling
- Documentation and record-keeping requirements

## 4. Storage Description

### 4.1 Overview
GSE Storage Management encompasses all processes and systems required to maintain, track, and optimize the use of ground support equipment and storage facilities. The management system integrates with airport operations, maintenance planning, and safety management systems.

Key management functions include:
- Real-time inventory tracking
- Equipment condition monitoring
- Predictive maintenance scheduling
- Storage space optimization
- Compliance documentation and audit support

### 4.2 Specifications

#### Management System Components
| Component | System/Tool | Function |
|-----------|-------------|----------|

| Equipment Tracking | RFID/Barcode System | Location and movement tracking |

### 4.3 Capacity and Requirements

#### Inventory Management Requirements
- **Real-time tracking**: All GSE assets tagged with RFID or barcode
- **Location accuracy**: Within 5m for outdoor storage, bay-level for indoor
- **Audit frequency**: Quarterly full physical inventory, monthly cycle counts
- **Data retention**: Minimum 7 years for compliance records
- **System uptime**: 99.9% availability for critical systems

#### Operational Procedures

**Daily Operations**
- Morning equipment readiness check
- Shift-change inventory reconciliation
- H2/cryogenic system status verification
- Environmental monitoring data review
- Incident log review and action closeout

**Weekly Operations**
- Full facility walkdown inspection
- Preventive maintenance task execution
- Storage space utilization review
- Equipment condition assessment
- Safety system functional tests

**Monthly Operations**
- Comprehensive inventory audit
- Maintenance backlog review
- Storage optimization analysis
- Training compliance verification
- Management review meeting

**Annual Operations**
- Regulatory compliance audit
- Storage strategy effectiveness review
- Capacity planning update
- Emergency response drill (full-scale)
- Management system certification audit
