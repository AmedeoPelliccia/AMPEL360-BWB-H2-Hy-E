# 03-60-02-02A - LH2 Dewar Storage

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies requirements for the storage, handling, and management of liquid hydrogen (LH2) dewars - transportable cryogenic vessels used for mobile hydrogen supply and temporary storage in ground support operations.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- LH2 dewar specifications and types
- Dewar storage area requirements
- Handling and transport procedures
- Maintenance and inspection requirements
- Safety and emergency response procedures

## 4. Storage Description

### 4.1 Overview
LH2 dewars are transportable vacuum-insulated cryogenic vessels ranging from 50 to 500 liters capacity, used for mobile hydrogen supply, temporary storage, and as buffer storage between bulk tanks and refueling operations. Dewars provide operational flexibility and enable hydrogen distribution to remote service points.

Key characteristics:
- Self-contained vacuum insulation
- Pressure-building circuit for self-pressurization
- Wheels or forklift pockets for mobility
- Typically 1-5 bar operating pressure
- 0.5-1.0% daily boil-off rate

### 4.3 Capacity and Requirements

#### Storage Area Design
- **Location**: Outdoor or well-ventilated covered area
- **Surface**: Concrete or asphalt, level (< 1% slope)
- **Capacity**: Minimum 20 dewar positions for typical operation
- **Spacing**: 2m between dewars, 3m aisles for forklift access
- **Restraints**: Chains or chocks to prevent tip-over
- **Signage**: NFPA 704 diamond, "Cryogenic Liquid - No Smoking"

#### Dewar Inventory Management
For typical airport operations:
- **Active Inventory**: 10-15 dewars in daily rotation
- **Reserve Inventory**: 5-10 dewars for surge capacity
- **Maintenance Pool**: 2-3 dewars undergoing inspection/repair
- **Total Fleet**: 17-28 dewars minimum

#### Handling Equipment Requirements
- **Dewar Cart**: Pneumatic tires, rated for 300 kg
- **Forklift**: Explosion-proof, smooth-cushion tires
- **Transfer Hoses**: Vacuum-jacketed, maximum 10m length
- **Fill Adapter**: CGA-compatible, with built-in PRV

### Handling Safety Procedures
1. **Before Moving Dewar**:
   - Check pressure (should be < 5 bar)
   - Verify all valves closed
   - Inspect casters/wheels
   - Secure loose components

2. **During Transfer**:
   - Use approved dewar cart or forklift
   - Maintain secure restraint at all times
   - Avoid sudden stops or turns
   - Keep away from pedestrian areas

3. **Connection/Disconnection**:
   - Wear cryogenic PPE
   - Relieve residual pressure before disconnecting
   - Purge lines with gaseous nitrogen
   - Cap all connections immediately after disconnection
