# 03-60-03-04A - Cryogenic Transfer Storage

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies requirements for cryogenic transfer and temporary storage systems, including transfer lines, mobile storage, and intermediate buffer storage used during cryogenic fluid handling operations.

## 2. Scope
This document covers:
- Vacuum-jacketed transfer lines and hoses
- Temporary and buffer storage systems
- Cryogenic pumps and transfer equipment
- Pre-cooling and conditioning procedures
- Transfer efficiency and loss minimization

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ISO 21009 (Cryogenic Vessels)
- CGA P-12 (Safe Handling of Cryogenic Liquids)
- EN 1251-3 (Cryogenic Vessels - Transportable Vacuum Insulated Vessels)
- NFPA 55 (Compressed Gases and Cryogenic Fluids Code)
- SAE AS6968 (Hydrogen Aircraft Refueling Systems)

## 4. Storage Description

### 4.1 Overview
Cryogenic transfer storage encompasses all temporary storage and transfer equipment between primary storage and point of use, including vacuum-jacketed piping, mobile dewars, buffer tanks, and transfer pumps. Efficient transfer systems minimize product loss and ensure safe, reliable cryogenic fluid movement.

### 4.2 Specifications

#### Transfer Line Types
| Type | Construction | Heat Ingress | Application |
|------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| Vacuum-Jacketed Rigid | Concentric pipe, vacuum annulus | 0.5-2 W/m | Fixed installations |
| Vacuum-Jacketed Flexible | Corrugated inner, flexible outer | 2-5 W/m | Mobile connections |
| Foam-Insulated | Single pipe, foam jacket | 10-20 W/m | Short runs, emergency |

#### Buffer Storage Capacity
| Application | Typical Capacity | Purpose |
|-------------|------------------|---------|
| Refueling Buffer | 500-2,000 L | Rapid fill capability |
| Pump Suction Vessel | 200-500 L | NPSH for cryogenic pump |
| Transfer Buffer | 100-300 L | Accommodate flow variations |

### 4.3 Capacity and Requirements

#### Transfer Line Design
- **Insulation**: Vacuum-jacketed preferred, foam acceptable for <10m
- **Slope**: Minimum 1% toward drain points
- **Supports**: Low-thermal-conductivity mounts every 3-5m
- **Expansion Loops**: Accommodate 100-300K temperature change
- **Drain Points**: Lowest points in system
- **Vent Points**: Highest points in system

#### Pre-Cooling Procedures
1. Verify line integrity and valve positions
2. Slowly introduce cryogenic liquid
3. Allow thermal contraction and gas venting
4. Monitor for leaks and proper drainage
5. Confirm stable flow before full-rate transfer
