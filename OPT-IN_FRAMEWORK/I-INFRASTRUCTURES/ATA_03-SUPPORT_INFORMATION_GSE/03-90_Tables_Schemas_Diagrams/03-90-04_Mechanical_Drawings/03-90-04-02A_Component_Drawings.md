# 03-90-04-02A - Component Drawings

## 1. Purpose

Define standards for detailed component drawings of individual mechanical parts for hydrogen GSE, providing manufacturing specifications and quality requirements.

## 2. Scope

Component drawings for GSE parts including valves, fittings, brackets, structural members, and custom-fabricated components for hydrogen systems.

## 3. Applicable Documents

- [ASME Y14.5](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-5-dimensioning-tolerancing) - Dimensioning and Tolerancing
- [ISO 2768](https://www.iso.org/standard/66273.html) - General Tolerances
- [ISO 1302](https://www.iso.org/standard/72075.html) - Surface Texture Indication

## 4. Documentation Description

### 4.1 Overview

Component drawings show individual parts with all dimensions, tolerances, materials, and manufacturing notes required for fabrication and inspection.

### 4.2 Format and Structure

| Element | Format | Standard |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Dimensions | Decimal inches or millimeters | ASME Y14.5 |
| Tolerances | General ± ISO 2768 or specific | Per functional requirement |
| Surface Finish | Ra values or symbols | ISO 1302 |
| Material Specification | ASTM/ASME designation | Full specification |

### 4.3 Content Requirements

#### 4.3.1 Part Information

- Part number and revision
- Material specification (e.g., ASTM A312 TP 316L)
- Heat treatment requirements (if applicable)
- Surface finish requirements
- Special processing (cryogenic treatment, oxygen cleaning)

#### 4.3.2 Inspection and Quality

- Critical dimensions flagged
- Inspection points (first article, in-process, final)
- NDT requirements
- Acceptance criteria
