# 03-80-08-04A - Energy Audit and Reporting

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies energy audit and reporting requirements for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) operations to assess performance, identify improvement opportunities, and ensure regulatory compliance.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- Energy audit types and methodologies
- Audit planning and execution
- Reporting requirements
- Performance metrics and benchmarking
- Continuous monitoring and reporting

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ISO 50002 (Energy Audits)
- ISO 50001 (Energy Management Systems)
- ISO 50006 (Measuring Energy Performance)
- ISO 50015 (Measurement and Verification)
- ASHRAE Level I/II/III Energy Audits
- IPMVP (International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol)

## 4. Energy System Description

### 4.1 Overview
Energy audits systematically assess energy use, identify inefficiencies, and recommend improvements. Regular reporting tracks performance, demonstrates compliance, and supports data-driven decision-making.

### 4.2 Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Energy Audit Frequency | Annual (minimum) | Level I or II audit |
| Detailed Audit | Every 3-5 years | Level II or III audit |
| Audit Coverage | 100% of energy use | All systems and end-uses |
| Reporting Frequency | Monthly (internal), Annual (external) | Performance and compliance |
| Energy Savings Identification | Minimum 10% of consumption | Target from each audit |

### 4.3 Performance Requirements

**Audit Objectives**:
- Quantify energy consumption by system and end-use
- Identify energy saving opportunities
- Estimate savings potential and implementation costs
- Prioritize opportunities based on cost-benefit analysis
- Track implementation progress

**Reporting Objectives**:
- Communicate energy performance to stakeholders
- Demonstrate regulatory and certification compliance
- Support continuous improvement initiatives
- Enable benchmarking and target setting

### 4.4 Energy Audit Types

#### 4.4.1 Level I - Walk-Through Audit
**Scope**: Preliminary assessment, identify low-cost/no-cost opportunities

**Activities**:
- Site walkthrough and visual inspection
- Review of energy bills and historical data
- Interview with operations and maintenance staff
- Identify obvious inefficiencies
- Estimate savings potential (rough order of magnitude)

**Duration**: 1-2 days
**Cost**: Low
**Frequency**: Annually or as needed

#### 4.4.2 Level II - Detailed Energy Audit
**Scope**: Comprehensive analysis, quantify savings, investment analysis

**Activities**:
- Detailed energy balance and end-use breakdown
- Sub-metering and data logging (if needed)
- Analysis of systems and equipment performance
- Identify and evaluate energy conservation measures (ECMs)
- Financial analysis (payback, NPV, IRR)
- Prioritized list of recommendations

**Duration**: 2-4 weeks
**Cost**: Medium
**Frequency**: Every 3-5 years or when major changes

#### 4.4.3 Level III - Investment-Grade Audit
**Scope**: Detailed engineering analysis for major capital projects

**Activities**:
- All Level II activities plus:
- Detailed measurements and monitoring
- Engineering calculations and simulations
- Detailed design and specifications
- Firm quotes from vendors/contractors
- Financial analysis suitable for investment decisions

**Duration**: 1-3 months
**Cost**: High
**Frequency**: As needed for major projects

### 4.5 Audit Methodology

#### 4.5.1 Planning
- Define audit scope and objectives
- Assemble audit team (internal staff, external consultants)
- Collect preliminary data (energy bills, drawings, equipment lists)
- Schedule site visit and interviews

#### 4.5.2 Data Collection
- Energy bills and tariff structures
- Weather data
- Operational schedules and production data
- Equipment nameplate data and specifications
- Existing energy management systems and controls
- Maintenance records

#### 4.5.3 Site Visit and Inspection
- Walkthrough of all facilities and systems
- Observe operations and equipment performance
- Identify obvious inefficiencies
- Take measurements (if Level II or III audit)
- Photograph equipment and conditions

#### 4.5.4 Analysis
- Energy balance: Account for all energy inputs and outputs
- End-use breakdown: Allocate energy to systems (lighting, HVAC, equipment, etc.)
- Benchmarking: Compare to industry standards or best practices
- Identify ECMs: List potential improvements
- Estimate savings: Calculate energy and cost savings for each ECM
- Financial analysis: Payback period, NPV, IRR for each ECM

#### 4.5.5 Reporting
- Executive summary: Key findings and recommendations
- Facility description: Operations, energy systems
- Energy use summary: Current consumption, end-use breakdown
- ECM descriptions: Technical details, savings estimates, costs
- Financial analysis: Prioritization matrix
- Implementation plan: Phasing, timeline, responsibilities

### 4.6 Performance Metrics and Reporting

#### 4.6.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Energy Consumption**: kWh, MWh per month/year
- **Energy Intensity**: Energy per unit output (kWh per vehicle serviced, per flight, etc.)
- **Energy Cost**: Total cost, cost per unit output
- **Renewable Fraction**: % of energy from renewable sources
- **Carbon Intensity**: Tons CO2e per unit output
- **Energy Savings**: kWh and cost savings from implemented ECMs

#### 4.6.2 Internal Reporting (Monthly)
**Audience**: Operations managers, energy team

**Content**:
- Energy consumption trend (vs. previous months, vs. target)
- Major variances and explanations
- Progress on energy projects
- New issues or opportunities

**Format**: Dashboard, brief report (1-2 pages)

#### 4.6.3 External Reporting (Annual)
**Audience**: Senior management, regulatory agencies, certification bodies, stakeholders

**Content**:
- Annual energy performance summary
- Progress toward energy and emission reduction targets
- Completed energy projects and savings achieved
- Compliance with regulations and certifications (ISO 50001, GHG reporting, etc.)
- Plans for the coming year

**Format**: Formal report, sustainability report, or integrated annual report

#### 4.6.4 Regulatory Reporting
**GHG Emissions**:
- ISO 14064, GHG Protocol
- Scope 1, 2, and relevant Scope 3 emissions
- Verification if required

**Energy Management**:
- ISO 50001 annual management review
- Progress on energy objectives and action plans

**Other**:
- Local energy reporting requirements
- Airport Carbon Accreditation (if applicable)

### 4.7 Continuous Monitoring

**Real-Time Monitoring**:
- Energy Management System (EMS) with dashboards
- Real-time visibility into energy consumption
- Automated alerts for abnormal conditions

**Periodic Reviews**:
- Weekly or monthly review of energy data
- Investigate variances and anomalies
- Adjust operations or schedule maintenance as needed

**Measurement and Verification (M&V)**:
- Per ISO 50015 or IPMVP
- Verify savings from implemented ECMs
- Baseline vs. post-implementation comparison
- Adjust for variables (weather, production, etc.)

### 4.8 Audit Follow-Up and Implementation

**Action Plan**:
- Prioritize ECMs based on savings, cost, strategic fit
- Assign responsibilities and timelines
- Secure funding and approvals

**Implementation**:
- Execute projects per plan
- Monitor progress and budget
- Commission and verify performance

**Tracking**:
- Document completed projects
- Calculate actual savings (M&V)
- Update energy performance metrics
- Communicate results to stakeholders
