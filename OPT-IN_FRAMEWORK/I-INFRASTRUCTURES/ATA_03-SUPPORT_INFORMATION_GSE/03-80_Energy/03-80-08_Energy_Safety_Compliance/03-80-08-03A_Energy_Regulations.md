# 03-80-08-03A - Energy Regulations

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies regulatory compliance requirements for Ground Support Equipment (GSE) energy systems, ensuring adherence to applicable laws, codes, standards, and regulations.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- Regulatory framework overview
- Electrical energy regulations
- H2 energy regulations
- Environmental and emissions regulations
- Compliance management and documentation

## 3. Applicable Documents
- Local and national electrical codes (NFPA 70, IEC 60364, etc.)
- H2 safety codes (NFPA 2, ISO 19880, SAE AS6968)
- Environmental regulations (EPA, EU directives, local air quality standards)
- Building and fire codes
- Occupational safety regulations (OSHA, local equivalents)
- Airport operating regulations and standards

## 4. Energy System Description

### 4.1 Overview
Regulatory compliance ensures GSE energy systems meet all applicable legal and standard requirements for safety, performance, environmental protection, and interoperability.

### 4.2 Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Compliance Verification | Annual audits | Internal and/or external |
| Documentation Retention | Minimum 10 years | Design, test, inspection records |
| Permit Compliance | 100% | All required permits obtained and maintained |
| Regulatory Updates | Quarterly review | Monitor for new/changed regulations |
| Non-Conformance Response | <30 days | Corrective action for identified issues |

### 4.3 Performance Requirements

**Regulatory Compliance**:
- Identify all applicable regulations
- Demonstrate compliance through design, testing, documentation
- Maintain compliance through operations and maintenance
- Respond promptly to regulatory changes and non-conformances

### 4.4 Electrical Energy Regulations

#### 4.4.1 Installation Standards
- **NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code)** or **IEC 60364** (International)
- Local electrical codes and amendments
- Compliance demonstration: Design review, inspection, testing

#### 4.4.2 Grid Interconnection
- **IEEE 1547** (USA) or local grid codes
- Utility interconnection agreements
- Protection, power quality, islanding requirements
- Compliance: Interconnection application, testing, commissioning

#### 4.4.3 EV Charging Standards
- **IEC 61851** (EV Charging System)
- **SAE J1772** (USA) or **IEC 62196** (International) connectors
- Safety, communication, interoperability requirements

### 4.5 H2 Energy Regulations

#### 4.5.1 H2 Safety Codes
- **NFPA 2** (Hydrogen Technologies Code) - USA
- **ISO 19880-1** (Gaseous Hydrogen Fueling Stations) - International
- **SAE AS6968** (Hydrogen Aircraft Refueling) - Aviation-specific
- Compliance: Hazard analysis, safety systems, operational procedures

#### 4.5.2 Pressure Equipment Regulations
- **ASME BPVC** (USA) or **EN 13445/PED** (Europe)
- Design, fabrication, testing, inspection of pressure vessels
- Compliance: Design certification, third-party inspection, documentation

#### 4.5.3 H2 Quality Standards
- **ISO 14687** (Hydrogen Fuel Quality)
- Purity specifications for fuel cell applications
- Compliance: Sampling, testing, certification

#### 4.5.4 H2 Transport Regulations
- **ADR/RID** (Europe), **DOT** (USA), **IATA DGR** (Air transport)
- Safe transport of compressed and liquid H2
- Compliance: Packaging, labeling, documentation, training

### 4.6 Environmental and Emissions Regulations

#### 4.6.1 Air Quality Regulations
- Local and national air quality standards (PM, NOx, SOx, VOC, etc.)
- Emission limits for diesel/gasoline engines, generators
- Compliance: Emissions testing, aftertreatment systems, reporting

#### 4.6.2 GHG Emissions Reporting
- **ISO 14064** (GHG Accounting)
- **GHG Protocol** (Corporate Reporting)
- Airport Carbon Accreditation (ACI) - if applicable
- Compliance: Annual GHG inventory, verification, reporting

#### 4.6.3 Environmental Management
- **ISO 14001** (Environmental Management System)
- Waste management, pollution prevention, resource conservation
- Compliance: EMS implementation, audits, certification

### 4.7 Building and Fire Codes

**Building Codes**:
- International Building Code (IBC) or local equivalent
- Structural, electrical, mechanical requirements for facilities

**Fire Codes**:
- NFPA 1 (Fire Code), NFPA 2 (H2), NFPA 70, NFPA 110 (Emergency Power), NFPA 855 (Energy Storage)
- Fire protection, egress, detection, suppression
- Compliance: Design review, fire marshal approval, inspections

### 4.8 Occupational Safety Regulations

**Safety Management**:
- **ISO 45001** (Occupational Health and Safety)
- **OSHA** (USA) or local equivalent regulations
- Workplace safety, hazard communication, PPE, training

**Specific Safety Standards**:
- **NFPA 70E** (Electrical Safety in Workplace)
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147** (Lockout/Tagout)
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146** (Confined Spaces)

### 4.9 Permits and Approvals

**Required Permits** (examples, varies by location):
- Electrical installation permit
- Building permit (new construction, major modifications)
- Fire safety permit
- H2 storage and dispensing permit
- Air quality permit (if applicable for emissions sources)
- Environmental permit (discharge, waste handling)

**Permitting Process**:
- Identify applicable permits early in project
- Submit applications with required documentation
- Address reviewer comments and revisions
- Obtain permits before construction/operation
- Maintain permits (renewals, updates for modifications)

### 4.10 Compliance Management

#### 4.10.1 Compliance Register
- Maintain list of all applicable regulations, codes, standards, permits
- Assign responsibility for compliance monitoring
- Update regularly (at least quarterly)

#### 4.10.2 Compliance Audits
- Internal audits: Annual or more frequent
- External audits: As required by regulations or certifications
- Audit findings: Track and close out corrective actions

#### 4.10.3 Documentation and Records
**Design and Construction**:
- Design drawings and calculations
- Equipment certifications and test reports
- Installation records and commissioning reports
- Permit applications and approvals

**Operations and Maintenance**:
- Operating procedures
- Maintenance records
- Inspection and testing records
- Training records
- Incident reports

**Retention**: Minimum 10 years, or as required by specific regulations

#### 4.10.4 Regulatory Updates
- Monitor for new and changed regulations
- Assess impact on GSE energy systems
- Implement changes as required
- Update compliance register and procedures

#### 4.10.5 Non-Conformance Management
- Identify non-conformances through audits, inspections, incidents
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective and preventive actions (CAPA)
- Verification of effectiveness
- Documentation and closeout
