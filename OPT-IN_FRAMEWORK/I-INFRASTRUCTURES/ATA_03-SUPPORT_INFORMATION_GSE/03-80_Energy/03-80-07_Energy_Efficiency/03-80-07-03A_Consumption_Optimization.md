# 03-80-07-03A - Consumption Optimization

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies strategies and techniques for optimizing energy consumption in Ground Support Equipment (GSE) operations to minimize waste and improve overall efficiency.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- Operational optimization strategies
- Equipment utilization optimization
- Process efficiency improvements
- Behavioral and organizational measures
- Data-driven optimization techniques

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ISO 50001 (Energy Management Systems)
- ISO 50006 (Measuring Energy Performance)
- Lean Six Sigma methodologies
- Total Productive Maintenance (TPM)

## 4. Energy System Description

### 4.1 Overview
Consumption optimization focuses on reducing energy waste and improving efficiency through operational improvements, better equipment utilization, process optimization, and organizational measures.

### 4.2 Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Energy Savings Potential | 10-30% | Through optimization measures |
| Implementation Cost | Low to medium | Many measures have low/no cost |
| Payback Period | <2 years | For most optimization measures |
| Continuous Improvement Rate | 2-3% annual reduction | Ongoing optimization |

### 4.3 Performance Requirements

**Optimization Objectives**:
- Minimize energy consumption per unit of output
- Eliminate energy waste (idle equipment, inefficient processes)
- Improve equipment utilization and load factors
- Enable data-driven decision-making

### 4.4 Operational Optimization

#### 4.4.1 Equipment Utilization
**Right-Sizing**:
- Match equipment capacity to actual needs
- Avoid oversized equipment operating at low efficiency

**Scheduling and Pooling**:
- Optimize GSE utilization schedules to minimize idle time
- Equipment pooling to improve overall fleet utilization
- Consolidate operations to reduce number of active units

**Idle Reduction**:
- Automatic shut-off for equipment idle >X minutes
- Training operators to turn off equipment when not in use
- Incentives for reducing idle time

#### 4.4.2 Process Optimization
**Workflow Efficiency**:
- Streamline GSE operational processes (reduce travel distances, waiting times)
- Lean principles to eliminate waste
- Just-in-time (JIT) approaches where applicable

**Load Optimization**:
- Maximize load per trip/operation (e.g., fuller cargo loaders)
- Optimize routing to minimize distance traveled

**Maintenance Optimization**:
- Predictive maintenance to maintain peak efficiency
- Timely replacement of worn components (seals, bearings, filters)
- Regular calibration and tuning

#### 4.4.3 Energy System Optimization
**Charging Optimization**:
- Smart charging algorithms to minimize losses
- Optimal charge/discharge profiles for battery longevity
- Load management to avoid simultaneous charging (peak demand)

**H2 Production Optimization**:
- Operate electrolyzers at optimal efficiency point
- Coordinate production with renewable generation
- Balance production vs. storage vs. demand

**Grid Interaction Optimization**:
- Time-of-use optimization (use energy during low-cost periods)
- Demand response participation
- Peak shaving to reduce demand charges

### 4.5 Data-Driven Optimization

**Energy Analytics**:
- Analyze energy consumption data to identify inefficiencies
- Correlate energy use with operational parameters
- Identify outliers and anomalies

**Machine Learning / AI**:
- Predictive models for energy demand
- Optimization algorithms for complex multi-variable systems
- Anomaly detection for early fault identification

**Digital Twin**:
- Virtual model of energy system
- Simulate different operating scenarios
- Optimize before implementing in real world

### 4.6 Behavioral and Organizational Measures

**Training and Awareness**:
- Energy efficiency training for operators and managers
- Awareness campaigns (posters, newsletters, meetings)
- Best practice sharing

**Incentives and Recognition**:
- Reward teams/individuals for energy savings achievements
- Energy efficiency competitions between departments/shifts
- Recognition programs

**Energy Champions**:
- Designate energy champions in each department
- Empower them to drive energy efficiency initiatives
- Provide training and resources

**Management Commitment**:
- Top management commitment to energy efficiency
- Energy performance included in KPIs and performance reviews
- Resources allocated for energy efficiency projects

### 4.7 Continuous Improvement

**Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) Cycle**:
- Plan: Set energy objectives, identify opportunities
- Do: Implement energy efficiency measures
- Check: Monitor and measure results
- Act: Adjust strategies based on results, standardize successful measures

**Energy Performance Indicators (EnPIs)**:
- Define relevant EnPIs (energy intensity, specific consumption, efficiency ratios)
- Track EnPIs regularly
- Set targets for improvement

**Energy Audits and Reviews**:
- Periodic energy audits to identify new opportunities
- Management review of energy performance
- Update energy action plans
