# 03-80-07-04A - Carbon Footprint Reduction

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies strategies and metrics for reducing carbon footprint of Ground Support Equipment (GSE) energy operations, supporting decarbonization goals and climate commitments.

## 2. Scope
This specification covers:
- Carbon footprint measurement and reporting
- Decarbonization strategies
- Renewable energy procurement
- Carbon offsetting and removal
- Net-zero pathway planning

## 3. Applicable Documents
- ISO 14064 (Greenhouse Gas Accounting and Verification)
- GHG Protocol (Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard)
- ISO 14001 (Environmental Management Systems)
- Airport Carbon Accreditation (ACI)
- Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)
- ICAO CORSIA

## 4. Energy System Description

### 4.1 Overview
Carbon footprint reduction strategies systematically decrease greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from GSE energy operations through efficiency improvements, renewable energy adoption, and offsetting residual emissions.

### 4.2 Specifications
| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Carbon Reduction Target | Net-zero by 2050 | Aligned with IATA goals |
| Interim Targets | 25% (2030), 75% (2040) | Progressive milestones |
| Renewable Energy Fraction | 100% by 2050 | Electricity and H2 |
| Carbon Intensity Reduction | 5-10% per year | Tons CO2e per operational unit |
| Reporting Standard | ISO 14064, GHG Protocol | Annual reporting |

### 4.3 Performance Requirements

**Carbon Accounting**:
- Comprehensive GHG inventory per ISO 14064
- Scopes 1, 2, and relevant Scope 3 emissions
- Annual reporting and verification

**Reduction Targets**:
- Set science-based targets (SBTi)
- Track progress against targets
- Transparent reporting

### 4.4 Carbon Footprint Measurement

#### 4.4.1 Emission Scopes
**Scope 1 (Direct Emissions)**:
- Fossil fuel combustion in GSE (diesel, gasoline, natural gas)
- Fugitive emissions (refrigerants, H2 leakage)

**Scope 2 (Indirect Emissions from Purchased Energy)**:
- Grid electricity consumption
- Purchased steam/heating/cooling

**Scope 3 (Other Indirect Emissions)**:
- Upstream emissions (fuel/electricity production and transport)
- Equipment manufacturing and disposal
- Employee commuting (if relevant to GSE operations)

#### 4.4.2 Carbon Intensity Metrics
- **Total Emissions**: Tons CO2e per year
- **Emission Intensity**: Tons CO2e per flight served, per vehicle serviced, per unit cargo handled
- **Grid Emission Factor**: Tons CO2e per MWh (location-based and market-based)

### 4.5 Decarbonization Strategies

#### 4.5.1 Energy Efficiency
- Reduce total energy consumption (see 03-80-07-03A)
- Lower emissions even with same energy mix

#### 4.5.2 Fuel Switching
- Diesel/gasoline → Battery-electric or H2 fuel cell
- Natural gas → Green H2 (for backup generators, heating)
- Zero direct emissions at point of use

#### 4.5.3 Renewable Energy Procurement
**On-Site Generation**:
- Solar PV, wind turbines
- Directly offset fossil fuel-based grid electricity
- See 03-80-04_Renewable_Energy_GSE

**Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs)**:
- Long-term contracts for renewable electricity
- Financial support for new renewable projects (additionality)

**Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) / Guarantees of Origin (GOs)**:
- Purchase certificates to match electricity consumption with renewable generation
- Ensures market-based Scope 2 emissions = zero
- Lower impact than PPAs (no additionality guarantee)

#### 4.5.4 Green Hydrogen Production
- Electrolysis with 100% renewable electricity
- Near-zero carbon intensity
- Critical for heavy-duty GSE and long-duration energy storage

#### 4.5.5 Carbon Capture and Removal (Residual Emissions)
**Carbon Offsetting**:
- Purchase verified carbon credits to neutralize residual emissions
- Projects: Reforestation, renewable energy projects, methane capture, etc.
- Quality standards: Gold Standard, Verified Carbon Standard (VCS), etc.

**Carbon Removal**:
- Direct Air Capture (DAC) and sequestration
- Bioenergy with Carbon Capture and Storage (BECCS)
- Nature-based solutions (reforestation, soil carbon sequestration)
- Emerging, higher cost, but necessary for net-zero

### 4.6 Net-Zero Pathway

**Phase 1 (2025-2030): Foundation**:
- Establish baseline emissions inventory
- Set reduction targets aligned with SBTi
- Begin transition to electric and H2 GSE
- Deploy initial renewable energy projects
- Target: 25% emission reduction

**Phase 2 (2031-2040): Acceleration**:
- Rapid fleet electrification and H2 conversion
- Scale up renewable energy (on-site and PPAs)
- Achieve 100% renewable electricity
- Target: 75% emission reduction

**Phase 3 (2041-2050): Net-Zero**:
- Complete fleet transition to zero-emission technologies
- 100% green H2 for all H2 applications
- Offset/remove residual emissions (if any)
- Target: Net-zero emissions

### 4.7 Carbon Reporting and Verification

**Annual GHG Inventory**:
- Calculate Scope 1, 2, 3 emissions per ISO 14064
- Use emission factors from IEA, EPA, or local sources
- Document assumptions and methodologies

**Third-Party Verification**:
- Independent verification of GHG inventory (recommended for credibility)
- ISO 14064-3 verification standard

**Public Disclosure**:
- Sustainability report or integrated annual report
- CDP (formerly Carbon Disclosure Project) submission
- Airport Carbon Accreditation (if applicable)

**Continuous Improvement**:
- Review performance vs. targets
- Adjust strategies as needed
- Engage stakeholders (airlines, airport, regulators, community)
