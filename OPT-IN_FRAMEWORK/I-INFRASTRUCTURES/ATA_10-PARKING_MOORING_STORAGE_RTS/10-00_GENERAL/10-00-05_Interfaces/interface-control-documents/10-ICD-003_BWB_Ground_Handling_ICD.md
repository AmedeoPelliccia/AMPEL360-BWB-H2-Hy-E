## 2. Purpose

This ICD consolidates all BWB (Blended Wing Body) specific ground handling interfaces, addressing the unique challenges and requirements of the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft configuration.

## 3. Scope

Covers BWB-specific aspects of:
- Structural attachment points and load paths
- Towing and pushback operations
- Jacking and leveling procedures
- Ground clearances and access
- Ground equipment compatibility

## 5. BWB Geometric Characteristics

### 5.1 Dimensions
- Maximum span: 88 m
- Overall length: 68 m
- Maximum width (center body): 42 m
- Height: 12.5 m
- Ground clearance (minimum): 2.8 m

### 5.2 Mass Properties
- Maximum ramp weight: 365,000 kg
- CG range (longitudinal): 45-58% MAC (wide range)
- CG range (lateral): ±0.3 m from centerline

## 6. BWB-Specific Ground Operations

### 6.1 Towing
- Wide turning radius due to 88 m span
- Heavy-duty tow vehicle required (>40 tons capacity)
- Minimum 2 wing walkers required
- Apron width: 65 m minimum for 180° turn

### 6.2 Jacking
- 6-point jacking system (3 forward, 3 aft)
- Optimized for BWB load distribution
- Lower ground clearance benefits accessibility
- Wide stance provides excellent stability

### 6.3 Parking
- Parking stand dimensions: 90 m × 90 m minimum
- Clearance from obstacles: 7.5 m (wing tips)
- Jetway compatibility: Custom passenger boarding solutions
- Ground service vehicle access: Under-body clearance 2.8 m

## 7. BWB Ground Handling Challenges

### 7.1 Non-Conventional Geometry
- Training required for personnel unfamiliar with BWB
- Specialized GSE may be required
- Clearance planning more complex

### 7.2 Access
- Large wing surface area limits access points
- Internal access routes may be lengthy
- Aerodynamic surface protection critical

### 7.3 Infrastructure Adaptation
- Standard airport infrastructure may require modification
- Hangar door width: 45 m minimum
- Custom ground support equipment

## 8. BWB Ground Handling Advantages

### 8.1 Stability
- Wide landing gear track provides excellent ground stability
- Low center of gravity reduces tip-over risk
- Resistant to cross-winds

### 8.2 Access
- Low profile enables ground-level access to most systems
- Reduced need for high-reach equipment
- Improved ergonomics for ground crew

### 8.3 Load Distribution
- Wide structure distributes loads over large area
- Reduced stress concentrations
- Efficient load paths
