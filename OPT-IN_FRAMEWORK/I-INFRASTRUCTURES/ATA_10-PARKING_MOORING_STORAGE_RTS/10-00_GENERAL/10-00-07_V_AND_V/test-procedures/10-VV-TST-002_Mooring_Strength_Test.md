## 2. Purpose

Verify mooring attachment points and mooring line strength for securing AMPEL360 BWB H₂ aircraft at coastal or water-adjacent facilities.

## 3. Scope

- Mooring attachment points (4 specimens)
- Mooring lines and hardware
- Combined static and dynamic loading
- Salt spray exposure testing

## 4. Requirements Verified

| Req ID | Requirement | Verification Method |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| REQ-10-02-001 | Mooring points withstand 2.0× maximum mooring load | Test |
| REQ-10-02-003 | Corrosion resistance in marine environment | Test |

## 5. Test Procedure Summary

1. Pre-test inspection and corrosion exposure (1000 hrs salt spray per ASTM B117)
2. Static load test to 200% design load
3. Dynamic cyclic loading (1000 cycles at 100% load)
4. Ultimate load test to failure
5. Post-test inspection and documentation

## 6. Pass/Fail Criteria

- No failure below 200% design load
- Corrosion does not reduce strength > 5%
- Dynamic cycling causes no crack initiation
