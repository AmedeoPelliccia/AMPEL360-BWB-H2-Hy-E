## 2. Purpose

Verify cryogenic system components function correctly at LH₂ temperature (-253°C) during parking and storage.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 Components Tested
- Cryogenic valves
- Insulation materials
- Seals and gaskets
- Structural joints
- Temperature sensors
- Pressure relief devices

### 3.2 Test Conditions
- Temperature: -253°C (LH₂ temperature)
- Thermal cycling: -253°C to +50°C (100 cycles)
- Vacuum insulation testing
- Material compatibility verification

## 4. Test Procedure Summary

1. Material compatibility test at -253°C (tensile, impact, leak)
2. Valve operation test at cryogenic temperature (100 cycles)
3. Insulation performance test (boiloff rate, thermal conductivity)
4. Seal leak test at -253°C (helium leak test)
5. Thermal cycling endurance test (100 cycles)
6. Pressure relief device function test at cryo temperature
