## 2. Purpose

Verify H₂ venting system safely disperses hydrogen during parking/storage with adequate flow rate and safe dilution.

## 3. Scope

- Vent valve operation at cryogenic temperature (-253°C)
- Flow rate verification
- H₂ dispersion pattern measurement
- Safe concentration verification at defined distances

## 4. Test Procedure Summary

1. Vent valve operational test at -253°C (10 cycles)
2. Flow rate measurement under various conditions
3. H₂ dispersion measurement (outdoor facility)
4. Concentration mapping at 5m, 10m, 15m, 30m distances
5. Wind effect testing (various wind speeds and directions)
6. Emergency vent scenario testing

## 5. Pass/Fail Criteria

- Vent valve operates reliably at -253°C (100% success rate)
- Flow rate meets design requirement (± 10%)
- H₂ concentration < 4% vol at 10m from vent outlet (all wind conditions)
- Emergency vent completes in < 5 minutes
