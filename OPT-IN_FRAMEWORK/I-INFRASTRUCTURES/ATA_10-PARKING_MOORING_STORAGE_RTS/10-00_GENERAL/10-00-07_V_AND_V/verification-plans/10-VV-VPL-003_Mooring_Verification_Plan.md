## 5. Verification Approach

### 5.1 Strength Testing
**Test Procedure**: 10-VV-TST-002 - Mooring Strength Test

**Test Objectives**:
- Verify mooring cable/hardware strength
- Test mast structural capacity
- Validate attachment point strength
- Demonstrate safety factors

**Test Method**:
- Static pull testing to ultimate load
- Dynamic loading for fatigue assessment
- Combined load testing (vertical + lateral)

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- No failure at ultimate load
- Safety factor ≥ 1.5 demonstrated

### 5.2 Environmental Testing
**Objectives**:
- Verify corrosion resistance
- Test weather exposure durability
- Validate UV resistance
- Demonstrate performance in temperature extremes

**Test Method**:
- Salt spray testing (ASTM B117 or equivalent)
- UV exposure testing
- Temperature cycling (-40°C to +60°C)
- Accelerated aging tests

**Duration**: Equivalent to TBD years service life

### 5.3 Wind Load Analysis
**Method**: CFD analysis + wind tunnel testing (optional)

**Objectives**:
- Determine wind loads on moored aircraft
- Validate mooring system adequacy
- Identify critical wind directions
- Optimize mooring configuration

**Analysis Conditions**:
- Wind speeds: 0 to design maximum
- Various wind directions
- BWB configuration considerations
- Ground proximity effects

**Document**: 10-VV-ANL-004 - CFD Analysis Verification

### 5.4 Fatigue Analysis and Testing
**Objectives**:
- Verify fatigue life of mooring components
- Validate cyclic load resistance
- Identify wear patterns

**Method**:
- Fatigue analysis per appropriate standards
- Cyclic testing to specified cycles
- Inspection for crack initiation
