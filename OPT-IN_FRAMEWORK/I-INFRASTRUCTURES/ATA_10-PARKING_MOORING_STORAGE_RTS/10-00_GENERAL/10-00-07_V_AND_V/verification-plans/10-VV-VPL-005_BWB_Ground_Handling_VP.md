## 2. Purpose

This Verification Plan addresses the unique verification needs for ground handling, parking, and storage operations specific to the Blended Wing Body (BWB) configuration of the AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 BWB-Specific Aspects Covered
- Non-traditional fuselage shape handling
- Wide stance landing gear configuration
- Center of gravity range effects on parking
- Large planform area considerations
- Access and servicing point locations
- Ground clearance and obstacle avoidance
- Unique towing and pushback requirements
- Wide body integration with facilities

### 3.2 Operations Covered
- Towing and pushback
- Parking and positioning
- Tiedown and mooring
- Access for servicing
- Ground support equipment integration
- H2 servicing with BWB configuration

## 6. Specific Verification Areas

### 6.1 Towing and Pushback

#### 6.1.1 Towing Force Verification
**Requirement**: Towing forces within standard GSE capability

**Verification**:
- Calculation of towing forces (level, grade, turn)
- Consideration of weight and rolling resistance
- Validation with actual towing measurements
- Comparison with available tow tractor capabilities

**Test**: Measure actual towing forces in representative conditions

#### 6.1.2 Tow Bar and Attachment
**Requirements**:
- Compatible with BWB nose gear
- Adequate strength for towing forces
- Safe attachment and detachment

**Verification**:
- Structural analysis of tow bar and attachment
- Load testing of tow bar
- Demonstration of attachment/detachment
- Safety assessment

#### 6.1.3 Clearance Verification
**Challenge**: Wide body clearances during towing

**Verification**:
- 3D CAD analysis of aircraft and facility clearances
- Physical measurements during demonstrations
- Validation of minimum clearances
- Identification of restricted paths

### 6.2 Parking and Positioning
