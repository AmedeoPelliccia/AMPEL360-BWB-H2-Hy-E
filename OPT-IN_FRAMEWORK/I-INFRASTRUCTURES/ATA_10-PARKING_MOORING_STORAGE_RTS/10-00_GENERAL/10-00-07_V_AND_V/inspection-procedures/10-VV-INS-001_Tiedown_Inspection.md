## 2. Purpose

Inspection checklist for tiedown inspection to verify continued airworthiness and safe operation.

## 3. Inspection Items

### 3.1 Visual Inspection
- General condition (no damage, corrosion, wear)
- Correct installation and security
- Markings and labels legible

### 3.2 Functional Inspection
- Operational checks per system
- Safety devices functional
- No anomalies observed

### 3.3 Documentation
- Inspection records current
- Calibration certificates valid (if applicable)
- Maintenance logs complete

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

All inspection items pass or discrepancies documented with corrective action.
