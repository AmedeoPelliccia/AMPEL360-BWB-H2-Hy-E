This is the **official Entry Into Service (EIS) release** of the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft ATA 10 (Parking, Mooring, Storage & RTS) systems, marking the transition from development to production and commercial operation.

## What's New in v1.0.0

### Major Features

✅ **Type Certificate Configuration**
- Complete ATA 10 system certified for production
- All CS-25 compliance demonstrations complete
- H2 fuel system certified per Special Condition
- BWB configuration certified per Special Condition

✅ **H2 Fuel System - Production Ready**
- LH2 tank system fully certified
- Triple-redundant H2 detection system
- Automated vent and pressure management
- Certified safety zones (5m/15m/25m)
- Ground handling procedures approved

✅ **BWB Configuration - Production Ready**
- Wide-body parking position system
- BWB-specific ground handling points
- Wing clearance monitoring certified
- Special mooring system for BWB structure

✅ **Operational Procedures - Certified**
- Standard parking procedures
- H2-specific parking and defueling procedures
- Long-term storage procedures
- BWB-specific handling procedures
- Emergency response procedures

## Known Limitations

### Operational Limitations

⚠️ **H2 System Limitations**:
- Maximum parking duration with LH2: 12 hours
- Requires airport H2 infrastructure
- Ground crew H2 certification mandatory
- Minimum 25m separation from ignition sources

⚠️ **BWB Configuration Limitations**:
- Requires wide parking positions (>X meters)
- Special ground handling equipment required
- Not all airports BWB-compatible (see Airport Compatibility List)

⚠️ **Environmental Limitations**:
- Maximum wind for mooring: 65 knots
- LH2 operations temperature range: [TBD]
- Altitude restrictions: Per AFM Section 2

None. All certification findings closed.
