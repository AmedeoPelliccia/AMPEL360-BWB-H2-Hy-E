## 1. Purpose

This Operations Manual serves as the master reference for all parking, mooring, storage, and return-to-service (RTS) operations for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft. It establishes operational policies, responsibilities, and procedures for safe and efficient ground handling.

- Aircraft parking procedures
- Mooring and tiedown operations

## 5. Operational Philosophy

### 5.1 Safety-First Approach

All operations prioritize:
1. **Personnel safety** — Zero-harm philosophy
2. **Aircraft protection** — Prevent damage
3. **Environmental protection** — Minimize emissions, prevent spills
4. **Regulatory compliance** — Full adherence to standards

### 5.2 H₂ Operations Principles

- **Assume leak until proven safe**: Continuous monitoring
- **Ventilation priority**: Prevent accumulation in confined spaces
- **Hot work prohibition**: No ignition sources near H₂ systems
- **Safety zones**: Enforce hot/warm/cold zone protocols
- **Emergency readiness**: Immediate response capability

### 5.3 BWB Operations Principles

- **Wide-body awareness**: Account for non-standard wingspan and clearances
- **Distributed load**: BWB geometry requires distributed mooring points
- **Access considerations**: Ground equipment positioning differs from conventional aircraft
- **Center of gravity**: BWB CG location affects towing and jacking

## 6. Pre-Operational Requirements

### 6.1 Personnel Qualifications

All personnel must:
- Complete general ground operations training
- Achieve role-specific certifications
- Pass safety knowledge assessments
- Maintain current medical fitness (where applicable)

**Additional H₂ Operations Requirements:**
- H₂ Handler Certification (see [10-00-14-61A](../personnel-qualifications/10-00-14-61A_H2_Handler_Certification.md))
- Cryo Handler Certification (see [10-00-14-62A](../personnel-qualifications/10-00-14-62A_Cryo_Handler_Certification.md))
- Emergency response training

### 6.2 Equipment Requirements

- Functional ground support equipment (GSE)
- H₂ detection equipment (calibrated, current)
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) — see [10-00-14-41A](../safety-operations/10-00-14-41A_PPE_Requirements.md)
- Communication equipment (radio, emergency alarms)
- Fire suppression equipment (appropriate for H₂ fires)

### 6.3 Environmental Conditions

Operations may be restricted based on:
- **Wind speed**: Max 45 kt for parking, 35 kt for mooring operations
- **Temperature**: Cryo operations limited to -20°C to +45°C ambient
- **Precipitation**: No active precipitation during H₂ venting operations
- **Visibility**: Minimum 1 statute mile for aircraft movement

## 7. Standard Operating Procedures

### 7.1 Pre-Parking Checklist

Prior to parking the aircraft:

1. **Aircraft Systems**
   - [ ] Main engines shut down
   - [ ] APU status confirmed (running/shut down as required)
   - [ ] H₂ system in safe mode
   - [ ] Electrical systems isolated (except monitoring)
   - [ ] Hydraulic systems depressurized

2. **H₂ System Checks** (Certified H₂ Handler required)
   - [ ] H₂ tank pressure within normal range
   - [ ] No active H₂ leaks detected
   - [ ] Vent system operational
   - [ ] Boil-off rate within limits
   - [ ] Ground vent connection ready (if required)

3. **Ground Area Preparation**
   - [ ] Parking spot inspected and clear
   - [ ] Drainage adequate for potential LH₂ spill
   - [ ] H₂ safety zone established
   - [ ] Fire suppression equipment positioned
   - [ ] Wind direction favorable (downwind venting)

4. **Personnel and Equipment**
   - [ ] Qualified personnel on duty
   - [ ] GSE functional and positioned
   - [ ] PPE available and inspected
   - [ ] H₂ detectors functional (bump tested)
   - [ ] Communication systems operational

## 8. Emergency Procedures

### 8.1 Emergency Classification

| Class | Description | Examples | Response |
|-------|-------------|----------|----------|
| **Critical** | Immediate threat to life or aircraft | H₂ fire, major leak | EVACUATE, call emergency services |
| **Major** | Significant hazard requiring urgent action | H₂ leak, cryo spill | Implement emergency procedures |
| **Minor** | Limited hazard, controlled response | Equipment failure, minor damage | Follow contingency procedures |

## 11. Training Requirements

### 11.1 Initial Training

All personnel must complete:
- General ground operations safety (8 hours)
- Aircraft-specific training (BWB configuration) (4 hours)
- H₂ hazard awareness (4 hours)
- Emergency response basics (4 hours)

**H₂ Handler Certification**: 40 hours (theory + practical)  
**Cryo Handler Certification**: 24 hours (theory + practical)

### 11.2 Recurrent Training

- **Annual**: H₂ safety refresher (4 hours)
- **Annual**: Emergency procedures review (2 hours)
- **Biennial**: Full ground operations recertification (16 hours)
