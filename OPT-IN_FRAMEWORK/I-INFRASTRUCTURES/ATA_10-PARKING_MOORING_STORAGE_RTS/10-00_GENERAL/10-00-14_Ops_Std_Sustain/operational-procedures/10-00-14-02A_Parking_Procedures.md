## 1. Purpose

Standard procedures for parking the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft at airports and maintenance facilities.

---

## 2. Scope

- Short-term parking (< 24 hours)
- Medium-term parking (24 hours - 7 days)
- Parking spot selection and preparation
- Aircraft positioning and securing
- Post-parking configuration

---

## 3. Parking Spot Selection

### 3.1 Criteria

- **Size**: Adequate for BWB dimensions (see [10-00-14-06A](10-00-14-06A_BWB_Ground_Ops_Procedures.md))
- **Surface**: Paved, level, load-bearing
- **Clearances**: Minimum clearances met
- **Services**: Ground power, H₂ venting capability (if needed)
- **Safety**: Clear of obstructions, good drainage

### 3.2 H₂ Considerations

For aircraft with H₂ fuel:
- **Ventilation**: Open area with good air circulation
- **Drainage**: Slope away from parking spot for potential spills
- **Safety Zones**: Space for hot/warm/cold zones (see [10-00-14-42A](../safety-operations/10-00-14-42A_H2_Safety_Zones.md))
- **Fire Access**: ARFF can access from multiple sides
- **Distance from Buildings**: [TBD] m minimum

## 4. Pre-Parking Checklist

- [ ] Parking spot selected and prepared
- [ ] Ground crew briefed on BWB and H₂ considerations
- [ ] Wheel chocks positioned and ready
- [ ] Ground power available (if needed)
- [ ] H₂ detection equipment operational (if applicable)
- [ ] Weather conditions acceptable for parking operations
- [ ] Communication established with flight crew

---

## 5. Parking Procedure

### 5.1 Aircraft Positioning

1. **Arrival**: Aircraft arrives at designated parking spot
2. **Guidance**: Marshaller guides aircraft to spot
3. **BWB Clearances**: Wing walkers monitor wingtip clearances
4. **Final Position**: Aircraft stops on centerline markings
5. **Parking Brake**: Set parking brake

### 5.2 Aircraft Securing

1. **Wheel Chocks**: Install chocks at main and nose gear (see BWB procedures)
2. **Ground Power**: Connect ground power if required
3. **Grounding**: Establish electrical bonding
4. **Safety Cones**: Position safety cones around aircraft
5. **H₂ Safety Zone**: Establish if H₂ operations required (see [10-00-14-05A](10-00-14-05A_H2_Operations_Procedures.md))

---

## 6. Post-Parking Configuration

### 6.1 Aircraft Systems

- **Engines**: Shut down
- **APU**: Running or shut down as required
- **H₂ System**: Safe mode, monitoring active
- **Electrical**: External power or battery as required
- **Hydraulics**: Depressurized

### 6.2 External Configuration

- **Doors**: Closed and secured (except for servicing)
- **Panels**: All access panels closed
- **Lights**: Exterior lights off (except safety/anti-collision)
- **Pitot Covers**: Installed if parking >24 hours

---

## 7. Short-Term Parking (<24 hours)

- Minimal securing required
- Wheel chocks mandatory
- H₂ monitoring every 4 hours (if applicable)
- Ground power optional
- Quick turnaround configuration

---

## 8. Medium-Term Parking (24 hours - 7 days)

- Full securing per procedure
- Wheel chocks and ground locks
- H₂ monitoring every 8 hours (if applicable)
- Ground power recommended
- Pitot covers and protective covers
- Regular inspections

---

## 9. Special Conditions

### 9.1 High Winds

- Parking not recommended if winds >45 kt forecast
- Consider mooring if winds >35 kt expected (see [10-00-14-03A](10-00-14-03A_Mooring_Procedures.md))

### 9.2 Extreme Temperatures

- **Cold (<-20°C)**: Additional H₂ monitoring, engine covers
- **Hot (>40°C)**: Shade aircraft if possible, monitor H₂ boil-off

### 9.3 Precipitation

- Position aircraft to minimize water pooling
- Ensure drainage clear
- Protect sensitive areas (H₂ vents, engine inlets)
