## 2. Primary H₂ Standards

### 2.1 NFPA 2 — Hydrogen Technologies Code

**Publisher:** National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)  
**Latest Edition:** 2023  
**Applicability:** H₂ production, storage, handling, and use

**Key Sections for Ground Operations:**
- **Chapter 7**: H₂ Storage Systems
- **Chapter 9**: H₂ Fueling and Defueling
- **Chapter 11**: Fire Protection
- **Chapter 13**: Operations and Maintenance

**Compliance:**
- All H₂ operations must comply with NFPA 2
- Referenced in [10-00-14-05A](../operational-procedures/10-00-14-05A_H2_Operations_Procedures.md)
- Emergency procedures align with NFPA 2 Chapter 14

---

### 2.2 ISO 13984 — Liquid Hydrogen — Land Vehicle Fuel Tanks

**Publisher:** International Organization for Standardization  
**Edition:** 2023  
**Applicability:** LH₂ tank design, construction, operation

**Key Requirements:**
- Tank pressure and temperature limits
- Venting and pressure relief
- Leak detection and monitoring
- Inspection and maintenance intervals

**Compliance:**
- Aircraft H₂ tanks designed to ISO 13984 principles
- Operational limits in [10-00-14-05A](../operational-procedures/10-00-14-05A_H2_Operations_Procedures.md)

---

### 2.3 ISO 13985 — Liquid Hydrogen — Land Vehicle Fueling System Interface

**Publisher:** International Organization for Standardization  
**Edition:** 2023  
**Applicability:** H₂ fueling connectors, interfaces, procedures

**Compliance:**
- Ground fueling equipment compatible with ISO 13985
- Interface design specified in ATA 12 (Servicing)

---

### 2.4 SAE AS6968 — Hydrogen Aircraft Ground Support Equipment

**Publisher:** SAE International (Society of Automotive Engineers)  
**Edition:** 2023  
**Applicability:** GSE for hydrogen-fueled aircraft

**Key Areas:**
- H₂ fueling trucks and equipment
- Safety systems and interlocks
- Personnel protection equipment
- Compatibility with aircraft systems

**Compliance:**
- All H₂ GSE procured to AS6968 requirements
- GSE certification and inspection per AS6968
