## 1. Purpose

Personnel evacuation procedures for ground operations emergencies.

---

## 2. When to Evacuate

**Immediate evacuation required for:**
- H₂ leak with >25% LEL detection
- H₂ fire
- Cryogenic spill (large)
- Any threat to personnel safety

**Orderly evacuation for:**
- Severe weather approaching
- Facility emergency (fire, hazmat, etc.)
- Bomb threat or security incident
- As directed by emergency commander

---

## 3. Evacuation Alert

### 3.1 Alert Methods

- **Radio**: "EVACUATE, EVACUATE, EVACUATE [reason] [direction]"
- **PA System**: Evacuation tone + voice announcement
- **Alarms**: Continuous siren or horn
- **Personnel**: Direct notification (if time permits)

### 3.2 Alert Information

Provide:
- **Emergency type** (H₂ leak, fire, storm, etc.)
- **Evacuation direction** (upwind, away from aircraft, etc.)
- **Assembly point** (primary or alternate)

## 5. Evacuation Procedure

### 5.1 Upon Hearing Evacuation Alert

1. **STOP work immediately**
2. **Leave tools and equipment** (unless safe to secure quickly)
3. **Alert nearby personnel** (shout, radio)
4. **Proceed to assembly point** via designated route
5. **Assist those needing help**
6. **Do not re-enter** until all-clear

### 5.2 Accountability

**At assembly point:**
- Report to supervisor/accountability officer
- Confirm all personnel accounted for
- Report any missing personnel immediately
- Remain at assembly point until released

---

## 6. Special Considerations

### 6.1 H₂ Emergency Evacuation

- Evacuate **upwind** if wind direction known
- Move to **higher ground** (H₂ vapor may pool initially if cold)
- Minimum distance: **50m** (100m for large leak/fire)

### 6.2 Evacuation of Visitors/Contractors

- Visitors evacuate with host
- Contractors evacuate with own supervisor
- All must report to assembly point

### 6.3 Disabled or Injured Personnel

- Assist evacuation if safe to do so
- Do not delay own evacuation if unsafe
- Report location of disabled/injured to emergency commander

---

## 7. All-Clear

**"ALL CLEAR"** announced when:
- Emergency resolved
- Area declared safe
- Emergency commander authorizes return

**Return procedure:**
- Return in organized manner
- Brief on situation and any restrictions
- Resume operations as directed

---

## 8. Evacuation Drills

- **Quarterly**: Practice evacuation from all work areas
- **Annual**: Full-scale drill with accountability
- **After changes**: Drill when evacuation routes or assembly points change
