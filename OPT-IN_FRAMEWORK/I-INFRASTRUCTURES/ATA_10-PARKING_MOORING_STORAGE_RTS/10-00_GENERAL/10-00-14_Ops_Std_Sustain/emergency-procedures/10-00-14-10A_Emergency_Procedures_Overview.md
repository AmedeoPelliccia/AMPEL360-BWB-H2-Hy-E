## 1. Purpose

Overview of emergency response procedures for ground operations of the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E aircraft, with emphasis on H₂ and cryogenic emergencies.

---

## 2. Emergency Classification

### 2.1 Severity Levels

| Level | Description | Response Time | Examples |
|-------|-------------|---------------|----------|
| **CRITICAL** | Immediate threat to life or aircraft | IMMEDIATE | H₂ fire, major H₂ leak, cryo spill |
| **MAJOR** | Significant hazard requiring urgent action | <5 minutes | Minor H₂ leak, equipment failure near aircraft |
| **MINOR** | Limited hazard, controlled response | <15 minutes | Small fluid leak, minor damage |

---

## 3. Emergency Response Framework

### 3.1 Immediate Actions (First 60 Seconds)

1. **ALERT**: Sound alarm, notify emergency services
2. **ASSESS**: Determine emergency type and severity
3. **EVACUATE**: Remove personnel from immediate danger zone
4. **COMMUNICATE**: Alert all affected parties
5. **ACT**: Begin emergency-specific procedures

### 3.2 Emergency Response Team

- **On-Scene Commander**: Senior ground operations person present
- **H₂ Safety Officer**: 24/7 on-call for H₂ emergencies
- **Airport ARFF**: Fire and rescue services
- **Medical**: EMS for casualties
- **Security**: Perimeter control, evacuation coordination
- **Operations Center**: Communication hub, resource coordination

## 5. Emergency Communication

### 5.1 Internal Communication

- **Radio**: Primary method, dedicated emergency channel
- **PA System**: For wide-area alerts
- **Alarms**: Automatic H₂ detection alarms, fire alarms

### 5.2 External Communication

- **Airport Operations**: Immediate notification
- **ARFF**: Automatic dispatch on CRITICAL emergencies
- **Local Emergency Services**: 911/112 as appropriate
- **Aircraft Manufacturer**: For technical support

### 5.3 Emergency Phrases

- **"H₂ LEAK - EVACUATE"**: H₂ leak detected
- **"H₂ FIRE - EVACUATE"**: H₂ fire confirmed
- **"CRYO SPILL - EVACUATE"**: Cryogenic spill
- **"ALL CLEAR"**: Emergency resolved, safe to return

---

## 6. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for Emergencies

### 6.1 H₂ Leak/Fire Response

- Fire-resistant clothing
- SCBA (self-contained breathing apparatus)
- Thermal imaging camera (detect invisible H₂ flame)
- H₂ detectors (portable)

### 6.2 Cryo Spill Response

- Insulated cryo gloves (to elbow)
- Face shield (full face)
- Insulated clothing
- Safety boots (steel toe, insulated)

---

## 7. Emergency Equipment Locations

- **H₂ Detectors**: Positioned around aircraft during operations
- **Fire Extinguishers**: Multiple ABC-rated, water spray capability
- **SCBA**: At operations center, ARFF vehicles
- **Thermal Cameras**: Operations center, ARFF vehicles
- **First Aid**: Operations center, all work areas
- **Spill Kits**: Operations center

---

## 8. Post-Emergency Actions

1. **All Clear**: Declare when safe to return
2. **Scene Preservation**: For investigation
3. **Casualty Care**: Medical attention as needed
4. **Incident Report**: Document emergency within 24 hours
5. **Investigation**: Root cause analysis
6. **Corrective Actions**: Implement improvements
7. **Training Review**: Update emergency training

---

## 9. Emergency Drills

- **Quarterly**: H₂ leak/fire tabletop exercises
- **Semi-Annual**: Full-scale emergency drill
- **Annual**: Multi-agency drill with ARFF
- **After Incidents**: Lessons-learned review
