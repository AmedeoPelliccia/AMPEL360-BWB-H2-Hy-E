## ⚠️ DANGER — CRYOGENIC LIQUID SPILL

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:**
1. **EVACUATE** spill area (frostbite and asphyxiation hazard)
2. **ALERT** emergency services
3. **AVOID CONTACT** with liquid or cold vapor (severe frostbite)
4. **VENTILATE** (outdoors: allow natural dispersion)
5. **MONITOR** for H₂ accumulation (if LH₂ spill)

**TEMPERATURE**: LH₂ at -253°C (-423°F) causes instant frostbite

---

## 1. Cryogenic Spill Characteristics

### 1.1 LH₂ Spill Behavior

**Upon release:**
- Liquid boils violently at ambient temperature
- Forms cold vapor cloud (visible as white fog)
- Cloud is heavier than air initially (due to cold temperature)
- Cloud warms and rises (H₂ lighter than air when warm)
- Rapid expansion: 1 liter LH₂ → 845 liters gaseous H₂

### 1.2 Hazards

| Hazard | Description | Protection |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Extreme Cold** | -253°C liquid causes instant severe frostbite | Insulated cryo PPE, avoid contact |
| **Asphyxiation** | Cold vapor displaces O₂ at ground level | Evacuate, O₂ monitoring |
| **Flammability** | Vapor cloud is flammable (4-75% H₂) | Eliminate ignition sources |
| **Cold Embrittlement** | Metals become brittle at cryo temps | Protect structures, equipment |
| **Pressure** | Rapid vaporization can cause pressure wave | Maintain distance |

---

## 2. Immediate Response (First 3 Minutes)

**Step 1: ALERT** (15 seconds)
- Sound alarm: **"CRYO SPILL - EVACUATE"**
- Radio operations center
- Activate emergency response

**Step 2: EVACUATE** (2 minutes)
- Evacuate 30m minimum (ground-level cold vapor)
- Move to higher ground if available (vapor pools in low areas)
- Move upwind if possible
- Account for all personnel

**Step 3: NOTIFY**
- ARFF
- H₂ Safety Officer
- Medical (potential frostbite casualties)
- Environmental (spill containment)

---

## 3. Spill Size Estimation

| Spill Size | Vapor Cloud | Evacuation | Duration |
|------------|-------------|------------|----------|
| **Small** (<10 L) | <2m radius | 15m | <5 minutes |
| **Medium** (10-100 L) | 2-10m radius | 30m | 5-15 minutes |
| **Large** (>100 L) | >10m radius | 50m | 15-60 minutes |
| **Tank Rupture** (>500 L) | >30m radius | 100m | 1-4 hours |

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

### 4.1 Cryo PPE (Spill Response)

**MANDATORY for approach to spill:**
- Cryo-insulated gloves (to elbow)
- Full face shield
- Insulated clothing (cryo suit or heavy jacket)
- Insulated safety boots
- SCBA (if O₂ concentration questionable)
- H₂ detector (if LH₂ spill)

**DO NOT approach with inadequate PPE**

---

## 5. Spill Containment and Cleanup

### 5.1 Containment (DO NOT attempt for large spills)

**Small spills only** (<10 L):
1. Don full cryo PPE
2. Approach from upwind
3. Use absorbent pads (caution: may freeze/shatter)
4. Collect frozen debris with long-handled tools
5. Allow liquid to evaporate naturally (preferred)

### 5.2 Large Spills

**DO NOT attempt containment**
- Evacuate and establish perimeter
- Allow natural evaporation
- Monitor H₂ concentration (if LH₂)
- Cool water spray may accelerate evaporation (use cautiously)
- Wait until all liquid evaporated and vapor cloud dissipated

### 5.3 Environmental Protection

- LH₂ spill has minimal environmental impact (evaporates to gas)
- Protect storm drains (vapor can accumulate)
- Avoid water pooling in spill area (flash freezing)

---

## 6. Frostbite First Aid

### 6.1 Signs of Frostbite

- Skin numb, white, or grayish
- Blistering (severe cases)
- Hard or waxy texture

### 6.2 First Aid Procedure

**DO:**
- Remove from cold area immediately
- Remove wet clothing gently
- Warm affected area gradually (body heat, warm water 37-40°C)
- Seek medical attention

**DO NOT:**
- Rub frostbitten area
- Apply direct heat
- Break blisters
- Allow refreezing

---

## 7. All-Clear Criteria

Declare **"ALL CLEAR"** when:
- [ ] All liquid evaporated (no visible cold vapor)
- [ ] H₂ concentration <10% LEL (if LH₂ spill)
- [ ] Ambient temperature restored in spill area
- [ ] No frost or ice remaining
- [ ] H₂ Safety Officer approval

---

## 8. Post-Spill Actions

1. **Inspect spill area** for cold damage to pavement, equipment
2. **Investigate cause** (equipment failure, human error, etc.)
3. **Document** spill size, response, cleanup
4. **Treat casualties** (frostbite)
5. **Incident report** within 24 hours
