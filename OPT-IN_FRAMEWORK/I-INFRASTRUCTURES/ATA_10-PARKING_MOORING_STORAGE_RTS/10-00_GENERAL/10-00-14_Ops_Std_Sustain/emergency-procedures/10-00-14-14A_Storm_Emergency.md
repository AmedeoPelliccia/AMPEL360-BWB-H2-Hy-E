## 1. Purpose

Procedures for securing aircraft and protecting personnel during severe weather events (high winds, thunderstorms, hurricanes/typhoons).

---

## 2. Weather Alert Levels

### 2.1 Alert Classifications

| Level | Conditions | Timeline | Actions |
|-------|-----------|----------|---------|
| **WATCH** | Severe weather possible | 24-48 hours | Review plans, prepare equipment |
| **WARNING** | Severe weather expected | 6-24 hours | Secure aircraft, prepare shelter |
| **IMMINENT** | Severe weather arriving | <6 hours | Final securing, shelter personnel |

---

## 3. Pre-Storm Preparation

### 3.1 Aircraft Securing (For Winds >35 kt forecast)

**24-48 Hours Before:**
- [ ] Review weather forecast
- [ ] Decide: shelter in hangar OR secure outside
- [ ] Gather mooring equipment (straps, anchors)
- [ ] Brief personnel on storm plan

**6-24 Hours Before:**
- [ ] Move aircraft to hangar (preferred) OR secure with tiedowns
- [ ] Install all tiedowns per [10-00-14-03A](../operational-procedures/10-00-14-03A_Mooring_Procedures.md)
- [ ] Remove loose equipment from ramp
- [ ] Secure or remove external covers (can become projectiles)
- [ ] H₂ system: consider defueling or ensure venting capability

**<6 Hours Before:**
- [ ] Final tiedown inspection and tensioning
- [ ] Shelter all personnel
- [ ] Post storm watch (if safe)
- [ ] Activate emergency operations center

### 3.2 Personnel Safety

- [ ] Identify shelter locations
- [ ] Brief all personnel on shelter plan
- [ ] Account for all personnel before storm arrival
- [ ] No outdoor operations once winds >45 kt

---

## 4. During Storm

### 4.1 Personnel Actions

- **Remain in shelter** until all-clear
- Monitor weather updates
- Monitor aircraft (if safe observation point available)
- Prepare for post-storm inspection

### 4.2 Aircraft Monitoring

**If storm watch maintained:**
- Observe from safe shelter
- Watch for aircraft movement, tiedown failure
- Note damage as it occurs
- **Evacuate shelter if threatened**

---

## 5. Post-Storm Inspection

### 5.1 Initial Assessment (Within 1 Hour)

**Once winds <25 kt:**
- [ ] Inspect aircraft from distance (assess major damage)
- [ ] Check tiedowns (still secure?)
- [ ] Look for obvious damage (wing, fuselage, control surfaces)
- [ ] H₂ system check (any visible damage, leaks?)

### 5.2 Detailed Inspection

- [ ] Complete walk-around inspection
- [ ] Document all damage with photos
- [ ] Check for FOD (debris on/around aircraft)
- [ ] Inspect tiedown attachment points for damage
- [ ] H₂ system detailed inspection (if H₂-fueled)

### 5.3 Airworthiness Determination

- Minor damage: Log and assess per maintenance procedures
- Major damage: Ground aircraft, notify authorities
- Suspected H₂ system damage: Treat as H₂ leak until proven safe

---

## 6. Hurricane/Typhoon Preparation

For extreme weather (winds >65 kt):
- **Evacuate aircraft** (fly to safe location) if possible
- **Hangar aircraft** if evacuation not possible
- **Maximum tiedown** if hangar unavailable (12-point mooring)
- **Defuel H₂** (reduce fire risk)
- **Evacuate all personnel** well in advance
