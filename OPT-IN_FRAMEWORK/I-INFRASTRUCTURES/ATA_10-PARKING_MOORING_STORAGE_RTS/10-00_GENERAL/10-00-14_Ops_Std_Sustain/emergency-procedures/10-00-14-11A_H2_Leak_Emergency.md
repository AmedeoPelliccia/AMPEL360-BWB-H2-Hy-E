## ⚠️ DANGER — HYDROGEN LEAK

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:**
1. **EVACUATE** hot zone (0-7.5m from source)
2. **ALERT** emergency services and H₂ Safety Officer
3. **ELIMINATE** all ignition sources
4. **VENTILATE** area (outdoor operations — rely on wind)
5. **MONITOR** H₂ concentrations continuously

**DO NOT** attempt to stop leak unless trained and equipped

---

## 1. H₂ Leak Characteristics

### 1.1 Detection

**H₂ is:**
- Colorless, odorless (no natural warning)
- Lighter than air (rises and disperses quickly outdoors)
- Wide flammability range (4-75% in air)
- Low ignition energy (can ignite from static electricity)

**Detection Methods:**
- H₂ detectors (alarm at 25% LEL = 1% H₂ in air)
- Visual: frost/ice on components (LH₂ leak)
- Sound: hissing (high-pressure leak)
- Thermal imaging: cold plume (LH₂ leak)

### 1.2 Leak Severity Classification

| Severity | H₂ Concentration | Description | Action |
|----------|------------------|-------------|--------|
| **MINOR** | <10% LEL (<0.4% H₂) | Small leak, dispersing | Controlled response |
| **MAJOR** | 10-25% LEL (0.4-1% H₂) | Significant leak | Immediate action required |
| **CRITICAL** | >25% LEL (>1% H₂) | Large leak or confined space | EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY |

## 2. Immediate Response (First 5 Minutes)

### 2.1 Upon Detection of H₂ Leak

**Step 1: ALERT** (10 seconds)
- Sound alarm: **"H₂ LEAK - EVACUATE"**
- Radio alert to operations center
- Activate H₂ emergency response plan

**Step 2: EVACUATE** (60 seconds)
- Evacuate hot zone (0-7.5m) IMMEDIATELY
- Move to warm zone (7.5-15m) or beyond
- Account for all personnel
- No re-entry to hot zone

**Step 3: ELIMINATE IGNITION SOURCES** (2 minutes)
- Shut down all non-essential equipment
- No vehicles in hot/warm zones
- No smoking, open flames, hot work within 50m
- De-energize electrical equipment if safe to do so

**Step 4: NOTIFY** (Simultaneous with above)
- Airport ARFF (automatic for >25% LEL)
- H₂ Safety Officer (immediate call)
- Airport operations center
- Aircraft operator
- Local emergency services if needed

**Step 5: ESTABLISH PERIMETER** (3 minutes)
- Set up barriers at 15m (cold zone boundary)
- Post personnel to prevent entry
- Establish upwind command post

## 3. H₂ Detection and Monitoring

### 3.1 Portable H₂ Detector Use

**Approach Procedure:**
1. Don full PPE (SCBA, fire-resistant clothing)
2. Approach from upwind if possible
3. Monitor continuously with portable detector
4. **STOP** if >25% LEL detected
5. **RETREAT** if >50% LEL detected

### 3.2 Leak Localization

**Visual Indicators:**
- Frost/ice formation (LH₂ leak from tank/lines)
- Condensation (cold vapor cloud)
- Component damage or misalignment

**Detector Survey Pattern:**
- Start at perimeter, move inward
- Check ground level (heavier-than-air at first if cold)
- Check overhead (H₂ rises as it warms)
- Identify leak source location (do not approach)

---

## 4. Leak Source Control (Trained Personnel Only)

### 4.1 Decision to Attempt Control

**Attempt leak control ONLY if:**
- H₂ concentration <25% LEL in approach path
- Trained H₂ handler with full PPE available
- H₂ Safety Officer authorizes action
- Safe approach path identified
- Backup personnel ready

**DO NOT attempt if:**
- H₂ concentration >25% LEL
- Fire or ignition risk present
- Unsafe approach conditions
- Inadequate PPE or training

### 4.2 Leak Control Methods

**If safe to approach:**

1. **Isolate H₂ Source**
   - Close upstream isolation valve (if accessible)
   - Actuate emergency shutoff (if available)
   - Vent H₂ to safe location

2. **Contain Leak (DO NOT plug leak directly)**
   - Never attempt to plug high-pressure H₂ leak
   - Allow controlled venting to continue
   - Monitor for leak reduction

3. **Cool LH₂ Components (if applicable)**
   - Apply water spray from distance (fire hose)
   - Prevent rapid heating that could increase leak rate
   - Maintain safe distance (minimum 3m)

---

## 5. Ventilation and Dispersion

### 5.1 Outdoor Operations (Normal Case)

- **Natural Ventilation**: H₂ disperses rapidly outdoors
- **Wind Direction**: Monitor wind, ensure personnel upwind
- **Avoid Confinement**: Do not allow H₂ to accumulate under aircraft or in recesses

### 5.2 Indoor Operations (Hangar)

**If H₂ leak in hangar:**
1. **EVACUATE BUILDING IMMEDIATELY**
2. Activate mechanical ventilation (if safe)
3. Open all doors and vents
4. DO NOT re-enter until H₂ cleared (<10% LEL)
5. Consider controlled ignition by fire service (if applicable)

---

## 6. Fire Prevention Measures

### 6.1 Eliminate Ignition Sources

**Within 50m of leak source:**
- No smoking, open flames, welding, grinding
- No vehicle operations (except emergency vehicles)
- De-energize non-essential electrical equipment
- Ground all equipment to prevent static discharge
- No cell phones or radios (use intrinsically safe equipment only)

### 6.2 Fire Suppression Readiness

- Position fire suppression equipment upwind
- Water spray apparatus ready (for cooling, not extinguishing H₂)
- ARFF on standby
- **Note**: H₂ fire cannot be extinguished; must burn out or source stopped

## 7. All-Clear Criteria

Declare **"ALL CLEAR"** only when:
- [ ] Leak source controlled or isolated
- [ ] H₂ concentration <10% LEL throughout area (sustained)
- [ ] No H₂ detection for 15 minutes minimum
- [ ] Ventilation confirmed adequate
- [ ] No residual frost or cold components
- [ ] H₂ Safety Officer approval obtained

---

## 8. Post-Emergency Actions

### 8.1 Immediate (Within 1 Hour)

1. **Personnel Accountability**: Verify all personnel safe
2. **Scene Preservation**: Maintain perimeter for investigation
3. **Equipment Inspection**: Inspect H₂ detectors, PPE used
4. **Initial Briefing**: Brief response team on findings

### 8.2 Within 24 Hours

1. **Incident Report**: Document emergency in detail
   - Time of detection, leak source, H₂ concentrations
   - Response actions taken
   - Personnel involved
   - Equipment used
2. **Notify Authorities**: Per regulatory requirements
3. **Root Cause Analysis**: Begin investigation

### 8.3. Within 7 Days

1. **Investigation Complete**: Determine root cause
2. **Corrective Actions**: Implement fixes
3. **Training Review**: Update training if needed
4. **Procedure Updates**: Revise procedures if lessons learned

## 9. Special Scenarios

### 9.1 H₂ Leak with Fire

- See [10-00-14-12A_H2_Fire_Emergency.md](10-00-14-12A_H2_Fire_Emergency.md)
- **DO NOT** attempt to extinguish H₂ fire
- Cool surrounding areas with water spray
- Let H₂ burn in controlled manner

### 9.2 H₂ Leak in Confined Space

- **EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY** — Do not enter
- Mechanical ventilation essential
- H₂ concentration may reach explosive levels quickly
- Consider controlled venting or ignition by fire service

### 9.3 LH₂ Tank Rupture

- Massive H₂ release — EVACUATE 100m minimum
- Cold vapor cloud — avoid contact (frostbite risk)
- Potential flash fire if ignited
- Allow to vent and disperse naturally
