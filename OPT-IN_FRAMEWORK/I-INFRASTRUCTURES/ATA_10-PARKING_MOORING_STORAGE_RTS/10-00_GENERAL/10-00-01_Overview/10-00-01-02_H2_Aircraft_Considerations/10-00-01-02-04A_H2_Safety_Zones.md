# 10-00-01-02-04A - H2 Safety Zones

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes comprehensive safety zone definitions, requirements, and management procedures for hydrogen operations during parking, mooring, and storage of the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Scope

This document defines:

- Safety zone classifications and dimensions
- Personnel access control requirements
- Equipment and ignition source restrictions
- Emergency response zones
- Environmental monitoring requirements

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Safety zones for hydrogen aircraft operations are established based on:

1. **Hazard Assessment**: Hydrogen release scenarios and consequences
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Controlling ignition sources and personnel exposure
3. **Regulatory Compliance**: Meeting NFPA 2 and international standards
4. **Operational Flexibility**: Balancing safety with operational efficiency

### 4.2 H2/LH2Considerations

#### 4.2.1 Safety Zone Classifications

**Zone 1: Exclusion Zone (0-3 meters from H2 components)**

*Purpose:* Prevent ignition of hydrogen in the event of a release

*Characteristics:*
- Highest hydrogen concentration potential
- Immediate hazard area for personnel
- Strictest equipment and activity restrictions

*Requirements:*
- Personnel: Trained H2 handlers only, maximum 4 personnel
- PPE: Cryogenic protection, anti-static clothing, safety footwear
- Equipment: Intrinsically safe only, no spark-producing tools
- Ignition Sources: Absolutely prohibited
- Monitoring: Continuous H2 detection, audio/visual alarms
- Communications: Explosion-proof radios or direct line-of-sight
- Access Control: Physical barriers, warning signs

**Zone 2: Restricted Zone (3-8 meters from H2 components)**

*Purpose:* Control potential ignition sources with engineering controls

*Characteristics:*
- Moderate hydrogen concentration potential
- Controlled work area with permits
- Enhanced safety protocols required

*Requirements:*
- Personnel: H2 safety awareness training required
- PPE: Safety glasses, anti-static footwear, fire-resistant clothing
- Equipment: Approved electrical equipment, grounded tools
- Ignition Sources: Hot work prohibited without special permit
- Monitoring: Periodic H2 detection (minimum every 15 minutes)
- Communications: Standard aviation ground communication
- Access Control: Warning signs, controlled access points

**Zone 3: Caution Zone (8-15 meters from H2 components)**

*Purpose:* Awareness and emergency response preparation

*Characteristics:*
- Low hydrogen concentration potential
- Normal operations with awareness
- Enhanced emergency response readiness

*Requirements:*
- Personnel: General H2 safety briefing
- PPE: Standard airport safety equipment
- Equipment: Standard ground support equipment
- Ignition Sources: Normal airport operations permitted
- Monitoring: Area H2 detection system
- Communications: Standard procedures
- Access Control: Warning signage, no physical barriers

**Zone 4: Public Safety Zone (15-50 meters from H2 components)**

*Purpose:* Public protection and emergency vehicle access

*Characteristics:*
- Minimal direct hydrogen hazard
- Emergency response staging area
- Public access control

*Requirements:*
- Personnel: General public excluded during H2 operations
- Equipment: Emergency response vehicles staged
- Monitoring: Perimeter H2 detection
- Access: Emergency vehicle routes maintained clear

#### 4.2.2 Zone Dimensions for AMPEL360-BWB-H2

**LH2 Tank System Zones:**
- Tank vent outlets: Zone 1 extends vertically 5 meters above discharge
- Tank fill/drain connections: Zone 1 radius of 3 meters
- Pressure relief devices: Zone 1 radius of 3 meters, 5 meters vertical
- Normal vent path: Zone 2 extends in prevailing wind direction 10 meters

**H2 Distribution System Zones:**
- Transfer lines (active): Zone 1 radius of 2 meters
- Coupling connections: Zone 1 radius of 3 meters during connection/disconnection
- Valves and fittings: Zone 2 radius of 2 meters

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

#### 4.3.1 BWB-Specific Zone Mapping

The Blended Wing Body configuration affects zone implementation:

- **Integrated Tanks**: H2 tanks integrated within BWB structure require zone mapping to account for wider aircraft footprint
- **Multiple Vent Locations**: BWB may have multiple vent outlets requiring overlapping zone analysis
- **Access Limitations**: BWB design may limit physical barrier placement for Zone 1
- **Ground Personnel Routes**: Safe approach routes mapped around BWB geometry

#### 4.3.2 Zone Visualization

For the AMPEL360-BWB-H2:
- Total parking area required (including all zones): Approximately 50m x 60m
- Zone 1 footprint: Approximately 150 m² (multiple areas)
- Zone 2 footprint: Approximately 600 m²
- Zone 3 footprint: Approximately 1,800 m²

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 Zone Establishment Procedures

**Before Aircraft Arrival:**
1. Survey parking position for compliance with zone requirements
2. Install physical barriers for Zone 1 (where practical)
3. Post warning signs at zone boundaries
4. Verify H2 detection system operational
5. Stage emergency equipment
6. Brief ground personnel on zone restrictions

**During Parking Operations:**
1. Verify zones established before H2 operations commence
2. Maintain zone integrity throughout parking period
3. Monitor for zone incursions
4. Log all Zone 1 entries and activities

**Before Aircraft Departure:**
1. Verify no personnel in Zone 1
2. Remove physical barriers (if installed)
3. Inspect parking area for FOD or hazards
4. Document zone operations for next parking event

### 6.2 Zone Incursion Response

**Unauthorized Zone 1 Entry:**
1. Immediately halt all H2 operations
2. Direct person to exit Zone 1 by shortest safe route
3. Verify no H2 release or ignition occurred
4. Investigate cause of incursion
5. Re-brief personnel before resuming operations

**Zone 1 Ignition Source Detected:**
1. Emergency shutdown of H2 operations
2. Secure ignition source immediately
3. Verify no H2 release in progress
4. Conduct H2 atmosphere check before resuming
5. Investigate and correct procedure violation

### 6.3 Emergency Situations

**H2 Release in Zone 1:**
1. Activate emergency alarm
2. Evacuate all personnel to Zone 4
3. Notify airport fire services immediately
4. Do not attempt to stop release unless trained and safe to do so
5. Monitor wind direction and H2 dispersion
6. Maintain Zone 4 perimeter until all-clear given

**Fire in H2 Safety Zone:**
1. Activate emergency alarm
2. Evacuate all personnel to Zone 4
3. Notify airport fire services immediately
4. Do not attempt firefighting unless trained and equipped
5. H2-fed fires may be allowed to burn under control
6. Prevent fire spread to other structures

### 6.4 Environmental Factors

**Wind Conditions:**
- Wind speed < 5 m/s: Zones as specified
- Wind speed 5-10 m/s: Extend Zone 2 downwind by 5 meters
- Wind speed > 10 m/s: Consider delaying H2 operations, extend Zone 2 downwind by 10 meters

**Confined Spaces:**
- Never perform H2 operations in enclosed spaces without forced ventilation
- Hangar operations require continuous forced ventilation (minimum 6 ACH)
- H2 detection at ceiling level in hangars (hydrogen rises)
