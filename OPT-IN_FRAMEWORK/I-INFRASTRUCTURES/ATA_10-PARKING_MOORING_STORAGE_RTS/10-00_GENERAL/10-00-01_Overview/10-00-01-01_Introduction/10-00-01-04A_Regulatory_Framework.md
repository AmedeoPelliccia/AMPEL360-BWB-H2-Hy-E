# 10-00-01-04A - Regulatory Framework

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the regulatory framework applicable to parking, mooring, storage, and return-to-service operations for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 hydrogen-powered aircraft.

## 2. Scope

This document identifies:

- Applicable airworthiness regulations and certification specifications
- Hydrogen-specific safety standards and codes
- Airport and infrastructure requirements
- Operational approval requirements

## 3. Applicable Documents

- [CS-25 - Certification Specifications for Large Aeroplanes](https://www.easa.europa.eu/document-library/certification-specifications/cs-25-large-aeroplanes)
- [FAR Part 25 - Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-25)
- [ICAO Annex 14](https://www.icao.int/safety/airnavigation/Pages/Annex-14.aspx) - Aerodromes
- [EASA Part 21](https://www.easa.europa.eu/document-library/regulations/easa-part-21) - Certification of Aircraft and Related Products
- [SAE AS6968](https://www.sae.org/) - Hydrogen Aircraft Ground Support Equipment
- [NFPA 2 - Hydrogen Technologies Code](https://www.nfpa.org/)
- [ISO/TR 15916](https://www.iso.org/) - Basic considerations for the safety of hydrogen systems

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The regulatory framework for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 parking, mooring, and storage operations encompasses:

1. **Airworthiness Regulations**: Aircraft design and operational standards
2. **Hydrogen Safety Standards**: Specialized requirements for H2/LH2 systems
3. **Airport Requirements**: Infrastructure and operational standards
4. **Environmental Regulations**: Emissions, noise, and environmental protection

### 4.2 H2/LH2Considerations

#### 4.2.1 Primary H2 Safety Standards

**NFPA 2 - Hydrogen Technologies Code**
- Establishes safety requirements for hydrogen systems
- Defines minimum separation distances for H2 operations
- Specifies fire protection and emergency response requirements
- Applies to: Ground storage, dispensing, and aircraft operations

**SAE AS6968 - Hydrogen Aircraft Ground Support Equipment**
- Standards for H2 GSE design and operation
- Interface requirements between aircraft and ground systems
- Safety protocols for H2 servicing operations
- Applies to: Fueling, defueling, and system maintenance

**ISO/TR 15916 - Safety of Hydrogen Systems**
- Basic safety considerations for hydrogen systems
- Risk assessment methodologies
- Safety distance calculations
- Material compatibility requirements

#### 4.2.2 Cryogenic System Regulations

- Pressure vessel standards for LH2 storage
- Vacuum-insulated system requirements
- Boil-off management regulations
- Personnel safety for cryogenic operations

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

#### 4.3.1 Airworthiness Standards

**CS-25 / FAR Part 25 Applicable Sections:**

- **CS-25.1309** - Equipment, systems, and installations
  - System safety assessment for ground operations
  - Failure condition classification
  - Safety analysis requirements

- **CS-25.1529** - Instructions for Continued Airworthiness
  - Maintenance procedures for BWB configuration
  - Ground handling instructions
  - Storage and preservation requirements

#### 4.3.2 Airport Infrastructure Requirements

**ICAO Annex 14 - Aerodromes:**
- Aircraft stand dimensions and spacing
- Taxiway and parking area geometry
- Ground support equipment access requirements
- Safety zones and clearances

**BWB-Specific Considerations:**
- Extended wingspan accommodation
- Modified parking stand layouts
- Specialized ground handling procedures
- Infrastructure modifications for BWB operations

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 Certification Basis

The AMPEL360-BWB-H2 certification basis includes:

1. **Type Certificate Requirements**
   - CS-25 or FAR Part 25 compliance
   - Special Conditions for hydrogen fuel systems
   - Special Conditions for BWB configuration

2. **Operational Approvals**
   - H2 ground handling procedures
   - Airport infrastructure modifications
   - Personnel training and qualification

3. **Continuing Airworthiness**
   - Maintenance program approval
   - Storage and preservation procedures
   - Return-to-service inspection requirements

### 6.2 Regulatory Authorities

- **EASA** (European Union Aviation Safety Agency): Primary certification authority
- **FAA** (Federal Aviation Administration): Bilateral validation
- **National CAAs**: Country-specific operational approvals
- **Airport Authorities**: Local infrastructure and operational approvals

### 6.3 Compliance Demonstration

Compliance with regulatory requirements is demonstrated through:

- **Analysis**: Engineering calculations and assessments
- **Testing**: Ground and flight test programs
- **Inspection**: Physical verification of installations
- **Similarity**: Comparison to certified designs where applicable
