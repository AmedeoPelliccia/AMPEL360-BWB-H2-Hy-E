# 10-00-01-02A - Scope and Applicability

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the scope and applicability boundaries for ATA Chapter 10 documentation as it pertains to the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft parking, mooring, storage, and return-to-service operations.

## 2. Scope

This document establishes:

- The operational boundaries of ATA Chapter 10 for AMPEL360-BWB-H2
- Applicable phases of ground operations covered
- Interfaces with other ATA chapters and operational systems
- Limitations and exclusions

## 3. Applicable Documents

- [ATA iSpec 2200](https://www.atastandards.org/) - Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance
- [ATA 100 Chapter 10](https://www.atastandards.org/) - Parking, Mooring, Storage and Return to Service
- [ICAO Annex 14](https://www.icao.int/safety/airnavigation/Pages/Annex-14.aspx) - Aerodromes
- [CS-25.1309](https://www.easa.europa.eu/document-library/certification-specifications/cs-25-large-aeroplanes) - Equipment, Systems, and Installations

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

ATA Chapter 10 for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 encompasses all ground-based operations when the aircraft is not in active flight service, specifically addressing hydrogen-powered aircraft and BWB configuration requirements.

### 4.2 H2/LH2 Considerations

The scope explicitly includes:

- **LH2 System Management**: Cryogenic system monitoring, boil-off management, and safety protocols
- **H2 Venting Operations**: Controlled venting procedures during parking and storage
- **Thermal Management**: Maintaining LH2 system temperatures within operational limits
- **H2 Safety Zones**: Establishing and maintaining hydrogen safety perimeters

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

BWB-specific scope includes:

- **Geometric Compatibility**: Ensuring parking infrastructure accommodates BWB footprint
- **Access Management**: Procedures for accessing systems in BWB configuration
- **Ground Handling**: BWB-specific towing, positioning, and securing procedures
- **Integration with Facilities**: BWB compatibility with hangars, ramps, and parking areas

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 Operational Boundaries

- **Short-Term Parking** (< 24 hours): Full H2 system monitoring, crew available
- **Medium-Term Parking** (24 hours - 7 days): Periodic H2 system checks, reduced manning
- **Long-Term Storage** (> 7 days): Enhanced preservation, comprehensive H2 system safing
- **Return to Service**: Progressive system reactivation with full safety verification

### 6.2 Excluded Operations

The following are **outside the scope** of ATA Chapter 10:

- Active flight operations (covered by ATA 02 - Operations Information)
- Routine maintenance tasks (covered by respective system ATA chapters)
- Major modifications and overhauls (covered by ATA 51-57 structures chapters)
- Fueling and defueling operations (covered by ATA 28 - Fuel)
- Engine operations (covered by ATA 70-79 engine chapters)

However, interfaces with these operations are documented where relevant to parking and storage.
