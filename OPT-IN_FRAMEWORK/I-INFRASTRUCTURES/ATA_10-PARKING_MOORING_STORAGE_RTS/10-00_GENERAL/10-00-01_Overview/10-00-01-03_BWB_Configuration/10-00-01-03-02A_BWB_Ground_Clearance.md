# 10-00-01-03-02A - BWB Ground Clearance

## 1. Purpose

This document specifies ground clearance requirements and limitations for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Blended Wing Body aircraft during parking, mooring, and storage operations, ensuring safe ground handling and equipment operations.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- Ground clearance zones and measurements
- Equipment height and positioning restrictions
- Ground surface requirements
- Jacking and maintenance access considerations

## 3. Applicable Documents

- [ATA iSpec 2200](https://www.atastandards.org/) - Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance
- [ATA 100 Chapter 10](https://www.atastandards.org/) - Parking, Mooring, Storage and Return to Service
- [ATA 07 - Lifting and Shoring](../../../../ATA_07-LIFTING_SHORING/)
- [ICAO Annex 14](https://www.icao.int/safety/airnavigation/Pages/Annex-14.aspx) - Aerodromes

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The BWB configuration presents unique ground clearance characteristics:

- **Variable Clearance**: BWB body clearance varies from center to outer edges
- **Wide Footprint**: Ground clearance distributed over larger area than conventional aircraft
- **Integrated Structure**: No distinct fuselage creates different clearance profile
- **Access Limitations**: Some areas have restricted clearance for ground equipment

### 4.2 H2/LH2Considerations

Ground clearance impacts H2 operations:

- LH2 tank components and insulation positioned within BWB structure
- Ground-level H2 detection sensors must account for BWB geometry
- Vent discharge height adequate for H2 dispersion
- Ground equipment must not contact cryogenic components

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

#### 4.3.1 Ground Clearance Zones

**Zone A: Center Body (Maximum Clearance)**
- Location: Aircraft centerline, ±5 meters lateral
- Clearance with aircraft empty: 2.8 meters
- Clearance with aircraft MTOW: 2.5 meters
- Clearance with aircraft over-gross: 2.3 meters (emergency only)
- Suitable for: Standard ground equipment, personnel access

**Zone B: Mid-Body (Moderate Clearance)**
- Location: ±5 to ±20 meters from centerline
- Clearance with aircraft empty: 2.3 meters
- Clearance with aircraft MTOW: 2.0 meters
- Clearance with aircraft over-gross: 1.8 meters
- Suitable for: Low-profile equipment, restricted personnel access

**Zone C: Outer Wings (Minimum Clearance)**
- Location: ±20 to ±40 meters from centerline (wingtips)
- Clearance with aircraft empty: 2.0 meters
- Clearance with aircraft MTOW: 1.8 meters
- Clearance with aircraft over-gross: 1.6 meters
- Suitable for: Inspection only, no equipment passage

#### 4.3.2 Ground Clearance Measurements

**Reference Points:**
- Datum: Ground surface (level, paved)
- Measurement: Lowest point of aircraft structure to ground
- Load condition: Specified (empty, operating weight, MTOW)
- Temperature: Ambient 15°C (59°F), standard conditions

**Critical Clearance Points:**
1. **Main Landing Gear Doors** (closed): 2.6m clearance
2. **Center Body Fairing**: 2.5m clearance (lowest point)
3. **Wingtip Trailing Edge**: 1.8m clearance
4. **APU Exhaust Area**: 2.3m clearance (hot zone)
5. **LH2 Tank Insulation Jacket**: 2.4m clearance
6. **Access Panel Hinges** (closed): 2.5m clearance

#### 4.3.3 Ground Equipment Height Restrictions

**Equipment Maximum Heights by Zone:**

**Zone A (Center Body):**
- Baggage loaders: 2.3 meters maximum
- Cargo loaders: 2.3 meters maximum
- Tugs and tow vehicles: 2.0 meters maximum
- Ground power units: 2.0 meters maximum
- Service trucks: 2.0 meters maximum

**Zone B (Mid-Body):**
- Inspection stands: 1.8 meters maximum
- Work platforms: 1.8 meters maximum
- Cargo handling equipment: 1.8 meters maximum (approach from sides only)

**Zone C (Outer Wings):**
- No equipment passage permitted
- Hand tools and inspection equipment only
- Personnel access for visual inspection only

#### 4.3.4 Ground Surface Requirements

**Parking Surface:**
- Surface type: Paved (concrete or asphalt)
- Levelness: Maximum 2% slope in any direction
- Smoothness: No depressions > 50mm (2 inches)
- Debris-free: FOD prevention critical due to low clearance

**Soft Ground Operations:**
- Not recommended for BWB configuration
- If unavoidable, require engineering evaluation
- Ground pressure distribution analysis required
- Risk of belly contact with uneven surfaces

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 Ground Equipment Operations

**Pre-Operation Checks:**
1. Verify aircraft loading condition (weight and CG)
2. Confirm ground surface level and smooth
3. Measure actual ground clearance if uncertain
4. Check equipment height against zone restrictions
5. Verify personnel awareness of clearance limitations

**During Operations:**
1. Approach aircraft slowly with equipment
2. Observe clearance continuously
3. Use spotters for low-clearance areas
4. Stop immediately if contact suspected
5. Report any clearance anomalies

### 6.2 Personnel Safety

**Under-Aircraft Access:**
- Minimum personnel clearance: 1.5 meters (standing)
- Crawling access permitted only in Zone A with clearance > 2.0m
- Hardhat required for all under-aircraft work
- Flashlight/headlamp for low-light conditions
- Two-person minimum for under-aircraft work

**Hazards:**
- Head strike on aircraft structure
- Equipment strike on aircraft structure
- Entrapment between equipment and aircraft
- Reduced visibility in low-clearance areas

### 6.3 Environmental Factors

**Ground Clearance Variations:**
- Landing gear tire pressure affects clearance (±50mm)
- Aircraft fuel load significantly affects clearance
- Thermal expansion/contraction: Minimal effect (±10mm)
- Landing gear strut servicing affects clearance (±30mm)

**Weather Effects:**
- Standing water: Maximum depth 25mm before operations prohibited
- Snow/ice accumulation: Remove before operations
- Mud/soft ground: Prohibits operations
- Frozen ground: Evaluate for surface irregularities

### 6.4 Maintenance Considerations

**Jacking Operations:**
- Main jack points: Located in Zone A, 3.5m height when jacked
- Wing jack points: Located in Zone B, 3.0m height when jacked
- Nose jack point: Single point, 3.8m height when jacked
- Level check required before jacking
- Jacking procedure specific to BWB load distribution

**Ground Running:**
- Engine ground run requires additional clearance monitoring
- Jet blast deflector positioning critical for BWB
- FOD ingestion risk due to low clearance
- Enhanced FOD inspection required after ground running
