# 10-00-01-03-01A - BWB Parking Geometry

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the geometric specifications and spatial requirements for parking the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Blended Wing Body aircraft, including dimensional constraints and parking infrastructure requirements.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- BWB aircraft dimensions and footprint characteristics
- Parking stand geometry and spacing requirements
- Clearance envelopes and obstacle limitations
- Comparison with conventional aircraft parking requirements

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The Blended Wing Body configuration presents unique geometric characteristics that significantly impact parking requirements:

- **Integrated Wing-Fuselage**: No distinct fuselage/wing separation
- **Wide Footprint**: Significantly wider than conventional aircraft of similar capacity
- **Low Profile**: Lower overall height compared to tube-and-wing designs
- **Distributed Mass**: Different center of gravity and weight distribution
- **Non-Traditional Control Surfaces**: Unique locations for flight control access

### 4.2 H2/LH2Considerations

BWB geometry affects H2 safety zone implementation:

- Wider aircraft footprint increases H2 safety zone coverage area
- Integrated LH2 tanks within BWB structure require multiple safety zone centers
- Vent locations distributed across BWB upper surface
- Ground personnel approach routes optimized for BWB geometry and H2 safety

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

#### 4.3.1 AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Dimensions

**Overall Dimensions:**
- Wingspan: 80.0 meters (262.5 ft)
- Overall Length: 58.0 meters (190.3 ft)
- Overall Height: 18.0 meters (59.1 ft)
- Wing Area: Approximately 1,200 m² (12,917 ft²)

**Parking Footprint:**
- Nose to tail: 58.0 meters
- Wingtip to wingtip: 80.0 meters
- Rectangular footprint: 58m x 80m = 4,640 m²
- Actual aircraft footprint (plan view): Approximately 3,200 m²

**Ground Clearance:**
- Minimum ground clearance (body): 2.5 meters
- Minimum ground clearance (wingtips): 1.8 meters
- Landing gear height: 5.0 meters (main), 4.5 meters (nose)
- Belly clearance for ground equipment: 2.5 meters minimum

#### 4.3.2 Parking Stand Requirements

**Minimum Parking Stand Dimensions:**
- Stand length: 78 meters (nose to tail + 10m safety margin each end)
- Stand width: 100 meters (wingtip to wingtip + 10m safety margin each side)
- Total stand area: 7,800 m² (minimum)

**With H2 Safety Zones:**
- Total required area: 100m x 120m = 12,000 m²
- Includes Zone 3 (15-meter radius from H2 components)

**Comparison with Conventional Aircraft:**
- Boeing 777-300ER stand: Approximately 75m x 85m = 6,375 m²
- AMPEL360-BWB-H2 requires approximately 88% more stand area

#### 4.3.3 Clearance Envelopes

**Lateral Clearances:**
- Minimum clearance to adjacent aircraft: 25 meters (wingtip to wingtip)
- Minimum clearance to buildings: 20 meters (includes H2 safety zone)
- Minimum clearance to ground equipment parking: 10 meters
- Minimum clearance to taxiways: Per ICAO Annex 14 (typically 25-47.5m depending on code)

**Vertical Clearances:**
- Minimum overhead clearance: 25 meters (for H2 vent dispersion)
- Hangar door height requirement: 22 meters minimum
- Ground service equipment maximum height: 15 meters

**Approach/Departure Clearances:**
- Tug/tow vehicle approach lane: 8 meters wide
- Ground crew walkways: 3 meters wide
- Emergency vehicle access: 6 meters wide (maintained clear)

#### 4.3.4 Parking Position Marking

**Ground Markings Required:**
- Nose-wheel stop position: High-visibility marking
- Wingtip position guides: Painted lines extending 15m forward
- Main landing gear position: Left and right markings
- BWB centerline: Dashed line from nose to tail parking limit
- Safety zone boundaries: Yellow hatched markings per NFPA 2
- Equipment positioning marks: For stairs, GPU, GSE

**Visual Guidance:**
- Marshaller positions: Two positions (left and right) for BWB width
- Visual docking guidance system (VDGS): Modified for BWB profile
- Illuminated wingtip markers (required for night operations)
- Lead-in line: Contrasting color for pilot reference

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 Geometric Hazards

**Wingtip Awareness:**
- BWB wingtips extend significantly beyond traditional aircraft
- Ground personnel may misjudge lateral clearances
- Increased risk of wingtip contact with ground equipment or structures
- Enhanced visual markers and barriers required

**Low Ground Clearance Areas:**
- Certain BWB areas have reduced ground clearance vs. conventional aircraft
- Risk of equipment contact with aircraft belly
- Restricted routing for ground vehicles under aircraft
- Specific procedures for low-profile GSE

**Center of Gravity:**
- BWB CG location different from conventional aircraft
- Affects jacking points and towing attachment locations
- Tiedown point load distribution unique to BWB
- Special considerations for uneven parking surfaces

### 6.2 Operational Procedures

**Parking Guidance:**
1. VDGS system configured for BWB profile
2. Dual marshallers positioned for wingtip visibility
3. Ground crew spotters at wingtips during final approach to stand
4. Final stop position verified before shutting down engines
5. Parking brake set, chocks positioned

**Post-Parking Verification:**
1. Aircraft position verified within stand markings
2. Clearances to adjacent aircraft/objects confirmed
3. H2 safety zones established
4. Ground equipment positioning verified safe
