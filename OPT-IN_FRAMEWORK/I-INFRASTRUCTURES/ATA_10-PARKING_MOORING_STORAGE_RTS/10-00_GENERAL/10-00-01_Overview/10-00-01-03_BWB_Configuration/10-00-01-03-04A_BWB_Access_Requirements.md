# 10-00-01-03-04A - BWB Access Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes access requirements and procedures for ground personnel and equipment to service and inspect the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Blended Wing Body aircraft during parking, mooring, and storage operations.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- Personnel access points and procedures for BWB configuration
- Ground support equipment (GSE) positioning and access
- Safety considerations for BWB-specific access
- Passenger and crew boarding/deplaning procedures

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

The BWB configuration presents unique access challenges:

- **Integrated Body**: No distinct fuselage creates different access point locations
- **Wide Footprint**: Access points distributed across wider area
- **Low Profile**: Some access points lower than conventional aircraft
- **Multiple Levels**: Internal structure may require multiple access platforms

### 4.2 H2/LH2Considerations

Access operations must account for H2 safety:

- Personnel access routes avoid H2 vent discharge areas
- Ground equipment positioned outside Zone 1 (3-meter exclusion zone)
- Access platforms must not obstruct H2 safety zones
- H2 safety briefing required before aircraft access during fueling/defueling

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

#### 4.3.1 Personnel Access Points

The AMPEL360-BWB-H2 is equipped with the following access points:

**Passenger Doors (4 doors):**
- **Forward Left Door (Door 1L)**: Primary passenger boarding door
  - Location: 15m from nose, left side
  - Sill height: 4.2 meters above ground
  - Width: 1.07 meters (42 inches)
  - Access: Requires passenger boarding stairs or jetway

- **Forward Right Door (Door 1R)**: Secondary passenger door
  - Location: 15m from nose, right side
  - Sill height: 4.2 meters above ground
  - Width: 1.07 meters
  - Access: Requires passenger boarding stairs or jetway

- **Aft Left Door (Door 2L)**: Aft passenger door
  - Location: 42m from nose, left side
  - Sill height: 4.0 meters above ground
  - Width: 1.07 meters
  - Access: Requires passenger boarding stairs

- **Aft Right Door (Door 2R)**: Aft passenger door
  - Location: 42m from nose, right side
  - Sill height: 4.0 meters above ground
  - Width: 1.07 meters
  - Access: Requires passenger boarding stairs

**Cargo Doors (3 doors):**
- **Forward Cargo Door**: Bulk cargo compartment
  - Location: 20m from nose, centerline (belly)
  - Sill height: 1.8 meters above ground
  - Dimensions: 1.8m × 1.2m
  - Access: Via cargo loader

- **Aft Cargo Door (Left)**: Main cargo compartment
  - Location: 35m from nose, left side (belly)
  - Sill height: 1.8 meters above ground
  - Dimensions: 2.4m × 1.8m
  - Access: Via cargo loader (ULD compatible)

- **Aft Cargo Door (Right)**: Main cargo compartment
  - Location: 35m from nose, right side (belly)
  - Sill height: 1.8 meters above ground
  - Dimensions: 2.4m × 1.8m
  - Access: Via cargo loader (ULD compatible)

**Service Access Panels (Multiple locations):**
- **Avionics Bay**: Forward belly, 8m from nose
  - Access: Via work stand, 2.0m platform height
  - Panel size: 1.0m × 0.8m

- **LH2 System Access (Left)**: Mid-body, 28m from nose, left side
  - Access: Via specialized platform, 3.0m height
  - Panel size: 1.2m × 1.0m
  - **H2 Safety Zone**: Zone 1 applies when panel open

- **LH2 System Access (Right)**: Mid-body, 28m from nose, right side
  - Access: Via specialized platform, 3.0m height
  - Panel size: 1.2m × 1.0m
  - **H2 Safety Zone**: Zone 1 applies when panel open

- **APU Access**: Aft belly, 48m from nose
  - Access: Via work stand, 2.5m platform height
  - Panel size: 1.5m × 1.2m

- **Landing Gear Bays** (3 locations): Various
  - Access: Ground level for inspection, work stands for maintenance

#### 4.3.2 Ground Support Equipment Access

**Passenger Boarding:**
- **Jetway Compatibility**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2 compatible with standard jetways
  - Door 1L and 1R heights: 4.2 meters
  - Jetway apron drive-up distance: 8 meters from aircraft side
  - BWB width requires longer jetway extension than conventional aircraft

- **Passenger Stairs (Mobile)**: 
  - Height range: 3.5 - 4.5 meters
  - Platform width: Minimum 1.2 meters
  - Position: Approach perpendicular to aircraft side
  - Clearance: Maintain 0.5m from aircraft skin

**Cargo Loading:**
- **Main Deck Cargo Loaders**: 
  - Height range: 1.0 - 2.5 meters (adjustable)
  - ULD compatible (LD3, LD7 containers)
  - Approach: Perpendicular to aircraft belly
  - Position: Centered on cargo door

- **Bulk Cargo Handling**:
  - Belt loaders for forward bulk cargo
  - Height: 1.2 - 2.0 meters adjustable
  - Approach: From sides or rear

**Service and Maintenance:**
- **Work Stands**: 
  - Mobile maintenance platforms
  - Height range: 1.5 - 3.5 meters
  - Platform size: Minimum 2.0m × 1.5m
  - Guardrails and toe boards required
  - Non-marking wheels to prevent aircraft skin damage

- **Lavatory Service Trucks**:
  - Access: Aft belly service panels
  - Service height: 2.0 meters
  - Position: Approach from rear

- **Potable Water Service**:
  - Access: Forward belly service panel
  - Service height: 2.2 meters
  - Position: Approach from side

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 Personnel Access Safety

**General Safety Rules:**
- Three points of contact when climbing stairs/ladders
- PPE: Hard hat, safety footwear, high-visibility vest
- No loose articles that could become FOD
- Communication with ground crew before accessing aircraft
- Weather considerations (ice, rain, wind)

**BWB-Specific Hazards:**
- Wide footprint increases fall distance from outer wings
- Low ground clearance in some areas (head strike potential)
- Non-traditional door locations require crew familiarization
- Wingtip extremities less visible than conventional aircraft

### 6.2 GSE Positioning Safety

**Pre-Positioning:**
1. Verify aircraft parking brake set and chocks installed
2. Check GSE for damage or malfunction
3. Verify operator qualifications for BWB operations
4. Brief on approach route and final position
5. Assign spotter for close approach

**During Positioning:**
1. Approach slowly (< 5 km/h within 10m of aircraft)
2. Use spotter guidance for final approach
3. Stop short if clearance uncertain
4. Verify equipment stable before personnel boarding
5. Set parking brake on GSE

**Post-Positioning:**
1. Verify GSE properly positioned and secured
2. Install safety barriers/cones around GSE
3. Check for contact points with aircraft (prevent damage)
4. Document GSE position if required for maintenance

### 6.3 Emergency Access

**Emergency Evacuation:**
- All passenger doors equipped with emergency escape slides
- Slide deployment clearance: 3 meters laterally
- Evacuation capacity: All passengers in 90 seconds (certification requirement)
- Ground crew must maintain evacuation routes clear

**Fire/Emergency Response Access:**
- Designated emergency vehicle access points
- Minimum 6-meter-wide access lanes
- Access to all aircraft sides within 2 minutes
- Emergency equipment staging areas marked

### 6.4 H2 Safety During Access

**H2 System Access Panels:**
1. Verify H2 system status before panel opening
2. Conduct H2 atmosphere check
3. Establish Zone 1 (3-meter exclusion zone)
4. Only qualified H2 technicians within Zone 1
5. Continuous H2 monitoring during work
6. Close and secure panels before departing

**Personnel Briefing:**
- Location of H2 vents and safety zones
- H2 detection alarm recognition
- Emergency shutdown procedures
- Evacuation routes from H2 areas
