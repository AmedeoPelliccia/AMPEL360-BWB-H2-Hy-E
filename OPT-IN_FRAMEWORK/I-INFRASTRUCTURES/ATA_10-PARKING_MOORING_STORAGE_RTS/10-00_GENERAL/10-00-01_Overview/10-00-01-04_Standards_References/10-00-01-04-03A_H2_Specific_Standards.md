# 10-00-01-04-03A - H2 Specific Standards

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies and describes hydrogen-specific standards, codes, and regulations applicable to parking, mooring, and storage operations for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 hydrogen-powered aircraft.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- Hydrogen safety standards and codes
- Cryogenic system standards
- H2 ground support equipment specifications
- Emerging hydrogen aviation standards

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

Hydrogen-powered aircraft introduce unique safety and operational considerations requiring specialized standards beyond traditional aviation regulations. These standards address:

- Hydrogen flammability and explosion hazards
- Cryogenic storage and handling
- Ground support equipment design
- Personnel safety and training

### 4.2 H2/LH2 Considerations

Hydrogen safety standards are critical for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 operations due to:

- **Wide Flammability Range**: 4-75% H2 in air
- **Low Ignition Energy**: 0.017 mJ (vs. 0.24 mJ for Jet-A)
- **High Flame Speed**: Rapid fire propagation
- **Cryogenic Temperature**: -253°C storage temperature
- **Embrittlement Risk**: Hydrogen embrittlement of materials
- **Asphyxiation Risk**: Displacement of oxygen in confined spaces

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

BWB-integrated H2 systems require standard adaptations:

- Multiple H2 tank locations within BWB structure
- Distributed vent system across BWB upper surface
- Unique access requirements for H2 system maintenance
- Modified safety zone geometries for BWB footprint

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 NFPA 2 - Hydrogen Technologies Code

**Primary hydrogen safety code for stationary installations and mobile applications**

**Chapter 5: General Requirements**
- Personnel training and qualification
- H2 safety awareness for all ground personnel
- Qualified H2 handler requirements for Zone 1 access
- Emergency response training

**Chapter 6: Hydrogen Systems**
- System design and installation requirements
- Pressure relief devices
- Venting systems
- Detection and alarm systems

**Chapter 7: Operating Requirements**
- Safety zones and separation distances
  - Hazard Zone 1: 0-3 meters (exclusion zone)
  - Hazard Zone 2: 3-8 meters (restricted zone)
  - Hazard Zone 3: 8-15 meters (caution zone)
- Ignition source control
- Hot work permits
- Emergency procedures

**Chapter 9: Portable and Mobile Hydrogen Systems**
- Mobile H2 equipment requirements (applicable to aircraft)
- Vehicle and aircraft H2 storage systems
- Refueling operations

### 6.4 ISO 21013 - Cryogenic Vessels

**Standard for cryogenic vessel design and safety devices**

**Part 1: Fundamental Requirements**
- Pressure vessel design criteria
- Materials selection for cryogenic service
- Insulation systems
- Structural integrity

**Part 2: Operational Requirements**
- Installation and commissioning
- Operational procedures
- Inspection and maintenance
- Decommissioning

**Part 3: Pressure Relief Devices**
- Relief valve sizing and selection
- Vent system design
- Discharge routing and dispersion
- Testing and certification

**AMPEL360-BWB-H2 LH2 Tanks:**
- Double-wall vacuum-insulated design
- Inner vessel: Aluminum 2219 or equivalent
- Outer jacket: Aluminum 6061 or CFRP
- Vacuum level: < 10^-3 mbar
- Pressure relief: Dual redundant valves
- Design pressure: 6 bar (87 psi)
- Design temperature: -253°C to +65°C
