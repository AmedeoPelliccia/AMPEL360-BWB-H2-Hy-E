# 10-00-01-04-02A - ICAO Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies and describes the applicable ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) requirements and standards relevant to parking, mooring, and storage operations for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- ICAO Annexes applicable to ground operations
- Aerodrome design and operational standards
- Airport infrastructure requirements
- International operational harmonization

## 3. Applicable Documents

- [ICAO Annex 14](https://www.icao.int/safety/airnavigation/Pages/Annex-14.aspx) - Aerodromes, Volume I: Aerodrome Design and Operations
- [ICAO Annex 6](https://www.icao.int/safety/airnavigation/Pages/Annex-6.aspx) - Operation of Aircraft
- [ICAO Doc 9157](https://www.icao.int/) - Aerodrome Design Manual
- [ICAO Doc 9184](https://www.icao.int/) - Airport Planning Manual
- [ICAO Doc 9859](https://www.icao.int/) - Safety Management Manual

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

ICAO establishes international standards and recommended practices (SARPs) for civil aviation, including airport design, aircraft operations, and safety management. These standards ensure global harmonization and safety for international flight operations.

### 4.2 H2/LH2 Considerations

ICAO is developing guidance for hydrogen-powered aircraft operations:

- Airport hydrogen infrastructure standards (in development)
- H2 safety zones and separation requirements
- Emergency response procedures for hydrogen aircraft
- Personnel training and qualification standards

Current operations must reference:
- Existing hazardous materials handling procedures
- Cryogenic materials safety guidance
- Adaptation of current fuel handling standards

### 4.3 BWB Configuration Considerations

BWB aircraft require adaptations to ICAO standards:

- Modified aerodrome reference code (ARC) considerations
- Enhanced parking stand dimensions
- Adapted taxiway and apron geometry
- Specialized ground handling procedures

## 6. Safety Considerations

### 6.1 ICAO Annex 14 - Aerodromes

**Volume I: Aerodrome Design and Operations**

**Chapter 2: Aerodrome Data**
- Aircraft characteristics data for AMPEL360-BWB-H2
- Aerodrome Reference Code (ARC) determination
- Operating limitations and special procedures

**Chapter 3: Physical Characteristics**
- Runway length and width requirements
- Taxiway dimensions for BWB width
- Apron size and spacing (modified for BWB and H2 zones)
- Aircraft stand clearances

**AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Aerodrome Reference Code:**
- **Code Element 1**: 4 (Reference field length ≥ 1800m)
- **Code Element 2**: F (Wingspan 65m < 80m)
- **Designation: 4F** (Equivalent to Boeing 747-8, Airbus A380)

**Modified Requirements for H2 Operations:**
- Additional safety zone: 15-meter radius from H2 venting points
- Parking stand separation: 50 meters center-to-center (vs. 45m standard)
- Overhead clearance: 25 meters (for H2 vent dispersion)

### 6.2 ICAO Doc 9157 - Aerodrome Design Manual

**Part 2: Taxiways, Aprons and Holding Bays**

**Aircraft Stand Design:**
- Stand dimensions: 100m × 120m (including safety zones)
- Pavement strength: PCN 80/R/B/W/T or greater
- Surface gradient: 1-2% for drainage
- Stand markings: Enhanced for BWB geometry

**Taxiway Design:**
- Taxiway width: 25 meters minimum (Code F)
- Taxiway-to-object clearance: 47.5 meters (Code F)
- Taxiway centerline to stand centerline: 97.5 meters minimum

**Apron Layout:**
- Aircraft-to-aircraft separation: 25 meters wingtip clearance
- Aircraft-to-building clearance: 20 meters minimum (with H2 safety zone)
- Service road width: 6 meters minimum
- Emergency vehicle access: Maintained clear at all times

### 6.3 ICAO Annex 6 - Operation of Aircraft

**Part I: International Commercial Air Transport - Aeroplanes**

**Chapter 4: Flight Operations**
- Ground operations procedures
- Parking and mooring requirements
- Aircraft servicing during turnaround

**Chapter 6: Aeroplane Instruments, Equipment and Flight Documents**
- Ground operation equipment requirements
- H2 detection and monitoring systems
- Emergency equipment specifications

**AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Specific Procedures:**
- H2 fuel system status verification before engine start
- Cryogenic system monitoring during ground operations
- BWB-specific checklists for parking and departure

### 6.4 ICAO Doc 9859 - Safety Management Manual

**Safety Management System (SMS) Elements:**

**4.1 Safety Policy and Objectives:**

### 6.5 Emergency Response Requirements

**Part 7: Airport Emergency Planning:**
- Emergency response plan for H2 incidents
