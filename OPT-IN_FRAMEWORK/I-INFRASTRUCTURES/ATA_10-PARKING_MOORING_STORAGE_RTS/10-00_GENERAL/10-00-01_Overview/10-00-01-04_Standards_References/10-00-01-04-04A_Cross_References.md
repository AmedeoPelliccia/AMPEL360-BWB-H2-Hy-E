# 10-00-01-04-04A - Cross References

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive cross-reference matrix linking ATA Chapter 10 (Parking, Mooring, Storage & Return to Service) documentation to related ATA chapters, regulatory documents, and standards for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- Cross-references to related ATA chapters
- Links to applicable regulatory requirements
- References to hydrogen and BWB-specific standards
- Traceability between requirements and implementation

## 3. Applicable Documents

- [ATA iSpec 2200](https://www.atastandards.org/) - Information Standards for Aviation Maintenance
- All documents referenced in sections 4-7 below

## 4. Description

### 4.1 Overview

This cross-reference document serves as a central navigation hub for ATA Chapter 10 documentation, enabling users to quickly identify relationships between:

- Operational procedures and system documentation
- Safety requirements and compliance evidence
- H2-specific procedures and general aviation standards

- H2 parking procedures to ATA 28 (Fuel) specifications

- BWB parking geometry to ATA 51-57 (Structures)
