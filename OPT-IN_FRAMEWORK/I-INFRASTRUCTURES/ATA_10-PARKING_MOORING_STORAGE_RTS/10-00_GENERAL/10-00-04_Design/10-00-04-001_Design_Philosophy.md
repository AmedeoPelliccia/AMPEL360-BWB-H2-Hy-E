## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the overarching design philosophy for all parking, mooring, storage, and return-to-service (RTS) systems and provisions for the AMPEL360 BWB-H2-Hy-E (Q100) aircraft.

---

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Safety-First Design

- All ground handling and storage provisions must prevent inadvertent aircraft movement
- H₂ and high-voltage (HV) systems require fail-safe isolation mechanisms
- Clear visual indicators for all safety-critical states (locked, isolated, grounded)

### 2.2 BWB-Specific Considerations

- Wide-body, blended wing design requires distributed mooring and jacking points
- Center of gravity considerations for non-traditional aircraft geometry
- Integration with unconventional fuselage structure

### 2.3 Hydrogen Safety Integration

- Dedicated provisions for LH₂ tank isolation during storage
- Boil-off management and venting systems
- Leak detection and monitoring integration
- Ground vent connection points for safe storage

### 2.4 High-Voltage System Safety

- 800V+ electrical system isolation requirements
- Lockout/tagout (LOTO) provisions for maintenance safety
- Ground fault protection
- Clear arc flash boundaries and warning systems

### 2.5 Operational Efficiency

- Quick-connect/disconnect mechanisms for rapid turnaround
- Tool-less or common-tool operations where practical
- Standardized interfaces compatible with existing ground support equipment (GSE)
- Remote monitoring capabilities for storage conditions

---

## 3. Design Approach

### 3.1 Modular Assembly Design

- Self-contained assembly modules with clear interfaces
- Replaceable/serviceable components
- Standardized mounting provisions

### 3.2 Material Selection

- Corrosion-resistant materials for long-term durability
- Compatible with cryogenic temperatures (H₂ systems)
- High-strength alloys for load-bearing structures
- Non-conductive materials where appropriate for HV isolation

### 3.3 Human Factors

- Ergonomic access for ground crew operations
- Clear labeling and color-coding
- Intuitive operation sequences
- Minimal training requirements
