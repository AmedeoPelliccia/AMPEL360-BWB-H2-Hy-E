# 10-INST-GS-001 - GPU Connection Points

## 1. Purpose

Installation procedures for Ground Power Unit (GPU) connection points for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Scope

- GPU receptacle installation on parking stands
- Connection types and specifications (28V DC, 115V AC)
- Cable routing and protection
- Integration with aircraft electrical system

## 3. GPU Connection Types

### 3.1 28V DC Power

**Specification**: MIL-STD-704 compliant
**Current Capacity**: 200-400 Amps
**Connector**: MS3509 series or equivalent
**Application**: Aircraft battery charging, avionics power

### 3.2 115V AC Power (400 Hz)

**Specification**: MIL-STD-704 compliant
**Capacity**: 90 kVA minimum
**Connector**: MS3509 series (6-pin)
**Application**: Main aircraft electrical system

## 4. Installation Requirements

### 4.1 Receptacle Positioning

**Location**: Port side of aircraft, forward of wing
**Distance from aircraft**: 15-20 ft (cable reach)
**Height**: 3-4 ft above ground
**Mounting**: Pedestal or underground vault

### 4.2 Electrical Installation

1. Install conduit run from power source to receptacle
2. Wire per electrical code (NEC Article 680 for outdoor)
3. Install ground fault protection
4. Bond to aircraft grounding system
5. Test for proper voltage and frequency

### 4.3 BWB Considerations

- GPU connection may be in different location than conventional aircraft
- Verify cable length adequate for BWB geometry
- May require longer cables due to wide body

## 5. H2 Considerations

- GPU equipment must be outside H2 Zone 1 (>25 ft from fuel interface)
- Use explosion-proof or intrinsically safe equipment if in Zone 2
- Verify electrical bonding before H2 fueling
