# 10-INST-GS-002 - Ground Power Installation

## 1. Purpose

Installation of ground power systems and infrastructure for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft parking stands.

## 2. Scope

- Ground power distribution system
- Cable and connector specifications
- Safety interlocks and protection
- Power quality requirements

## 3. Ground Power System Design

### 3.1 Power Distribution

**Main Power Source**: Airport electrical grid or dedicated generator
**Distribution**: Underground or overhead to parking stands
**Voltage**: 115V AC 400Hz and/or 28V DC
**Capacity**: 90 kVA minimum per stand

### 3.2 Power Conditioning

- Voltage regulation: ±5%
- Frequency regulation: ±2 Hz (400 Hz systems)
- Harmonic distortion: <5% THD
- Power factor correction: >0.95

### 3.3 Safety Systems

- Ground fault protection (GFCI)
- Overcurrent protection (circuit breakers)
- Emergency shutoff (accessible from aircraft)
- Status indication (power available, fault)

## 4. Installation Procedure

1. Survey electrical service routing
2. Install underground conduit or overhead cable tray
3. Pull power cables (sized per load and voltage drop)
4. Install power distribution panel at parking stand
5. Install receptacles and connection points
6. Ground all metal components
7. Test voltage, frequency, and safety systems
8. Commission system with load test

## 5. BWB/H2 Considerations

- Electrical equipment must be rated for outdoor/wet locations
- Equipment in H2 zones must be explosion-proof or intrinsically safe
- Bonding and grounding critical for H2 safety
