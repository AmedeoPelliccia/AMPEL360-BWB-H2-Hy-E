# 10-INST-GS-003 - Pneumatic Connections

## 1. Purpose

Installation of pneumatic (compressed air) connections for starting engines and supplying pneumatic power to AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Scope

- Pneumatic connection points and hoses
- Pressure regulation and safety valves
- Air quality requirements
- Integration with aircraft pneumatic system

## 3. Pneumatic System Specifications

### 3.1 Air Supply Requirements

**Pressure**: 40-60 psi (regulated)
**Flow Rate**: 60-120 lb/min (engine starting)
**Quality**: Oil-free, moisture-free
**Temperature**: -20°F to 120°F

### 3.2 Connection Interface

**Type**: Quick-disconnect coupling
**Size**: 1" to 2" diameter
**Material**: Aluminum or stainless steel
**Standard**: MS24484 or equivalent

## 4. Installation

### 4.1 Air Supply System

1. Install air compressor or connect to facility compressed air
2. Install air dryer and oil separator (critical for aircraft use)
3. Install pressure regulator (40-60 psi)
4. Install pressure relief valve (safety)
5. Route air lines to parking stand connection point

### 4.2 Connection Point Installation

1. Position connection point near aircraft (port side typically)
2. Install quick-disconnect coupling on pedestal
3. Install pressure gauge (visible to operator)
4. Install shutoff valve
5. Test for leaks and proper pressure

## 5. Air Quality

- Must be oil-free (aircraft systems sensitive to oil contamination)
- Must be dry (dew point <-40°F to prevent freeze-up)
- Filtration: 5 micron minimum
- Regular testing and maintenance of dryers/filters

## 6. BWB/H2 Considerations

- Pneumatic connections must be outside H2 Zone 1
- Verify no oil contamination (oil + H2 = fire hazard)
- Some H2 aircraft may use pneumatic systems for purge gas
