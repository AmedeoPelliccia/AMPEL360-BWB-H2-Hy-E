# 10-INST-PK-003 - Ground Locks Installation

## 1. Purpose

Installation procedures for landing gear ground locks (safety pins) for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during parking and maintenance operations.

## 2. Scope

- Ground lock types and specifications
- Installation procedures for main and nose gear
- Removal procedures and safety verification
- Documentation and placarding requirements

**Effectivity**: All AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during parking and maintenance

## 3. Applicable Documents

- **ATA 32** - Landing Gear
- **ATA 100 Chapter 10** - Parking procedures
- **Maintenance Manual** - Landing Gear Ground Locks

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**
- Aircraft must not be flown with ground locks installed
- Install "REMOVE BEFORE FLIGHT" streamers on all ground locks
- Verify removal of all ground locks before flight release
- Ground locks prevent gear retraction - DO NOT attempt retraction with locks installed

⚠️ **CAUTIONS**
- Use only approved ground locks for aircraft type
- Inspect ground locks before installation for damage
- Install ground locks only when aircraft on ground and stable

## 5. Ground Lock Specifications

### 5.1 Main Gear Ground Locks

**Function**: Prevent inadvertent main gear retraction during parking

**Design**:
- Type: Mechanical pin lock
- Material: Steel, cadmium or zinc plated
- Quantity: One per main gear strut
- Features: "REMOVE BEFORE FLIGHT" streamer (red, 18" minimum)

**Identification**:
- Part number permanently marked on lock body
- "MAIN GEAR GROUND LOCK" engraved
- Serial number for traceability
- Aircraft type compatibility marked

### 5.2 Nose Gear Ground Lock

**Function**: Prevent inadvertent nose gear retraction during parking

**Design**:
- Type: Mechanical pin lock
- Material: Steel, cadmium or zinc plated
- Quantity: One per nose gear strut
- Features: "REMOVE BEFORE FLIGHT" streamer (red, 18" minimum)

**Identification**:
- Part number permanently marked on lock body
- "NOSE GEAR GROUND LOCK" engraved
- Serial number for traceability
- Aircraft type compatibility marked

## 6. Installation Procedure

### 6.1 Pre-Installation Checks

1. **Verify aircraft is stable**
   - Parking brake set
   - Wheel chocks installed
   - Aircraft weight on gear (not jacked)
   - Hydraulic pressure released (gear system depressurized)

2. **Inspect ground locks**
   - No cracks, bends, or damage
   - Part numbers correct for aircraft
   - Streamers in good condition and securely attached
   - Retaining hardware present and functional

3. **Review maintenance documentation**
   - Verify ground lock installation is authorized for current operation
   - Check for any special instructions or modifications

### 6.2 Main Gear Ground Lock Installation

**Procedure**:

1. Locate main gear ground lock receptacle
   - Port main gear: Outboard side of strut
   - Starboard main gear: Outboard side of strut
   - Receptacle typically on upper portion of strut

2. Insert ground lock pin into receptacle
   - Align pin with receptacle hole
   - Insert pin fully until it seats
   - Verify pin is through both sides of receptacle

3. Install retaining hardware
   - Install retaining clip or cotter pin
   - Verify hardware is fully engaged
   - Check that ground lock cannot be removed without deliberate action

4. Position streamer
   - Ensure "REMOVE BEFORE FLIGHT" streamer is visible
   - Streamer should hang outside aircraft and be visible during walk-around
   - Do not tuck streamer where it could be hidden

5. Repeat for all main gear assemblies

### 6.3 Nose Gear Ground Lock Installation

**Procedure**:

1. Locate nose gear ground lock receptacle
   - Typically on forward or aft side of strut
   - May be on gear bay door or strut body

2. Insert ground lock pin into receptacle
   - Align pin with receptacle hole
   - Insert pin fully until it seats
   - Verify pin passes through both sides

3. Install retaining hardware
   - Install retaining clip or cotter pin
   - Verify secure engagement
   - Check that removal requires deliberate action

4. Position streamer
   - Ensure high visibility
   - Streamer visible from pilot position if possible
   - Do not obstruct gear bay or interfere with other equipment

### 6.4 Post-Installation Verification

1. **Visual inspection**
   - All ground locks fully inserted and secured
   - Retaining hardware properly installed
   - Streamers visible and in good condition
   - No interference with other systems

2. **Documentation**
   - Complete maintenance log entry
   - Install placard in cockpit: "GROUND LOCKS INSTALLED"
   - Update aircraft status board
   - Notify flight crew if aircraft turnover occurs

3. **Photographic documentation** (recommended)
   - Photograph each installed ground lock
   - Provides verification record
   - Useful for training and quality assurance

## 7. Removal Procedure

### 7.1 Pre-Removal Verification

**Before removing ground locks, verify**:
- Aircraft cleared for flight by maintenance
- Pre-flight inspection completed
- All maintenance work complete
- Hydraulic system serviceable

### 7.2 Removal Steps

1. **Remove retaining hardware**
   - Remove cotter pin or retaining clip
   - Store hardware with ground lock for next use

2. **Withdraw ground lock pin**
   - Pull pin straight out of receptacle
   - Do not bend or damage pin during removal
   - Verify pin comes out completely

3. **Inspect receptacle**
   - Check for damage or foreign objects
   - Verify receptacle is clean and undamaged
   - Report any issues before flight release

4. **Store ground lock**
   - Store in designated location on aircraft or in ground equipment
   - Secure to prevent loss
   - Keep streamers attached for next use

### 7.3 Post-Removal Verification

1. **Verify all ground locks removed**
   - Visual check of each gear strut receptacle
   - Account for all ground locks (count matches installation log)
   - Verify no streamers visible on aircraft

2. **Remove cockpit placard**
   - Remove "GROUND LOCKS INSTALLED" placard
   - Verify flight crew aware of ground lock removal

3. **Documentation**
   - Make log entry: "Ground locks removed, aircraft cleared for flight"
   - Sign and date log entry
   - Update aircraft status

## 8. BWB Considerations

### 8.1 Gear Access

- BWB may have unique gear configuration
- Access to ground lock receptacles may be limited
- May require access platforms or stands
- Ensure adequate lighting for installation/removal

### 8.2 Multiple Gear Bogies

- BWB may have multiple main gear assemblies
- Verify ground lock installed on each gear strut per design
- Maintain accurate count of required ground locks
- Update checklist for BWB-specific gear configuration

## 9. H2 Considerations

- Ground locks may be installed during H2 fueling operations
- Verify no interference with H2 ground equipment
- Streamers should not be near H2 vent outlets
- Include ground lock status in H2 fueling safety checklist

## 10. Quality Assurance

### 10.1 Ground Lock Management

- Maintain inventory of ground locks with serial number tracking
- Inspect ground locks annually for condition
- Replace damaged or worn ground locks immediately
- Ensure sufficient inventory for fleet size

### 10.2 Training Requirements

- All maintenance personnel must be trained on ground lock installation/removal
- Include ground lock procedures in aircraft type training
- Periodic refresher training on streamers and placarding
- Include ground locks in pre-flight inspection training for flight crews
