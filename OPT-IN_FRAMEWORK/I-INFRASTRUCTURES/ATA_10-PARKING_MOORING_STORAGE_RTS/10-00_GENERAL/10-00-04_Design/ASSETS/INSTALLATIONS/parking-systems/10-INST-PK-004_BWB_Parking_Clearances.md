# 10-INST-PK-004 - BWB Parking Clearances

## 1. Purpose

Specification of parking clearances and envelope dimensions specific to the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Blended Wing Body configuration.

## 2. Scope

- BWB-specific dimensional clearances
- Parking envelope definition
- Ground equipment positioning constraints
- Wing tip and surface clearance requirements

**Effectivity**: All AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft parking operations

## 3. Applicable Documents

- **IATA AHM 902** - Aircraft Stand Requirements
- **FAA AC 150/5300-13B** - Airport Design
- **ATA 02** - Weight and Balance (aircraft dimensions)

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**
- BWB configuration has significantly wider wingspan than conventional aircraft of similar weight
- Lower ground clearance requires careful equipment positioning
- Wing tips are close to ground - collision hazard for ground vehicles
- Large plan area requires greater parking space allocation

## 6. Parking Clearances

### 6.1 Minimum Clearances

**Lateral (Wing Tip to Obstacle)**:
- Absolute minimum: 10 ft
- Recommended: 15 ft
- Preferred: 20 ft (for maintenance access)
- High wind operations: 25 ft (wing deflection)

**Longitudinal (Nose/Tail to Obstacle)**:
- Nose clearance: 25 ft minimum
- Tail clearance: 20 ft minimum
- Recommended: 30 ft both ends
- Consider jet blast and tow vehicle requirements

**Vertical (Overhead Clearances)**:
- Minimum overhead clearance: Aircraft height + 10 ft
- For hangar: Aircraft height + 15 ft (door clearance)
- Consider jacking height if maintenance planned

**Ground Equipment**:
- Equipment to wing tip: 10 ft minimum
- Ground vehicles parking: 15 ft from wing tips
- Active GSE during operations: 5 ft minimum (with spotters)
- H2 fueling equipment: Per H2 safety requirements (ref 10-INST-H2-004)

### 6.2 Parking Envelope

**Definition**: The parking envelope is the protected 3D space around the parked aircraft where no obstacles or equipment may be positioned without authorization.

**BWB Parking Envelope Dimensions**:
- Length: Aircraft length + 50 ft (25 ft nose, 20 ft tail, +5 ft margin)
- Width: Wingspan + 30 ft (15 ft each wing tip)
- Height: Aircraft height + 10 ft overhead clearance

### 6.3 Taxiway/Taxilane Clearances

**While Taxiing**:
- Wing tip to taxiway edge: 25 ft minimum
- Wing tip to parked aircraft: 50 ft minimum
- Wing tip to building: 50 ft minimum
- Taxilane centerline to parking centerline: Per IATA standards + BWB width adjustment

## 7. Ground Equipment Positioning

### 7.1 Equipment Zones

**Zone A - Critical Area** (0-10 ft from aircraft):
- Essential GSE only (GPU, air conditioning, chocks)
- Slow movement, spotter required
- Wing tip protectors recommended
- No vehicles except during connection/disconnection

**Zone B - Controlled Area** (10-15 ft from aircraft):
- Normal GSE operations (fuel trucks, catering, etc.)
- Standard safety protocols
- Wing tip clearance monitoring
- Vehicles must have clearance approval

**Zone C - General Area** (15+ ft from aircraft):
- All ground equipment and vehicles allowed
- Standard airport vehicle operations
- Maintain awareness of aircraft envelope
- No special restrictions

### 7.2 Equipment-Specific Positioning

**GPU and Air Conditioning**:
- Position 15-20 ft from aircraft
- Typically on port side (left)
- Clear path for connection
- Outside H2 Zone 1 if applicable

**Fuel/H2 Equipment**:
- Position per H2 safety requirements
- Typically starboard side (right)
- Maintain clearance from wing tips
- See 10-INST-H2-004 for H2-specific requirements

**Catering and Servicing**:
- Position adjacent to service doors
- Maintain wing tip clearance
- Consider BWB upper surface access requirements
- May require non-standard equipment for BWB

**Tow Tractor**:
- Position at nose for pushback
- Verify clearance for tow bar attachment
- Account for BWB CG when towing (aft of conventional)
- Ensure adequate traction for tail-heavy aircraft

## 8. Special BWB Considerations

### 8.1 Wing Tip Protection

**Required Measures**:
- Install reflective wing tip markers (orange/white striped)
- Use foam wing tip protectors during ground operations
- Position safety cones 10 ft from wing tips
- Provide adequate lighting for night operations

**Visual Aids**:
- Paint wing tip position markers on parking stand
- Install LED edge lights on stand perimeter
- Use elevated markers visible to equipment operators
- Consider ground-level projected lines (laser or LED)

### 8.2 Lower Surface Protection

**Soft Ground Considerations**:
- BWB may settle on soft surfaces due to large lower surface area
- Use reinforced parking surfaces (concrete or asphalt)
- Avoid grass or gravel parking areas
- Monitor for ground subsidence during extended parking

**Access Considerations**:
- Limited access under aircraft due to low clearance
- May require specialized low-profile equipment
- Drainage must not create puddles under aircraft
- Snow/ice removal critical for lower surface clearance

### 8.3 Upper Surface Access

**Access Requirements**:
- Require elevated work platforms (20+ ft)
- Multiple access points due to wide upper surface
- Platforms must not contact wing surfaces
- Consider wind loads on elevated platforms due to BWB exposure

## 9. H2 Integration

**H2 Safety Zones**: (ref 10-INST-H2-001)
- Zone 1 (0-25 ft from H2 interface): No ignition sources
- Zone 2 (25-50 ft): Controlled access
- Zone 3 (50-100 ft): General safety awareness

**BWB H2 Parking Interaction**:
- H2 zones must be considered within parking envelope
- May require dedicated H2 parking stands
- Increased clearances for H2 operations
- Wind direction critical for H2 venting (affects parking orientation)

## 10. Parking Stand Design Recommendations

- Designate specific stands for BWB aircraft (clearly marked)
- Provide additional width vs conventional aircraft stands
- Consider adjacency to other stands (do not overcrowd)
- Provide adequate maneuvering room for tow operations
- Install BWB-specific guidance systems (visual docking)
- Consider prevailing wind for H2 operations orientation

- Annual survey of parking stand dimensions
- Verify clearance markers are visible and accurate
- Update stand documentation with any changes
- Train ground crew on BWB-specific clearances
