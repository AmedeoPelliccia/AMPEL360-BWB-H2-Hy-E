# 10-INST-PK-001 - Parking Stand Equipment

## 1. Purpose

Specification and installation procedures for ground support equipment required at parking stands for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft operations.

## 2. Scope

- Parking stand equipment requirements and layout
- Equipment positioning for BWB configuration
- H2-specific ground support equipment
- Safety zone markings and clearances

**Effectivity**: All parking stands supporting AMPEL360-BWB-H2 operations

## 3. Applicable Documents

- **ATA 03** - Ground Support Equipment
- **IATA AHM 902** - Aircraft Stand Requirements
- **SAE AS6968** - Hydrogen Aircraft GSE
- **NFPA 2** - Hydrogen Technologies Code

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**
- H2 detection sensors required within 15 ft of aircraft
- No ignition sources within H2 safety zone (25 ft radius from fuel interface)
- BWB configuration requires wider clearances than conventional aircraft
- Ground personnel must be trained on H2 safety procedures

## 6. Parking Stand Layout

### 6.1 Aircraft Positioning

**Lead-in Line**: Centerline marking from taxiway to parking position
- Width: 18" yellow stripe
- Length: 100 ft minimum
- Nose wheel position marker at stop point

**Aircraft Parking Position**:
- Marked with aircraft silhouette outline (BWB shape)
- Nose gear stop position clearly marked
- Wing tip position markers
- Tail clearance marker

**Clearances** (minimum):
- Wing tip to obstacle: 15 ft
- Nose to obstacle: 25 ft
- Tail to obstacle: 20 ft
- Side clearance (fuselage/wing): 10 ft

### 6.2 H2 Safety Zones

**Zone 1 - No Ignition Sources** (0-25 ft radius from H2 interface):
- No electrical equipment (except intrinsically safe)
- No vehicles with internal combustion engines
- No smoking or open flames
- H2 detection sensors required
- Personnel with H2 training only

**Zone 2 - Restricted Access** (25-50 ft radius from H2 interface):
- Controlled access during H2 operations
- H2 detection sensors recommended
- Fire suppression equipment positioned
- Personnel briefed on H2 hazards

**Zone 3 - General Safety** (50-100 ft radius):
- Normal GSE operations allowed
- H2 safety signage visible
- Emergency egress routes marked
- Assembly point identified

### 6.3 Equipment Positioning

**GPU and Air Conditioning**:
- Position on left side of aircraft (port)
- Distance from aircraft: 15-25 ft
- Clear path for connection hoses/cables
- Outside H2 Zone 1

**H2 Fueling Equipment**:
- Position on right side of aircraft (starboard) per design
- Distance from aircraft: 10-15 ft during connection
- Retract to 50 ft+ when not in use
- Within visual range of H2 operator station

**Fire Extinguishers**:
- 2 units positioned at 90° intervals around aircraft
- Maximum 100 ft from any point on aircraft
- Mounted on mobile carts or fixed stations
- Clearly marked and illuminated

## 7. Installation Procedure

### 7.1 Marking Parking Stand

1. **Survey and layout**
   - Establish aircraft parking position centerpoint
   - Mark nose gear stop position
   - Layout BWB wing outline using templates
   - Mark clearance zones

2. **Paint markings**
   - Lead-in line: 18" yellow stripe
   - Aircraft outline: 12" yellow stripe
   - Safety zones: Yellow hatching for H2 zones
   - Equipment positions: White boxes with labels

3. **Install signage**
   - H2 safety signs at zone boundaries
   - "STOP" sign at nose gear position
   - Aircraft type placard (AMPEL360-BWB-H2)
   - Emergency contact information

### 7.2 Equipment Installation

1. **Fixed equipment**
   - Install GPU pedestal with underground wiring
   - Install H2 detection sensor mounts
   - Install grounding point receptacle
   - Install emergency lighting

2. **Portable equipment positioning**
   - Position wheel chocks at main gear locations
   - Place fire extinguishers at designated locations
   - Position safety cones at clearance boundaries
   - Deploy H2 safety equipment

3. **H2 system verification**
   - Test H2 detection sensors (function and calibration)
   - Verify emergency vent system operation
   - Check H2 fueling interface for leaks
   - Test alarm and communication systems

### 7.3 Operational Verification

1. **Aircraft positioning test** (using substitute aircraft or simulation)
   - Verify lead-in line guides aircraft to correct position
   - Check clearances at all extremities
   - Confirm equipment access is adequate
   - Verify GSE connection reach

2. **Safety system test**
   - Test H2 detection and alarm system
   - Verify emergency lighting and signage visibility
   - Check fire suppression equipment access
   - Test emergency communication

3. **Documentation**
   - Photograph completed parking stand
   - Document equipment serial numbers and locations
   - Update airport parking stand database
   - Create parking stand quick reference card

## 8. H2/BWB Considerations

### 8.1 BWB Parking Envelope
