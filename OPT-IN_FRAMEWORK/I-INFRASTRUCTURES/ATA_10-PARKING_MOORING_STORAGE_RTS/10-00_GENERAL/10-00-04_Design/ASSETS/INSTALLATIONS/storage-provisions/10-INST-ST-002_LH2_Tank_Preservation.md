# 10-INST-ST-002 - LH2 Tank Preservation

## 1. Purpose

Specific procedures for preserving liquid hydrogen fuel tanks during long-term storage of AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Scope

- LH2 tank purging and drying procedures
- Inertization with dry nitrogen
- Moisture control and monitoring
- Periodic inspection during storage

**Effectivity**: All AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft LH2 tanks during storage >30 days

## 3. Applicable Documents

- **ATA 28** - Fuel System
- **ISO 13985** - Liquid Hydrogen storage
- **NFPA 2** - Hydrogen Technologies Code

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**
- Tanks must be completely purged of H2 before preservation work
- Use only dry, oil-free nitrogen for inertization
- Any moisture in tank will freeze and damage internal components
- Verify H2 concentration <1% before tank entry (if required)

## 5. LH2 Tank Preservation Procedure

### 5.1 Tank Purging

1. **Defuel aircraft** - transfer all LH2 to ground storage
2. **Warm purge** - circulate warm nitrogen (70-100°F) through tank
   - Purge flow rate: 100-500 SCFM
   - Duration: Until tank temperature >32°F throughout
   - Purpose: Vaporize any residual LH2
3. **Verify H2 removal** - sample tank atmosphere, confirm H2 <1%
4. **Continue nitrogen purge** until oxygen <2% (inert atmosphere)

### 5.2 Tank Drying

1. **Hot nitrogen drying**
   - Circulate heated nitrogen (100-150°F, not to exceed tank rating)
   - Flow rate: 200-500 SCFM
   - Duration: 4-8 hours minimum
2. **Dew point monitoring**
   - Install dew point sensor in tank exhaust
   - Continue drying until dew point <-40°F
   - Typical drying time: 8-24 hours
3. **Desiccant installation**
   - Install desiccant cartridges in tank if accessible
   - Use indicating desiccant (color change when saturated)
   - Quantity: 10 lb per 1000 gallons tank volume

### 5.3 Pressurization and Sealing

1. **Pressurize tank** with dry nitrogen (dew point <-40°F)
   - Pressure: 5-10 psig (positive pressure prevents moisture ingress)
   - Verify pressure holds (leak rate <1 psi/month acceptable)
2. **Seal all openings**
   - Cap fuel fill ports with gasketed caps
   - Cap vent outlets with moisture barriers
   - Tag all caps: "PRESERVED FOR STORAGE - DRY NITROGEN"
3. **Install pressure relief** (if not permanent)
   - Set relief at 15 psig (prevent over-pressure)
   - Vent relief to safe location (upward discharge)

### 5.4 Periodic Monitoring

**Monthly Checks**:
- Monitor tank pressure (should remain 5-10 psig)
- Inspect desiccant color (replace if saturated)
- Check caps and seals for integrity
- Document pressure readings

**Quarterly Actions**:
- Sample tank atmosphere (dew point, oxygen)
- Repressurize if pressure dropped below 5 psig
- Replace desiccant if required
- Inspect tank exterior for corrosion

## 6. Return from Storage

1. **Verify tank condition** - pressure, dew point acceptable
2. **Remove desiccant** packs
3. **Leak test** tank and all connections
4. **Functional test** tank systems (valves, instrumentation)
5. **Re-certify tank** per maintenance manual before refueling

## 7. BWB/H2 Considerations

- BWB may have multiple LH2 tanks - preserve each independently
- Large tank volume requires longer drying time
- Monitor for differential pressure between tanks
- Access to tank internals may be limited - use remote sensors
