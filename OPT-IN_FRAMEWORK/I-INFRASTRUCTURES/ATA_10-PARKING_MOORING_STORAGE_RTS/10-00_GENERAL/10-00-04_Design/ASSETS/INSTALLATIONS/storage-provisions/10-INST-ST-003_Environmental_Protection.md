# 10-INST-ST-003 - Environmental Protection

## 1. Purpose

Environmental protection measures for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during storage including covers, desiccants, and corrosion prevention.

## 2. Scope

- Aircraft covers and protective equipment
- Desiccant placement and monitoring
- Corrosion prevention compounds
- UV protection for composites

## 3. Environmental Protection Measures

### 3.1 Aircraft Covers

**Cockpit/Windscreen Covers**:
- Material: UV-resistant fabric, breathable
- Coverage: All windscreens and windows
- Purpose: Prevent UV damage, moisture ingress, bird nests

**Engine Inlet/Exhaust Covers**:
- Material: Heavy-duty nylon or vinyl
- Secure attachment: Straps or bungees
- Label: "REMOVE BEFORE FLIGHT"

**Wing/Surface Covers** (BWB-specific):
- Large covers for BWB upper surface (protect from UV, rain, snow)
- Breathable material (prevent moisture trap)
- Secure against wind

### 3.2 Desiccant Placement

- Cockpit: 5 lb per cockpit volume
- Avionics bays: 2 lb per bay
- Fuel tanks: Per 10-INST-ST-002
- Other enclosed spaces: 1 lb per 100 ft³

### 3.3 Corrosion Prevention

- Apply corrosion-preventive compound to exposed metal surfaces
- Lubricate hinges, latches, and moving parts
- Protect composite-metal interfaces

## 4. BWB Considerations

- Large upper surface area requires extensive cover system
- Monitor for moisture accumulation in integrated wing-body
- UV exposure is significant concern for composite BWB structure
