# 10-INST-ST-001 - Long-Term Storage Preparation

## 1. Purpose

Procedures for preparing AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft for long-term storage (>30 days) including preservation of systems, structures, and LH2 fuel systems.

## 2. Scope

- Aircraft preparation checklist for storage
- System preservation procedures
- Environmental protection measures
- Periodic inspection schedule during storage

**Effectivity**: All AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft entering long-term storage

## 3. Applicable Documents

- **ATA 10** - Parking, Mooring, Storage
- **ATA 28** - Fuel System Preservation
- **ATA 05** - Time Limits/Maintenance Checks
- **Maintenance Manual** - Storage Procedures

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**
- LH2 tanks must be completely purged and inerted before long-term storage
- Systems must be preserved per manufacturer requirements
- Aircraft must be secured against wind and weather
- Periodic inspection required even during storage

## 5. Storage Preparation Checklist

### 5.1 Fuel System (Critical for LH2)

- [ ] Defuel aircraft completely (LH2 tanks empty)
- [ ] Purge LH2 tanks with warm nitrogen (remove all H2)
- [ ] Dry LH2 tanks (remove moisture, dew point <-40°F)
- [ ] Pressurize tanks with dry nitrogen (5-10 psig positive pressure)
- [ ] Cap all fuel vents and openings (moisture barriers)
- [ ] Lubricate/preserve fuel system valves and pumps
- [ ] Install desiccant packs in accessible areas
- [ ] Tag system: "PRESERVED FOR STORAGE - DO NOT OPERATE"

### 5.2 Propulsion Systems

- [ ] Drain all fluids per manufacturer (oil, hydraulic, water/glycol)
- [ ] Preserve engines per engine manual
- [ ] Install engine inlet and exhaust covers
- [ ] Rotate engines monthly (if required by manual)
- [ ] Lubricate bearings and moving parts

### 5.3 Landing Gear and Brakes

- [ ] Inflate tires to maximum pressure
- [ ] Install wheel chocks and ground locks
- [ ] Jack aircraft (if extended storage) to relieve gear load
- [ ] Preserve brake systems, lubricate components
- [ ] Cover wheels to protect from UV

### 5.4 Electrical and Avionics

- [ ] Disconnect batteries or connect to trickle charger
- [ ] Desiccant in avionics bays
- [ ] Cover pitot/static ports
- [ ] Preserve electrical connectors (dielectric grease)

### 5.5 Environmental Protection

- [ ] Install aircraft covers (windscreen, cockpit, inlets/exhausts)
- [ ] Seal doors and hatches with weatherstripping
- [ ] Install desiccant packs throughout aircraft
- [ ] Apply corrosion-preventive compound to exposed metal
- [ ] Protect composite surfaces from UV

### 5.6 Securing for Weather

- [ ] Install tiedowns per 10-INST-TD-002 (BWB configuration)
- [ ] Position aircraft nose into prevailing wind
- [ ] Install wing tip protectors
- [ ] Remove or secure external equipment

## 6. Periodic Inspection During Storage

- **Weekly**: Visual walk-around, check tiedowns, look for damage
- **Monthly**: Open selected panels, check for moisture/corrosion, rotate engines (if req'd), check tire pressure
- **Quarterly**: Detailed inspection per maintenance manual, test preserved systems

## 7. Return to Service

- Complete return-to-service inspection per maintenance manual
- Depreserve all systems
- Functional test all systems before flight
- Update maintenance records
- Refer to ATA 10 Return to Service procedures

## 8. BWB/H2 Considerations

- BWB large surface area may accumulate more moisture - increase desiccant
- LH2 tank preservation critical - any moisture becomes ice
- BWB may require additional tiedown points for long-term storage
- Monitor for composite degradation (UV exposure on large wing surfaces)
