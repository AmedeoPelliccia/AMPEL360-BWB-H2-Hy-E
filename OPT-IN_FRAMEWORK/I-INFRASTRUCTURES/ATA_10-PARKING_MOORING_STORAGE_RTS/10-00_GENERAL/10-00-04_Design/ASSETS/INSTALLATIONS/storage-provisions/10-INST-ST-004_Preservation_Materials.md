# 10-INST-ST-004 - Preservation Materials

## 1. Purpose

Specification of materials and compounds used for aircraft preservation during storage.

## 2. Scope

- Desiccant types and specifications
- Corrosion-preventive compounds
- Protective coatings and covers
- Material compatibility with BWB/H2 systems

## 3. Preservation Materials

### 3.1 Desiccants

**Indicating Silica Gel**:
- Type: Grade 03, 3-8 mesh
- Capacity: Absorbs 40% of own weight
- Indicator: Blue to pink color change when saturated
- Packaging: Cloth bags, various sizes (1-10 lb)

**Molecular Sieve**:
- Type: 4A molecular sieve
- Application: Ultra-dry environments (LH2 tanks)
- Dew point capability: Down to -100°F

### 3.2 Corrosion Preventive Compounds (CPC)

**Water-Displacing CPC**:
- Spec: MIL-C-81309 Type II
- Application: Spray or brush on exposed metal
- Thickness: 0.001-0.003"
- Removal: Mineral spirits

**Long-Term CPC**:
- Spec: MIL-C-16173 Grade 3
- Application: Brush or spray on critical areas
- Protection: Up to 2 years
- Removal: Solvent required

### 3.3 Protective Covers

**Aircraft Covers**:
- Material: Solution-dyed acrylic or polyester
- UV protection: >95% UV block
- Breathability: Yes (prevent moisture trap)
- Fire resistance: Flame-retardant treated

### 3.4 Lubricants and Greases

**Preservation Lubricant**:
- Spec: MIL-PRF-16173
- Application: Moving parts, bearings
- Provides corrosion protection and lubrication

## 4. Material Compatibility

- All materials must be compatible with composite BWB structure
- No materials that outgas in LH2 environment
- No materials that become brittle at cryogenic temperatures (if near H2 system)
