# 10-INST-TD-001 - Tiedown Points Installation

## 1. Purpose

This document provides installation procedures for aircraft tiedown points on the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft. Tiedown points are critical structural attachments that secure the aircraft to ground anchors during parking, preventing movement due to wind, weather, or ground operations.

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Installation of primary tiedown attachment points on airframe structure
- Hardware specifications and torque requirements
- Load path verification and structural integrity checks
- Applicability to AMPEL360-BWB-H2 production aircraft and modifications

**Effectivity**: All AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft configurations

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**

- **STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY**: Improper installation may compromise aircraft structural integrity and lead to catastrophic failure during high-wind events.
- **LOAD PATH**: Ensure tiedown points are installed only at approved structural hard points with verified load paths.
- **TORQUE CRITICAL**: All fasteners are torque-critical. Use calibrated torque wrenches only.
- **FALL PROTECTION**: Work at height requires fall protection equipment and trained personnel.

⚠️ **CAUTIONS**

- Do not drill or modify aircraft structure without engineering approval
- Verify hardware is free from corrosion and damage before installation
- Ensure proper alignment to prevent eccentric loading
- Protect surrounding aircraft skin and systems during installation

## 7. Installation Procedure

### 7.1 Preparation

#### 7.1.1 Documentation Review
1. Review engineering drawings for tiedown point locations (ref: 10-INST-TD-002)
2. Verify part numbers and quantities against Bill of Materials
3. Confirm structural hard point locations on aircraft
4. Review any aircraft-specific Service Bulletins or modifications

#### 7.1.2 Pre-Installation Inspection
1. Inspect all hardware for:
   - Correct part numbers and traceability
   - Freedom from corrosion, damage, or defects
   - Proper plating and finish
   - Current shelf life (for time-limited items)

2. Inspect installation locations for:
   - Structural integrity (no cracks, corrosion, or damage)
   - Proper access for installation and inspection
   - Clearance for tiedown equipment
   - Correct identification markings

3. Verify torque wrench calibration:
   - Check calibration certificate is current (within 6 months)
   - Verify torque range appropriate for application
   - Test wrench operation before use

#### 7.1.3 Area Preparation
1. Clean installation area with approved solvent
2. Remove any protective coatings or paint at attachment points
3. Establish work zone with proper lighting
4. Install fall protection anchor points if working at height
5. Protect adjacent aircraft systems with covers as needed

### 7.2 Installation Steps

#### 7.2.1 Location Verification (Primary Tiedown Points)

**BWB-Specific Consideration**: The AMPEL360-BWB configuration requires minimum 6 tiedown points due to wide wingspan and unique center of gravity. Refer to 10-INST-TD-002 for approved locations.

1. Measure and mark primary tiedown locations per engineering drawing
   - Forward port location: Station XX, Buttock Line YY
   - Forward starboard location: Station XX, Buttock Line YY
   - Aft port location: Station XX, Buttock Line YY
   - Aft starboard location: Station XX, Buttock Line YY
   - Wing port location: Station XX, Buttock Line YY (BWB-specific)
   - Wing starboard location: Station XX, Buttock Line YY (BWB-specific)

2. Verify measurements with second inspector (independent verification required)

3. Check for interference with:
   - Fuel system components
   - Electrical harnesses
   - Hydraulic lines
   - Control surfaces
   - H2 venting system (if applicable)

#### 7.2.2 Fitting Installation

1. **Position tiedown fitting** at marked location
   - Align with aircraft datum reference
   - Ensure fitting orientation matches drawing
   - Check for proper surface contact (no gaps)

2. **Install fasteners hand-tight** (do not torque yet)
   - Insert fasteners from outside to inside where possible
   - Use anti-seize on threads per drawing notes
   - Ensure washers are correctly positioned
   - Verify lock washers are oriented correctly

3. **Check alignment** before final torque
   - Use level to verify fitting is perpendicular to load direction
   - Measure diagonal distances to verify square installation
   - Ensure no binding or preload in fittings

4. **Apply torque in sequence** per drawing notes
   - Start with center fasteners, work outward
   - Use star pattern for multiple fastener installations
   - Torque to specified value: **XXX ft-lb ±5%**
   - Re-check torque after 15 minutes (cold torque check)

5. **Apply torque stripe** across fastener head and structure
   - Use yellow paint for easy visual verification
   - Ensure stripe crosses both fastener and structure
   - Allow to dry before proceeding

#### 7.2.3 Load Path Verification

1. **Visual inspection** of load path components
   - Inspect for proper engagement of threads
   - Check for correct washer installation
   - Verify no gaps between fitting and structure
   - Look for any signs of binding or misalignment

2. **Tap test** to verify solid attachment
   - Use small hammer to tap around fitting
   - Listen for solid, uniform sound
   - Investigate any dull or hollow sounds

3. **Pull test** (if required by engineering)
   - Apply specified test load using calibrated equipment
   - Monitor for movement, deflection, or damage
   - Document test results

### 7.3 Verification

#### 7.3.1 Final Inspection

1. **Hardware inspection**
   - All fasteners torqued and marked
   - No missing washers or lock washers
   - Proper thread engagement (minimum 6 threads)
   - Torque stripe intact and continuous

2. **Fitting inspection**
   - Proper orientation and alignment
   - No cracks or damage visible
   - Identification markings legible
   - Surface finish intact

3. **Clearance verification**
   - Minimum 2" clearance for tiedown equipment attachment
   - No interference with adjacent systems
   - Access for inspection maintained
   - Safety clearance from personnel walkways

#### 7.3.2 Functional Test

1. **Attach test tiedown equipment** to verify:
   - Proper fit of tiedown rope/chain attachment
   - Smooth operation of locking mechanism (if applicable)
   - No binding or interference
   - Correct orientation for load application

2. **Check articulation** (if applicable)
   - Swivel fittings move freely through full range
   - No binding or resistance
   - Locking mechanism functions properly
   - Return to neutral position

#### 7.3.3 Documentation

1. Complete installation record with:
   - Date and time of installation
   - Installer name and certification number
   - Inspector name and certification number
   - Hardware lot numbers and traceability
   - Torque values achieved
   - Any deviations or non-conformances
   - Reference to engineering drawings

2. Update aircraft maintenance records

3. Place "INSTALLED" stamp in work order

4. Photograph installation for quality records (optional but recommended)

## 8. H2/BWB Considerations

### 8.1 BWB Configuration Specifics

**Center of Mass**: The AMPEL360-BWB configuration has a center of gravity farther aft than conventional aircraft due to the blended wing body design. This requires:
- Additional wing tiedown points (minimum 2, one per wing panel)
- Load distribution that accounts for the wide wingspan (approx. XXX ft)
- Asymmetric loading consideration during partial tiedown operations

**Structural Access**: BWB configuration may have limited access to some structural hard points due to integrated wing-body design. Ensure:
- Borescope inspection of internal structure where direct visual inspection is not possible
- Proper tooling for blind installations where required
- Alternative inspection methods (ultrasonic, eddy current) for hidden fasteners

**Ground Clearance**: The BWB design has lower ground clearance than conventional aircraft:
- Use appropriate work platforms to avoid damage to lower surface
- Be aware of limited vertical access for some tiedown points
- Protect lower surface during equipment positioning

### 8.2 H2 System Integration

**Fuel System Proximity**: Some tiedown points may be in proximity to LH2 fuel system components:
- Verify no penetration of fuel system boundaries
- Ensure proper clearance from cryogenic lines (minimum XX inches)
- Check for interference with H2 vent system
- Coordinate with ATA 28 procedures if fuel system access is required

**Bonding and Grounding**: H2 safety requires enhanced bonding:
- Verify electrical bonding at each tiedown point
- Check bond resistance (< 0.003 ohms aircraft to ground)
- Ensure bonding jumpers installed where required
- Test bonding before any H2 fueling operations
