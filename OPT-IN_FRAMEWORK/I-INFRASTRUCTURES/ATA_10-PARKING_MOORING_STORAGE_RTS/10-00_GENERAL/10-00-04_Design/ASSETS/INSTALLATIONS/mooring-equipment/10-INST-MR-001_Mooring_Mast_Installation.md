# 10-INST-MR-001 - Mooring Mast Installation

## 1. Purpose

Installation procedures for mooring mast systems used to secure the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during extended parking periods in high-wind conditions.

## 2. Scope

- Ground-based mooring mast installation
- Aircraft connection interface requirements
- Load capacity verification
- BWB-specific mooring considerations

**Effectivity**: All AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft, ground facilities with mooring capability

## 3. Applicable Documents

- **ATA 100 Chapter 10** - Parking, Mooring, Storage
- **MIL-STD-970** - Tiedown provisions
- **ASCE 7** - Minimum design loads
- **CS-25** - Certification specifications

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**
- Mooring mast must be designed for maximum aircraft weight plus wind loads
- Foundation must be engineered for local soil conditions
- Never connect aircraft to mast in winds >60 knots
- Personnel exclusion zone required during high-wind operations

⚠️ **CAUTIONS**
- Verify mast structural integrity before each use
- Inspect mooring lines for wear and damage
- Account for BWB center of gravity when positioning mast

## 6. Mooring Mast Specifications

### 6.1 Mast Design Requirements

**Type**: Fixed vertical mast with swivel head
**Height**: 25-30 ft above ground (to clear BWB upper surface)
**Load Capacity**: 25,000 lb minimum (vertical and lateral combined)
**Material**: Structural steel, hot-dip galvanized
**Foundation**: Reinforced concrete, 6 ft depth minimum

### 6.2 BWB-Specific Requirements

- **Mast Height**: Must clear maximum BWB height (approx. XX ft) plus 5 ft clearance
- **Positioning**: Located at aircraft CG longitudinal station for optimal load distribution
- **Multiple Masts**: May require 2 masts for aircraft >100,000 lb MTOW

## 7. Installation Procedure

### 7.1 Site Preparation

1. Survey site and mark mast location
   - Position at aircraft CG station when parked
   - Verify clearance from other structures (minimum 50 ft)
   - Check soil load-bearing capacity (min 5,000 psf)

2. Excavate foundation
   - Depth: 6 ft minimum (below frost line in cold climates)
   - Diameter: 4 ft minimum for single mast
   - Provide drainage at base

3. Install foundation reinforcement
   - Rebar cage per structural drawing
   - Anchor bolts positioned and secured
   - Verify bolt pattern matches mast base

### 7.2 Mast Erection

1. Pour concrete foundation
   - Minimum 4,000 psi compressive strength
   - Cure time: 28 days minimum
   - Verify anchor bolt alignment during pour

2. Erect mast using crane
   - Lift mast into position
   - Align with anchor bolts
   - Install mounting hardware

3. Torque mounting bolts
   - Torque to XXX ft-lb per bolt
   - Use star pattern sequence
   - Apply torque stripe for verification

### 7.3 Aircraft Connection

1. Position aircraft at mooring location
   - Align aircraft CG with mast
   - Maintain minimum 10 ft clearance during approach
   - Set parking brake

2. Install mooring bridle on aircraft
   - Connect to primary tiedown points (ref 10-INST-TD-002)
   - Use approved hardware (ref 10-INST-TD-003)
   - Verify load distribution is symmetric

3. Connect bridle to mast
   - Attach to mast swivel head
   - Adjust tension to remove slack without preload
   - Install safety locking mechanism

4. Verify connection
   - Inspect all connections for proper engagement
   - Check for binding or misalignment
   - Test swivel operation
   - Document configuration

## 8. H2/BWB Considerations

### 8.1 BWB Configuration

- Mast must be positioned to not interfere with wing surfaces during wind oscillations
- Account for BWB lower ground clearance when routing mooring lines
- Multiple mooring points may be required for wide wingspan

### 8.2 H2 Safety

- Maintain minimum 25 ft clearance from H2 venting areas
- Ensure mooring equipment is electrically bonded to aircraft
- No ignition sources within mooring area

## 9. Quality Assurance

- Foundation inspection before mast installation
- Load test mast to 150% of design load before first use
- Annual inspection of structural integrity
- Mooring connection inspection before each use
