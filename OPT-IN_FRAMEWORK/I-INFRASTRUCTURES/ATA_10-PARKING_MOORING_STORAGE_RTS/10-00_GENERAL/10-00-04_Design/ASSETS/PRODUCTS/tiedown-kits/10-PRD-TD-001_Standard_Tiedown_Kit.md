## 2. Product Overview

The Standard Tiedown Kit provides a complete solution for securing the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during normal parking operations in winds up to 65 knots. This kit includes all necessary ropes, chains, anchors, and hardware to secure the aircraft to ground anchoring points.

### 2.1 Key Features
- Wind resistance up to 65 knots (Class 1 storms)
- Quick-release mechanisms for rapid deployment/removal
- Corrosion-resistant materials (stainless steel, galvanized)
- Compatible with standard ground anchor systems
- Includes inspection and maintenance tools

### 2.2 Intended Use
- Routine short-term parking (up to 7 days)
- Indoor and outdoor parking areas
- Moderate weather conditions
- Standard airport apron operations

## 7. Installation/Usage Instructions

### 7.1 Installation Procedure
Refer to: **10-INST-TD-001 - Standard Tiedown Installation Procedure**

Key steps:
1. Verify ground anchor integrity (inspect for cracks, corrosion)
2. Position aircraft at designated tiedown location
3. Attach wing brackets to main spar hardpoints
4. Route ropes from wing points to ground anchors (45° angle minimum)
5. Apply tension to specified torque (120 Nm at tensioner)
6. Install chafe guards at all contact points
7. Install inspection tags with date/time
8. Complete installation checklist

### 7.2 Pre-Flight Removal
1. Inspect all components for damage or wear
2. Remove in reverse order of installation
3. Store in weather-resistant bag
4. Record usage in equipment log

### 7.3 Inspection Requirements
- **Pre-use**: Visual inspection for damage, wear, corrosion
- **Monthly**: Detailed inspection if equipment stored outdoors
- **Annual**: Load testing at certified facility

## 9. Storage & Handling

### 9.1 Storage Conditions
- **Temperature**: -40°C to +70°C
- **Humidity**: <90% RH

- **Protection**: Store in provided weather-resistant bag

### 9.2 Shelf Life

## 11. Maintenance and Inspection

### 11.1 Maintenance Schedule
| Task | Interval | Reference |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Visual inspection | Before each use | 10-INST-TD-002 |
| Detailed inspection | Monthly | 10-INST-TD-002 |
| Load testing | Annual | 10-INST-TD-003 |
| Rope replacement | 10 years or damage | 10-INST-TD-004 |

### 11.2 Inspection Criteria
- **Ropes**: Check for fraying, cuts, UV damage, contamination
- **Hardware**: Check for cracks, deformation, corrosion
- **Buckles**: Check operation, locking function
- **Tensioners**: Check for smooth operation, calibration

### 11.3 Rejection Criteria
- Rope fraying >5% of diameter
- Any visible cracks in metal components
- Corrosion affecting >10% of surface
- Buckle or tensioner malfunction
- Missing or illegible inspection tags
