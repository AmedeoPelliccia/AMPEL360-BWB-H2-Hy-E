## 2. Product Overview

The BWB Tiedown Kit is specifically engineered for the unique geometry of the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Blended Wing Body aircraft. Features custom attachment points, extended reach components, and specialized load distribution to accommodate the non-traditional aircraft shape and wider footprint.

### 2.1 Key Features
- Custom attachment brackets for BWB geometry
- Extended-length tiedown assemblies for wider wingspan
- Unique load distribution patterns
- Non-circular fuselage accommodation
- Specialized center-section tiedown points
- Wind resistance up to 75 knots

### 2.2 BWB-Specific Challenges Addressed
- Non-standard attachment point locations
- Wider aircraft footprint (extended apron space)
- Unique aerodynamic profile wind loading
- Non-circular fuselage cross-section
- Distributed mass loading vs. conventional aircraft
- Extended wingspan tiedown geometry

### 6.3 BWB Tiedown Point Configuration

- **Tail section**: 2 points (control surface attachment)
- **Total**: 10 points minimum, 12 points recommended

## 7. Installation/Usage Instructions

### 7.1 Installation Procedure
Refer to: **10-INST-TD-020 - BWB Tiedown Installation Procedure**

**Critical BWB-Specific Steps:**
1. Use BWB geometry template to mark anchor positions
2. Verify wider apron clearances (40m minimum width)
3. Install center section attachments first (establishes aircraft position)
4. Install wing points working outward from center
5. Maintain symmetric loading on both wings
6. Use load distribution plates on all attachment points
7. Verify clearance from H2 venting zones (minimum 5m)
8. Apply tension in sequence per procedure (avoid asymmetric loading)

### 7.2 Installation Time
- **Setup time**: 2-3 hours (4-person crew)
- **Removal time**: 1.5-2 hours
- **First-time setup**: Add 1 hour for geometry verification

### 7.3 BWB Positioning Considerations
- Aircraft must be centered within apron area
- Nose-to-tail alignment critical for load distribution
- Wind direction consideration for aerodynamic shape
- Main gear positioning relative to center section attachments

## 9. Storage & Handling

### 9.1 Storage Conditions
- Store in large container provided
- Temperature: -40°C to +70°C
- Humidity: <90% RH
- Keep BWB geometry template protected (do not fold)
