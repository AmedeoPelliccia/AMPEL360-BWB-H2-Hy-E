## 2. Product Overview

The Heavy Duty Tiedown Kit provides enhanced security for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during adverse weather conditions including storms with winds up to 100 knots (Class 3 storms). Features reinforced ropes, chain assemblies, and additional anchor points for maximum security.

### 2.1 Key Features
- Storm-rated wind resistance up to 100 knots
- Steel chain primary tiedowns with rope backup
- 8-point tiedown configuration (vs. 6-point standard)
- Heavy-duty ground anchor system
- Emergency quick-release capability

### 2.2 Intended Use
- Storm protection (hurricanes, typhoons)
- Extended outdoor parking (>7 days)
- High-wind exposure areas
- Critical asset protection
- Emergency preparedness

## 6. Application

### 6.1 Aircraft Applicability
- **Primary**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2 (storm protection)
- **Also compatible**: Large aircraft up to 250 tons MTOW

### 6.2 Use Cases
- Pre-storm aircraft securing
- Long-term outdoor parking (>30 days)
- High-wind coastal airports
- Hurricane/typhoon preparation
- Critical asset protection during extreme weather

### 6.3 Tiedown Configuration
- Wing tiedown: 6 points (3 per wing)
- Tail tiedown: 2 points (stabilizer base and tip)
- Fuselage tiedown: 2 additional points (optional)
- Total: 8 primary + 2 optional points

## 7. Installation/Usage Instructions

### 7.1 Installation Procedure
Refer to: **10-INST-TD-005 - Heavy Duty Tiedown Installation**

Key requirements:
- Installation time: 4-6 hours with 4-person crew
- Requires hydraulic tensioning equipment
- Ground anchor penetration depth: minimum 1.5m
- Chain tension: 180 Nm per tensioner
- Backup rope tension: 120 Nm

### 7.2 Storm Preparation Checklist
1. Monitor weather forecasts (activate if winds >65 knots expected)
2. Clear aircraft of loose items
3. Close and secure all doors/panels
4. Install heavy-duty tiedowns per procedure
5. Apply backup rope assemblies
6. Install load monitoring shackles
7. Verify ground anchor integrity
8. Document installation with photos
9. Post storm-warning placards

## 9. Storage & Handling

### 9.1 Storage Conditions
- Store in heavy-duty container provided
- Indoor storage recommended
- Lubricate chains every 6 months
- Temperature: -50°C to +80°C
- Maximum stacking: 3 kits high (weight consideration)

### 9.2 Shelf Life
- Chain components: 20 years with maintenance
- Rope backup: 10 years
- Inspection: Every 6 months if stored outdoors

### 9.3 Handling Precautions
- Requires mechanical handling equipment (heavy weight)
- Wear protective gloves when handling chains
- Check for sharp edges before handling
- Follow lifting procedures for heavy components
