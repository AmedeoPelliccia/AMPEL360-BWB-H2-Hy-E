## 2. Product Overview

Lightweight mooring kit for short-term outdoor parking (up to 7 days) in moderate conditions.

### 2.1 Key Features
- Lightweight and portable
- Quick deployment (<45 minutes)
- Wind resistance up to 55 knots
- Minimal ground preparation required

### 2.2 Intended Use
Short-term outdoor parking, temporary locations, moderate weather
