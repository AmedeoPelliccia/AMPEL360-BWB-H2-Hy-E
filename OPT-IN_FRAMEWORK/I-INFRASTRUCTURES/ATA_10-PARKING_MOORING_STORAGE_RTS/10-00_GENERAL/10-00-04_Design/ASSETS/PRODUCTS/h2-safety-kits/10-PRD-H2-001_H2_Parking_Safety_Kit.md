## 2. Product Overview

**⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY EQUIPMENT - MANDATORY FOR H2 AIRCRAFT OPERATIONS**

The H2 Parking Safety Kit is a comprehensive safety system required for all parking operations involving hydrogen-powered aircraft. Integrates detection, ventilation, signage, and emergency response equipment to ensure safe H2 aircraft parking per SAE AS6968 and NFPA 2 standards.

### 2.1 Key Features
- Multi-sensor H2 leak detection array
- Automated ventilation monitoring
- Safety zone demarcation equipment
- Emergency shutdown capability
- Real-time H2 concentration monitoring

- Integration with airport fire systems

## 6. Application

### 6.1 Aircraft Applicability
- **Required**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2 (mandatory)
- **Required**: All H2-powered aircraft

### 6.2 Use Cases
- **Mandatory scenarios**:
  - All routine parking operations
  - H2 refueling operations
  - Maintenance work near H2 systems
  - Long-term storage with H2 onboard
  - Ground operations with LH2 tanks filled

### 6.3 Safety Zone Configuration
- **Inner Zone** (0-5m): No ignition sources, continuous monitoring
- **Outer Zone** (5-15m): Controlled access, signage
- **Sensor Placement**: 6 sensors around aircraft H2 systems
  - 2 sensors near H2 tank vents
  - 2 sensors at fueling connection points
  - 2 sensors at ground level (H2 rises)

## 7. Installation/Usage Instructions

### 7.1 Setup Procedure
Refer to: **10-INST-H2-001 - H2 Parking Safety System Setup**

**Critical Setup Steps:**
1. **Pre-setup checks** (15 min):
   - Verify sensor calibration (within 30 days)
   - Check battery backup charge
   - Test alarm systems
   
2. **Safety zone establishment** (20 min):
   - Position barrier posts at 5m radius from H2 systems
   - Install warning signage at all access points
   - Mark "NO SMOKING" and "NO IGNITION SOURCES" zones
   
3. **Sensor deployment** (30 min):
   - Position sensors per aircraft-specific diagram
   - Connect sensors to control unit
   - Verify sensor communication (green lights)
   
4. **System activation** (10 min):
   - Power on control unit
   - Run self-test sequence
   - Verify alarm functions
   - Connect to airport fire system (if available)

### 7.2 Operational Monitoring
- **Continuous**: H2 concentration display on control unit
- **Log**: Automatic data logging every 5 minutes
- **Alerts**: 
  - 1% H2 concentration: Warning alarm
  - 2% H2 concentration: Evacuation alarm
  - Sensor fault: Immediate alert
  
### 7.3 Emergency Response
**If H2 detected (>1%):**
1. ACTIVATE emergency alarms
2. EVACUATE personnel from inner zone (5m)
3. NOTIFY fire department immediately
4. DO NOT attempt to approach aircraft
5. SHUT DOWN all electrical equipment
6. AWAIT emergency services

## 11. Safety Notes

⚠️ **CRITICAL SAFETY INFORMATION**

1. **H2 Properties**:
   - Colorless, odorless, tasteless (undetectable without sensors)
   - Lighter than air (rises and disperses)
   - Wide flammability range (4-75% in air)
   - Low ignition energy (0.02 mJ vs. gasoline 0.24 mJ)

2. **Ignition Sources to Eliminate**:
   - Smoking materials
   - Open flames
   - Sparking tools
   - Mobile phones (non-ATEX rated)
   - Static electricity
   - Hot surfaces

3. **Ventilation Requirements**:
   - Outdoor parking preferred
   - Indoor: Minimum 4 air changes per hour
   - Monitor ventilation continuously

4. **Personnel Requirements**:
   - H2 safety training mandatory
   - Minimum 2 persons for H2 operations
   - Emergency contact list posted
