## Usage Instructions

1. **Create a copy** of this checklist for each part requiring approval
2. **Fill in** Part Information section at the top
3. **Work through** each section, checking boxes as requirements are met
4. **For H2 system parts**, ensure Section 2 is thoroughly completed
5. **Obtain signatures** from all required reviewers
6. **Make approval decision** and document in Section 10
7. **File completed checklist** with part documentation
8. **Update** part status in part document after approval
