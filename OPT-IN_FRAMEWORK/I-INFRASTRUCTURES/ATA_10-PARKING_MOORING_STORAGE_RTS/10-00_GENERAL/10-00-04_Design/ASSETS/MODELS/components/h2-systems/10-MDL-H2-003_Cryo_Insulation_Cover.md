# 10-MDL-H2-003 — Cryo Insulation Cover

## 1. Purpose

Removable cryogenic insulation cover for H2 fuel system components during extended parking and storage. Minimizes boil-off losses, maintains safe cryogenic temperatures, and protects against environmental exposure.

## 2. Scope

This component applies to:

- **Aircraft Type**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E
- **System**: H2 fuel tank and distribution system
- **Quantity**: 2 covers per aircraft (main fuel system access points)
- **Temperature Range**: -253°C to +50°C

## 8. Installation and Removal

### Installation Procedure
1. Ensure area is clear and H2 levels are safe
2. Unfold cover from storage bag
3. Position over fuel system access area
4. Align mounting points with aircraft reference marks
5. Secure with Velcro strips (perimeter)
6. Engage snap clips (8 locations)
7. Verify vent paths are unobstructed
8. Install temperature monitoring probes (if equipped)
9. Attach "Remove Before Flight" streamer

### Removal Procedure
1. Check H2 levels are safe
2. Remove "Remove Before Flight" streamer
3. Disconnect temperature monitoring (if equipped)
4. Release snap clips (8 locations)
5. Peel back Velcro strips
6. Carefully remove cover from aircraft
7. Fold and store in bag
8. Inspect for damage before next use

## 9. Maintenance

**Inspection Intervals:**
- Pre-use visual inspection (every use)
- Detailed inspection every 50 uses
- Annual thermal performance test

**Inspection Criteria:**
- No tears or punctures in outer shell
- No delamination of layers
- Fasteners functional
- Labels legible
- No contamination

**Cleaning:**
- Vacuum outer surface to remove debris
- Spot clean with mild detergent if required
- Air dry completely before storage
- Do not machine wash or dry clean
