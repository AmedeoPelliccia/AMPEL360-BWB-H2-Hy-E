# 10-MDL-TD-002 — Tiedown Fitting

## 1. Purpose

Tiedown fitting and cable connector for attaching tiedown cables to aircraft tiedown rings. This fitting provides a secure, adjustable connection between the cable and ring with quick-release capability.

## 2. Scope

This component applies to:

- **Aircraft Type**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E
- **Usage**: All tiedown points (6 per aircraft)
- **Load Capacity**: 80 kN (safety factor 1.6)
