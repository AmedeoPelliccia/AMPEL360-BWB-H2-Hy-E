# 10-MDL-PK-001 — Wheel Chock

## 1. Purpose

Aircraft wheel chock designed for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft landing gear. Prevents aircraft movement during parking, maintenance, and ground operations.

## 2. Scope

This component applies to:

- **Aircraft Type**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E
- **Landing Gear**: Main landing gear wheels
- **Quantity**: 4-6 chocks per aircraft (2-3 per main gear)
