# 10-MDL-MR-003 — Mooring Clamp

## 1. Purpose

Cable clamp and tensioning device for mooring cables. Provides secure attachment, tension adjustment, and load monitoring for mooring system operations.

## 2. Scope

This component applies to:

- **Aircraft Type**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E
- **Usage**: All mooring cable connections (16 per aircraft - 2 per cable)
- **Load Capacity**: 80 kN working load
