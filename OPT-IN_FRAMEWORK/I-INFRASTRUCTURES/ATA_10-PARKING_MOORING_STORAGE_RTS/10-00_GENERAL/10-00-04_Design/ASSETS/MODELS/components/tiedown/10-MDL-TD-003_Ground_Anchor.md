# 10-MDL-TD-003 — Ground Anchor

## 1. Purpose

Ground-mounted anchor point for securing tiedown cables. Provides a permanent, high-strength attachment point in parking areas for aircraft tiedown operations.

## 2. Scope

This component applies to:

- **Aircraft Type**: AMPEL360-BWB-H2-Hy-E (and general use)
- **Installation**: Concrete aprons and parking pads
- **Load Capacity**: 100 kN (safety factor 2.0)
