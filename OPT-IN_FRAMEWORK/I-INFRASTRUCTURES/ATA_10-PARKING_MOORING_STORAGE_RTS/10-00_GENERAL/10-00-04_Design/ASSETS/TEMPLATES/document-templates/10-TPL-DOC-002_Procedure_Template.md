- **Leak Detection**: Ensure H2 detection systems are operational before starting
- **Ventilation**: Verify adequate ventilation to prevent H2 accumulation
- **Ignition Sources**: Eliminate all ignition sources in work area
- **Personnel**: Only H2-qualified personnel shall perform H2-related steps
- **Emergency Procedures**: Review emergency procedures before starting

### 2.3 Cryogenic Safety Precautions (LH2 at -253°C)

**⚠️ CRYOGENIC WARNINGS**

- **Cold Burn Hazard**: LH2 contact causes severe cold burns
- **Personal Protective Equipment**: Full cryo-PPE required (face shield, cryo-gloves, apron)
- **Material Embrittlement**: Common materials become brittle at LH2 temperatures
- **Oxygen Enrichment**: Liquid air condensation can create oxygen-rich atmosphere
- **Pressure Hazard**: Rapid vaporization can cause pressure buildup
- **Frostbite**: Exposed skin contact with cold surfaces causes immediate frostbite

### 2.4 BWB-Specific Safety Considerations
