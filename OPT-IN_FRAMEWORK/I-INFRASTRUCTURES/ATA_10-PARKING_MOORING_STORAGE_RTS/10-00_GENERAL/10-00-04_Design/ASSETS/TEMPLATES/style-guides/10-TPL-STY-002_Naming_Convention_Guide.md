## Purpose

Standardized naming conventions for ATA 10 documents and files.

## Document Numbering

Format: `XX-YY-ZZ-NNN`

- XX = ATA chapter (10)
- YY = Subsection
- ZZ = Lifecycle phase
- NNN = Sequential number

## Template Numbering

Format: `10-TPL-<TYPE>-<nnn>_<Name>`

- TYPE = DOC, DWG, CHK, RPT, H2, BWB, STY
- nnn = 3-digit number
- Name = PascalCase with underscores
