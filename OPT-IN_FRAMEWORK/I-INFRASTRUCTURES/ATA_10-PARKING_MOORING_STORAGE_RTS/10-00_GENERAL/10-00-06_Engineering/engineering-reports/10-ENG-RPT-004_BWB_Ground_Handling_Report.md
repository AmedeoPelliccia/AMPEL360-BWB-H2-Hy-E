## 2. Executive Summary

Engineering report addressing unique ground handling considerations for Blended Wing Body (BWB) configuration applied to AMPEL360-H2 aircraft.

## 3. BWB Configuration Characteristics

### Geometry
- Wingspan: 68m (wide compared to conventional)
- Low profile: Minimal fuselage height
- Integrated wing-body structure
- Non-circular cross-section

### Impacts on Ground Operations
- Wide mooring points required
- Distributed tiedown locations
- Low ground clearance considerations
- Access and egress unique
- Jacking point placement critical

## 4. BWB-Specific Engineering

### Structural Analysis (10-ENG-STR-004)
- Load paths through integrated structure
- Wing-body junction stress analysis
- Ground clearance verification
- 8% structural weight advantage vs. conventional

### Aerodynamic Loading
- Large side area in crosswind
- Reduced frontal area in headwind
- Weathervaning characteristics unique
- Wind load distribution analyzed

### Ground Support Equipment
- Custom GSE required for wide span
- Towing equipment adapted for BWB geometry
- Jacking equipment positioned strategically
- Maintenance platforms for upper surface access

## 5. Design Solutions

### Tiedown System
- 6-point configuration optimized for BWB
- Outboard points address large roll moments
- Distributed load paths through wing structure

### Mooring System
- Single-point mooring with weathervaning capability
- Accommodates wide fuselage
- Low-friction bearing for free rotation

### Ground Locks
- Multiple control surface locks
- Distributed elevon system addressed
- Clear visual indicators for lock status

## 6. Operational Considerations

### Advantages
- Wide upper deck for maintenance access
- Low CG enhances ground stability
- Large internal volume for equipment installation
- Fuel system integration efficient

### Challenges
- Requires specialized GSE
- Wide wingspan clearance critical
- Training for BWB-specific procedures
- Hangar space requirements larger

## 7. Conclusions

BWB configuration presents unique challenges but offers solutions through integrated design approach. Ground handling systems adapted successfully for BWB geometry.

## 8. Recommendations

1. Develop BWB-specific ground handling procedures
2. Procure appropriate GSE for BWB operations
3. Train personnel on BWB characteristics
4. Establish clearance and safety protocols
5. Coordinate with airport authorities on infrastructure requirements
