## 2. Executive Summary

This Preliminary Design Report documents the engineering analysis and design approach for ATA Chapter 10 (Parking, Mooring, Storage & Return to Service) systems for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 3. Scope

Covers preliminary design of:
- Tiedown and mooring systems
- Ground locks and restraints
- H2 safety systems for ground operations
- BWB-specific ground handling considerations

## 4. Design Approach

Design approach integrates:
- Structural analysis for load-bearing systems
- H2 safety analysis for hazard mitigation
- Thermal analysis for cryogenic management
- Risk-based design methodology

## 5. Key Design Features

### Tiedown System
- 6-point configuration
- 50 kN capacity per point
- BWB-optimized attachment locations

### H2 Safety
- 20m safety zones
- ATEX Zone 2 classification
- H2 detection and monitoring
- Venting and boiloff management

### BWB Considerations
- Wide-span mooring requirements
- Low-profile ground clearance
- Distributed structural load paths
