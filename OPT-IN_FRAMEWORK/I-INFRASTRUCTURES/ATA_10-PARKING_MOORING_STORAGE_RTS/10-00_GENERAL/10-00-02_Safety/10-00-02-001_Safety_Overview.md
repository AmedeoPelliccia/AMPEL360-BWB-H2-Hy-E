# 10-00-02-001 — Safety Overview

## 1. Purpose

This document provides an overview of the safety framework for ATA Chapter 10 (Parking, Mooring, Storage, and Return to Service) operations, with specific emphasis on hydrogen-electric aircraft safety considerations.

## 2. Scope

This safety overview encompasses:

- Ground operations safety during parking and mooring
- Storage facility safety requirements
- Return to Service (RTS) safety protocols
- Hydrogen-specific safety considerations
- High voltage system safety
- Cryogenic handling safety
- Fire protection and emergency response

## 3. Safety Philosophy

The AMPEL360 Q100 safety philosophy is built on three fundamental principles:

1. **Prevention First**: Design out hazards wherever possible
2. **Multiple Barriers**: Implement defense-in-depth strategies
3. **Continuous Improvement**: Learn from incidents and near-misses

### 3.1 Hydrogen Safety Principles

Given the unique properties of liquid hydrogen (LH₂):

- **Temperature**: −253°C cryogenic storage
- **Flammability**: Wide flammability range (4–75% in air)
- **Buoyancy**: Rapid dispersion upward if released
- **Invisibility**: Hydrogen flames are nearly invisible in daylight

Special safety measures are required at all times.

### 3.2 High Voltage Safety Principles

The Q100 employs 800V+ electrical systems requiring:

- Qualified personnel with HV certification
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures
- Arc flash protection protocols
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
