## Purpose

This document defines the manufacturing processes and quality requirements for tiedown system components used to secure the AMPEL360 BWB-H2 aircraft during parking and storage operations.

## Scope

### Included Components

- **Tiedown Fittings**: Aircraft attachment points
- **Tiedown Anchors**: Ground anchor points  
- **Tiedown Cables/Straps**: Load-bearing restraints
- **Hardware**: Shackles, turnbuckles, attachment hardware

## Manufacturing Processes

### Material Requirements

- **High-Strength Steel**: AISI 4340, AMS 6415 for fittings
- **Aluminum Alloys**: 7075-T6, 2024-T3 for lightweight components
- **Stainless Steel**: 316L for corrosion resistance
- **Synthetic Straps**: High-tenacity polyester per MIL-DTL-5040

### Fabrication Methods

#### Forged Fittings
- Hot forging from billet stock
- CNC machining to final dimensions
- Heat treatment (quench and temper)
- Surface treatment (cadmium plating or approved alternative)

#### Machined Components
- CNC machining from bar stock
- Precision dimensions per engineering drawings
- Deburring and edge radiusing
- Surface finish: 63 μin Ra typical

### Heat Treatment

Per AMS 2759:
- Quenching: Oil or polymer quench
- Tempering: Per specification for required hardness
- Hardness verification: Rockwell C scale

### Surface Treatment

- **Cadmium Plating**: Per AMS 2400 (Type II, Class 2)
- **Alternative**: Zinc-nickel plating per AMS 2417 (environmentally preferred)
- **Corrosion Protection**: >500 hours salt spray per ASTM B117

## Quality Control

### Inspection Requirements

- **Dimensional Inspection**: 100% critical dimensions, sample for others
- **Visual Inspection**: 100% for surface defects
- **NDT**: Magnetic particle inspection of high-stress areas
- **Load Testing**: Sample destructive testing to 1.5× rated load

### Test Requirements

- **Proof Load Test**: 100% of fittings to 1.25× rated load
- **Destructive Test**: Sample testing to failure (minimum 1.5× rated load)
- **Fatigue Testing**: Representative samples per MIL-STD-810
