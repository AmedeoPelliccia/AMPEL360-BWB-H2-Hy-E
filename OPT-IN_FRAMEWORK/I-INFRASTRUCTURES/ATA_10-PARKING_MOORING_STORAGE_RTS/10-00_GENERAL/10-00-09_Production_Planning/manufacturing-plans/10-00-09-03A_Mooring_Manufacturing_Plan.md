## Purpose

This document defines the manufacturing processes for mooring system components that provide secure attachment points for the BWB-H2 aircraft during extended parking and storm conditions.

## Scope

### Included Components

- **Mooring Fittings**: Integrated aircraft attachment points
- **Mooring Pads**: Ground anchor pads and foundations
- **Connection Hardware**: Quick-release mechanisms, load limiters
- **Monitoring Systems**: Load cells, tension indicators

## Manufacturing Processes

### Structural Mooring Fittings

#### Material Selection
- **Primary Structure**: 7075-T6 aluminum for airframe integration
- **Fittings**: 15-5PH stainless steel for corrosion resistance and strength
- **Fasteners**: A286 CRES per NAS standards

#### Fabrication
- Precision machining from forgings or plate
- Close tolerances for attachment interfaces (±0.005")
- Controlled surface finish for fatigue life

### BWB-Specific Adaptations

The Blended Wing Body configuration requires unique mooring point locations:
- **Wing-Body Blend Area**: Distributed load points
- **Aft Body**: Tail mooring points
- **Forward Body**: Nose mooring interface

### Integration with Aircraft Structure

- FEA validation of load paths
- Static load testing to 1.5× limit load
- Fatigue testing per AC 20-107B

## Quality Control

### Structural Inspection

- **100% Dimensional Inspection**: Critical attachment dimensions
- **NDT Requirements**: 
  - Liquid penetrant for all machined surfaces
  - Ultrasonic for thick sections
  - Eddy current for crack detection

### Load Testing

- Proof load: 1.0× limit load (all production units)
- Ultimate load: 1.5× limit load (sample testing)
- Fatigue: 2× design life (qualification testing)

## Safety Factors

Per FAR 25.561 and CS-25:
- **Limit Load**: Maximum expected in service
- **Ultimate Load**: 1.5× limit load (no failure)
