## Purpose

This document defines the certification strategy specifically for the novel Blended Wing Body (BWB) configuration as it relates to parking, mooring, storage, and return to service operations for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## Scope

This plan addresses certification of:
- BWB-specific ground stability requirements
- Novel configuration parking procedures
- Mooring point distribution and loading
- Ground handling equipment compatibility
- Emergency access during ground operations
- Rescue and firefighting considerations
- Ground crew training for BWB operations

## BWB Configuration Overview

### Key Characteristics Affecting Ground Operations

#### Geometric Characteristics
- Wide, flat fuselage blended with wings
- Low profile compared to conventional aircraft
- Distributed landing gear configuration
- Center of gravity characteristics unique to BWB
- Large planform area affecting wind loading

#### Ground Contact Points
- Main landing gear configuration
- Nose/auxiliary gear configuration
- Ground clearance considerations
- Towing and jacking points

#### Novel Aspects vs. Conventional Aircraft
- Different weight distribution
- Modified center of gravity range
- Novel structural load paths
- Non-traditional access points
- Unique emergency egress locations

## Regulatory Framework

### Applicable Regulations

#### CS-25 / FAR 25 (with BWB-Specific Special Conditions)
Since CS-25/FAR 25 are based on conventional tube-and-wing configuration, special conditions are required for:
- Ground stability requirements
- Mooring point requirements
- Emergency access requirements
- Ground handling procedures

**Reference**: [CS-25](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/certification-specifications/cs-25-large-aeroplanes)

#### Relevant CS-25 / FAR 25 Paragraphs
- **CS 25.1581 / FAR § 25.1581**: Operating Limitations
  - Must address BWB-specific limitations
  - Ground slope limitations
  - Wind limitations for parking
- **CS 25.1583 / FAR § 25.1583**: Operating Limitations
  - BWB-specific operational restrictions
- **CS 25.1585 / FAR § 25.1585**: Operating Procedures
  - Novel procedures for BWB ground operations

## Special Conditions for BWB Configuration

### SC-BWB-01: Ground Stability Requirements
**Requirement**: Aircraft must demonstrate stable parking on various ground conditions accounting for BWB aerodynamic and geometric characteristics.

**Specific Considerations**:
- Wide planform creates larger wind loading area
- Low profile affects wind flow patterns
- Distributed gear affects ground pressure
- Novel CG location affects tipping stability

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Stable on slopes up to X degrees (TBD through analysis)
- No tipping in winds up to Y knots from any direction (TBD)
- Adequate static stability margin: > 15% of wheelbase
- Ground pressure within surface type limitations

**MOC**: 
- Stability analysis (static and dynamic)
- Wind tunnel testing (parked configuration)
- Ground testing (various slopes and wind conditions)
- CFD analysis for wind loading

**Reference**: [10-00-10-32A_BWB_Special_Conditions.md](../special-conditions/10-00-10-32A_BWB_Special_Conditions.md)

### SC-BWB-02: Mooring Point Distribution
**Requirement**: Mooring points must be distributed to safely restrain BWB configuration against aerodynamic and ground loads.

**Specific Considerations**:
- Large planform requires distributed mooring points
- Load distribution differs from conventional aircraft
- Novel structural load paths
- Tie-down point locations accessible to ground crew

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Minimum of X mooring points (TBD through analysis)
- Load capacity per point: Y kN (TBD)
- Balanced load distribution
- Withstand Z knots wind from any direction (TBD)
- Accessible mooring points clearly marked

**MOC**:
- Structural analysis
- Load testing
- Wind load analysis
- Mooring system demonstration

### SC-BWB-03: Emergency Access Requirements
**Requirement**: Emergency egress and rescue access must be maintained during all ground operations.

**Specific Considerations**:
- BWB has different emergency exit locations than conventional aircraft
- Low profile affects rescue equipment access
- Wide fuselage affects emergency vehicle positioning
- Novel configuration requires adapted rescue procedures

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- All emergency exits accessible from ground level or with standard equipment
- Rescue and firefighting access demonstrated
- Emergency lighting visible from ground positions
- Emergency procedures compatible with airport equipment

**MOC**:
- Design review
- Mock-up demonstrations
- Coordination with airport authorities
- Emergency drill validation

### SC-BWB-04: Ground Handling Equipment Compatibility
**Requirement**: BWB configuration must be compatible with ground handling equipment or specify required adaptations.

**Specific Considerations**:
- Towing point locations unique to BWB
- Jacking points distributed differently
- Ground power and service points in novel locations
- Wide aircraft may require special tugs or procedures

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Standard or adapted ground equipment specified
- Towing procedures validated
- Jacking procedures demonstrated
- Service point access validated

**MOC**:
- Equipment compatibility analysis
- Ground handling demonstrations
- Procedure validation

## Certification Activities

### 1. Stability Analysis and Testing

#### Ground Stability Analysis
**Objective**: Demonstrate BWB remains stable during all parking and mooring conditions.

**Activities**:
- Static stability analysis (various CG positions, slopes)
- Dynamic stability analysis (wind loading)
- CFD analysis for aerodynamic forces in parked configuration
- Worst-case loading scenarios

**Deliverables**:
- Stability analysis report
- Wind loading analysis
- Maximum slope determination
- Maximum wind speed determination

#### Ground Testing Program
**Phase 1**: Static Tests
- Slope testing on various angles
- Weight distribution verification
- CG position effects

**Phase 2**: Dynamic Tests
- Wind simulation testing
- Mooring system load testing
- Ground vibration testing

**Phase 3**: Operational Tests
- Full parking and mooring procedures
- Emergency procedures
- Ground crew operations

### 2. Structural Validation

#### Mooring Point Structural Analysis
**Objective**: Verify mooring point structures can safely handle BWB-specific loads.

**Activities**:
- Finite Element Analysis (FEA) of mooring point structures
- Load path analysis in BWB structure
- Fatigue analysis for repeated mooring cycles
- Ultimate load testing

**Deliverables**:
- Structural analysis report
- Load testing results
- Installation specifications
- Inspection procedures

#### Landing Gear Ground Loads
**Objective**: Verify landing gear can support BWB in all ground conditions.

**Activities**:
- Static load analysis
- Dynamic load analysis (taxiing, towing)
- Ground surface compatibility
- Long-term parking effects

**Coordination**: ATA 32 - Landing Gear

### 3. Operational Procedure Validation

#### Parking Procedures
**Procedures to Validate**:
- Approach to parking position (BWB-specific considerations)
- Parking brake application
- Chock placement for BWB gear configuration
- Ground power connection
- Safety zone establishment

**Validation Method**: Ground demonstrations with production aircraft

#### Mooring Procedures
**Procedures to Validate**:
- Mooring point identification
- Tie-down equipment selection
- Load tensioning procedures
- Inspection procedures
- Release procedures

**Validation Method**: Full mooring demonstrations in various wind conditions

#### Storage Procedures
**Procedures to Validate**:
- Long-term storage preparation
- Environmental protection (covers, plugs)
- Preservation procedures
- Periodic inspection procedures
- Return to service procedures

**Validation Method**: Procedure trials, long-duration storage test

### 4. Ground Crew Training and Human Factors

#### Training Requirements
**BWB-Specific Training Topics**:
- BWB configuration recognition and characteristics
- Novel ground handling points
- Mooring procedures for wide configuration
- Emergency access procedures
- Safety considerations unique to BWB

**Training Development**:
- Training manual development
- Visual aids and videos
- Hands-on training program
- Qualification procedures

**Deliverables**:
- Ground crew training manual
- Qualification checklist
- Training materials
- Instructor guide
