## Purpose

This Master Certification Plan defines the overarching strategy, approach, and coordination framework for certifying the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft's parking, mooring, storage, and return-to-service (RTS) operations with EASA and FAA authorities.

## Scope

This plan covers:
- Overall certification strategy for ATA 10 systems and operations
- Coordination with other ATA chapters and certification activities
- Special conditions for hydrogen (H2) and liquid hydrogen (LH2) systems
- Special conditions for Blended Wing Body (BWB) configuration
- Integration with Part 21 Design and Production Organization Approval (DOA/POA)
- Authority engagement and submission planning

## Certification Approach

### 1. Phased Certification Strategy

The certification follows a coordinated phased approach aligned with overall aircraft certification:

#### Phase 1: Conventional Systems Foundation
- Standard parking and mooring systems
- Basic ground handling procedures
- Conventional fuel (if applicable) ground operations
- **Timeline**: Months 1-12
- **Deliverables**: Initial MOC documentation, baseline procedures

#### Phase 2: Hydrogen Systems Integration
- H2 fuel system ground operations
- LH2 handling and storage procedures
- Hydrogen safety systems and protocols
- Cryogenic system special conditions
- **Timeline**: Months 6-24
- **Deliverables**: H2 Special Conditions, MOC for H2 systems, safety assessments

#### Phase 3: BWB Configuration Specific
- Novel configuration ground handling
- BWB-specific mooring and parking requirements
- Emergency access and rescue considerations
- **Timeline**: Months 12-30
- **Deliverables**: BWB Special Conditions, conformity documentation

#### Phase 4: Integrated Operations
- Complete RTS procedures
- Integrated safety demonstrations
- Full operational procedures validation
- **Timeline**: Months 24-36
- **Deliverables**: Complete certification package, authority approval

### 2. Special Conditions Strategy

Given the novel design elements, special conditions are required for:

#### H2/LH2 fuel systems
- Hydrogen leak detection requirements
- H2 venting and defueling procedures
- Safety zones and ground crew protection
- Cryogenic temperature management (-253°C)
- Boiloff management during ground operations
- **Reference**: [10-00-10-30A_H2_Special_Conditions.md](../special-conditions/10-00-10-30A_H2_Special_Conditions.md)

#### BWB configuration
- Novel configuration parking requirements
- Ground stability and support systems
- Emergency egress considerations during ground operations
- Rescue and firefighting access
- **Reference**: [10-00-10-32A_BWB_Special_Conditions.md](../special-conditions/10-00-10-32A_BWB_Special_Conditions.md)

### 3. Methods of Compliance (MOC)

Primary methods for demonstrating compliance:
- **Analysis**: Safety assessments, thermal analysis, structural analysis
- **Testing**: Ground testing, system validation, leak detection testing
- **Simulation**: Validated models for cryogenic systems, emergency scenarios
- **Inspection**: Design conformity, manufacturing conformity
- **Demonstration**: Operational procedures, ground crew training
- **Reference**: [10-00-10-20A_MOC_Summary.md](../means-of-compliance/10-00-10-20A_MOC_Summary.md)

## Key Certification Challenges

### 1. Hydrogen Ground Operations
**Challenge**: No existing certification precedent for LH2 aircraft ground operations
**Approach**:
- Develop special conditions based on NFPA 2 and ISO 13984
- Collaborate with EASA/FAA on hydrogen-specific requirements
- Extensive hazard analysis and safety assessment
- Ground testing program for H2 systems

### 2. BWB Ground Handling
**Challenge**: Limited precedent for BWB configuration ground operations
**Approach**:
- Develop BWB-specific parking and mooring requirements
- Validate ground stability through analysis and testing
- Demonstrate emergency egress and firefighting access
- Ground handling demonstrations

### 3. Cryogenic Systems
**Challenge**: Extreme low temperature (-253°C) system management during ground operations
**Approach**:
- Thermal protection system validation
- Boiloff management procedures
- Ground crew protection procedures
- Temperature monitoring and control systems

### 4. Integration with Novel Aircraft Configuration
**Challenge**: Coordinating ATA 10 certification with novel BWB airframe and H2 propulsion
**Approach**:
- Close coordination with ATA 53 (Fuselage), ATA 28 (Fuel), ATA 71 (Powerplant)
- Integrated safety assessments
- Cross-functional certification working groups
