## Purpose

This matrix tracks compliance with [NFPA 2 (2020) - Hydrogen Technologies Code](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/all-codes-and-standards/list-of-codes-and-standards/detail?code=2) as it applies to aircraft hydrogen systems during ground operations.

## Scope

NFPA 2 Chapter 6.3 specifically addresses aircraft and aerospace applications. This matrix focuses on requirements applicable to parking, mooring, storage, and RTS operations.

- Direction away from ignition sources
- Rate: < 1% per day for normal parking
