# 10-00-12-21A - AOG Support

## 1. Purpose

Define Aircraft on Ground emergency support procedures ensuring minimum downtime for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 operators.

## 2. Scope

- AOG declaration criteria
- Immediate response procedures
- Parts expediting
- On-site technical assistance
- Communication protocols

## 3. Key Areas

### 3.1 AOG Declaration
Aircraft declared AOG when unable to dispatch due to:
- Safety-critical system failure
- H2 system malfunction preventing safe operation
- Structural damage affecting airworthiness
- Regulatory non-compliance

### 3.2 Response Protocol
1. **Immediate**: AOG notification received (phone/email/app)
2. **T+15 min**: Technical specialist assigned
3. **T+30 min**: Initial troubleshooting guidance provided
4. **T+1 hour**: Parts availability confirmed, shipping initiated
5. **T+4 hours**: Field service engineer dispatched (if needed)
6. **Continuous**: Support until aircraft returns to service

### 3.3 AOG Parts
- Critical spares pre-positioned at major hubs
- Expedited shipping (next flight out)
- Loaner/exchange program for expensive components
- H2 components maintain higher inventory levels

### 3.4 AOG Costs
- AOG support: No additional charge (covered by warranty/support contract)
- Parts: Standard pricing
- Shipping: Expedited costs may apply
- Field service: Travel costs per contract terms
