# 10-00-12-22A - Field Service Representative

## 1. Purpose

Define field service representative program providing on-site technical expertise for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 operators.

## 2. Scope

- On-site technical support
- Troubleshooting assistance
- Training and knowledge transfer
- Quality assurance oversight
- Operator liaison

## 3. Key Areas

### 3.1 FSR Services
- On-site troubleshooting and diagnostics
- Maintenance procedure guidance
- Complex repair oversight
- New aircraft acceptance support
- Incident investigation assistance

### 3.2 FSR Availability
- **Resident FSR**: Full-time on-site for launch customers
- **Visit FSR**: Scheduled periodic visits
- **AOG FSR**: Dispatched for AOG situations
- **Remote FSR**: Virtual support via video/phone

### 3.3 FSR Qualifications
- EASA Part 66 license (Category B1/B2)
- AMPEL360-BWB-H2 type certification
- H2 safety specialist certification
- Minimum 5 years aircraft maintenance experience
- Excellent communication skills

### 3.4 Support Activities
- Daily: Operational readiness review
- Weekly: Technical bulletin review with maintenance team
- Monthly: Reliability analysis and trend reporting
- Quarterly: Training refresher for operator personnel
