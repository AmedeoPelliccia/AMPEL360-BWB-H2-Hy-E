# 10-00-12-24A - Cryo Technical Support

## 1. Purpose

Define specialized technical support for cryogenic LH2 systems operating at -253°C.

## 2. Scope

- Cryo system diagnostics
- Vacuum jacket integrity support
- Boil-off troubleshooting
- Thermal protection guidance
- Cold hazard incident response

## 3. Key Areas

### 3.1 Cryo Support Team
- **Cryo Engineers**: LH2 system specialists
- **Vacuum Specialists**: Vacuum jacket experts
- **Thermal Analysts**: Heat leak diagnostics
- **Materials Experts**: Low-temperature materials

### 3.2 Common Cryo Issues
- **Excessive Boil-Off**: Insulation degradation, vacuum loss
- **Vacuum Loss**: Leak in vacuum jacket, outgassing
- **Icing**: External moisture accumulation
- **Valve Freeze-Up**: Cryogenic valve malfunction
- **Thermal Cycling Damage**: Stress from temperature changes

### 3.3 Diagnostic Tools
- Vacuum pressure monitoring
- Thermal imaging analysis
- Boil-off rate trending
- Helium leak detection (for vacuum jacket)
- Acoustic emission testing (for cracks)

### 3.4 Cryo Incident Response
Immediate support for:
- Vacuum jacket breach
- Rapid pressure rise (potential insulation failure)
- Cryogenic spills or leaks
- Personnel cold burn injuries
- Equipment cold damage
