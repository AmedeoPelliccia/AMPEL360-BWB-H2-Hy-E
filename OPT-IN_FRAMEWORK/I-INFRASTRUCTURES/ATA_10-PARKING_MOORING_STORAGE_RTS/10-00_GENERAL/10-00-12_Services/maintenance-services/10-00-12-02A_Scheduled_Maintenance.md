# 10-00-12-02A - Scheduled Maintenance

## 1. Purpose

This document defines scheduled maintenance services for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft based on MSG-3 methodology, establishing preventive maintenance intervals and tasks for safe parking, mooring, and storage operations.

## 2. Scope

- Scheduled maintenance check structure (A, B, C, D checks)
- Task intervals and work scope
- H2/Cryo system scheduled inspections
- BWB-specific scheduled tasks
- Maintenance planning and coordination

## 3. Maintenance Check Structure

### 3.1 A-Check (Light Maintenance)

**Interval**: 500-750 Flight Hours or 60-90 Days (whichever occurs first)

**Duration**: 8-12 hours

**Key Tasks**:
- Visual inspection of aircraft exterior and H2 system
- Operational checks of H2 leak detection system
- Fluid level checks (excluding LH2)
- Landing gear inspection
- Flight control system operational test
- BWB leading edge inspection
- H2 vent system functional check

**Location**: Can be performed at line maintenance facility

### 3.2 B-Check (Intermediate Maintenance)

**Interval**: 4-6 Months or 1,500-2,000 Flight Hours

**Duration**: 2-3 days

**Key Tasks**:
- All A-Check tasks
- H2 sensor calibration verification
- Cryogenic insulation visual inspection
- Landing gear detailed inspection
- APU inspection
- Avionics system checks
- Structural inspection (zonal)
- H2 pressure regulator function test
- Boil-off rate verification

**Location**: Requires hangar facility with H2 safety certification

### 3.3 C-Check (Heavy Maintenance)

**Interval**: 18-24 Months or 6,000-8,000 Flight Hours

**Duration**: 2-3 weeks

**Key Tasks**:
- All B-Check tasks
- Complete H2 system integrity inspection
- Cryogenic vacuum jacket pressure test
- Major structural inspection
- Interior refurbishment
- Component overhaul (per schedule)
- H2 leak detection system calibration
- Thermal protection system inspection
- BWB structural hot spots inspection
- Systems functional tests

**Location**: Requires certified heavy maintenance facility with H2/Cryo capabilities

### 3.4 D-Check (Major Overhaul)

**Interval**: 6-10 Years or 30,000-40,000 Flight Hours

**Duration**: 2-3 months

**Key Tasks**:
- Complete aircraft inspection and refurbishment
- H2 fuel system overhaul
- Cryogenic system recertification
- Major structural modifications
- Systems upgrade and modernization
- Full aircraft repaint
- Interior complete refurbishment
- Life-limited component replacement

**Location**: OEM facility or approved heavy maintenance center

## 6. Maintenance Planning

### 6.1 Scheduling Coordination

- Coordinate with operator flight schedules
- Reserve hangar space for B/C/D checks
- Arrange H2 safety zone clearances
- Pre-order long-lead-time components

### 6.2 Documentation Requirements

- Approved maintenance program reference
- Task cards from Aircraft Maintenance Manual
- Component maintenance manual procedures
- H2 safety work permits
