# 10-00-12-01A - Maintenance Services Overview

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive overview of all maintenance service offerings available for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during parking, mooring, and storage operations. It establishes the framework for both scheduled and unscheduled maintenance services, with specialized focus on hydrogen and cryogenic systems.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- Maintenance service catalog and offerings
- Service delivery methodology and standards
- MSG-3 based maintenance program framework
- H2/LH2 system maintenance requirements
- BWB-specific maintenance considerations
- Service level agreements and response commitments
- Maintenance organization approvals and certifications

## 4. Maintenance Service Categories

### 4.1 Scheduled Maintenance

Preventive maintenance performed at predetermined intervals based on MSG-3 analysis:

- **A-Check**: Light maintenance checks (every 500-750 flight hours)
- **B-Check**: Intermediate checks (every 4-6 months)
- **C-Check**: Heavy maintenance checks (every 18-24 months)
- **D-Check**: Major overhaul (every 6-10 years)

See [10-00-12-02A_Scheduled_Maintenance.md](./10-00-12-02A_Scheduled_Maintenance.md) for details.

### 4.2 Unscheduled Maintenance

Corrective maintenance performed as needed:

- Defect rectification
- Troubleshooting and diagnostics
- Component replacement
- Emergency repairs
- AOG (Aircraft on Ground) support

See [10-00-12-03A_Unscheduled_Maintenance.md](./10-00-12-03A_Unscheduled_Maintenance.md) for details.

### 4.3 H2 System Maintenance

Specialized maintenance for hydrogen fuel systems:

- H2 leak detection system maintenance
- H2 vent valve inspection and testing
- H2 sensor calibration and verification
- Safety system functional checks
- H2 line integrity inspections

See [10-00-12-04A_H2_System_Maintenance.md](./10-00-12-04A_H2_System_Maintenance.md) for details.

### 4.4 Cryogenic System Maintenance

Specialized maintenance for LH2 cryogenic systems:

- Vacuum jacket integrity testing
- Thermal insulation inspection
- Cryogenic valve maintenance
- Boil-off rate verification
- Cold barrier inspection

See [10-00-12-05A_Cryo_System_Maintenance.md](./10-00-12-05A_Cryo_System_Maintenance.md) for details.

## 5. Service Delivery Framework

### 5.1 Maintenance Organization Approval

All maintenance services are performed by organizations approved under:

- **EASA Part 145**: European maintenance organization approval
- **FAA Part 145**: US maintenance organization approval
- **Additional Approvals**: H2 handling certifications, cryogenic system qualifications

### 5.2 Personnel Qualifications

Maintenance personnel must hold appropriate certifications:

| Maintenance Type | Required Certification | Additional Training |
|------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| General Aircraft | EASA Part 66 B1/B2 | BWB familiarization |
| H2 Systems | Part 66 + H2 Certification | NFPA 2 compliance |
| Cryo Systems | Part 66 + Cryo Certification | ISO 13984 training |
| Avionics | EASA Part 66 B2 | H2 safety awareness |

### 5.3 Special Tooling and Equipment

H2/BWB-specific maintenance requires specialized tools:

- **H2 Leak Detection Equipment**: Certified hydrogen sensors
- **Cryogenic Service Tools**: Low-temperature rated tools
- **BWB Access Equipment**: Custom platforms and lifts
- **Vacuum Testing Equipment**: For cryo system integrity
- **Safety Equipment**: H2-rated PPE and ventilation

## 7. MSG-3 Maintenance Program

The AMPEL360-BWB-H2 maintenance program is developed using MSG-3 methodology:

### 7.1 Systems and Powerplant (SSI)

- Structured analysis of aircraft systems
- Task selection based on failure consequences
- H2 fuel system given highest priority (safety-critical)
- Cryogenic systems included in environmental controls

### 7.2 Structures (SI)

- Zonal inspection program
- Corrosion prevention and control
- BWB-specific structural inspection points
- Damage tolerance evaluation

### 7.3 Lightning/High Intensity Radiated Fields (L/HIRF)

- Electromagnetic effects analysis
- H2 system EMI/EMC verification
- Bonding and grounding checks

### 7.4 Program Development

- Initial task intervals based on design analysis
- Escalation/de-escalation based on service experience
- Continuous monitoring and optimization

## 8. H2/Cryo Specific Maintenance Requirements

### 8.1 Safety Protocols

All H2/Cryo maintenance requires:

1. **Pre-Work Safety Brief**: H2 hazards and emergency procedures
2. **System Isolation**: Verify H2 system purged and safe
3. **Monitoring**: Continuous H2 detection during work
4. **Fire Protection**: H2-rated fire suppression available
5. **Environmental Control**: Adequate ventilation for H2 operations

### 8.2 Specialized Inspections

- **Daily**: Visual inspection of H2 system for leaks (during operations)
- **A-Check**: H2 leak detection system functional test
- **B-Check**: H2 sensor calibration verification
- **C-Check**: Full H2 system integrity inspection
- **Annual**: Cryogenic vacuum jacket integrity test

### 8.3 Component Overhaul Intervals

| Component | Overhaul Interval | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| H2 Pressure Regulators | 3,000 FH or 36 months | Earlier if leak detected |
| H2 Vent Valves | 6,000 FH or 60 months | Function test at each C-check |
| Cryo Vacuum Jackets | 10 years | Integrity test annually |
| H2 Leak Sensors | 2,000 FH or 24 months | Calibration at each B-check |
| Cryo Transfer Lines | 15,000 FH or 10 years | Thermal imaging inspection |

## 9. BWB-Specific Maintenance Considerations

### 9.1 Access and Servicing

The BWB configuration presents unique maintenance access challenges:

- **Wide-Body Access**: Extended platforms required for upper surfaces
- **Low Ground Clearance**: Limited under-fuselage access in certain zones
- **Non-Standard Panels**: BWB-specific access panel locations
- **Internal Volume**: Larger internal spaces may require additional lighting

### 9.2 Ground Support Equipment

BWB-specific GSE requirements:

- Custom maintenance stands for BWB geometry
- Extended-reach tools for wide-body coverage
- Specialized towing and jacking equipment
- BWB-compatible hangar facilities

## 10. Quality Assurance

### 10.1 Independent Inspection Requirements

Critical maintenance tasks require independent inspection:

- All H2 system work
- Cryogenic system integrity
- Flight control systems
- Structural repairs
- Life-limited component installation

### 10.2 Documentation and Records

All maintenance must be documented per EASA/FAA requirements:

- Maintenance work orders with task descriptions
- Component removal/installation records
- Inspection findings and corrective actions
- Certification of airworthiness release (EASA Form 1 or equivalent)
- Digital records maintained for aircraft lifetime

## 11. Continuous Improvement

### 11.1 Reliability Program

- Monitor component reliability and failure rates
- Track H2 system incidents and trends
- Analyze maintenance task effectiveness
- Recommend task interval adjustments

### 11.2 Service Bulletin Compliance

- Timely incorporation of manufacturer service bulletins
- Priority handling of H2/safety-related bulletins
