# 10-00-12-52A - Predictive Maintenance

## 1. Purpose
Predictive maintenance analytics to optimize maintenance planning and reduce unscheduled events.

## 2. Predictive Models
### 2.1 Component Life Prediction
- Statistical analysis of component failures
- Weibull analysis for life-limited parts
- Condition-based monitoring
- Trend analysis

### 2.2 H2 System Predictions
- H2 sensor degradation prediction
- Leak detection system performance
- Cryogenic insulation effectiveness
- Boil-off rate trending

## 3. Data Sources
- Fleet-wide maintenance history
- Component removal data
- Operational parameters
- Environmental conditions
- H2 system telemetry

## 4. Recommendations
- Task interval optimization (escalation/de-escalation)
- Component replacement timing
- Scheduled vs. condition-based maintenance
- Spares inventory optimization

## 5. Benefits
- Reduced unscheduled maintenance
- Improved dispatch reliability
- Optimized maintenance costs
- Enhanced safety through early detection
