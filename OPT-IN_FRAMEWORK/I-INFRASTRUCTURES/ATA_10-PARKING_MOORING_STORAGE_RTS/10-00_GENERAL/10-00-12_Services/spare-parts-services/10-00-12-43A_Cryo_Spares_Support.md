# 10-00-12-43A - Cryo Spares Support

## 1. Purpose
Specialized spare parts support for cryogenic LH2 system components.

## 2. Cryo Critical Spares
### 2.1 High Priority
- Cryogenic valves (stock: 2 per type per hub)
- Vacuum jacket seals
- Cryo transfer line flex sections
- Multi-layer insulation (MLI) repair kits

### 2.2 Essential Spares
- Cryo-rated gaskets and seals
- Low-temperature sensors
- Vacuum jacket fittings
- Thermal protection materials

## 3. Special Requirements
All cryo components must:
- Be rated for LH2 service (-253°C)
- Have material certification for cryogenic use
- Be stored in moisture-free environment
- Include handling instructions

## 4. Long-Lead Items
Some cryo components have extended delivery:
- Custom vacuum jackets: 6-12 months
- Large cryo valves: 3-6 months
- MLI blanket assemblies: 2-4 months

Operators should maintain stock or plan ahead.

## 5. Technical Support
Cryo component selection assistance:
- Material compatibility verification
- Temperature rating confirmation
- Sizing and specification support
- Installation guidance
