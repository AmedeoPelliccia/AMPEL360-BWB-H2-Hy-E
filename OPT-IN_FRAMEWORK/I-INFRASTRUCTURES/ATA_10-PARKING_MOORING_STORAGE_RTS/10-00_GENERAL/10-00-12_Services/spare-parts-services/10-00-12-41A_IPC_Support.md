# 10-00-12-41A - IPC Support

## 1. Purpose
Illustrated Parts Catalog (IPC) support for part identification and ordering.

## 2. IPC Access
- Digital IPC platform (web-based)
- Mobile app access
- Searchable by part number, description, location
- Interactive 3D models

## 3. IPC Content
- Part numbers and descriptions
- Illustrations and diagrams
- Effectivity information
- Interchange data
- Supersession information

## 4. Parts Ordering
### 4.1 Standard Process
1. Identify part in IPC
2. Verify part number and effectivity
3. Submit order via portal/phone/email
4. Receive confirmation and ETA

### 4.2 AOG Process
1. Contact AOG hotline
2. Part availability confirmed within 15 minutes
3. Shipping arranged (next flight out)
4. Tracking information provided

## 5. IPC Updates
- Monthly updates for revisions
- Immediate updates for safety-critical changes
- Service bulletins integrated
- Modification effectivity tracked

## 6. Support Services
- Part number verification
- Supersession assistance
- Alternative part identification
- Ordering assistance
