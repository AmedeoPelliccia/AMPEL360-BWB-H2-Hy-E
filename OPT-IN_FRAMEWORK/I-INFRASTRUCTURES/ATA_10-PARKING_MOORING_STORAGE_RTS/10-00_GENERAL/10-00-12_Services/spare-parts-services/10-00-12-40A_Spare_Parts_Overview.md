# 10-00-12-40A - Spare Parts Overview

## 1. Purpose
Comprehensive spare parts support program for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 operators.

## 2. Spare Parts Categories
- Consumables and rotables
- Life-limited components
- H2 system spares
- Cryogenic components
- BWB-specific parts

## 3. Parts Availability
### 3.1 Critical Spares (AOG Impact)
- Stocked at regional hubs
- 24/7 availability
- Next flight out shipping
- Loaner/exchange programs

### 3.2 Essential Spares
- Stocked at main warehouse
- 24-hour availability
- Expedited shipping available

### 3.3 Standard Spares
- Available on demand
- 3-5 day delivery
- Operator inventory recommended

## 4. Inventory Management
- Initial provisioning analysis (IPC-based)
- Fleet reliability data analysis
- Automated reorder systems
- Consignment stock programs

## 5. H2/Cryo Spares
Higher inventory levels maintained for:
- H2 leak sensors
- H2 pressure regulators
- Cryogenic valves
- Vacuum jacket components
