# 10-00-12-42A - H2 Spares Support

## 1. Purpose
Specialized spare parts support for hydrogen fuel system components.

## 2. H2 Critical Spares
### 2.1 High Priority (AOG Impact)
- H2 leak sensors (stock: 10 per hub)
- H2 pressure regulators (stock: 5 per hub)
- H2 vent valves (stock: 3 per hub)
- H2 pressure relief valves (stock: 3 per hub)

### 2.2 Essential Spares
- H2 line fittings and gaskets
- H2 filters
- H2 sensor calibration kits
- H2 detection system components

## 3. Special Handling
All H2 components require:
- H2-clean packaging (no petroleum contamination)
- Certification of H2 compatibility
- Storage in controlled environment
- Shelf-life tracking (some components)

## 4. Certification Requirements
- Material certificates provided with all H2 parts
- Traceability to H2-approved specifications
- Test certificates for pressure components
- Calibration certificates for sensors

## 5. Emergency Availability
- 24/7 AOG support
- Global distribution network
- Next flight out shipping
- Temporary loaner program for expensive items
