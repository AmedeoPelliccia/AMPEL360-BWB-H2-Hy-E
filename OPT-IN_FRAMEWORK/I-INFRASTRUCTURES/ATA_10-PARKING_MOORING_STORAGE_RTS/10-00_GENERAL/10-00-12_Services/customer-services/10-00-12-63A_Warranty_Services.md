# 10-00-12-63A - Warranty Services

## 1. Purpose
Warranty administration for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft and components.

## 2. Warranty Coverage
### 2.1 Aircraft Warranty
- **Duration**: 24 months from delivery or 4,000 flight hours (whichever first)
- **Coverage**: Material and workmanship defects
- **Exclusions**: Normal wear, operator damage, improper maintenance

### 2.2 Component Warranty
- **Duration**: Varies by component (12-60 months typical)
- **Coverage**: Per component manufacturer warranty
- **H2 Components**: Extended warranty available (36 months)

### 2.3 Extended Warranty
- Available for purchase
- Covers up to 10 years or 40,000 FH
- Includes H2/Cryo systems
- Predictive maintenance included

## 3. Warranty Claims
### 3.1 Claim Process
1. Contact warranty hotline
2. Describe defect and impact
3. Technical review (48 hours)
4. Claim approval/denial
5. Corrective action coordination

### 3.2 Required Documentation
- Maintenance records
- Defect description
- Component serial number
- Operational history
- Photos (if applicable)

## 4. Warranty Repairs
- Performed by authorized facilities
- Parts provided at no charge
- Labor covered (if in warranty)
- Loaner components available

## 5. H2 System Warranty
Special provisions for H2 components:
- Enhanced leak monitoring data required
- H2 safety incident investigation
- Root cause analysis for all H2 failures
- Fleet-wide safety bulletins if systemic issue
