# 10-00-12-30A - Training Services Overview

## 1. Purpose
Comprehensive training programs for all AMPEL360-BWB-H2 ground and maintenance personnel.

## 2. Training Categories
- Ground crew operations
- Maintenance personnel
- H2 safety certification
- Cryogenic handling
- BWB ground operations

## 3. Training Delivery
- Classroom instruction
- Hands-on practical training
- Computer-based training (CBT)
- On-the-job training (OJT)
- Recurrent training programs

## 4. Certification Requirements
All personnel working on AMPEL360-BWB-H2 must complete:
- Aircraft type familiarization
- H2 safety awareness (mandatory)
- Role-specific training
- Annual recurrent training

## 5. Training Facilities
- AMPEL360 Training Center (location TBD)
- Mobile training units for operator sites
- Virtual reality (VR) simulators for H2 emergencies
- H2 safety mockup trainer
