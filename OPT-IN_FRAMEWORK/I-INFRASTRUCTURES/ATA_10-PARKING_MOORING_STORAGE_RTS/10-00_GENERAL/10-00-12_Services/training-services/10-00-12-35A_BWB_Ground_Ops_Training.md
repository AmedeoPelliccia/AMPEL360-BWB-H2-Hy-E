# 10-00-12-35A - BWB Ground Ops Training

## 1. Purpose
Specialized training for ground operations personnel on BWB-specific procedures.

## 2. Course Content
### 2.1 BWB Geometry Awareness (1 hour)
- BWB dimensions and clearances
- Wing tip hazard awareness
- Low clearance areas
- Access point locations

### 2.2 BWB Ground Handling (2 hours)
- Parking and marshalling
- Towing and pushback
- Tiedown procedures (7 points vs. 3)
- Equipment positioning

### 2.3 BWB-Specific Equipment (1 hour)
- Custom maintenance platforms
- Specialized tow bars
- BWB jack points
- Access equipment

### 2.4 Safety Considerations (0.5 hours)
- Wing walker positioning
- Clearance verification
- Communication protocols
- Emergency procedures

## 3. Practical Training
- BWB mockup walk-around
- Marshalling practice
- Equipment setup drill
- Clearance measurement

## 4. Certification
- BWB Ground Operations Certificate
- Valid for 24 months
- Recurrent training every 2 years

## 5. Prerequisites
- Basic ground crew training
- Airport security clearance
- No aircraft-specific experience required
