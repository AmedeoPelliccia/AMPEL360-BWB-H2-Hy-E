# 10-00-12-32A - Maintenance Training

## 1. Purpose
Comprehensive maintenance training for EASA Part 66 licensed personnel.

## 2. Course Content
### 2.1 Type Training (80 hours)
- Aircraft systems overview
- BWB structural design
- H2 fuel system (16 hours)
- Cryogenic systems (12 hours)
- Maintenance procedures
- Troubleshooting techniques

### 2.2 Practical Training (40 hours)
- Hands-on maintenance tasks
- H2 system servicing (supervised)
- Special tooling familiarization
- Safety procedures practice

## 3. Prerequisites
- Valid EASA Part 66 B1 or B2 license
- H2 safety pre-training completed
- English language proficiency

## 4. Certification
- AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Type Certificate
- Valid for life (with recurrent training)
- Required for certification authority approval

## 5. Recurrent Training
- Annual: 8 hours (safety and bulletins)
- Every 3 years: 16 hours (refresher)
