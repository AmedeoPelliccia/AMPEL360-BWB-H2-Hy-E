# 10-00-12-33A - H2 Safety Training

## 1. Purpose
Mandatory hydrogen safety training for all personnel working near AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Course Content
### 2.1 H2 Properties and Hazards (2 hours)
- Physical and chemical properties
- Flammability and explosion limits
- Comparison with other fuels
- Environmental behavior

### 2.2 H2 Detection and Response (1 hour)
- H2 detection methods
- Leak response procedures
- Safety zone protocols
- Emergency evacuation

### 2.3 PPE and Safety Equipment (0.5 hours)
- Personal protective equipment
- H2 detection instruments
- Fire suppression equipment
- Communication devices

### 2.4 Emergency Procedures (0.5 hours)
- H2 leak scenarios
- Fire response
- First aid for H2 exposure
- Incident reporting

## 3. Practical Exercises
- H2 detector operation
- Emergency response drill
- PPE donning/doffing
- Communication procedures

## 4. Certification
- H2 Safety Certificate (valid 12 months)
- Mandatory for access to H2 safety zones
- Annual recurrent training required

## 5. Regulatory Basis
- NFPA 2 - Hydrogen Technologies Code
- SAE AS6968 - H2 Aircraft GSE
- ISO/TS 19880 - H2 Fueling Stations
