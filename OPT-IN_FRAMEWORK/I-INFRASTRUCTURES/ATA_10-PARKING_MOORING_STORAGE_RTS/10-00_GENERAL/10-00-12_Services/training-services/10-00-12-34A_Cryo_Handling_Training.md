# 10-00-12-34A - Cryo Handling Training

## 1. Purpose
Specialized training for personnel handling cryogenic LH2 systems at -253°C.

## 2. Course Content
### 2.1 Cryogenic Hazards (2 hours)
- Ultra-low temperature effects (-253°C)
- Cold burns and frostbite
- Material embrittlement
- Oxygen displacement hazard
- Pressure hazards from expansion

### 2.2 Cryo Equipment and PPE (1 hour)
- Cryo-rated gloves and clothing
- Face shields and eye protection
- Respiratory protection
- Specialized tools and equipment

### 2.3 LH2 Handling Procedures (2 hours)
- Transfer operations
- Vent system operations
- Vacuum jacket maintenance
- Leak detection and response
- Spill containment

### 2.4 Cold Burn First Aid (0.5 hours)
- Recognition of cold injuries
- Immediate treatment (gradual warming)
- When to seek medical attention
- Do's and Don'ts

## 3. Practical Training
- Cryo PPE practice
- Simulated LH2 transfer (with LN2)
- Emergency spill response drill
- Cold burn first aid practice

## 4. Certification
- Cryogenic Handler Certificate (valid 24 months)
- Required for LH2 system work
- Bi-annual recurrent training

## 5. Prerequisites
- H2 Safety Training completed
- Part 66 license (for maintenance personnel)
- Medical fitness certification
