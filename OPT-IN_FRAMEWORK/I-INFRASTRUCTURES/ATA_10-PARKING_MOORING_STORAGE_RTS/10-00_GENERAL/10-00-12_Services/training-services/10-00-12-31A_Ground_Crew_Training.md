# 10-00-12-31A - Ground Crew Training

## 1. Purpose
Training program for ground crew personnel operating AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Course Content
- Aircraft familiarization (BWB geometry)
- H2 safety awareness (4 hours)
- Parking and marshalling procedures
- Ground servicing operations
- Emergency procedures
- Communication protocols

## 3. Duration
- Initial training: 16 hours (2 days)
- Practical assessment: 4 hours
- Recurrent training: 4 hours annually

## 4. Certification
Upon completion, personnel receive:
- AMPEL360-BWB-H2 Ground Crew Certificate
- Valid for 12 months
- Renewal requires recurrent training

## 5. Prerequisites
- Airport security clearance
- Medical fitness
- No specific aircraft experience required
