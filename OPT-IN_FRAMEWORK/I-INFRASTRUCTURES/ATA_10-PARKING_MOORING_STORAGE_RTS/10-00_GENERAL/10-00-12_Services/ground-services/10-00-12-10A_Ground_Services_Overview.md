# 10-00-12-10A - Ground Services Overview

## 1. Purpose

This document provides an overview of all ground service operations for the AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during parking, mooring, and storage, with specialized procedures for hydrogen systems and BWB configuration.

## 2. Scope

- Parking operations and procedures
- Mooring and tiedown services
- Storage management (short-term and long-term)
- H2-specific ground operations
- BWB-specific ground handling
- Ground support equipment (GSE) requirements

## 3. Ground Service Categories

### 3.1 Parking Services
Standard and extended parking operations with H2 safety zone management.
See [10-00-12-11A_Parking_Services.md](./10-00-12-11A_Parking_Services.md)

### 3.2 Mooring Services
Aircraft tiedown and securing for extended parking periods.
See [10-00-12-12A_Mooring_Services.md](./10-00-12-12A_Mooring_Services.md)

### 3.3 Storage Services
Long-term and short-term storage with preservation requirements.
See [10-00-12-13A_Storage_Services.md](./10-00-12-13A_Storage_Services.md)

### 3.4 H2 Ground Services
Hydrogen-specific ground operations including safety monitoring and venting.
See [10-00-12-14A_H2_Ground_Services.md](./10-00-12-14A_H2_Ground_Services.md)

### 3.5 BWB Ground Services
Blended Wing Body specific ground handling procedures and equipment.
See [10-00-12-15A_BWB_Ground_Services.md](./10-00-12-15A_BWB_Ground_Services.md)

## 4. Ground Support Equipment

### 4.1 Standard GSE
- Tow bars and tractors (BWB-compatible)
- Ground power units
- Air conditioning units
- Hydraulic test equipment
- Wheel chocks and cones

### 4.2 H2-Specific GSE
- H2 detection and monitoring equipment
- H2-rated fire suppression equipment
- Ventilation and dispersal fans
- Emergency response equipment
- H2 safety zone barriers and signage

### 4.3 BWB-Specific GSE
- Custom maintenance platforms for wide-body
- Extended-reach service equipment
- Specialized towing equipment
- BWB geometry-compatible jacking points

## 5. Safety Requirements

### 5.1 H2 Safety Zones
- Minimum 15m radius around H2 venting points
- No ignition sources within H2 safety zone
- Continuous H2 monitoring during ground operations
- Personnel training on H2 hazards

### 5.2 BWB Clearances
- Wider parking space requirements (TBD m wingspan)
- Lower ground clearance areas identified
- Wing tip and tail clearance management
- Ground crew awareness of BWB geometry

## 6. Personnel Qualifications

All ground personnel must complete:
- Aircraft type familiarization (AMPEL360-BWB-H2)
- H2 safety awareness training (4 hours minimum)
- BWB ground operations training (2 hours minimum)
- Emergency response procedures

See [10-00-12-31A - Ground Crew Training](../training-services/10-00-12-31A_Ground_Crew_Training.md)

## 7. Service Level Agreements

- **Standard Service**: Within 30 minutes of aircraft arrival
- **Premium Service**: Pre-positioned equipment, immediate service
- **24/7 Service**: Available at major hubs
