# 10-00-12-11A - Parking Services

## 1. Purpose

Define parking procedures for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft with H2 safety protocols and BWB geometry considerations.

## 2. Scope

- Standard parking procedures
- H2 safety zone establishment
- BWB-specific parking requirements
- Parking position assignment
- Ground power and servicing connections

## 3. Parking Procedures

### 3.1 Pre-Parking Checklist

- Verify parking position suitable for BWB dimensions
- Establish H2 safety zone markers
- Position H2 detection equipment
- Brief ground crew on H2 hazards
- Verify ground power unit H2-rated (if applicable)

### 3.2 Aircraft Positioning

- Use BWB-specific marshalling signals
- Verify wing tip clearances
- Check for overhead obstructions
- Position wheel chocks
- Connect ground power

### 3.3 H2 System Monitoring

- Activate continuous H2 monitoring
- Verify H2 vent system operational
- Monitor boil-off rate
- Log H2 system status

## 4. H2 Safety Zones

- **Inner Zone (0-5m)**: No personnel without H2 certification, continuous monitoring
- **Middle Zone (5-15m)**: Controlled access, no ignition sources
- **Outer Zone (15-25m)**: Normal operations, awareness required

## 5. BWB Parking Requirements

- Parking space: TBD m x TBD m (wider than conventional aircraft)
- Wing tip clearance: Minimum 3m each side
- Nose-to-tail clearance: Minimum 10m
- Access lanes: Minimum 4m width
