# 10-00-12-14A - H2 Ground Services

## 1. Purpose

Define hydrogen-specific ground operations for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during parking, mooring, and storage.

## 2. Scope

- H2 safety zone management
- H2 venting during parking
- H2 monitoring during storage
- Emergency response procedures
- Coordination with airport H2 infrastructure

## 3. H2 Safety Zone Management

### 3.1 Zone Definition

- Inner Zone (0-5m): H2-certified personnel only, continuous monitoring
- Middle Zone (5-15m): Controlled access, no ignition sources
- Outer Zone (15-25m): Public awareness, signage required

### 3.2 Monitoring Requirements

- Minimum 2 H2 detectors per safety zone
- Alarm at 25% LEL (1% H2 in air)
- Visual and audible alarms
- Continuous operation during aircraft presence

## 4. H2 Venting Operations

### 4.1 Normal Venting

- LH2 boil-off vented through designated vent stack
- Vent directed upward, away from aircraft
- Continuous H2 monitoring at vent outlet
- Weather conditions monitored (wind direction)

### 4.2 Emergency Venting

- Rapid depressurization if required
- Evacuate H2 safety zones
- Fire response team on standby
- Coordinate with airport fire services

## 5. H2 Ground Equipment

- H2 leak detectors (portable and fixed)
- H2-rated fire extinguishers
- Ventilation fans
- Safety barriers and signage
- Emergency communication equipment

## 6. Personnel Requirements

All personnel in H2 safety zones must have:
- H2 safety training (current within 12 months)
- Understanding of H2 properties and hazards
- Emergency response training
- Appropriate PPE

See [10-00-12-33A - H2 Safety Training](../training-services/10-00-12-33A_H2_Safety_Training.md)

## 7. Emergency Procedures

### 7.1 H2 Leak Response

1. Activate H2 leak alarm
2. Evacuate safety zones
3. Alert fire services
4. Isolate H2 source if safe
5. Ventilate area
6. Do not re-enter until <1% H2 confirmed

### 7.2 Fire Response

- H2 fires: Let burn in controlled manner if safe
- Use water fog to cool surroundings
- Do not extinguish unless fuel source can be shut off
- Evacuate minimum 50m radius

## 8. Coordination with Airport

- Airport H2 infrastructure compatibility
- Emergency response coordination
- Communication protocols
- H2 incident reporting
