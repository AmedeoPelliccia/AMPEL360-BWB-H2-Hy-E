# 10-00-12-12A - Mooring Services

## 1. Purpose

Define mooring and tiedown procedures for securing AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft during extended parking and storage.

## 2. Scope

- Tiedown procedures and equipment
- Wind load calculations for BWB
- H2 system considerations during mooring
- Emergency unmooring procedures

## 3. Tiedown Requirements

### 3.1 BWB Tiedown Points

- Forward tiedown: Main landing gear
- Mid-body tiedowns: Wing hard points (4 positions)
- Aft tiedown: Tail structure
- Total: 7 tiedown points (vs. 3 for conventional aircraft)

### 3.2 Tiedown Strength

- Minimum breaking strength: 1,500 kg per tiedown
- Wind resistance: Up to 65 knots (120 km/h)
- Load distribution: Balanced across all tiedown points

## 4. H2 System Considerations

- Verify H2 tank pressure within normal range
- Ensure vent system unobstructed
- Position H2 monitors if long-term mooring
- Weather protection for H2 system components

## 5. Mooring Equipment

- Tiedown ropes/chains (7 required)
- Tiedown anchors (ground or apron mounted)
- Tension indicators
- Protective sleeves for BWB surfaces
