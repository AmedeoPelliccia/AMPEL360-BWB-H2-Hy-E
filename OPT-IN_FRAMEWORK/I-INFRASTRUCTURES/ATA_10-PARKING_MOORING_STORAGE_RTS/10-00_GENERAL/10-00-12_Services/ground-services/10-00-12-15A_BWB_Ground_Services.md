# 10-00-12-15A - BWB Ground Services

## 1. Purpose

Define Blended Wing Body specific ground handling procedures and equipment requirements for AMPEL360-BWB-H2 aircraft.

## 2. Scope

- BWB-specific ground handling procedures
- Special GSE requirements
- Clearance management
- Towing and pushback procedures
- Access and servicing considerations

## 3. BWB Geometry Considerations

### 3.1 Dimensions

- Wingspan: TBD m (significantly wider than conventional)
- Length: TBD m
- Height: TBD m (lower profile)
- Ground clearance: TBD m (varies by location)

### 3.2 Critical Clearance Points

- Wing tips: Require enhanced wing walkers
- Leading edge: Low clearance areas
- Trailing edge: Control surface clearances
- Under-body: Limited ground clearance in center section

## 4. Special GSE Requirements

### 4.1 Maintenance Platforms

- Custom-designed for BWB wide-body access
- Multiple height adjustments
- Span entire wing width if needed
- Non-metallic contact surfaces

### 4.2 Towing Equipment

- BWB-compatible tow bars (wider attachment points)
- Reinforced towing tractors
- Center of gravity considerations
- Turning radius limitations

### 4.3 Jacking Equipment

- BWB-specific jack points (per AMM)
- Increased number of jacking points vs. conventional
- Load distribution requirements
- Specialized jack adapters

## 5. Towing and Pushback

### 5.1 Procedures

- Enhanced visual contact (wider span)
- Additional wing walkers required (minimum 4)
- Slower speeds due to wider turning radius
- Ground crew positioning for visibility

### 5.2 Restrictions

- Maximum turn angle: TBD degrees
- Minimum turning radius: TBD m
- Maximum towing speed: 5 km/h
- Dedicated towing lanes may be required

## 6. Servicing Access

### 6.1 Service Panel Locations

- Non-conventional locations due to BWB design
- Some panels on upper surface (require platforms)
- Enhanced markings for ground crew identification
- Access equipment pre-positioning recommended

### 6.2 Fuel Access

- LH2 fuel connection: [Location TBD]
- Requires H2-rated coupling and personnel
- Extended reach may be required
- H2 safety zone around fuel connection

## 7. Hangar Requirements

- Wider door clearance
- Higher hangar utilization (less aircraft per space)
- Specialized hangar markings for BWB
- Wing tip protection systems

## 8. Ground Crew Training

All BWB ground operations personnel must complete:
- BWB geometry familiarization
- Special handling procedures
- Equipment positioning training
- Safety awareness for low-clearance areas
