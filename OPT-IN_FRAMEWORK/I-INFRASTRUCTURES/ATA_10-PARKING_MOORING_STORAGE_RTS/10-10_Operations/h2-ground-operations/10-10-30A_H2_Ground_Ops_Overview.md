---

## 5. H2 Safety Philosophy

All H2 ground operations prioritize safety through: comprehensive training, continuous monitoring, strict safety zones, emergency preparedness. H2 Safety Officer must be present for all H2 operations.

---

## 6. Operations Scope

H2 venting, monitoring, safety zone establishment, detector checks, system safing. All procedures follow SAE AS6968 and NFPA 2 standards.
