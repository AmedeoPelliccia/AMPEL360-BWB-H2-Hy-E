---

## 5. Safety Zone Dimensions

Inner zone: 7.5 m (no ignition sources)
Outer zone: 15 m (no open flames/smoking)
Venting zone: 25 m (active venting only)

---

## 6. Zone Setup

1. Calculate zone dimensions based on operation
2. Place physical barriers (cones, ropes)
3. Install signage (HYDROGEN OPERATIONS - NO SMOKING)
4. Position H2 detectors
5. Enforce access control

---

## 7. Access Control

Only H2-qualified personnel within inner zone. Fire extinguishers positioned at zone boundary. Emergency vehicle access maintained.
