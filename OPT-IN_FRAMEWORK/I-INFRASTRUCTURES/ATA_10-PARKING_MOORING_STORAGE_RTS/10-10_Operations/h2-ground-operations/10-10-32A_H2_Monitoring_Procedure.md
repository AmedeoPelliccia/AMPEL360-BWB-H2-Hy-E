---

## 5. Detector Placement

Minimum 2 portable detectors: upwind and downwind of aircraft at 3 m and 6 m distance, 2 m height (H2 rises). Fixed detectors if available at parking position.

---

## 6. Monitoring Thresholds

<25 ppm: Normal, continue monitoring
25-100 ppm: Investigate, increase monitoring
100-1000 ppm: Evacuate, emergency response
>1000 ppm: Major evacuation, fire/rescue alert

---

## 7. Documentation

Log all H2 concentration readings. Record detector calibration dates. Document any exceedances.
