## 5. Detector Requirements

Portable detectors: 0-1000 ppm range, 4-20 mA output, intrinsically safe. Calibration required every 30 days or per manufacturer. Functional test before each use.

---

## 6. Functional Test Procedure

1. Verify battery charge >50%
2. Power on detector
3. Confirm zero reading in clean air
4. Test alarm functions
5. Verify 4-20 mA output if applicable
6. Document test results

---

## 7. Calibration

Calibrate using certified H2 test gas (typically 50% LEL = 20,000 ppm). Follow manufacturer procedure. Document calibration date and technician.
