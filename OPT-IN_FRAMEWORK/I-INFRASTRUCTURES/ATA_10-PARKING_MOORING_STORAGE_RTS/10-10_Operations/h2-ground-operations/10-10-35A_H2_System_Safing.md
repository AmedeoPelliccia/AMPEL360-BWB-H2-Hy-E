## 5. Safing Requirements

H2 system safing required for: maintenance entry, long-term parking >24 hrs, storage, abnormal conditions. Performed by qualified H2 technician with H2 Safety Officer present.

---

## 6. Safing Procedure

1. Reduce tank pressure to minimum safe level
2. Close all H2 isolation valves
3. Verify valve positions
4. Vent residual pressure safely
5. Confirm H2 concentration <25 ppm
6. Post safety placards
7. Document system configuration

---

## 7. System Configuration

Document all valve positions, tank pressure, H2 concentration readings, and safing completion. Retain records for 2 years.
