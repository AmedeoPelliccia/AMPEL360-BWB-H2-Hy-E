## 5. Venting Requirements

H2 venting required for: pressure relief, tank defueling, system purging. Venting must disperse upward and away from personnel/ignition sources.

---

## 6. Procedure

1. Establish 25 m venting safety zone
2. Verify wind conditions (<15 kts)
3. Position H2 detectors
4. Open vent valves as required
5. Monitor H2 concentration continuously
6. Document venting operation

---

## 7. Completion Criteria

H2 concentration <25 ppm at ground level, vent flow ceased, valve closed and verified, safety zone maintained until all-clear.
