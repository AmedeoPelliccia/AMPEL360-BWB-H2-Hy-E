## 1. Purpose

Inspection procedures for cryogenic insulation systems.

---

## 2. Scope

- Visual inspection
- Vacuum pressure checks
- Thermal performance

---

## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Inspection

Check for frost/condensation on external surfaces (indicates insulation failure). Verify vacuum insulation pressure <0.01 mbar. No visible damage to insulation panels.

---

## 5. Frequency

Pre-flight: visual check. Weekly: detailed inspection. Monthly: vacuum pressure verification if equipped with monitoring.
