## 1. Purpose

Controlled warmup procedures for cryogenic systems.

---

## 2. Scope

- Warmup rate limits
- Thermal stress management
- Monitoring

---

## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Procedure

Maximum warmup rate: 5 K/hour to prevent thermal stress. Allow natural warmup when possible. Monitor temperature gradient across tank. Vent increased boiloff during warmup.

---

## 5. Completion

Tank at ambient temperature (typically 12-24 hours for complete warmup). Verify all cryogenic systems stable before maintenance entry.
