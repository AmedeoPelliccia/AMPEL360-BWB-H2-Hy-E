## 1. Purpose

Procedures for monitoring and managing LH2 boiloff during ground operations.

---

## 2. Scope

- Boiloff rate monitoring
- Pressure management
- Venting control

---

## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Monitoring

Normal boiloff: <2% per day. Monitor tank pressure every 15 min during operations, hourly when parked. Document all readings.

---

## 5. Control

Automatic venting maintains tank pressure 3-6 bar. Manual intervention if pressure exceeds 6 bar or boiloff rate excessive.
