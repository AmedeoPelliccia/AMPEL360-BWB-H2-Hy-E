## 4. Pre-Operations

□ H2 Safety Officer present
□ H2 detectors calibrated (within 30 days)
□ H2 detectors functional tested
□ Safety zone established (15 m minimum)
□ Fire suppression equipment positioned
□ Personnel trained (H2 Level 2+)
□ Weather acceptable (<15 kts for venting)
□ Emergency procedures reviewed

---

## 5. During Operations

□ H2 concentration <25 ppm
□ Tank pressure 3-6 bar
□ Boiloff rate normal (<2%/day)
□ No leaks detected
□ Continuous monitoring active
□ Safety zone enforced
