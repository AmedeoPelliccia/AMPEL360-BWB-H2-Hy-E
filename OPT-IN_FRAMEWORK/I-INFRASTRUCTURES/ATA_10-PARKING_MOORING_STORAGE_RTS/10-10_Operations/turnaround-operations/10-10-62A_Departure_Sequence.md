## 1. Purpose

Pre-departure preparation and aircraft release procedures.

---

## 2. Scope

- Systems verification
- H2 system status
- Release procedures

---

## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Sequence

1. Pre-departure inspection
2. H2 system verification
3. Passenger/cargo loading
4. Final walk-around
5. Remove ground equipment
6. Remove chocks
7. Release aircraft for pushback/taxi

---

## 5. H2 Departure

Verify H2 system ready: adequate fuel quantity, normal pressure, no leaks detected, all H2 safety systems operational.
