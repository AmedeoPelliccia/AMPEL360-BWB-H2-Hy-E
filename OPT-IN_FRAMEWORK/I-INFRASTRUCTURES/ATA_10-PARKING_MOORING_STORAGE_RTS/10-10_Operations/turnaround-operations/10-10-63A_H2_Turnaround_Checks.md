## 1. Purpose

H2 system-specific checks during turnaround operations.

---

## 2. Scope

- H2 status verification
- Leak checks
- Detector verification
- Documentation

---

## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Checks

1. Verify H2 detector operational
2. Check H2 concentration <25 ppm
3. Inspect external tank condition
4. Monitor tank pressure and temperature
5. Check for any H2 system faults
6. Document H2 system status

---

## 5. Criteria

H2 system acceptable for next flight if: no leaks detected, pressure 3-6 bar, boiloff rate normal, all safety systems operational, adequate fuel for next sector.
