## 4. Sequence

1. Gate/parking position coordination
2. Aircraft marshalling and parking
3. Chock and connect ground power
4. H2 system status check
5. Passenger/cargo off-loading
6. Post-flight inspection
7. Refueling if required

---

## 5. H2 Arrival

Monitor H2 system status upon arrival. Check boiloff rate, detector readings, tank pressure. Document system condition.
