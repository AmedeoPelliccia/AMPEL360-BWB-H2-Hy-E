## 1. Purpose

This document establishes specialized parking procedures for AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft with hydrogen fuel systems, ensuring safe operations through proper H2 system monitoring, safety zone establishment, and risk mitigation.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Pre-parking H2 system status verification
- H2 safety zone establishment and enforcement
- H2 detection and monitoring requirements
- Venting considerations and procedures
- Post-parking H2 system safing
- Emergency response procedures for H2 incidents

### 2.1 Applicability
- All AMPEL360 aircraft with LH2 fuel systems
- Aircraft with active H2 systems or recent H2 operations
- All parking durations and operational scenarios

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS**

1. **HYDROGEN FIRE HAZARD**
   - Hydrogen burns with invisible flame
   - Thermal imaging required for H2 fire detection
   - Wide flammability range (4-75% in air)
   - Low ignition energy (0.017 mJ)

2. **ASPHYXIATION HAZARD**
   - Hydrogen displaces oxygen in confined spaces
   - Odorless and colorless gas
   - Ventilation critical for safety

3. **CRYOGENIC HAZARD**
   - LH2 temperature: -253°C (-423°F)
   - Severe frostbite on contact
   - Material embrittlement
   - Rapid expansion on vaporization

4. **EXPLOSION HAZARD**
   - Explosive mixture in confined spaces
   - Static electricity ignition risk
   - No smoking or open flames within 15 m

⚠️ **MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS**

- H2 Safety Officer present during all H2 parking operations
- Operational H2 detectors verified before approaching aircraft
- H2 safety zone established before parking
- Personnel must complete H2 safety training Level 3 minimum
- Fire suppression equipment positioned and ready

## 6. Pre-Parking H2 System Assessment

### 6.1 System Status Verification

**Before approaching aircraft, H2 Safety Officer must verify:**

1. **H2 Fuel System Status**
   - [ ] LH2 tank quantity: ______ kg
   - [ ] LH2 tank pressure: ______ bar (normal: 3-6 bar)
   - [ ] Boiloff rate: ______ kg/hr (normal: <2% per day)
   - [ ] Last refueling: ______ hours ago
   - [ ] System temperature: ______ K

2. **Venting Status**
   - [ ] Automatic vent valve status: Open / Closed / Auto
   - [ ] Manual vent valve status: Closed (verify)
   - [ ] Vent discharge direction: Confirmed upward
   - [ ] Vent discharge clear of obstructions

3. **Leak Detection System**
   - [ ] H2 leak detection system: Operational / Fault
   - [ ] Last system self-test: ______ (max 24 hr ago)
   - [ ] Current H2 concentration: ______ ppm (must be <25 ppm)
   - [ ] No active leak warnings

4. **Cryogenic System Integrity**
   - [ ] Vacuum insulation status: Good / Degraded
   - [ ] Insulation pressure: ______ mbar (normal: <0.01 mbar)
   - [ ] External surface temperature: Normal (no frost/condensation)
   - [ ] No visible damage to insulation system

**If any parameter is out of limits, STOP and consult maintenance before parking.**

## 7. H2 Safety Zone Establishment

### 7.1 Safety Zone Dimensions

**Minimum H2 Safety Zones (per NFPA 2 and SAE AS6968):**

| Zone Type | Radius | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Inner Safety Zone | 7.5 m (25 ft) | No ignition sources, restricted access |
| Outer Safety Zone | 15 m (50 ft) | No open flames, smoking, or hot work |
| Venting Safety Zone | 25 m (82 ft) | Active venting operations only |

### 7.2 Safety Zone Setup Procedure

1. **Position Portable H2 Detectors**
   - Place 2 detectors at 3 m and 6 m from aircraft centerline
   - Height: 2 m above ground (H2 rises)
   - Detector 1: Upwind of aircraft
   - Detector 2: Downwind of aircraft

2. **Establish Physical Barriers**
   - Place safety cones at 15 m radius
   - Install "HYDROGEN OPERATIONS — NO SMOKING" signs
   - Rope/chain barriers at access points

3. **Verify H2 Detector Operation**
   - [ ] Detector 1 reading: ______ ppm (must be <25 ppm)
   - [ ] Detector 2 reading: ______ ppm (must be <25 ppm)
   - [ ] Detector alarms tested and functional
   - [ ] Detector batteries >50% capacity

4. **Position Fire Suppression Equipment**
   - Fire extinguishers at 10 m and 15 m
   - Fire watch personnel positioned (if venting)
   - Emergency vehicle access confirmed

5. **Enforce Access Control**
   - Only qualified H2 personnel within inner zone
   - Safety zone boundaries clearly marked
   - Security/ramp personnel briefed on restrictions

## 8. H2 Aircraft Parking Procedure

### 8.1 Pre-Parking Coordination

**H2 Safety Officer Briefing:**
- Confirm H2 system status with flight crew
- Establish safety zone boundaries
- Verify H2 detector operation
- Brief parking personnel on H2 hazards
- Confirm wind direction and speed
- Review emergency procedures

### 8.2 Parking Sequence

**Step 1: Initial Positioning**
1. Aircraft taxied or towed to designated parking position
2. H2 Safety Officer monitors H2 concentration (continuous)
3. Wing walkers maintain clearance awareness
4. Parking coordinator directs final positioning

**Step 2: Aircraft Shutdown Coordination**
5. Confirm aircraft parking brake set
6. Monitor H2 system during shutdown sequence
7. Observe for any H2 venting or boiloff increase
8. Monitor H2 detector readings (must remain <25 ppm)

**Step 3: Wheel Chocking**
9. Install wheel chocks per [10-10-04A](./10-10-04A_Wheel_Chock_Procedure.md)
10. Verify chocks properly seated (6-8 chocks for BWB)
11. H2 Safety Officer maintains H2 monitoring

**Step 4: Post-Parking H2 Assessment**
12. Wait 5 minutes after shutdown
13. Verify H2 concentration <25 ppm (all detectors)
14. Check for unusual boiloff or venting
15. Inspect external tank surface (no frost, no damage)
16. Document H2 system status

### 8.3 H2 Venting During Parking

**If H2 venting occurs during or after parking:**

1. **Assess Venting**
   - Normal automatic venting: Expected with boiloff
   - Excessive venting: May indicate pressure issue

2. **Establish Venting Safety Zone**
   - Extend safety zone to 25 m during active venting
   - Evacuate non-essential personnel
   - Position fire watch

3. **Monitor Wind Conditions**
   - Wind speed: <15 kts for safe dispersion
   - Wind direction: Verify upward plume dispersion
   - H2 concentration: Must remain <25 ppm at ground level

4. **Vent Procedure**
   - Follow [10-10-31A_H2_Venting_Procedure.md](../h2-ground-operations/10-10-31A_H2_Venting_Procedure.md)
   - Continuous H2 monitoring during venting
   - Document vent duration and quantity

**Do not park aircraft in enclosed or semi-enclosed areas during active venting.**

## 9. Post-Parking H2 Monitoring

### 9.1 Continuous Monitoring Requirements

**Monitoring Schedule:**
- **First hour:** Continuous H2 concentration monitoring
- **1-8 hours:** H2 concentration every 15 minutes
- **8-24 hours:** H2 concentration every hour
- **>24 hours:** H2 concentration every 4 hours

**Monitored Parameters:**
- H2 concentration (both detectors)
- LH2 tank pressure
- Boiloff rate
- Weather conditions (wind, temperature, precipitation)

### 9.2 Monitoring Thresholds and Actions

| H2 Concentration | Action Required |
|------------------|-----------------|
| <25 ppm | Normal — continue monitoring |
| 25-100 ppm | Investigate source, increase monitoring frequency |
| 100-1000 ppm | Evacuate safety zone, activate H2 emergency response |
| >1000 ppm (10% LEL) | Evacuate area, activate fire/rescue, no ignition sources |

**LEL (Lower Explosive Limit) for H2 in air: 4% = 40,000 ppm**

### 9.3 Boiloff Management

**Normal Boiloff Rate:** <2% of tank capacity per day

**If boiloff exceeds normal:**
1. Check insulation integrity
2. Verify vent valve operation
3. Monitor tank pressure (must be <6 bar)
4. Consult maintenance if excessive

**Reference:** [10-10-41A_LH2_Boiloff_Management.md](../cryo-operations/10-10-41A_LH2_Boiloff_Management.md)

## 10. H2 System Safing (Long-Term Parking)

For parking >24 hours or entry into maintenance, follow H2 System Safing procedure:

**Safing Overview:**
1. Reduce LH2 tank pressure to minimum safe level
2. Verify all H2 valves in safe positions
3. Implement continuous monitoring or defuel
4. Document system configuration
5. Post safety placards

## 11. Emergency Procedures

### 11.1 H2 Leak Detection

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Activate H2 emergency alarm
2. Evacuate all personnel from 15 m safety zone
3. Alert fire/rescue services
4. Notify airport emergency services
5. H2 Safety Officer monitors from safe distance

**Do Not:**
- Enter safety zone without SCBA (Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus)
- Attempt to stop leak without proper equipment and training
- Use any ignition sources or electrical equipment

**Follow-On Actions:**
6. Approach from upwind with SCBA if safe
7. Attempt to isolate H2 source if possible
8. Continue H2 concentration monitoring
9. Document incident per [incident-report-template.md](../operations-templates/incident-report-template.md)

### 11.2 H2 Fire

**Recognition:**
- Invisible flame (use thermal imaging camera)
- Heat shimmer in air
- Scorching or melting of nearby materials
- H2 detector readings increasing rapidly

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Activate fire alarm
2. Evacuate all personnel from 25 m
3. Alert fire/rescue services immediately
4. Do not attempt to extinguish H2 fire unless trained
5. Let H2 fire burn if no exposure hazard

**Fire/Rescue Actions:**
- Water spray to cool surrounding structures (not on fire itself)
- Foam application to prevent spread to combustibles
- Allow controlled burnoff if safe

### 11.3 Cryogenic Exposure

**Personnel Exposure to LH2 or Cold Surfaces:**
1. Move person to warm area
2. Remove contaminated clothing
3. Do not rub affected areas
4. Seek medical attention immediately
5. Treat as severe frostbite

**LH2 Spill:**
1. Evacuate area (100 m radius)
2. Alert fire/rescue
3. Allow LH2 to evaporate naturally
4. Water spray may increase vaporization rate (use with caution)
5. Monitor H2 concentration continuously
