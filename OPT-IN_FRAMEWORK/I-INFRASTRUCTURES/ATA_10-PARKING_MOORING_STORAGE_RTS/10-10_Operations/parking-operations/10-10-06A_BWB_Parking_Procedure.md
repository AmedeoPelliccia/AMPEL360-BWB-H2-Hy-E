## 1. Purpose

This document provides BWB configuration-specific parking procedures, addressing the unique geometry and clearance requirements of the Blended Wing Body design.

## 5. BWB Geometry Factors

BWB configuration features: wide wingspan (>80 m typical), low wingtip height, wide center body, distributed weight. Requires enhanced spatial awareness and clearance management.

---

## 6. Clearance Requirements

Minimum clearances: 8 m from other aircraft, 10 m from buildings, 5 m from ground equipment when stationary. Wing walkers must maintain visual contact with wingtips at all times.

---

## 7. Spotter Positioning

Minimum 3 spotters: Forward left wingtip, forward right wingtip, aft center. Fourth spotter recommended for congested ramps. Spotters communicate continuously via radio.

---

## 8. Parking Limitations

Reduced turn radius during towing, lower ground clearance under center body, wider parking spot required. Consult airport parking plan for BWB-compatible positions.

---

## 9. Post-Parking Configuration

Verify wingtip clearances, center body clearance from GSE, adequate access for maintenance, proper chocking of all main gear bogies.
