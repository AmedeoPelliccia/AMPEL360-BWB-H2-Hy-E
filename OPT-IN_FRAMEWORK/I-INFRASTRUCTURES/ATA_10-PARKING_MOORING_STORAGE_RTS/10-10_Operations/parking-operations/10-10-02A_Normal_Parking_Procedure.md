## 1. Purpose

This document provides the standard procedure for parking the AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft under normal operational conditions.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Normal parking procedures for line operations
- Aircraft positioning and alignment
- Wheel chocking and securing
- Post-parking inspections and documentation

### 2.1 Applicability
- All AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft variants
- All operational environments where applicable
- Personnel trained and qualified for this procedure

⚠️ **CAUTIONS**

---

## 5. Procedure Overview

The normal parking procedure consists of aircraft positioning, securing, and post-parking verification. This procedure applies to routine line operations and short-term parking situations.

---

## 6. Personnel Requirements

Minimum 3 personnel: Ramp Coordinator, and 2 Wing Walkers/Spotters. All personnel must be qualified in ground handling operations and BWB aircraft familiarization.

---

## 7. Equipment Required

Wheel chocks (6-8), communication equipment (radios), safety cones, and marshalling wands.

---

## 8. Parking Procedure

1. Coordinate with ramp control for parking position
2. Guide aircraft to designated spot using standard marshalling signals
3. Verify aircraft positioned on centerline
4. Apply parking brake
5. Install wheel chocks per 10-10-04A
6. Conduct post-parking inspection
7. Document parking completion

---

## 9. Post-Parking Verification

Verify wheel chocks properly installed, aircraft on centerline, clearances adequate, and no FOD present. Document parking position and time.
