## 1. Purpose

This document establishes procedures for securing the AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft using tiedown equipment for extended parking or adverse weather conditions.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Tiedown point identification and usage
- Tiedown equipment specifications
- Installation and tensioning procedures
- Weather-related tiedown requirements

### 2.1 Applicability
- All AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft variants
- All operational environments where applicable
- Personnel trained and qualified for this procedure

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**

- Follow all applicable safety procedures
- Wear required personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Maintain clear communication between all personnel
- Be aware of environmental conditions and limitations

⚠️ **CAUTIONS**

- Ensure aircraft is properly secured before beginning procedure
- Verify all equipment is in good working condition
- Document any deviations from standard procedure

---

## 5. Tiedown Requirements

Tiedowns required for: winds >35 kts forecast, extended parking >72 hours, severe weather warnings, or as directed by airport authority.

---

## 6. Tiedown Points

BWB configuration includes 12 tiedown points: 4 forward wing, 4 aft wing, 2 center body, 2 empennage. Refer to aircraft structural manual for exact locations.

---

## 7. Equipment Specifications

Tiedown ropes: minimum 3/4 inch diameter, 3000 lb breaking strength. Anchors: ground anchors or approved hardstand tie rings. Hardware: galvanized steel, corrosion-resistant.

---

## 8. Installation Procedure

1. Identify tiedown points
2. Inspect tiedown equipment
3. Attach ropes to aircraft tiedown points
4. Secure to ground anchors at 45° angle
5. Tension ropes evenly
6. Verify all connections
7. Inspect every 12 hours

---

## 9. Weather Monitoring

Monitor weather conditions continuously. Adjust tiedown tension as needed. Inspect tiedowns after any weather event.
