This document defines the procedure for proper installation and removal of wheel chocks for the AMPEL360 BWB aircraft configuration.

## 5. BWB Wheel Chock Requirements

BWB aircraft requires 6-8 wheel chocks due to multiple main landing gear bogies. Chocks must be rated for aircraft weight and capable of preventing movement on 2° slope.

---

## 6. Chock Specifications

Material: high-density polyurethane or aluminum. Dimensions: minimum 12 inches length, 8 inches height, with rubber pads. Weight: minimum 15 lbs each.

---

## 7. Chocking Procedure

1. Ensure parking brake set
2. Place chocks fore and aft of each main gear wheel
3. Chocks must contact tire and ground simultaneously
4. Verify chocks seated properly
5. Minimum 2 chocks per main gear bogie
6. Document chock installation

---

## 8. Removal Procedure

1. Verify aircraft ready for movement
2. Confirm personnel clear
3. Remove chocks in reverse order of installation
4. Store chocks properly
5. Document chock removal
