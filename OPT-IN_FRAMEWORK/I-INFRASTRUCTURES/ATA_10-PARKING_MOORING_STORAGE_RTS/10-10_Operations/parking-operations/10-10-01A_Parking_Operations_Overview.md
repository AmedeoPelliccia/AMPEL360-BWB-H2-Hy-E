## 1. Purpose

This document provides an overview of parking operations for the AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft, establishing the framework, philosophy, and general procedures for safely parking aircraft in various operational scenarios.

This overview covers:
- Parking operations philosophy and safety principles
- General parking procedures and sequencing
- Personnel roles and responsibilities
- Equipment and facilities requirements
- Special considerations for H2-powered and BWB configuration aircraft
- Weather limitations and environmental factors
- Integration with airport operations

### 2.1 Applicability
- All AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft variants
- All operational environments (line operations, maintenance, storage)
- All weather conditions within operational limits

## 4. Parking Operations Philosophy

### 4.1 Safety-First Approach

All parking operations prioritize:

1. **Personnel Safety**
   - Clear communication protocols
   - Designated safety zones
   - Personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements
   - H2 safety awareness and training

2. **Aircraft Safety**
   - Protection of critical systems
   - Prevention of ground damage
   - Safeguarding of H2/cryogenic systems
   - Environmental protection

3. **Operational Efficiency**
   - Standardized procedures
   - Coordinated team operations
   - Effective use of GSE
   - Minimal turnaround time

### 4.2 Risk Management

Parking operations incorporate:
- Pre-parking hazard assessments
- Continuous risk monitoring
- Emergency response readiness
- Weather impact evaluation

## 6. Personnel Requirements

### 6.1 Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities | Qualifications |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| **Ramp Coordinator** | Overall parking supervision, coordination | Ramp operations certification |
| **Wing Walker / Spotter** | Clearance monitoring, communication | Ground handling training |
| **Tow Operator** | Aircraft towing (if required) | Tow operator certification, BWB qualified |
| **H2 Safety Officer** | H2 system monitoring, safety zone enforcement | H2 safety training Level 3+ |
| **Maintenance Technician** | Post-parking system checks | A&P License or equivalent |

### 6.2 Minimum Staffing

**Normal Parking:** 3 personnel (Coordinator + 2 spotters)  
**H2 Aircraft Parking:** 4 personnel (Coordinator + 2 spotters + H2 Safety Officer)  
**BWB Configuration:** Add 1 additional spotter for wingspan clearance

## 7. Equipment and Facilities

### 7.1 Required Equipment

| Equipment | Specification | Quantity | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|----------|-------|
| Wheel Chocks | BWB-compatible, min 2 per main gear | 6-8 | See 10-10-04A |
| Tow Bar | BWB-specific towbar | 1 | If towing required |
| Communication Equipment | Two-way radios | 3-4 | Per personnel |
| H2 Detector (Portable) | 4-20 mA output, 0-1000 ppm range | 2 | H2 operations |
| Fire Extinguisher | Class D (metal fires), 20 lb min | 2 | H2 operations |
| Safety Cones/Barriers | Reflective, high-visibility | 12+ | Safety zone marking |

### 7.2 Parking Position Requirements

**Surface Requirements:**
- Level surface (max 2° slope)
- Adequate load-bearing capacity for BWB weight distribution
- Clear of FOD (Foreign Object Debris)
- Proper drainage for weather conditions

**Clearances:**
- Minimum 10 m (33 ft) from buildings/structures
- Minimum 5 m (16 ft) from other aircraft (conventional)
- Minimum 8 m (26 ft) from other aircraft (BWB wingspan consideration)
- Minimum 15 m (49 ft) H2 safety zone from ignition sources

**Utilities:**
- Ground power access (if required)
- Fire suppression equipment within 50 m
- Emergency vehicle access

## 8. H2/Cryogenic Considerations

### 8.1 Pre-Parking H2 Assessment

Before parking H2-powered aircraft:

1. **System Status Check**
   - H2 fuel quantity and pressure
   - Boiloff rate and venting status
   - Cryogenic insulation integrity
   - Leak detection system status

2. **Environmental Assessment**
   - Wind direction and speed
   - Ventilation adequacy
   - Proximity to ignition sources
   - Emergency response availability

3. **Safety Zone Establishment**
   - 15 m minimum safety zone from ignition sources
   - Personnel access control
   - H2 detector placement and verification
   - Fire suppression equipment positioning

**Reference:** [10-10-33A_H2_Safety_Zone_Setup.md](../h2-ground-operations/10-10-33A_H2_Safety_Zone_Setup.md)

### 8.2 Continuous Monitoring

During parking of H2 aircraft:
- H2 concentration monitoring (continuous)
- Boiloff pressure monitoring (every 15 min)
- Weather condition monitoring (continuous)
- Visual inspection (every hour)

## 9. BWB Configuration Considerations

### 9.1 Unique Geometry Factors

The BWB configuration requires special attention to:

1. **Wingspan**
   - Wide wingspan (>80 m typical) requires enhanced clearance
   - Low wingtip height increases ground contact risk
   - Wide turn radius during towing

2. **Center Body**
   - Wide center body affects ground equipment positioning
   - Multiple access points for maintenance
   - Distributed weight requires proper chocking

3. **Ground Clearance**
   - Lower overall profile than conventional aircraft
   - Increased risk of GSE contact with undersurface
   - Specialized jacking points

### 9.2 Spotter Positioning

**BWB-Specific Spotter Positions:**
- **Forward Left Wingtip**: Monitor left wing clearance
- **Forward Right Wingtip**: Monitor right wing clearance
- **Aft Center**: Monitor tail clearance and overall positioning
- **Optional 4th Spotter**: Complex parking environments

**Reference:** [10-10-06A_BWB_Parking_Procedure.md](./10-10-06A_BWB_Parking_Procedure.md)

## 10. Weather Limitations

### 10.1 Wind Limitations

| Condition | Maximum Wind | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Normal Parking | 35 kts | Any direction |
| Normal Parking (Crosswind) | 25 kts | Across wingspan |
| H2 Venting Operations | 15 kts | Safe dispersion required |
| Towing Operations | 20 kts | Reduced for BWB |

### 10.2 Environmental Limitations

- **Temperature:** -40°C to +50°C operational envelope
- **Precipitation:** Avoid heavy precipitation during H2 venting
- **Visibility:** Minimum 500 m for safe positioning
- **Lightning:** No parking during lightning within 5 NM

## 11. Emergency Procedures

### 11.1 H2 Leak During Parking

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Activate emergency H2 response
2. Evacuate personnel from safety zone (15 m minimum)
3. Alert fire/rescue services
4. Monitor H2 concentration
5. Initiate H2 system safing (if safe to approach)

**Reference:** [10-10-35A_H2_System_Safing.md](../h2-ground-operations/10-10-35A_H2_System_Safing.md)

### 11.2 Ground Contact / Collision

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Stop all movement
2. Assess damage
3. Notify maintenance and safety
4. Secure aircraft
5. Document incident

### 11.3 Weather Deterioration

**Actions:**
1. Assess continued parking viability
2. Implement enhanced securing (tiedown) if required
3. Monitor weather forecasts
4. Prepare for mooring operations if necessary

**Reference:** [10-10-03A_Tiedown_Procedure.md](./10-10-03A_Tiedown_Procedure.md)

## 12. Documentation and Records

### 12.1 Required Documentation

All parking operations must document:
- Aircraft identification and parking position
- Personnel names and roles
- H2 system status (if applicable)
- Weather conditions at parking time
- Equipment used
- Any discrepancies or non-standard conditions

### 12.2 Record Retention

Parking records retained per:
- Standard parking: 30 days
- H2 operations parking: 2 years
- Incident-related parking: 7 years
