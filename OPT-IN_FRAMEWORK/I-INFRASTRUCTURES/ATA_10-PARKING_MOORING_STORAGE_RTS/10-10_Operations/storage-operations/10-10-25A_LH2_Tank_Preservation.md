## 5. Controlled Warmup

LH2 tank warmup rate: maximum 5 K/hour to prevent thermal stress. Monitor temperature gradient. Allow tank to reach ambient temperature naturally.

---

## 6. Purge and Inert

After warmup, purge with dry nitrogen (dewpoint <-40°C). Maintain positive pressure 0.1-0.2 bar. Verify dryness before sealing.

---

## 7. Insulation Protection

Protect vacuum insulation from moisture ingress. Maintain vacuum pressure <0.01 mbar if possible. Cover external surfaces.
