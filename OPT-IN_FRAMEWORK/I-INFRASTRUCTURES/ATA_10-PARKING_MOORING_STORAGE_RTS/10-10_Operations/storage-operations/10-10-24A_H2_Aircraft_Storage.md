---

## 5. H2 System Safing

Complete defueling of LH2 tanks required for storage >7 days. Purge with dry nitrogen or helium. Verify no residual H2. Seal and protect all H2 system components.

---

## 6. LH2 Tank Preservation

After defueling: warmup tank slowly (controlled rate), purge with dry inert gas, maintain slight positive pressure, protect vacuum insulation, seal all penetrations.
