---

## 4. Jacking Points

Refer to structural manual for exact locations. BWB typically has 8-12 jacking points distributed across center body and wing structure. Must use all points for full jacking.

---

## 5. Procedure

1. Position jacks at designated points
2. Raise jacks evenly in sequence
3. Monitor aircraft level continuously
4. Install safety stands
5. Verify load distribution
