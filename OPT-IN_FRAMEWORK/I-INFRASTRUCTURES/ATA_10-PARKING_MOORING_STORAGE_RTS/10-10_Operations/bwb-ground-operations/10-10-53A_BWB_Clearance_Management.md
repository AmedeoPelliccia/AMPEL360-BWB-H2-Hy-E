## 1. Purpose

Spatial clearance management for BWB aircraft.

---

## 2. Scope

- Wingspan clearance
- Height clearance
- Obstacle avoidance
- Verification

---

## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Requirements

Wide wingspan requires 8+ m clearance from obstacles. Low wingtip height increases ground contact risk. Center body width affects GSE positioning.

---

## 5. Verification

Visual verification by spotters at both wingtips. Measurement if uncertain. Document clearances for congested ramps.
