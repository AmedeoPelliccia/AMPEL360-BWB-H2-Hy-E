## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Procedure

BWB requires specialized towbar attached to center body. Minimum 3 spotters (both wingtips + aft). Maximum tow speed: 5 km/h due to wingspan. Turn radius limited to prevent wingtip contact.

---

## 5. Clearances

Verify 8 m clearance both wingtips before movement. Spotter radio communication continuous. Stop immediately if clearance uncertain.
