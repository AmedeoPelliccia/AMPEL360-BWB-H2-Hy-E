## 2. Scope

---

## 3. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**: Follow all safety procedures and wear required PPE.

---

## 4. Positioning

BWB center body width and low profile restrict GSE approach paths. Position GSE to avoid wingtips and undersurface contact. Maintain access to multiple entry/service points.

---

## 5. Equipment

Passenger stairs, cargo loaders, refueling equipment, ground power must all be BWB-compatible and positioned carefully.
