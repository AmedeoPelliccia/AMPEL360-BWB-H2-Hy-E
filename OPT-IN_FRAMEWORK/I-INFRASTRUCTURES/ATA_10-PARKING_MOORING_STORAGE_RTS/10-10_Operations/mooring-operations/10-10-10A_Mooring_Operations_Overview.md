## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**

- Follow all applicable safety procedures
- Wear required personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Maintain clear communication between all personnel
- Be aware of environmental conditions and limitations

⚠️ **CAUTIONS**

- Ensure aircraft is properly secured before beginning procedure
- Verify all equipment is in good working condition
- Document any deviations from standard procedure

---

## 5. Mooring Requirements

Aircraft must be moored when winds exceed 35 kts or as required by forecast conditions. BWB configuration requires specialized mooring procedures due to large wingspan and unique geometry.

---

## 6. Equipment

Mooring ropes, anchors, hardware per specifications. Minimum 12 mooring points for BWB aircraft.

---

## 7. Procedure Overview

Install mooring equipment at designated points, tension properly, inspect regularly during moored period.
