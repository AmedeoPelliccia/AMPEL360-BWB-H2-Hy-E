## 1. Purpose

Standard mooring procedure for routine weather conditions and extended parking.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers:
- Normal mooring installation
- Equipment setup
- Tensioning
- Inspection

### 2.1 Applicability
- All AMPEL360 BWB H2-Hybrid Electric aircraft variants
- All operational environments where applicable
- Personnel trained and qualified for this procedure

## 4. Safety Precautions

⚠️ **WARNINGS**

- Follow all applicable safety procedures
- Wear required personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Maintain clear communication between all personnel
- Be aware of environmental conditions and limitations

⚠️ **CAUTIONS**

- Ensure aircraft is properly secured before beginning procedure
- Verify all equipment is in good working condition
- Document any deviations from standard procedure

---

## 5. Installation

1. Identify 12 mooring points on BWB
2. Attach mooring ropes to aircraft
3. Secure to ground anchors at proper angle
4. Tension evenly
5. Inspect connections

---

## 6. Inspection Schedule

Inspect mooring every 12 hours during moored period. Check rope tension, connection integrity, and anchor security.
