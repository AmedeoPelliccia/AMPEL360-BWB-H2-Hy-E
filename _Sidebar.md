## LIFE-CYCLE CHANNELS (LC)

### LC-01 — Design & Systems Engineering

**Home:** [[LC-01_Design_Engineering_Home]]
**Scope:** Airframe, systems, propulsion, software, integration and performance engineering.
**Typical roles:** Airframe designers, system engineers, performance engineers, software/controls engineers, integration leads.

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-01A_Airframe_and_Structures]] — Geometry, loads, FEM/CFD interfaces, structural design and weight control.
* [[LC-01B_Systems_and_Equipment]] — ATA 20–49 systems design, equipment selection, installation, interfaces.
* [[LC-01C_Flight_Controls_and_Auto_Flight]] — FBW, control laws, autopilot/flight director, handling qualities.
* [[LC-01D_Software_Control_and_Embedded]] — Control/embedded SW design, real-time architectures, safety SW.
* [[LC-01E_Integration_Architecture_and_Performance]] — System integration, aircraft-level architecture, performance.

---

### LC-02 — Certification & Authorities

**Home:** [[LC-02_Certification_Home]]
**Scope:** Safety assessment, DAL allocation, MoCs, authority interaction, Type Certification.
**Typical roles:** DERs, certification engineers, safety analysts, authority liaisons, chief engineers.

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-02A_Safety_and_Hazards]] — FHA, PSSA, SSA, CCA, hazard logs, DAL allocation.
* [[LC-02B_V_and_V_and_Means_of_Compliance]] — V&V planning, MoCs, coverage, test evidence and traceability.
* [[LC-02C_Authorities_Interfaces_and_Audits]] — CRIs, authority Q&A, findings, audits, formal submissions.

---

### LC-03 — Operations & MRO

**Home:** [[LC-03_Operations_MRO_Home]]
**Scope:** Flight operations, ground/turnaround operations, line/base maintenance, reliability.
**Typical roles:** Airline ops, OCC, dispatch, turnaround coordinators, maintenance planners, MRO engineers.

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-03A_Flight_Operations]] — Dispatch, flight planning, fleet status, operational constraints.
* [[LC-03B_Ground_and_Turnaround_Operations]] — Turnarounds, ramp operations, H₂/GSE, circularity on ground.
* [[LC-03C_MRO_and_Workcards]] — Task definitions, workcards, packages, maintenance programs, reliability.

---

### LC-04 — Data, AI & Digital Product Passport (DPP)

**Home:** [[LC-04_Data_AI_DPP_Home]]
**Scope:** Data schemas, pipelines, AI/ML models, DPP registry, AI governance and monitoring.
**Typical roles:** Data engineers, AI/ML engineers, MLOps, DPP owners, AI safety/governance.

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-04A_Data_Engineering_and_Schemas]] — Canonical schemas, data contracts, lineage, quality.
* [[LC-04B_AI_ML_Models_and_Training]] — Model design/training, evaluation, serving, model cards.
* [[LC-04C_DPP_and_AI_Governance]] — DPP templates, AI policies, risk, regulatory and audit mapping.

---

### LC-05 — Organization & Management

**Home:** [[LC-05_Org_Management_Home]]
**Scope:** Program governance, strategy, policies, risk, decision-support.
**Typical roles:** Program managers, senior management, PMO, governance boards, risk managers.

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-05A_Program_and_Portfolio_Management]] — Roadmaps, milestones, portfolio and scope management.
* [[LC-05B_Governance_and_Policies]] — Policies, standards, decision frameworks, governance bodies.
* [[LC-05C_Risk_and_Decision_Support]] — Risk registers, dashboards, decision-support views, scenario analyses.

---

### LC-06 — Resources & Finance

**Home:** [[LC-06_Resources_Finance_Home]]
**Scope:** Costs, industrialization, supply chain, investment and business cases.
**Typical roles:** Finance, controlling, procurement, industrialization, strategy, investors.

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-06A_Costing_and_Business_Cases]] — CAPEX/OPEX models, business cases, unit cost, ROI.
* [[LC-06B_Supply_Chain_and_Industrialization]] — Supplier network, rates, ramp-up, make/buy, logistics.
* [[LC-06C_Investment_and_Funding_Views]] — Funding rounds, investor decks, risk/return profiles.

---

### LC-07 — Passengers & Community

**Home:** [[LC-07_Passengers_Community_Home]]
**Scope:** Passenger experience, public-facing safety/sustainability, community impact.
**Typical roles:** Customer experience, communications, marketing, community relations, NGOs, regulators (public-facing).

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-07A_Passenger_Information_and_Journey]] — Passenger journey, cabin experience, informational materials.
* [[LC-07B_Community_and_Noise_Impact]] — Community noise/impact views, corridors, mitigation strategies.
* [[LC-07C_Public_Sustainability_Communication]] — Carbon-negative narrative, sustainability metrics, public reports.

---

### LC-08 — Crew & Medical Operations

**Home:** [[LC-08_Crew_Medical_Home]]
**Scope:** Flight and cabin crew operations, training, medical and aeromedical procedures.
**Typical roles:** Flight crew, cabin crew, trainers, company medical, aeromedical experts.

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-08A_Flight_Crew]] — Flight crew procedures, checklists, QRH-derived material (non-reg text layer).
* [[LC-08B_Cabin_Crew]] — Cabin procedures, safety demos, service flows, emergency roles.
* [[LC-08C_Medical_and_Aeromedical]] — Medical equipment, medical protocols, aeromedical constraints.

---

### LC-09 — Agentic & Manufacturing Systems (Non-Human)

**Home:** [[LC-09_Agentic_Manufacturing_Home]]
**Scope:** Non-human consumers of data: MES, robots, agents, optimization engines.
**Typical roles:** MES/automation engineers, digital factory, agentic systems developers (plus the agents themselves).

**Subchannels:**

* [[LC-09A_Manufacturing_and_Robotics_Systems]] — Manufacturing/assembly robots, MES interfaces, cell schemas.
* [[LC-09B_Agentic_Orchestration_and_Optimization]] — Multi-agent orchestration, schedulers, optimizers.
* [[LC-09C_External_Platforms_and_Integrations]] — External platforms/APIs, partner systems, ecosystem integration.
